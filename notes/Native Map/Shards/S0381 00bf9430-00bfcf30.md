## Classes and subsystems

- **game.movement:** 00bfba00, 00bfba80, 00bfba20, 00bfbaa0, 00bfbca0, 00bfbd70, and 00bfbde0 cover the ProxiedCharacterMovementController base and its rail controller. 00bfbaa0 advances one route cursor, clamps it to the route length, computes a transform, and releases the route state at its end.
- **game.movement:** 00bfbfb0, 00bfbe10, 00bfc0e0, and 00bfc170 cover the relative-rail controller. It stores two route references and progresses two clamped cursors before applying a transform.
- **game.movement:** 00bfc190, 00bfc280, 00bfc3e0, 00bfc440, and 00bfc460 cover the seek controller and its embedded Target. 00bfc190 resets target payload through the Target subobject; 00bfc460 smooths speed and updates the controlled object's transform. 00bfc1c0 computes point-to-clamped-segment distance for that update (engine.math).
- **game.character:** 00bfb4d0, 00bfb550, 00bfb5c0, 00bfb5e0, and 00bfb600 implement PlayerSwitchProfileEvent construction, cleanup, flag access, and conditional deletion. 00bfb620 checks profile-required slots, reports a missing item, then emits local profile and animation events.
- **ui.datasource:** 00bfca90 is CharacterDataSource's field-name switch. It maps indices 0..0x1b to 28 labels spanning identity, wallet/progression, membership, demographics, and social-profile fields. 00bfcc70, 00bfcd30, 00bfcdd0, 00bfce80, and 00bfcf30 route values through 010cb770, whose body constructs the _OnDataChanged Lua parameters. Numeric selectors 0, 2, 3, 10, and 12 are visible; their meanings remain unknown.

## Wire and server-contract hypothesis

00bf9430 is the only shard target with an observed packet path. In one gated movement branch it constructs PacketZoneSafeTeleportRequest (00bf7a50) and calls the guarded tunnel builder (00bf9410); that path reaches the generic gateway send helper. The packet constructor stores opcode 0x7a (122) and no payload. wire therefore records a client send.

The original-server response is **hypothesis only**. [[Research/Pieces/058 Death and revive]] records two community emulator handlers that answer the request with ClientUpdatePacketUpdateLocation 38/12 and teleport enabled; they are third-party reconstructions. Neither the native client nor those emulators establish original server logic. The same note and [[Research/Pieces/077 Warpstones and travel]] report no preserved original capture for opcode 122. No contradiction found.
