## Security

Threat note (secure-code-guardian, security-reviewer). Every trust boundary the replay touches:

- **Role to display and staff jobs (login).** The referee flag and the staff-job grant come only from the
  account-store role read by `bind_account_role`. Only the host calls it, after the handoff ticket and the
  character owner are verified (`select_character_from_login`). No client field feeds either. An
  unreadable store gives role Player, so no flag and no grant (fails closed through `refresh_role`). L042R2's
  authority path is untouched: every staff command still re-reads the role per message, and the flag and
  jobs grant no command, item, coin, XP, quest gate or trainer.
- **Client 26/13 job select.** The only client input is the existing length-checked i32. A player's 58 or
  20 takes the existing "not been unlocked" refusal with nothing changed (L052's spoofed-select test,
  canary L052-role). The reply does not depend on whether the id is a staff job, so it is no new oracle.
- **Reward reloads (resolution 2).** A minigame, claim or quest payout replaces the saved progression from
  a server-computed `RewardState` and copies only the session's staff part from the live `Profiles`. The
  payout cannot grant staff jobs: `adopt` copies what the session already has, and with
  `current = None` it yields none (L052 test). It cannot drop them either (new test, canary R1). The tip's
  `npc_quest_profiles_changed` still runs, so a level crossing credits its goal (canary R2). A refused
  payout (no profile content) still changes nothing (`fishing_catch_without_profile_content_changes_no_reward_state`).
- **Saves (L036 pets in the document).** The staff part is not in `ProfileState`, so neither the character
  document nor the pets field can hold 58, 20 or the outfit. `Profiles::load` refuses a staff id as a
  player job. Saves still go through `game_store::save` (atomic replace), and the replay adds no write path.
  L052's save compare passes with the pets field present.
- **Enumeration.** Unchanged and by design (OSFR): viewers in range see a staff character's referee flag,
  and the staff job while it is worn. It depends on neither the viewer nor any request.
- **Logs.** No new log line, audit row or file. No secrets, names or chat text in code, tests or notes.
