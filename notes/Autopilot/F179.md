## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

- Hostile input: a 165/2 may name up to 4,096 arbitrary, duplicated or undefined ids; a client may
  repeat it in up to 128 bundle children; a bundle may mix a combat or NPC interaction child with
  the large reads.
- Refusals: a malformed or oversized id list gets one empty incomplete 165/3; a request whose
  uncaptured records exceed 4 MiB gets the same answer before any record is copied (the scan holds
  borrowed rows and a saturating byte count, then copies only after the check); a whole bundle or
  a split child whose staged replies cross 8 MiB gets the outbound-budget error before the
  crossing child commits, and only that player's session ends. A whole bundle's staged clone is
  discarded on refusal; a split's earlier ordered children are committed by W116 and the notes
  state that. The shared NPC menu is inside the staged `Gameplay` (F155), so the menu refusal
  changes nothing.
- Bounds: one child's definition payload is at most 4 MiB; the split sender queue is 32 MiB; the
  session budget counter uses `saturating_add`, so a hostile total cannot wrap. No new item,
  coin, gift, price or quantity path is opened; the existing merchant/owned-instance/quantity and
  server-price checks are unchanged.
- No player names or chat text are used in the code, tests or notes.
