## Overview

Lakeshore is a lake town "northeast of Sanctuary, southwest of Shrouded Glade", "surrounded mostly by water". The wiki lists Rainbow Lake fishing, the Lakeshore well, the Durango Zoo, Lakeshore Fields farming, Checkers & Chess, jump pads, a cooking table with a crafting station, and the Robgoblin Treasure Trove as the nearest dungeon (wiki Lakeshore, rev 2023-04). [wiki] The client calls the town "Lakeshore", "Lakeshore Village", "Lakeshore Fishing Village" and "the fishing town of Lakeshore" (4003517562, 2754441877, 2249462015) and names Lakeshore Fields / "Lakesfield" for harvesting (1123250316), the Lakeshore Pier and Frog Lilies, the Lakeshore Docks (3842100636), a Lakeshore Warpstone (3725731521) and the launch pads with a fiery hoop (872876111). [client] Position words in the client: "northeast of Sanctuary" (3189575644, Bry), "southeast of Snowhill" (9582519, Merri; 3628813876, Norvin), "southwest of Shrouded Glade" (wiki), "Thunder Falls ... southeast of Lakeshore" (4187361903), Robgoblin Camp "north" and "northwest" of the town (501743235, 1910421005), and a second camp (Raeya and Ivan) "just south of Lakeshore" (1902395840, 2194763594). [client]

How to read this page:
- **Exact NPC names are the client's `ucdt` name strings.** The number after each name is its `ucdt` id. Where the client has a second `ucdt` row for the same name, both are shown.
- Tags: `[client]` locale text, `[video]` Haiku frame reads (frame key shown), `[wiki]` fan wiki. Client beats video beats wiki. A name that only the wiki gives is marked **wiki-only**. `[client name]` means the client has the name but no sentence placing the NPC.
- A client name entry proves the name exists, **not** that the NPC stands in Lakeshore. Standing place comes from a client sentence ("in Lakeshore"), a video frame, or the wiki; each row says which.
- Quest links go to the job page that holds the full row. Quests that belong to no job are in [[#Town and area quests]] below. The client stores quest titles and descriptions apart, so a link between a title and a description is "by wording" unless stated.
- Per the coordinator's instruction, the **Robgoblin Camp**, **Forest Troll Village** and **Thunder Falls Speedway** NPCs are not repeated here. [[NPCs - Wilds]] holds their full rows; [[#Cross-references to NPCs - Wilds]] lists them with the reason each is tied to Lakeshore.
- The **Durango Zoo** has its own placeholder ([[NPCs - Durango Zoo]]) and is left out. The wiki says its owner Sam is "located in Lakeshore" (wiki Sam, rev 2013-07).
- Player names, player chat and player-chosen pet or lot names are never recorded.

## Notes: quest givers and trainers

- **Ty** starts and gates [[Ninja]] (level 10). His hut stands by the water; the quest line sends the player north-east to the Forest Troll Village and Fort over the pixie kidnappings, then to Chloe in Blackspore, Snowhill and back.
- **Fizzlesticks** (Robgoblin Camp, see [[NPCs - Wilds]]) starts [[Wizard]]; **Natali** supplies the four repeatable contracts that level it.
- **Rourke Ironfist** and **Chaz Strongarm** are the Brawler Club: Rourke gates level 20 and sells the club weapon; Chaz sets the work after joining. The wiki marks the Rourke quests members only.
- **Bry** is the Lakeshore stop of [[Card Duelist]]: three duel quests, and a tournament rematch after Garrisoning Garrison ("Bry will continue playing in Lakeshore", 1359032719).
- **Merri De'Flor** ties Fisherman, Chef and Demo Derby together. Her customers (the "Serve" list) are Lakeshore's regulars: Chaz, Lacy Trembletoes, Bry, Waterwings, Lan Spryleaf, Kimi and Bluerose.
- **Bluerose, Selvi and Waterwings** are the fishing helpers; **Mister Nooklings** and **Norvin** carry the delivery and forging side.
- **Lan Spryleaf, Miles Sourpetal and the guards** (Tyn Redwing, Mort McFly, Len Tyril) carry the robgoblin theft story that Campaign 4 may build on.

## Conflicts

1. **Robgoblin Camp position.** Wiki: "northwest of Lakeshore" and "located in Lakeshore" (Fizzlesticks, Bizzie). Client: "north of Lakeshore", "northwest", "near Lakeshore", "just outside Lakeshore" (1172705421), and "northeast of the Greenwood Camp" (1849268972). A different camp (Raeya, Ivan) is "just south of Lakeshore". [[NPCs - Wilds]] keeps the camp; this page only cross-references it.
2. **Thunder Falls ownership.** The wiki NPC pages and job pages say Lakeshore; the wiki Central Wilds list names it as a separate site; the client says "southeast of Lakeshore" and Laurin "to the north" (47260073, from Stillwater Crossing). Not settled. Kept in [[NPCs - Wilds]].
3. **Thunder Falls spelling.** Wiki Kart Driver page: "Thunderfalls Speedway"; wiki merchant pages: "Thunder Falls Raceway" and "Speedway"; client: "Thunder Falls Speedway" (3678127570) and old "Sacred Grove Speedway/Raceway near Lakeshore" (1777152555, 3565340124). Client used.
4. **Forest Troll Village and Grosh.** The [[Ninja]] page sends readers to this page for Grosh. Client puts the village "northeast of the lake"; [[NPCs - Wilds]] keeps him. Not duplicated here.
5. **Chaz's surname and race.** Name entries and most text say "Chaz Strongarm". One text says "Chaz Armstrong" (609969195) and an asset reads "human_m_chaz_armstrong" (2066002209), while "fairy_m_chaz_strongarm" (1256018269) is a pixie. Wiki: pixie. Strongarm and pixie used.
6. **Ty's town.** Wiki text: Lakeshore. Wiki infobox: Lakeshore and Seaside. Client: Lakeshore (163808205). [[NPCs - Seaside]] lists him under its Part H. Lakeshore used.
7. **Natali has no client location.** The wiki puts her in Lakeshore; the client has only name rows and quest text. The row here is wiki placement.
8. **Queen of the Forest Troll Fort.** Ty's quest text calls her "Vera" (1454027080); other text "Verda" (78392849, 1678948563) and "Varda" (350466328). Name entry "Queen Verda" used (see [[Forest Troll Fort]]).
9. **Hildegarde.** Name entries "Hildegarde" (2692431280) and "Wizard Hildegarde" (2259903998); one text spells "Hidegarde" (3434283447). Name entries used.
10. **Kim and Katie/Kati McScuttle.** Name entry "Kim McScuttle" only; texts say "Kati" and "Katie". Whether they are one NPC or two sisters is unknown.
11. **Lakeshore and Snowhill geography.** Client: Merri and Norvin "southeast of Snowhill"; wiki and Bry's line: "northeast of Sanctuary". Not a contradiction by itself but the two reference points are used by different sources.
12. **Mino Tendersole.** Resolved: [[NPCs - Briarwood]] now ties him to his mother Dori Tendersole of Lakeshore. The client puts the search start at the waterfall east of Lakeshore and the follow-up in Briarwood's hedge maze; it never states his home zone, only where he plays. [client]
13. **Mister Nooklings in video.** One nameplate reads "Mistie Nooklings" (`0IaICGnTWWQ`); client spelling used.
14. **Wiki merchants versus the town list.** The wiki Lakeshore page lists a Ninja clothing and weapons shop but no Ninja merchant is tied to the town; Coiled Viper is filed under Northern Wilds. Not resolved.
15. **Kimi/Kristane/Alexander location.** All say "near the Lakeshore Cooking Table" (wiki); Keren and River say "south of the Thunder Falls Raceway". The merchant dates (Aug to Oct 2013) come only from wiki trivia.
