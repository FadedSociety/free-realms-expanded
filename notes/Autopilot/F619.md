## Open finding for the lead (not fixed; outside this brief)

**`realm reload behaviours` strips the shared field enemies' behaviours (R: from code and the probe,
not checked natively).** Every production gameplay takes its shared fields at load
(`bin/osfr-gateway.rs:952,1023,3391`), which leaves the engine's `field_sim.enemies` empty
(`encounter/shared.rs:296`). `load_behaviours` -> `rebuild_behaviours` (`encounter.rs:1128-1160`)
rebuilds `behaviour_of` only from dungeons, world enemies and `field_sim.enemies`. The probe showed
the staged wolf behaviour as `None` after the load. The behaviours target has no shared-field guard,
so a standalone `realm reload behaviours` succeeds. After it, whenever that session is the shared
tick's driver (`bin/osfr-gateway.rs:3118-3123`), every shared field enemy falls back to
`Behaviour::default()` (`encounter/field.rs:508`). There are two possible fixes in the
`encounter.rs` hub: rebuild from the template actors of the shared guids, or refuse the behaviours
reload while the world owns the field. Both need a lead decision. My test asserts `!=` on the staged
behaviour, so it stays green after either fix.
