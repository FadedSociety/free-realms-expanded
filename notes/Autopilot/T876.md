## The client fact the rule rests on

`ClientAreaData::Enable` (0x00bed0c0, disassembled): it raises the reference count at +0x1b0. On the first reference, an
area whose parent id (+0x1ac) is -1 notifies its own property listeners (+0x190 list: music, ambience, effects). An area
WITH a parent id instead looks the parent up (0x008b80a0, the lookup opcode 89 uses) and calls the parent's Enable. So
entering a child area plays the parent's music and ambience, and a child has no behaviour of its own: a music area's real
extent is the area plus all its descendants. `SnowHill_Bed_2..25` are children of `Snowhill_Bed_1` (id 3); `TheWild_Bed_*`
are children of `TheWild_Bed_1` (id 88). The T753 grouping used only top-level rows and so ignored every child.

## Rule

- Place shape = the T753 prefix group (unchanged, 590 rows reproduced byte for byte) plus the family (root and every
  `parentAreaId` descendant, enabled, positive-radius sphere or box) of each top-level group area that carries a
  `Music(Background)` or `Sound(Background)` property. The `*_Bed*` areas are exactly this where they exist.
- Region 2..10 shape = union of those families over the places whose `region_id` is the region. Region 1 stays the client
  Newbiezone sphere (already a client row). Ties, priorities (100 / 50 / 30 / 10 / 5) and `place_at` / `region_at` code are
  unchanged.
- New place: Pixie Nursery (`Global.Text.18535`; client area `Pixie_Nursery` id 90 carries Music(Background); 12 prefix rows).
