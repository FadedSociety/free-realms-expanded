## Overview

**The client barely names the zoo's people, and the video archive never shows the zoo.** About 60 client strings mention the zoo (41 of them say "Durango"). They hold ten individual names and one generic entry. The wiki has a place page and two NPC pages (Sam and Mr. Miles, both last edited 15 July 2013) and nothing else. No video frame carries a zoo zone banner, a zoo warpstone prompt or a nameplate that is certainly at the zoo. The page is short for that reason.

- **What the wiki says.** "The Durango Zoo is a public open-air zoo with fenced enclosures for animals located on the northern banks of Lakeshore. It is a place where people can see the wild animals of Sacred Grove such as bears, antelopes, and more. The zookeeper of Durango Zoo is Sam Durango." Infobox: type Zoo, location Lakeshore. The wiki Lakeshore page lists it as a point of interest and the wiki World page lists it as its own "town and village" entry. A goat photo carries the caption "Bucks in the Durango Zoo". [wiki Durango Zoo, rev 2026-06-27; wiki Lakeshore; wiki World; wiki Goats]
- **What the client says about where it is.** "Durango Zoo, just southwest of Shrouded Glade" (2665998091); from the Shrouded Glade side, "Durango Zoo to the southwest" (1252591188); "Lakeshore has a farm just south of the zoo across the bridge" (581721380); "the well just north of Durango Zoo" (628942850); "Old Gregory at his camp just north of Durango Zoo" (973743465); from the Archer line, "Durango Zoo to the north" (4190297834). Wandering bears frighten "the tourists in Lakeshore" (3191433081). [client]
- **What is inside.** Client exhibit names: the Bear and Goat Exhibits (2540198350), the Antelope Pen (877368778), the Overlook Bridge (502393034), the Squirrel Tree (2323539137) and a Bixie Defense table "in the middle of Durango Zoo" (1933120559). The Park Tour from Sanctuary ("Use the park guide to visit all of the exhibits in Durango Zoo.", 3925096968) walks the player past these, ending at "the Lakeshore Pier and Frog Lilies" (352122327). Visitor chatter: "Wow, lots of goats here at the zoo" (3198132802); "Why we here at zoo to see squirrels? I see them in our yard." (4089848086); "Sonya and I are going to spend a couple days at the Durango Zoo. I hear they let you feed the bears there!" (2157080451). [client]
- **A community event** starts here: "Jump on your ride and bring your pets! It's a stampede from Durango Zoo to Seaside." (273240694; title "Community Event: Stampede!", 1412550682). [client]

Row counts: Part A 7 rows, B 1, C 3: 11 NPC rows (one is the generic "Zoo Keeper" entry). The cross-reference table has 14 rows and the quest table 18 rows.

## Notes: quest givers and trainers

- **Mr. Miles** is the one zoo NPC the job pages use. He is the second step of the Archer smuggler chain ([[Archer]], rows 20 and 21), and the only zoo NPC who has a wiki page and a video chat line. His bear job (Bears on the Loose!) is the best-supported zoo-only quest.
- **Shana Hailglow, Nugglewug, Zanna Sugarzing and Tom Shalsey** each own one small errand. Their quests have exact client titles for two of them (Run off the Robgoblins, Glenn's Gone!); Zanna's and Tom's are matched by wording.
- **Old Gregory** has the longest chain at the zoo. It runs through three places: his camp, the Memorial Caverns (Lorna) and Shrouded Glade (Fedrick), so a build needs those two zones' NPCs before it can complete.
- **Sam Durango** is a name with almost no content: one wiki line, one client name and one "Return to Sam Durango" hand-in.
- **Harold and Hewey** are a zoo story that plays in Highroad Junction. The zoo page only points at them.

## Conflicts

1. **Where the zoo is.** Wiki: "northern banks of Lakeshore" and Location Lakeshore; the wiki World page lists it as a separate town.
