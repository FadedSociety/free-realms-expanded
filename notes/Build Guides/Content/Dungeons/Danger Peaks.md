## Conflicts

1. **Goal panel.** The wiki lists a bonus goal ("Release the 10 Caged Birds") but the video panel reads "Goals (1)" with the Taka goal only. The client has the string "Release the 10 Caged Birds." and a bonus group. Possible reasons: the bonus appears later, or the recorded run was an earlier build. Unresolved. The client string is kept as the bonus text.
2. **Panel description.** The wiki quote is not in the client. Other client descriptions exist (see Overview).
3. **Ninja count.** The video quest "Bad Ninjas" counts to 10, the client text elsewhere says twelve (`loc 1138236311`). Different quest variants are likely, not tied to the dungeon goal.
