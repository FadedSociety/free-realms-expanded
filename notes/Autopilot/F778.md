## Security note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client can send 53/16 with an arbitrary `slot`, `pet` and `item`, 53/19 with any pet id,
53/28 with a water flag, and 53/32 with any definition/tint pair. Protections:

- 53/16: the slot is accepted only in 1..=7, and an out-of-range slot now produces **no** packet
  (before this fix the raw slot was echoed into the client's unchecked record index - the review
  blocker). The pet must be owned and non-vanity (R); the item must be an owned instance whose
  serialized row says type 4, slot = slot + 199 and a class the pet's model lists. Refusals change
  nothing and echo only the unchanged valid slot.
- 53/19: the feed needs the summoned pet, a bowl in slot 5 and a configured food in slot 6 that
  the bag still holds, the owner not in water, not busy per the occupation table, and no feed
  already running. Every refusal sends only the 53/20 unlock and mutates nothing; the client never
  reports a result the server trusts.
- 53/20/53/3 are server-authored; the fed food is consumed exactly once at the server's own clock
  through the staged `care` (one unit, bowl kept), and the last unit's slot clear is server-derived.
- 53/32: the definition/tint pair is resolved to an owned instance and the exact configured
  adoption definition; the certificate is consumed inside the staged inventory; no client word is
  used as a pet or instance id.
