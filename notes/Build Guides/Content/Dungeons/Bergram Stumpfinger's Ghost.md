# Bergram Stumpfinger's Ghost

Part of [[Content]] › [[Dungeons]]. Tags: **[client]** = game locale text (`loc <id>`), **[video]** = player recordings (frame key), **[wiki]** = fan wiki. Precedence client > video > wiki.

> Bergram Stumpfinger's Ghost is a **special-spawn boss battle**, not an Atlas dungeon. The wiki files it under *Battle*, *Special Spawn* and *Blackspore*. The player clicks a tombstone; once a battle is accepted the tombstone disappears for an unknown time. [wiki]

## Conflicts

1. **Medic garment.** The wiki writes "Energy Transfer Medic Pants". The client has Energy Transfer Medic Fatigues (`loc 3725608581`), Boots, Gloves, Jacket, Helmet and Backpack, and no "Pants". The Medic wheel garment is probably the Fatigues, unproven; the wiki name is kept and flagged.
2. **Enemy name.** The wiki writes "Necrowart Zombies". The client has "Necrowart Zombie2", "Necrowart Zombie3" and "Mini Necrowart Zombie". Which spawn is unproven.
3. **Stars.** "16 Stars" is far below the other level-13 battles on the wiki (for example 311 at [[Briarheart Caverns]]); unconfirmed.
4. **Game-data name.** Both "Bergram Stumpfinger" and "The Ghost of Bergram Stumpfinger" exist in the client; it is unknown if they are two entities (for example the quest ghost and the battle ghost).
