## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

Hostile client input in play: 26/8 clicks, pet requests (summon/dismiss/water/care/trick), movement, and housing family 127. The change
narrows what the server will do on its own, it does not add parsing.
- A forged 26/8 for anything but the owner's live chest falls through to the existing NPC path; the owner, lifetime and range checks are
  unchanged and remain server-side (client sends only the guid and its flag).
- The new gate makes the hunt refuse while any start-gate activity runs. A hostile client can still only stop *its own* pet from hunting
  (walk, water, house, craft); nothing is granted by that, and a chest is now never created while the open's busy gate is closed, so a
  player cannot be tricked into spending a find and a cooldown on a chest the busy rule would refuse.
- The busy refusal is read-only: it runs before `open_treasure` and before any staging, so the chest, roster and inventory are unchanged
  (tested three times in a row). Rewards still come only from the server's roll at the find; the click sends nothing the roll reads.
- The test-only housing setup drives the production 127/23 and 127/19 decoders with the fixture's own content copy; no new input path.
