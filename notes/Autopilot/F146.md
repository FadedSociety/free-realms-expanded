## Security

Ponytail (full) and secure-code-guardian were applied. The full hostile-input
rationale is in `notes/Autopilot/F112.md` ("Threat note"): kart credit is a
one-shot server-owned claim (the finish marker is consumed on a staged copy and
the speedway is reported only after the content-priced payout commits), so a
replayed `33/7` emits no quest event; fishing pays only from the server-owned
round; client win/score text never settles a minigame. The strengthened kart
test is the regression that proves a replay cannot advance the quest.

  pays and credits nothing. No new rule was added by this task.
- (R) The objective must require two completions to expose a duplicate event;
  the test content uses that requirement.
