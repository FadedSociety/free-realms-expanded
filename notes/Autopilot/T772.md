# T772: bought or granted pet items become pets at once (lead build)

Native test 2026-10-26: `realm give 70352` and buying the Crab at Gloria reached the client but showed in neither the
bag nor the Pets window. The bag hides category-200 rows by design (`00a6d9b0`), and the Pets window reads only the
53/5 roster (`00b596f0`). The original created the pet at purchase (R; a reconstruction hint, not recovered).

- `PetService::adopt_gained(before, inventory, now)`: adoption items gained since `before` become pets. Each unit is
  consumed into its own pet through `adopt_with_replies`, and units past the roster's room stay owned (R). The 53/5
  roster closes the replies once. `PetService::room()` is the free slots out of 50.
- `CoinShop::sync_pet_room` beside `sync_inventory`: a coin buy of more bound pet items than the roster holds is refused
  in `buy_once` with the invalid-quantity result, before money moves (one gate for 165/4 and 66/43).
- Hooks: the 165/4 coin-store buy and the 66/43 vendor buy (gameplay/npc.rs) and `realm give` (gameplay.rs) call
  `Gameplay::adopt_gained_pets` after the transaction, in the same staged change, so a failure rolls the coins back too.
  "Get it now"/claims (53/32) keep adopting from the owned unit and summoning, as before.
- F767 folded in (T456 review findings 1 and 4): the 53/5 sex byte is the client's bool (`00912cf0` stores
  `value != 0`); the GENDER→bool mapping is reconstructed (R) from the one recorded sample (model 364), so Models
  GENDER 2 (female) sends 1 and anything else 0; a pet adopted from a tinted item takes that
  tint inside `adopt_with_replies` (the owned instance is the source), and the post-commit tint write in the 53/32
  path is gone. Findings 2 (tint alias composed from the item, needs the tint-name table), 3 (test decoder duplicates
  `piece_091::PetRecord::decode`) and the minors stay in F767.

Not here: the Marketplace Pets category (T486, now waiting on this) and quest or battle rewards that grant pet items
(other `grant_reward` callers have no clock yet; follow-up).

Tests: `shop_sale_safety` (165/4 and 66/43 buys adopt; a full roster refuses the 51st buy with coins and save
unchanged), `pet_routing::gained_adoption_items_become_pets_up_to_the_roster_room`, the pet list record tests for the
sex byte. Threat note: the client cannot name a pet or a count; adoption reads only what the server's own buy or grant
added to the inventory, bounded by the 50-pet roster.
