## Overview

Briarwood is the dark forest south-east of Sanctuary, ruled by Darkthorne and patrolled by chugawug guards. The wiki splits it into four sub-regions: **Thistlerow** (first area from the Wilds, holds the hedge maze), **Bristlewood** (the middle, with a warpstone, farms and the Mother Lode Quarry), **Nettleseed** (the last section before the Tanglewood Fort) and **Briarheart** (behind the fort: Briarheart Palace). [wiki Briarwood, Thistlerow, Bristlewood, Nettleseed, Briarheart] The client uses all four sub-region names and also "Briarwood" for the whole forest, often for a spot that is really in Bristlewood or Nettleseed. [client] The wiki says you must "prove yourself" to the fort commander to enter Briarheart the first time, then a warpstone inside lets you skip this. [wiki Briarheart]

Client place names seen in the sentences below: the Hedge Maze and the Snarling Hedges, Treeleaf's Retreat, Gorg's Camp, Snorg's Camp, Brambleback's farm and Brambleback's Bayou, Darklit Lagoon, Mother Lode quarry, Vale of the Ancients, Bristlewood Glade, Briar Patch, Tangletrack Speedway, Sperry's Spore Farm, Dooba's fishing hole, Tanglewood Fort, Briarheart Palace and Briarheart Caverns. [client]

The wiki lists these shops for Briarwood without naming NPCs: Archer and Brawler weapons (level 15 Archer, level 5 Brawler), crafting supplies, Medic weapons (level 15), Miner clothing and tools (level 17 tools, level 20 clothes) and potions. [wiki Briarwood] The named gear merchants are in [[#Gear merchants (wiki-era shops, 2013)]].

How to read this page:
- **Exact NPC names are the client's `ucdt` name strings.** The number after each name is its `ucdt` id. Where the client has more than one `ucdt` row for the name, the extra ids are shown. Every `ugdt` row carries the same text.
- Tags: `[client]` locale text, `[video]` Haiku frame reads (frame key shown), `[wiki]` fan wiki. Client beats video beats wiki. A name that only the wiki gives is marked **wiki-only**.
- A client name entry proves the name exists, **not** that the NPC stands in Briarwood. Standing place comes from a client sentence (for example "near the entrance to Nettleseed"), a video frame or the wiki; each row says which. NPCs the sources tie to another zone are in [[#Not standing in Briarwood despite a tie]].
- Quest links go to the job page that holds the full row. Quests that belong to no job are in [[#Town and area quests]] below. Client quest titles and their description text are stored apart, so a link between a title and a description is "by wording" unless stated.
- Video coverage is thin. Only a few frames carry a zone label; most NPC sightings are distance markers on a flight over the forest, so a video tag proves the NPC exists in some Briarwood-like purple zone, not the exact stand.
- Player names, player chat and player-chosen pet or lot names are never recorded.

## Notes: quest givers and trainers

- **Gorg, Babbina, Spinnerette and Snorg** form the first quest cluster after the maze: coals for Gorg's camp, the spider Spinnerette and the Urgent Missive minigame. Snorg is also the Mining Guild gatekeeper at the Mother Lode, where Pinkelug sets the three Miner quests before him.
- **Docaloc** is the Medic level 15 trainer; Mayor Crystalline of Snowhill sends the player here. His quests mix spider venom from [[Arachnia's Lair]], dewpetal leaves from [[Bristlewood Glade]] and the patrol lost in the [[Vale of the Ancients]].
- **Sven** is the Wizard level 20 gate; his wand-parts quests take the player to the [[Briar Patch]], [[Treeleaf's Retreat]], [[Arachnia's Lair]] and, finally, a rescue in [[Briarheart Caverns]].
- **Orson** and **Vicki** carry the late Archer line; Orson sets the Croaking Vale boss kill.
- **Tangletrack Speedway** holds the Kart Driver and Demolition Derby trainers and the whole fourth Kart tier (Antonio, Linx, Mirana, Shamus, the SG Tour 400 final).
- **Fort and palace route.** Players cross the Tanglewood Fort and reach Captain Chugug and Darkthorne; Lieutenant Snibug stands in front of the fort.
- **The Nettleseed folk** (Chugilda, Sperry, Gigafrug, Chief Bilgragum, Dooba) chain into each other: Chief Bilgragum wants vegetables for Chugilda, Sperry supplies them, Gigafrug is fed and Dooba supplies sasparilla.
- **Warpstones.** The wiki names a Bristlewood warpstone, a Briarwood warpstone and a Briarheart warpstone; the client says Lyrral Pathfinder surveys a warpstone site in Bristlewood.

## Gaps

- **Quest rewards.** Only one number: the wiki page for Forgotten Lunchbox shows 24 coins. Every other Briarwood quest reward is unknown.

- **Vendor stock.** Only the wiki 2013 merchant lists exist; no client or video check of their stock or prices. Clack's stock is unseen. No NPC is tied to Briarwood's "Potions", "Crafting supplies" or "Medic Weapons" shops except Wumperwug (wiki Medic page) and Bishop.

- **Sunstone Valley** is not covered here; it has its own placeholder in NPCs.md.
- **Retired and limited-time NPCs.** The wiki gives introduction dates (August to October 2013) for the gear merchants only; whether they stayed to the end is unknown. Tower defense, Toad Tag and Pig Pen Panic are minigame NPCs whose rules belong on the minigame pages.
