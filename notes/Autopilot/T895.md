## Finding: the census count was wrong

The census counted a missing `enabled` field as `false`. The loaders treat a missing field as **true**
(`quest_engine.rs` split loader `unwrap_or(Value::Bool(true))`, `content.rs enabled_by_default`,
`tests/support/quest_records.rs read_quests`). Of the 71 records it listed, 39 were already enabled (the
`zone_*` files, 3000021/22/59/60, 3020014, 3020024, 3030007/8/21, 3050011, 3100020/23, 3110002/7, 3130032 and
others). 141 quest records are enabled today, not 97. Only **32** records carry objectives with an explicit
`enabled: false`; this task reviewed those 32 (3070026 and 3130015 are T882's and are not in the set).

## Threat note

A hostile client could forge a 49/2 accept for 3010011 or 3010012 out of order, with the wrong job or below level 5, or
replay a 49/14. The engine refuses each (offer state, prerequisite, job and level gate, completion latch); the new
test asserts the out-of-order accept leaves the saved state byte-equal. Rewards come only from the server-verified
kill and cast events, never from a client claim.
