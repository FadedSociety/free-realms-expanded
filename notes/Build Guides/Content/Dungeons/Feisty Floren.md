## Conflicts

1. **Prize weapons.** The battle page lists Horse Bow of Explosions, Hammer of Pummeling, Scalpel of Alarm, Blade of Shuriken Storm, Axe of Earthquake, Wand of Firestorm. The wiki item pages for those weapons list Petty Yetis!, Robgoblin Creek and others, **not** Feisty Floren! The level 8 weapons Archer's Composite Bow of Splintering (`loc 1099163227`), Brawler's Anvil Hammer of Roundhouse (`2041612733`), Medic's Bonesaw of Immunization (`1644410929`), Ninja's Scythe of Flame Wave (`988546178`), Warrior's Battle Hammer of Warcry (`3112655282`) and Wizard's Bone Wand of Tsunami (`4270451721`) list Feisty Floren!, Bristlewood Glade and Floren Forest. The battle page may be copied from Petty Yetis. Unresolved (wiki against wiki); the battle-page list is kept above.
2. **Location.** Wiki: south-west of Wugachug. Client: the Pixie Nursery. Another wiki page: Northern Wilds.
3. **Bonus.** The wiki has no bonus goal; the video and client show one (10 targets).
4. **Stars.** Wiki 119; the video banner shows 11 stars.
