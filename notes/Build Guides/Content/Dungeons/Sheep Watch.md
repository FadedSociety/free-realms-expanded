## Conflicts

1. **Bonus goal.** The client has a "Sheep Watch Bonus Objective" group, but the wiki lists only "Defeat all the wolves" and has no bonus rewards section. The client wins on existence. The text of the bonus is not confirmed.
2. **Panel description.** Two near-identical client strings exist (`loc 1190485307` "at this farm", `loc 238913308` "at Farnum Farmstead"). The wiki quote matches the second.
3. **Boss count.** The wiki says two bosses (Alpha Female and Alpha Male); the client has hidden objectives and lines only for the female.
