## Security note (secure-code-guardian)

Threat surface: a hostile client can send shared-field ability, interaction and
select intents (`36/10`, `26/8`, `26/10`, `26/13`) and can disconnect/reconnect
at will. It cannot report a kill, damage, an actor definition, a player's
presence, a quest id, an objective count or a reward. The credit is keyed by the
authenticated sender stored by `with_shared_fields`; the carried definition is
the server-owned field actor row; the claim event carries `count: 1` and is
capped by the quest objective. F276's reward retention is now explicitly
reward-only: leaving the field forfeits the quest event, so a client cannot use
a fast reconnect to reclaim an event after leaving, cannot replay the claim
(the credit is removed when claimed or expired), and cannot fabricate progress.
The bounded state is unchanged (`MAX_CREDITS` 16 per player, `CREDIT_TTL_MS`
10 s). Refused/ignored: non-lethal/corpse/out-of-range/malformed requests, a
zero or negative definition, a credit dropped by the cap, an expired credit, a
player outside the instance, and a session with no bound quest catalogue (no-op,
credit consumed). No new `?`, no new unwrap of client data, no save/session
change.
