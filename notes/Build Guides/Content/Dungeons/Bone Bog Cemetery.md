# Bone Bog Cemetery

Part of [[Content]] › [[Dungeons]]. Tags: **[client]** = game locale text (`loc <id>`), **[video]** = player recordings (frame key), **[wiki]** = fan wiki. Precedence client > video > wiki.

This dungeon has **two versions**. The normal one has the Banshee as boss. During the October **Super Spooktacular** event the boss is the **Pumpkin Prince**, with the NPC Lucinda helping. Both use the same map and bonus goal.

## Conflicts

1. **Event level.** The dungeon is level 5 (difficulty 3), but the wiki says the Pumpkin Prince battle in it needs level 13+. A Brawler 20 was recorded in the event version. No source shows what a level-5 to 12 player sees during the event.
2. **Cross Wind item slot.** For the same prize frame, the client-text match said "Cross Wind Archer Gloves" while the image read "Cross Wind Archer Boots". The wiki lists Boots. Boots is used.
3. **Main goal in event version.** The recording's first-goal line is partly unreadable. "Defeat the Pumpkin Prince" is confirmed only from chat log lines, not the goal panel.
