## Overview

**The client mentions Shrouded Glade a lot.** About 350 locale lines name the town (quests, objectives, collections, lots, events, items), and most of the NPCs below are named in a quest line that mentions it. The zone is one of the better-covered ones in the client. The video archive holds about 30 frames with the town's warpstone prompt, its collection banners or its postbox marker, plus one long recording of the Gloam invasion (`ct2JrtOiO4g`). The wiki has a town page, about 30 pages in its Shrouded Glade category (the invasion cast, the merchants and a few pixies) and almost nothing on the ordinary quest givers (Ceard, Tilda, Arestol, Perci, Embir, Cella, Halle and so on have no wiki page). So most rows rest on [client] alone.

**Name.** The client writes "Shrouded Glade" (`ucdt` 1059092551, 1502322157, 1181305787 with a stray full stop; `ugdt` 2026856148, 2595466617, 2761298400), "Shrouded Glade Warpstone" (`ucdt` 287427522, 3749780919; `ugdt` 3923924332) and, for the invaded state, "Shrouded Gloam" (lots "Shrouded Gloam Lot" `ucdt` 2223560327; `ugdt` 1050477918; one quest says "Shrouded Glad" in 3786805322, a typo). A welcome line (speaker not named): "Welcome to Shrouded Glade, revered home of the druids and their distinguished academy. Pixies from all over Free Realms come here to study, and hopefully one day master the bond with nature that all druids possess." (462127041). "Now entering Shrouded Glade." is visible as a chat line in video (`LdlcmLtfi80/00018`). [client] [video]

**Where it is.** Wiki: "a town in northeastern Sacred Grove ... nestled at the base of Misty Mountain", "only accessible through a tunnel or by a warpstone"; the road from the tunnel runs to the Druid Academy, which sits above the town between two waterfalls; a second bridge near the Academy leads to Sunstone Valley. Client: Vidor is "in Shrouded Glade, northeast of here" (3429029038, spoken elsewhere); Snowhill is "west of Shrouded Glade" (2872091535); Mugwort's Hollow is "west of Shrouded Glade" (2867760776, 4097395734) and "outside of Shrouded Glade" (2107188895); Durango Zoo is "just southwest of Shrouded Glade" (2665998091); the Academy's water "flows southwest to the fishing town of Lakeshore" (2249462015). The wiki Lakeshore page agrees ("southwest of Shrouded Glade"). The wiki's Birthday Bash party stands "southwest of the warpstone, at the entrance of the tunnel that leads into the Wilds", and a Hunting Treasure clue names "the entrance tunnel to Shrouded Glade". **Story role.** Wiki: the Druid Academy was founded by the druids who survived the Unbinding; on **15 June 2012** the Gloam invaded and corrupted the town (the "Shrouded Gloam" event, a quest line plus open-world fights). The client carries the whole line: Queen Valerian dreams of the invasion ("I saw it in a dream... an army of mechanical monsters destroying everything in their path", 1630444228) and sends the player to Captain Sunspark; the player chooses druids (Headmaster Merk) or dwarves (Gus Geargrinder), then a joint finale ends in "This Glade is Ours!". After it, Arch Druid Camellia sends the player to the Freewheelers in Sunstone Valley ("Aiding our Allies"). The invasion fights ran at set times: the wiki says the Gloam Invasion and General Drahk fights occurred every 15 minutes. The wiki gives no end date ("an unknown time"), and the 2013 merchant dates show the town in its normal state again. Everything else in the town is a collection of small quests for the Academy's staff and students. [client] [wiki Shrouded Gloam]

The client stores titles and descriptions as separate entries; "by wording" means I paired them myself. Player names, player chat and player-chosen names are never recorded. Rewards are unknown unless stated.

## Notes on important NPCs

- **The Academy's quest givers** (Ceard, Tilda, Arestol, Tov, Edvard, Phyll, Embir, Cella, Halle, Emil, Sera Bella) give small errands that mix Shrouded Glade with Misty Mountain, Mugwort's Hollow, Lakeshore, Snowhill and the Vale of the Ancients. None of them has a wiki page or a title the client text names; the client strings carry the quests. [client]
- **The Arch Druid story.** The client builds one small mystery out of several errands: Camellia is weak since the invasion, Ceard buys Perci's potions in secret, Daphne holds the last stock, Edvard grows a flower to cheer her, and Lavender (the Queen's twin) was the rightful choice. The wiki Timeline agrees. [client] [wiki]
- **Captain Sunspark, Headmaster Merk, Gus and Dorn Geargrinder** run the invasion; the player picks a side, then the two arcs merge for the finale. The wiki says each arc can lead into the other. [client] [wiki]
- **Tald Daybreaker and Justin Roderic** are the two NPCs the video shows at the warpstone most; Tald's only quest is Druid in the Mist, and Justin has none found. [video] [client]
- **Brin Galeglow, Postmaster Lane and Vidor Stormshroud** are the job trainers for the town ([[Postman]], [[Pet Trainer]]); **Kosha** and **Arestol** serve [[Ninja]] and [[Warrior]] lines; **Chef Safrie** serves [[Chef]]. [client] [wiki]
- **Merchants.** The seven wiki merchants all date from August to October 2013 and carry level-V job gear. [wiki]
