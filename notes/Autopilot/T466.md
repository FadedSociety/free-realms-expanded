## Deviations from the brief (with reasons)

- **Objectives carry `text_string_id` = the fork NameId.** The brief says "add no field", but
  T403 has merged since: `tests/quest_engine.rs::enabled_split_quests_send_their_goal_text_and_live_keys`
  and `scripts/content_check.py` require a positive locale `text_string_id` on every objective of
  an enabled quest (a zero is the client's `<STRING 0 NOT FOUND>`). The NameId is also in each
  objective's legacy-data tag, as the brief asks.
- **No greeting was bound.** Cole's tour intros (93398, 93419, 93449) and Yancy's 101408 would
  pass the QuestOffer proof, but `tests/npc_greetings_seaside_briarwood.rs`
  (`the_existing_greetings_and_other_regions_are_unchanged` and the unbound-row list check) and
  `notes/Autopilot/T317.md` "Unbound drawable rows" pin every unlisted Seaside row unbound. Binding
  either would mean editing another slice's suite and note, outside this brief's owning files. All
  six NPCs got `BLANK_WITH_REASON` entries that name the searched guide row, the third-person ids
  and the quest-step ids. Open item for the lead below.
- Cole's `quests_accepted_reason` text was updated with the new entry; leaving "No explicit
  quoted hand-in" next to a hand-in entry would be wrong.
- Two more count pins (`content_jobs.rs`, `content_npcs.rs`) were not named in the brief; both
  read the quest directory and the full suite would fail without the update.

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client can send 49/2 for an unoffered or prerequisite-locked quest, 49/14 before the
steps or twice, a menu turn-in choice early, Talk to an out-of-order target, or a position outside
the radius or in another zone. Every path goes through the unchanged `QuestEngine` gates
(`can_offer`, ordered objectives, `begin_turnin` completeness, `within` + zone match), and the
tests assert each refusal leaves the serialized save byte-equal. Rewards are fixed content values
(30 coins, at most 30 XP), granted only on the server-judged turn-in.
