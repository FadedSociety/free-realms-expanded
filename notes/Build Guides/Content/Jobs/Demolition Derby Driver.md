## How to read this page

- Tags: **[client]** is the game's locale text (`en_us_data.dat`), **[client code]** is our mapped client code in [[Demo Derby]], **[video]** is player recordings (frame key `video/frame`), **[wiki]** is the fan wiki. Precedence is client, then video, then wiki. Numbers in parentheses, for example `(1565667320)`, are client locale ids. Each quest title has a `ucdt` entry and usually a `ugdt` twin with the same text.
- **Matching caveat.** The locale file stores quest titles, descriptions, objectives and dialog as separate entries with no link between them, and the quest prerequisite and reward tables are not decoded. Every objective below was matched to a title by wording. **exact** means a string carries the title's own words; **direct** means a string names the same NPC and place as the title; a dagger (†) means "fits, not proven". Nothing is invented: rewards, givers and levels read "unknown" unless a source names them.
- **Truncated dialog.** Long dialog strings in this locale dump stop at about 256 characters (for example 2408435545 ends mid-word). Quoted dialog below is cut where the file cuts it.
- **The wiki has no Demo Derby quest list.** The fan wiki names the four trainers, the Crash Cup, two titles, the achievements and five karts. Every quest row below therefore rests on [client] text, and on [video] only where a frame is cited.
- **Order.** Quests are grouped into story series by the NPCs and places their strings share. Order inside a series is a reading of the dialog, not a decoded prerequisite chain.

## Overview

- Demo Derby Driver is a **freestyle** job whose gameplay is the Demo Derby minigame: players drive karts in an arena, knock opponents out and score points. [wiki Demolition Derby Driver: "a driving job, specializing crashing cars together. A freestyle class job, it has many upgrades and extras"] [client 3285429884 "Demo derby."] [client code: see [[Demo Derby]]]
- **Max level 20.** Level achievements are at 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20; the level-20 title is "The Master of Destruction". [client 2620894938 and the other four `Demo Derby Driver: Level N` titles] [wiki Achievements, Titles]
- **Members status.** The wiki Jobs list marks Warrior, Wizard, Chef, Kart Driver, Pet Trainer and Postman as members only and does **not** mark Demolition Derby Driver, so the job is treated as free to start. [wiki Jobs] The client has a members-only helmet and members-only decals for it (1296221694 "Members Only Demo Derby Driver Helmet", 436709450, 2142049162, 3980850020), a "Demo Derby Driver Membership Upgrade Bundle" (2406693809), and the upgrade prompt "Want to keep playing as a Demolition Derby Driver? Upgrade to unlock more fun!" (2024628057). The same prompt exists for almost every job, including free ones, so it reads as the non-member level-cap prompt. The wiki says non-members stop at level 4 on most jobs [wiki Jobs]; the derby cap itself is unknown. See Conflicts 2.
- **How to unlock.** Speak to any derby trainer at a speedway: Mad Jack (Wildwood), Crash Davis (Thunder Falls), Burt 'Wrecking' Crewe (Frost Ridge) or Rip Ringa (Tangletrack). [wiki] [client 514462904] "Get the Kart Driver or Demolition Derby Driver job from any of the trainers at any of the speedways around the world." Job switching: entering a minigame switches the job to the matching one; a video message at the Frost Ridge gate reads "Your job has been switched to one that matches the minigame you are about to join." [video `fktAs_GRrjM/00178`]
- **Town referrals.** Several town quests send new players to a trainer: Shakey sends the player to Mad Jack (435085027), Laurin to Crash Davis (2308044933), the Ricky Danger quest to Johnny Thunder and Mad Jack (2454371300), Francis Panic's quest to Dane Blueshift and Crash Davis (2917648096), and Raina Rush's pen hunt to Burt 'Wrecking' Crewe (see Related quests). A Snowhill-area scout sends high-skill drivers to Wildwood (144865826). [client]
- **Quest line shape.** The client has **44** quest titles beginning "Demo Derby:", two tutorial titles, one duel title (Razzle's Racket) and five "Demo Derby Driver: Level N" titles. They fall into six story series (below). The line ends with the achievement "Demolisher" (Complete the Demo Derby Questline) and the title "Demolisher". [client 20476293, 104920183, 1422414851] [wiki Achievements, Titles]
- **Crash Cup.** Winning three special-event derbies (Thunder Falls Crash for Coin, Wildwood Derby 1000, Frost Ridge Invitational) claims the "Sacred Grove Crash Cup" (2353093926). The wiki lists "Crash Cup" as a Demolition Derby Driver weapon, level 1, gained by completing the questline. [client] [wiki Crash Cup]
- **Rewards are largely unknown.** No quest-complete frame for a Demo Derby quest shows coin or XP numbers. Reward hints are listed per row and under Gear.

### Derby karts

Derby karts end in "Wrecker" or "Crusher". The wiki confirms five in the Derby Karts category (Barbed Crusher, Bear Wrecker, Crazy Crusher, Cutlass Wrecker, Sea Serpent Wrecker), sold in the Station Cash Marketplace with a 10 percent member discount; Cutlass Wrecker costs 700 Station Cash, or 630 for members. [wiki Derby Karts pages] The client has these further names matching the same pattern: Bandit Crusher, Black Dragon Crusher, Cardie Crusher, Chaos Crusher, Charged Crusher, Dueler Wrecker, Evil Crusher, Flame Boost Wrecker, Flytrap Crusher, Grasshopper Wrecker, Heart Crusher, Killer Crusher, Lightning Wrecker, Ripper Wrecker, Shark Crusher, Tank Wrecker, USA Wrecker, War Crusher. 1. **Job name.** The client job card and level titles use "Demo Derby Driver" (687975022, 844969600, `Demo Derby Driver: Level N`) and the job bar in video reads "Demo Derby Driver 7" (`A2kA_ji8PFs/00024`) and the job window "Demo Derby Driver" (`0IaICGnTWWQ/00010`). The wiki page is "Demolition Derby Driver" but its Achievements and Titles tables say "Demo Derby Driver". The page keeps the file name and uses "Demo Derby Driver" as the job name in frontmatter.
2. **Members only.** The wiki Jobs list does not mark Demolition Derby Driver members only but marks Warrior, Wizard, Chef, Kart Driver, Pet Trainer and Postman. The client has members-only helmet and decals, an upgrade bundle and the generic upgrade prompt. Treated as free to start with a non-member level cap (value unknown). [wiki Jobs] [client]

4. **Sandstorm and Seaside.** The wiki says Sandstorm offers no Demo Derby. The client has "Seaside Demo Derby" (790323428) and a Sandstorm derby bonus notice (3690461906). Unresolved; also in [[Kart Driver]] Conflicts 7.
