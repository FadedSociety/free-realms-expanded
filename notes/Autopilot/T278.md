## Security threat note (secure-code-guardian)

- A hostile client can send 26/8, 26/10, 26/6, 49/2 and 49/14 at any time, and
  can move/report its position. None of these layouts was changed and no new
  parser or `application_handler` selector was added.
- A forged 49/2 accept while no 49/1 is pending is denied by the engine (no
  49/3) and leaves the full `GameplayState` and save bytes equal. A 49/14 with
  no end screen, a wrong-NPC Talk, a missing prerequisite, and an
  out-of-radius/out-of-zone visit all change nothing (test 4).
- The offer gate is server-side: profile 11 must be the active, unlocked job,
  the prerequisite quests must be Completed, and 3090006 additionally needs the
  server eligibility rank 5 (from saved XP). The client cannot claim a level,
  a job or a prerequisite. Test 3 proves rank 4 gets no offer and rank 5 does.
- The reward comes only from the content rows; no client amount, item id or
  outcome reaches `apply_grant`. `job_xp` is keyed to profile 11 and the
  existing `quest:<character>:<quest>` grant key pays once; duplicate 49/14,
  replayed Talk and replayed accept pay nothing (tests 2, 3, 4). The reward
  item list is empty, so there is no free item to sell or re-grant.
- The near-cap test exercises the wallet and profile checked-add paths for one
  player: each refusal is returned as an error, leaves that player's state and
  save unchanged, and the other player's turn-in and the session remain usable
  (test 7). No `unwrap` of client-controlled data was added.
- The visit is a server position check: `gateway.rs` passes an already validated
  pose to `Gameplay::on_location`, and the objective compares the server-held
  zone/position. The client does not report a quest completion for it.

   side table. 49/14 still stages the engine and `RewardState` on clones and
