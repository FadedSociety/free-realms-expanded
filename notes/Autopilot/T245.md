## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

Attack surface: none of the new values are client-supplied. The rows are trusted startup/reload
content; 35/2 AddNpc is server-to-client only and its reader (`0x00921A10`) has no level field for a
client to echo. A hostile client cannot create an actor, choose a spawn centre, set a level, award a
reward, or move another player's field state through this slice.

- Why bad content is refused: `load_field_enemies` rejects duplicate actor GUIDs/spawn-area ids,
  unknown group members, duplicate membership, level outside 1..=20, a band that excludes the
  actor's level, respawn outside 1..=3600 s, aggro outside (0, 40], leash outside (0, 1000],
  radius > leash, `centre != position`, empty/control-character ids, and empty sources or a
  non-`(R)` reconstructed source. The new rows stay well inside every bound (levels 5-10, timers
  90/120, leash 12).
- Economy: no coins, items, XP or client-reported result was added. `power_up_table: "standard"`
  is the existing server-side participation table; field kills grant no client-named reward. Level
  is content-side, so a client cannot scale an enemy up for a larger drop.
- Atomicity: a refused reload keeps the previous field (existing
  `malformed_group_reload_is_atomic`, re-run in `field_enemy_spawn_groups` against the larger file).
- The retry changes only test expectations (`wolves()` scoping, a clear cast point); it adds no
  trust boundary, parser, save, reward or wire field. The threat surface above is unchanged.
- No logging, secrets, session or trust-boundary change.
