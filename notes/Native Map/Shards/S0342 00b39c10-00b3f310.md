## Client in-game-purchase manager

This range contains the `ClientInGamePurchaseManager` configuration method at `00b3df80` (vtable slot 7). It writes CAS and URAM settings, including platform/account values, API version, session id and certificate paths. This establishes client configuration only; it does not establish a successful external payment or server transaction.

The manager owns typed `SoeUtil::Array` collections for `GameCommerce::CreditCardAttributes`, `VirtualCurrencySkuData`, `MembershipSkuData`, `CountryCode`, and `StateCode`. Constructors, inline-capacity allocators, assignment methods, destructors and scalar deleting destructors appear from `00b3c320` through `00b3ebe0`. Inline capacities evidenced by their vtables are 8, 8, 8, 16 and 64 respectively. Record append/read helpers use strides including 0x60, 0x214, 0x80 and 0x48 (`00b3c810`, `00b3c9f0`, `00b3cbd0`, `00b3cf40`).

Manager behaviors include the accepted-credit-card refresh at `00b3b2d0`, store-session cache clearing at `00b3c480`, sale-list filtering and active-sale reselection at `00b3c1b0`, transaction tracker reset at `00b3c610`, and bundle-content equipment through the Lua-facing call path at `00b3b3b0`. The session statistics tracker list is backed by a 0x58-byte block pool: list node allocation/free thunks route to pool helpers, and neighboring functions `00b3ef90..00b3f710` total span capacity, locate spans in a red-black tree, add/free blocks and release fully free spans. The tree helpers include insert/delete rebalancing and rotations (`00b3f080`, `00b3f140`, `00b3eee0`, `00b3ef30`).

Raw bytes resolve a Ghidra signature error: `00b3ee20` and `00b3ee60` return successor/predecessor nodes in EAX (`ret 4`), although their decompiler signatures say `void`; `00b3f370` and `00b3eff0` consume those results. The four short functions `00b3b410..00b3b440` are ECX-adjusting thunks to `00b348f0`, with adjustments 0x3a3bc, 0x3a440, 0x3a3c4 and 0x3a548. Their owning classes remain unknown.

## Existing-note alignment and open questions

`notes/Research/Pieces/086 Currency and economy audit.md` identifies Station Cash as a separate account wallet and says its response layout was unrecovered. This shard partially narrows that gap: `00b3e120` calls the wallet reader `00b3a6e0`, which consumes two signed 16-bit fields, a status dword and a nested record; `00b2b450` reads a boolean, three dwords, two length-prefixed byte regions and a final boolean. The response path also passes the status and manager field `[this+0x3b394]` to `OnWalletInfo`. The semantic mapping of those values and the complete wallet contract remain unknown, so the note's warning about unresolved layout semantics still applies.

Most important unresolved points are the server-side place-order transaction/grant behavior; the semantics of several wallet and SKU fields; the missing catalogue subtypes above (plus the exact Station Cash and subscription SKU response classes); and the ownership/type of the four ECX-adjusting thunks. `00b3a450` is tied to transaction-tracker maps by its vtable and caller, but the exact duplicate-key/tracker policy remains uncertain.
