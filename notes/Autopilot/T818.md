# T818: character select is not cut off 10 minutes after login (lead build, native 2026-10-27)

## Why
Native 2026-10-27 (runs client-baseline-20261027T084132Z and T102248Z): a client left at character select lost its login
connection about 10-11 minutes after logging in (Login.log: authenticated 02:42:29, "BaseApi connection terminated (LoginUdp_6)
reason=DisconnectReasonTimeout LastReceive=90009" at 02:53:40; again 04:23:36 -> 04:34:47). The client's keep-alive (SOE
opcode 6, about every 29 s; the trace labels it SessionDisconnect) was answered until ~10 minutes, then osfr-login dropped the
session at its fixed 600 s absolute lifetime and the client timed out 90 s later.

## Change
`src/bin/osfr-login.rs`: the expiry rule is the small `expired(age, idle, awaiting_auth, logged_in)` with named (R) constants:
pre-auth deadline 30 s (key-pair mode, unchanged), idle timeout 120 s (unchanged; keep-alives count), lifetime 10 minutes before
login (unchanged) and 4 hours after login. `src/login.rs`: `LoginService::logged_in()` exposes the existing flag.

## Threat note
Only a session whose login was accepted gets the longer lifetime, and it still dies after 120 s without traffic; the
pre-authentication deadline, the 32-peer cap, the per-peer and per-IP buckets and the RC4/reliability state are unchanged. The
worst case is 32 logged-in idle-but-pinging sessions held for up to 4 hours, the same peer cap as today.

## Structure
One pure function plus four constants in the existing binary; one accessor on the existing service. No new module.

## Gates
`cargo test --bin osfr-login` (new test `a_logged_in_session_outlives_the_pre_login_lifetime_while_it_keeps_alive`), the
login* suites, app_fuzz, spine_rules; rustfmt clean. Native: leave the client at character select for 15 minutes, then enter.
