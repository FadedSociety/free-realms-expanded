# T827 - pets trail their owner while the owner moves (autopilot builder, 2026-10-27)

Brief: native 2026-10-27 (dragon pet) - after a run the pet teleported beside the owner, and on short walks it
waited about a second after the owner stopped before walking or teleporting. The original pet trailed its owner
continuously. Module owners: `prototype/osfr-tools/src/pet_follow.rs` (T455 follow step) and
`PetService::follow_owner` in `prototype/osfr-tools/src/pets.rs`; idle glue in
`prototype/osfr-tools/src/pets/idle.rs`.

## Cause found

`Gameplay::on_location` -> `follow_pet` -> `PetService::follow_owner` already ran on every accepted owner move
and on the world tick, so "move on every move" was already true. The defects were about the motion:

- the old step estimated the owner's pace as `moved / dt`, where `dt` is the 50 ms server tick while the client
  reports a position only about every 500 ms; the estimate saturated at the old 12 m/s cap and stayed there after
  a stop;
- it sent one 125 per 500 ms (`send_ms`), a 5-6 m hop at that estimate, so a snap read as a teleport;
- it sent no base animation, so the actor slid instead of walking;
- it only stopped/repositioned after the owner had been still for `settle_delay_ms`, which is the wait after a
  stop.

## Client evidence (IDA, read-only queries this run)

- 125 reader `008e5940`: u16 opcode, u64 guid (two dwords), 3 x f32 position, 3 x f32 heading, u8 state, u8
  unknown = 36 bytes. Our frame is the shared `piece_052`/`enemies::movement_packet` (state 1, unknown 0); this
  task does not change it.
- 35/8 reader `008e5dd0`: u16 opcode, u16 subtype via `008d6830`, u64 guid, i32 animation id, i32 0, u8 flags =
  21 bytes. At `00931636`-`00931649` the flags byte is `test dl, 1`; bit 0 set assigns `[eax+51Ch] = animation
  id` (base), bit 0 clear falls through to `00931668` and `sub_96C780` (play/queue). Our gait frames set bit 0
  and keep bit 1 clear.
- Owner position cadence `00934a20`: the next report is scheduled at `a4 + v15`, where `v42 = 500` (50 in some
  states) and `v15` is `v42` or 50 depending on the actor state. This is the source of `OWNER_UPDATE_MS = 500`
  and the `settle_delay_ms >= 600` rule.
- Locomotion ids: the existing pinned `piece_052` constants map `loc_stand` -> 1, `loc_walk` -> 2, `loc_run` ->
  3; the AddNpc spawn template carries the walk/run/stand ids (`piece_052::locomotion_animation_ids`). The pet
  actor spawns with the 5.0 m/s `movement_speed` (`pets.json`).

## Reconstructed (R) rules

- The pet's pace (from `movement_speed`), catch-up gain/cap/start, leash, settle and send cadence are data
  defaults; the client does not supply a pet speed.
- Whether every pet model has `loc_walk`/`loc_run` slots is a native check; the ids exist in the client mapping
  and the earlier wolf/gloamling checks found them.
- Whether the client interpolates between 125 frames or snaps is unverified. At the shipped 500 ms and
  5 m/s a frame is 2.5 m; at the 20 m/s catch-up cap it is 10 m. F863 restored 500 ms after the 100 ms
  attempt; if native shows hopping in a catch-up, that is a lead rate decision, not a free data edit.
- The 750 ms settle is the existing (R) value; not changed here.

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

- What a hostile client could send: the only inputs are the owner position (already validated and clamped by
  `movement.rs` before `on_location`, whose comment says the gateway calls it only after that) and the 53/26 pet
  follow click (existing path, validated for finiteness, the client's 8-40 m band and walkability). There is no
  new opcode, field, item id, quantity, chat text, role or save input.
- Why it is refused or harmless: a non-finite owner or ground value skips the step and keeps the pose; walk
  frames are paced by the server clock (`send_ms`, 500 ms at the shipped value), a gait change adds one frame
  between due ones, and a settle/pause/arrival adds one Stop, so a client cannot demand a frame stream. An
  accepted click is bounded by the client's own 200 ms interval and 10 m step gate, and its walk (and so its
  arrival Stop) takes server time. A warp needs a validated owner jump over `warp_owner_jump_m` (30 m), a
  blocked walk for `warp_far_ms`, a climb
  over `warp_climb_m`, or the 100 m / 5 s leash; a refused click returns flag 0 and changes nothing (tested);
  the pet is session-only and a follow step never saves, so no persistence, economy or privilege state can be
  forged. No secret, key, name, chat text or absolute path is added.
