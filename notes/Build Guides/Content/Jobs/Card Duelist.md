Locale ids are the numeric ids from the client locale file; each title has a `ucdt` entry and, usually, a `ugdt` twin. **How the quest data was matched.** The client locale stores quest titles and quest text as separate entries with no id link between them. Every objective or description below was matched to a title by one of three methods, shown in the "Text match" column: **exact** (the string repeats a distinctive word or phrase of the title, or the video shows the same string under that title), **direct** (the string names the same NPC, place and goal that the wiki or video gives for that title), or **inferred** (the title and string fit by theme or order but nothing names the title; rows with no string at all are also marked inferred and say "No matching string found"). Quote ids were checked against the locale file when this page was built. Quest order is the wiki's list order and the order the strings chain; the client's quest prerequisite table is not decoded, so treat order as likely, not confirmed.

## Overview

- Card Duelist is a **freestyle** job built around the in-world Free Realms Trading Card Game (TCG). The wiki says "experience is gained by competing against NPCs in the Free Realms TCG and completing the Duelist quests". [wiki Card Duelist] The client lobby text says "Enter the TCG and you can learn to play, practice against Kai in a single-player match, or take on other Card Duelists!" (2787814996) [client]
- **Max level 20.** Level achievements are at 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 (see Level achievements). Level 20 is a gate: every later quest pack says the player must have "attained Level 20 as a Card Duelist" (2124354028, 4030662277, 1027028212). Video shows Card Duelist 6 at Cardie Party, and Card Duelist 20 at Quizzical Yucca and the Rumbledome finale. [client] [video `wI2eLQKFFHg/00048`, `2nq801iM8kU/00122`, `2nq801iM8kU/00202`]
- **Members only?** Card Duelist is not marked "Members only" in the wiki Jobs list. [wiki Jobs] The client says "TCG Sets 1 - 4 Quests now free to play!" (2912101907) and the quest-pack texts say "purchased and completed" (2124354028), so the packs were bought items at least for a time. Treated as non-members for the base line; the packs are unresolved. See Conflicts.
- **How to unlock.** The wiki says to activate a quest line with Poe Tatters or Sam Potts in Sanctuary, or Garrison Gold in Snowhill; speaking to Shifty the Shuffler in Wugachug adds an optional quest that directs the player to Poe Tatters. [wiki Card Duelist] In the client, Sam Potts is the trainer named by every town NPC that refers the player: for example "To become a Card Duelist, you need to find Sam Potts." (1943395019), "Learn how to be a card duelist by finding any trainer." (3889668378), "Obtain the Card Duelist job" (2526869862), "Find Sam Potts at Sanctuary to become a Card Duelist." (1454484288). Older text puts Sam at Pixiewood (1155984690). The client also holds town "first-time experience" quest names "Cobblestone Village Card Duelist FTE" (2113549917), "Stillwater Card Duelist FTE" (2584360675) and "Highroad Card Duelist FTE" (3677584990) that point new players to a trainer. [client]
- **Quest line shape.** Five parts: (1) the base job line, Sanctuary to Lakeshore to Snowhill to Wugachug to Seaside to Merry Vale to Farnum Farmstead, 28 quests in the wiki list order; (2) the Briarwood and Greenwood "Shattered Past" quest packs (TCG Series 2); (3) the three "Baron von Darkcheat" quest packs in Snowhill (Series 3); (4) the Realmkeeper quests with Madam Zelda and the Baron's henchmen (Series 4); (5) the Rumbledome quests in Sunstone Valley (Series 5). Each pack is described in the client as a set of "four games each" against named opponents for "eight promo cards". (2124354028, 4030662277, 1027028212) [client] [wiki Trading Card Game]
- **Match rule.** Every NPC duel is "Be the first to score 12 points to win the match" (124348745). Hero NPCs have four decks (Trick, Creature, Resource, Tournament); each win gives a card from that deck, and the Tournament Deck gives the hero card. Some NPCs start with a card already in play to raise the difficulty (for example (3312146079)). [client]
- **Rewards are mostly cards.** Known rewards: promo cards for every hero-deck win (listed under Duel opponents), 1 Skullz Chopper and a trophy for the Rumbledome line [video `2nq801iM8kU/00202`], a Gloamstone hoverboard after the Baron line (520919097), the achievement "Duelist" (Complete the Card Duelist Questline, Gold Trophy 15 points, Title: Card Commander) [wiki Achievements] (590106873, 3270070510). Coins and XP per quest are unknown. See Gaps.
