## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

Attack surface: none of the new values are client-supplied. The rows are trusted
startup/reload content; 35/2 is server-to-client and its reader has no level field. A
hostile client cannot create or move an actor, choose a spawn centre, set a level, choose a
reward band, or award coins/XP through this slice. The reward comes only from the server's
alive-to-dead HP transition on a loader-validated, server-owned actor, and the actor level
is content, not a client field, so a client cannot scale an enemy for a larger payout.

Refused/ignored by existing production code (verified against `src/encounter/field.rs`):

- `load_field_enemies`: duplicate actor GUID, duplicate area id, unknown/duplicate group
  member, level outside 1..=20, a band that excludes the actor's level, respawn outside
  1..=3600 s, aggro outside (0, 40], leash outside (0, 1000], radius > leash,
  `centre != position`, empty/control-character ids, and a group whose source has no
  non-empty reconstructed `(R)` row.
- `reload_field_enemies`: a refusal restores the previous field and behaviour (test 7).
- `FieldKillRewards`: overlapping/out-of-bounds bands, duplicate overrides, missing
  provenance; the lookup returns `None` for an uncovered level (test 9).
- Replayed lethal and corpse hits pay nothing; a killed actor respawns once after 180 s
  (tests 4/6); no item is granted (test 4 snapshots the inventory); a near-cap wallet or
  active job refuses the whole payout with the existing logged system line and `Ok`.
- No logging, secrets, session or trust-boundary change.
