# T369: Demo Derby Driver chapter 1, and a finished derby as a server-observed finish event

## Result

Quests 3060004 "Demo Derby: Rules of the Road" (Dalton Narrowbrow) and 3060005 "Demo Derby: License
to Drive" (Crash Davis) are now the job's first playable chapter. A fully finished demolition derby
credits `QuestEvent::FinishMinigame { minigame_id: 39 }` exactly once, through the same
`take_completion` ledger that pays the flat completion reward. 39 is the package's own game id
(`derby_entry.json` `game_id`, `DerbyGateway::game_id`), the MiniGameData row the 39/16 offer
already names. The credit does not depend on the flat payout: a payout that fits, a refused payout
and a package tuned to 0 coins / 0 XP all credit the finish once.

The trigger is the server's `DerbyPhase::Results`, reached only through `DerbyService::finish_match`.
Two things call it: the score target (`score_target`) and the time limit (`time_limit_ms`). The
shipped package has `time_limit_ms: null` and `score_target: 22`, so Results comes only when the
server's score, built from client 34/16 contact reports that the service bounds (pose age, contact
radius, cooldown, max impact), takes a driver to 22 points. A client can therefore drive its own
match to Results by reporting contacts. T219 accepted that for the flat reward, and the chapter
credit is once per character, so the risk is small. No 39/18 claim, cancel, quit, lobby timeout,
reconnect or replay can emit the event. A payout the wallet or job progress cannot represent is a
player-local refusal (one bounded chat line), not an `io::Error` that ends the session. The
finished-match ledger row and the quest credit stay committed (lead sign-off in the brief).

### Blocker 2: the quest credit depended on a non-zero payout (confirmed)

`take_completion` returned `None` for a 0 coin / 0 XP package. Both fields are serde defaults, and
`validate_content` accepts 0/0. A valid economy tune would therefore have made 3060005 impossible
while every audit stayed green. Fix: `take_completion` records every unseen Results instance (the
ledger stays bounded at `MAX_PAID_MATCHES` = 32). `completion_grant` returns `None` for a 0/0
package, which is now the only place the amount matters. `pay_derby_completion` marks the outcome
changed as soon as the ledger row is taken, emits the event, and skips `apply_grant` when there is
no grant (no toast, no line). New test `a_zero_reward_package_still_credits_the_finish_once`.

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client owns the 26/x, 49/x, 39/x and 34/x streams:

- **Forged NPC target/choice (26/8, 26/10).** The router resolves the runtime GUID, proves 12 m
  reach and zone, and accepts only an option the open 26/9 menu listed. An out-of-range 26/8 is
  `Err` and leaves `GameplayState` equal (test 7). A forged derby choice cannot start a match
  without `admit`.
- **Forged quest accept/turn-in (49/2, 49/14).** The engine needs an open `pending_offer` /
  `pending_turnin` for the named quest. The missing prerequisite blocks the offer, and a pre-finish
  49/14 is refused with nothing changed (test 7). No quest id, amount or profile comes from the
  client.
- **Forged or farmed finish.** The credit is emitted only in `pay_derby_completion`, after
  `take_completion` (phase `Results` + ledger miss). Results needs the score target. The score comes
  from client 34/16 contact reports that the service bounds (pose age, radius, cooldown, impact)
  but cannot independently verify. A client can therefore finish its own match by reporting
  contacts. That pays the flat reward once per match (T219 policy) and credits the chapter once per
  character (the quest engine ignores further events). A 39/18 claim, cancel/quit/lobby timeout,
  replayed results record or reconnect credits nothing (tests 1, 2, 4, 7).
- **Wrong-game credit.** The event carries the package's MiniGameData row (39), so a derby finish
  cannot credit another game's quest. A TCG (row 11) producer can no longer credit License to Drive.
- **Replay/farming.** `paid_matches` is the once-per-instance guard, and the instance counter resumes
  above it. A *different* finished match pays the flat reward again by T219 design.
- **Economy.** The chapter grants content coins/XP only, with no items and checked arithmetic. A
  near-cap wallet gets nothing and still consumes the ledger, so a refused payout cannot be retried
  into a double pay. A 0/0 package pays nothing and sends no toast.
- **Session isolation.** The refusal returns `Ok` with one line. A capped player keeps playing and
  others are untouched (test 3, the two-session test, the app_fuzz seed).
- **Greetings.** Content-only body ids read by T378's existing path. The client sends nothing new.
