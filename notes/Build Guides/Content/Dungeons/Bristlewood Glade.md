## Conflicts

1. **Wheel coins and members bonus.** The wiki text shows blanks ("coins"). The template fields hold 128 and 162. Mapping them to the members bonus and the wheel is an inference.
2. **Level gate.** No video shows the "Requires a level 5 combat job" line for this dungeon; it comes from the tier rule.
3. **Monster names.** "Vine Bears", "Vine Wolves", "Vileblooms" and "Arboreants" are in the wiki list. The client has Vilebloom and Arboreant names but only a "Vine Wolf Companion" card, no plain Vine Wolf or Vine Bear.
