## Conflicts

1. **Unlocking quest.** The wiki says Bigfoot only spawns for "Putting Your Foot Down". The video shows the lair entered during "Confronting Bigfoot". Treat both as valid triggers (video outranks wiki). Whether the node is shared or two different spawns is unknown.
2. **Page type.** The wiki lists this as a Battle (difficulty 3, Special Spawn), so it should not appear on the Atlas. The index page still lists it with the Combat Mini-game entries.
3. **Coins.** The wiki shows **990 Coins** on every prize wheel. The other level-5 entries in this guide pay 26 to 117 coins on their wheels. The figure may be a transcription error, but no video confirms or refutes it.
