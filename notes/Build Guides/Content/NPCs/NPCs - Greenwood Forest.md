## Overview

The wiki describes Greenwood Forest as "the large, heavily wooded area west of the Lavender Coast Pass warpstone", in Sacred Grove, with the local places Ambrose's Dig Site, Greenwood Camp, Southvale Quarry and Farnum's Farm. Its location box says Southern Wilds, and it says the forest "leads to two camps: Camp Chatty and Camp Robbie". [wiki Greenwood Forest] The wiki's Greenwood Forest category holds the camp, the Greenwood Camp, Southvale Quarry and the NPC pages used below. [wiki]

The client agrees on the cast and adds detail:

- **Greenwood Camp.** The chugawug archer camp, with three client names: "Greenwood Camp" (ugdt 846513991, ucdt 1097773386), "Greenwood Chugawug Camp" (991005917) and "Greenwood Archer camp" (1891915058). One Farnum line says "Greenwood Bandit Camp" (3104751287). It lies "in a camp west of the Lavender Coast Pass warpstone" (1117625082), "southwest of Sanctuary" (2313781218, 3739059730) and "to the southwest of Cobblestone Village" (2504933996). A Robgoblin Camp line puts the Robgoblin Camp "to the northeast of the Greenwood Camp" (1849268972). [client] The wiki says the camp is "in the southwestern corner of Sacred Grove", "home of Lugabow, a famous archer", reached by the Lavender Coast Pass warpstone and "heading west". [wiki Greenwood Camp]
- **Summer Camp (Camp Chatty and Camp Robbie).** The client calls the whole thing "Summer Camp": "Talk to the Summer Camp counselors around the lake near the Greenwood Forest warpstone" (3550878236), "Chatty's Summer Camp Party" (4246405752) and "Robbie's Summer Camp Party" (2741767061). Chatty's side is "Camp Chatty" (e.g. 166769791) and Robbie's side is "Camp Robbie" (e.g. 325011452). The campers pick a team: "I think he chose to be on Team Robbie" (4107037730). [client] The wiki: both camps are "at the perimeter of the Greenwood Forest" and are rivals; both came into the game on 4 June 2010. [wiki Camp Chatty, Camp Robbie, Chatty, Robbie]
- **Greenwood Forest Warpstone and Lavender Coast Pass.** The client has both names as separate entries (Greenwood Forest Warpstone ugdt 1750255830, ucdt 3262679133; Lavender Coast Pass ucdt 730190219, ugdt 1778183435; Lavender Coast Warpstone ucdt 2062654165). Whether the first is the same stone as the wiki's "Lavender Coast Pass warpstone" is not stated. See Conflicts 1. [client] [wiki]
- **Southvale.** Client names are "Southvale" (ugdt 740977041, ucdt 160608198), "Southvale Quarry" (ugdt 2012944397, ucdt 3173963098), "Southvale Outpost" and "Southvale Mining Camp" ("Iron ore is plentiful at the Southvale Mining Camp", 575950185). It is "far to the south, by the path leading to Lavender Coast" (1593153207), "down by the path to Lavender Coast, in the far south, west of Farnum's Farmstead" (803481107). [client] The wiki: "a mining corporation located in the Lavender Coast Pass warpstone toward the Greenwood Camp", with iron ore "only open to level 10+ miners", and the mines "south-east of the outpost, up a path that leads inside the mountains". [wiki Southvale Quarry]

The client also names the group in a world description: "The Southern Wilds are home to a wide variety of groups including the Queensfields Bixies, Greenwood Archers, Shadowtalon Ninjas, and Fairy Cultists." (3629363041). [client]

**What this page covers.** The NPCs the sources place in Camp Chatty, Camp Robbie, the Greenwood Camp, the forest and roads around them, Ambrose's Dig Site, the Bandit Hideout and Fleetfoot Ninja end of the forest, and Southvale. **What it leaves out.** Danger Peaks bosses Taka the Shadow and Sarah are on [[NPCs - Wilds]] Part I. ## Part A. The Summer Camp: Camp Chatty and Camp Robbie

Two rival camps by a lake near the Greenwood Forest warpstone. Camp Chatty is run by Chatty, a treeble, with four counselors; Camp Robbie is run by Robbie, a robgoblin, with four counselors. [wiki] [client] Both camps have a Treasure Hunt minigame and "Chatty Only" and "Robbie Only" quests (wiki). The camp quests are almost all repeatable (the wiki marks eight of them "repeatable"). The two Bigfoot battles ([[Bigfoot's Lair]], [[Bigfoot Defender]]) are the combat hooks and are started from camp quests. The camp lake is the course for the counselor Jake's run ("beat the current best time for running around the lake", 3570151688).

## Part B. The Greenwood Camp (chugawug archer camp) and the merchants around it

The base of the Greenwood Chugawugs: soldiers and archers who "are usually keeping bandits in line" (2610710507), led by Glugmort. The camp trains Archers (Lugabow) and is the base for the thugawug bandit and Shadow Talon quests. The camp perimeter holds ambient "Archers on Patrol" and target-dummy archers ("ambient NPCs firing arrows at target dummies in the Greenwood Camp", 607784854). [client] The wiki lists three shops at the camp (Burps, Belches, Jormajorm), a cooking table, and the two dungeons [[Bandit Hideout]] and [[Danger Peaks]]. [wiki Greenwood Camp]

## Notes: quest givers and trainers

- **The Greenwood Camp is the Archer hub, not the bandit quest hub only.** Lugabow starts the [[Archer]] line, and Hilforn (north of the camp) takes it to level 5. Glugmort, Blorple and Burnetta run the camp's own non-job quests, which have no job prefix: the "Chugawug Bandits" and "Feeding Glugmort" lines from the wiki (A Bandit's Life, What is a Shadow Talon?, The Mysterious Birds, Bad Ninjas, My Work is Done; Glugmort's Palate, A Fancy Meal for Glugmort), and Glugmort's dev-chapter bandit line. The Archer page already treats them as camp quests, not Archer-line quests. [client] [wiki]
- **The Wilds South book.** Chapter 2 (Military Intelligence, Thugawhomp, Scouting the Stompers, Searching the Hideout, Stompenmugg) and chapter 3 (Jojo A-go-go, Jojo's Mojo, Jojo's Dojo, Jojo's Ninjato, Dojo Mojo) are the book quests for the Greenwood end of the Wilds. The Larkspur text that sends the player to Glugmort (453460099) is the hand-off from chapter 1. Chapter 4 (The Mystery Caravan, Combat the Cultists, Secrets of the Den, Mayor McFairy!, Unmasking the Villain) belongs to Merry Vale. [client]
- **Camp quests are repeatable, and short.** Seven counselors give one quest each and Edmond gives three; the wiki marks eight of the ten counselor quests repeatable, and most wrap a minigame (feed the animals, tower defence, lake race, dig, scavenger hunt, fishing). Edmond holds the Bigfoot line; Robbie holds Rags to Riches, Certified Treasure Hunter and Bigfooted Friend. [wiki] [client]
- **Bigfoot is two quests from two sides.** Edmond's Confronting Bigfoot and Putting Your Foot Down lead to [[Bigfoot's Lair]]; Jacen's Bigfoot's Endangered leads to [[Bigfoot Defender]]. Chatty and Robbie are the two mascots (wiki) and Chatty's lines call Robbie "Bob". [client] [wiki]
- **Southvale is a two-trade camp.** Mili Ironcall serves both Miner and Blacksmith players; Supply Master Leo receives the Blacksmith scalpel delivery and sends the player on; Grumpore holds the Miner level 15 gate; Gerald Gemcutter and Yanaco are the jeweller and the merchant. See [[Miner]] and [[Blacksmith]].
- **Merchants** around the Greenwood Camp are all wiki-era shops: Jormajorm (level 1 Archer weapons, level 20 Archer armor), Burps (potions), Belches (Chef supplies), Roberge and Gilbert (Archer sets, added 6 Aug 2013), Samune (Blacksmith set, 6 Aug 2013), Ferd (Postman set, 1 Oct 2013) and Yanaco (Miner set, 15 Oct 2013). [wiki]

## Gaps

- **No coordinates.** The sources give directions only. The recordings carry distance markers and a few zone labels; none puts any Greenwood NPC at a map position.
- **Video coverage is thin.** The recordings show Chatty (as a guide, not in the camp), Counselor Edmond, Tyra, Jenny and Wes, Robbie, Carson, Isabella, Blorple (inside Danger Peaks), Royal Courier, Thugamug (tracker only) and Muggenstomp. No recording shows Glugmort, Lugabow, Hilforn, Burnetta, Roroo, any Southvale NPC, Jacen, Cammy, Kaela, Jake, Matron Murgla, Zek or Ambrose, and no recording carries a Greenwood zone label. Most rows therefore rest on [client] and [wiki].
- **Quest titles for camp and forest errands.** Client titles are stored apart from descriptions. Titles with no matched description: Thugawhomp, A Bandit's Life, Jojo's Dojo, Scalpel for Mili, the Southvale titles, Friendly Competition, Turning the Tables and Digging for Glory. Descriptions with no title: Carson's ducks and report, the weapons-testing text, Roroo's care package (only "NOT LIVE" titles), Gerald's turquoise, Burnetta's feeding and contest errands, Trusty's letter (possibly Lofty Letter).
- **Rewards and levels.** Almost no source states a reward for a camp, forest or Southvale quest. Known: the Camp Chatty Hat for Confronting Bigfoot (video) and the Rob Robbie daily loot tiers (wiki, client strings). Levels for every quest here are unknown except the Archer and Miner/Blacksmith gates on their job pages.
- **Standing place not stated.** Argo, Arpi, Lopanio, Counselor Amira, Carson, Gerald Gemcutter, Jason Maskface's exact spots and Ambrose's actual position have no usable place line.
- **Wiki-only merchants.** Jormajorm, Belches, Burps, Roberge, Gilbert, Ferd, Samune and Yanaco are wiki-only for place and stock (item names were checked in the client). Jormajorm, Belches and Burps have no stock list at all.
- **Jason Maskface and One of Three** are 2013 event NPCs; their spawn rules are not in the client locale. Camp Chatty and Camp Robbie came in on 4 June 2010 (wiki); the merchants were added on 6 August, 1 October and 15 October 2013, so the shop rows reflect the 2013 game, not launch.
- **Retired content.** No NPC on this page is marked "no longer in-game" by the wiki. "NOT LIVE" titles exist for the Greenwood Care Package, which may be cut content.
- **Group sizes, boss health and fight scripts** for the Bigfoot battles, Bandit Hideout and Fleetfoot Ninja are on the dungeon pages, not here.
- **The Summer Camp events** ("Chatty's Summer Camp Party", 4246405752; "Robbie's Summer Camp Party", 2741767061; "A luau party has started in the Greenwood Forest!", 363800339; "a dinner party tonight at the Greenwood Forest", 1327266146; and a Free Realms Treasure War arena "in Greenwood Forest", 3336288510) are event content with no NPC rows and no further text found.
