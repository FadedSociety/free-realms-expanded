# T659: holiday seasons from the schedule evidence

Brief: `client_areas.json` defined one season (Halloween); the T462 scheduler gates events on season names. The schedule
pass (`lab/references/quest-lines/wiring/seasons.json`, 15 groups, 58 rows, every row `final` and `yearly_season`) gives
more.

## What changed

- `data/content/client_areas.json`: seven new season rows. Halloween is untouched.
- `src/client_areas.rs`: a season may now have an empty `area_ids` (one line removed from `validate`, one doc line on the
  field). It still gates events through `is_season_active`; `held`, `world_ready_packets` and `season_changes` already
  handle it (an empty list adds and removes no area, so a boundary crossing sends no packet).
- `tests/client_areas.rs`: the "empty season" refusal case is gone (the loader accepts it now); two new tests.

## Client areas

Read every `<AreaDefinition>` in `FabledRealmsAreas.xml` (1,279; 138 with `AreaEnabled="0"`, the only ones the server can
switch). The only holiday-named switchable areas are Halloween's (3992-4004, already in the Halloween row). No area
name carries Snow Days, 12 Days, Hearts, Birthday, Charity Drive, Summer Camp or Turkey (names searched: snow, winter,
holiday, heart, valent, birthday, party, cake, turkey, thanks, summer, camp, charity, gift, present, anniv, bash, fest,
cupid, love, pumpkin, spook, ghost, haunt, trick; the `snowhill_*_pad` rows and `SnowHill_Stage` are generic pads and
the concert stage). The 3607-3753 `Seaside/Farnum/Lakeshore` Meatballs/Vegetables/Fruits rows and the
`DaresConcert`, `Robgoblin_Rockband`, `Tidepool_pad`, `gloam_invasion` rows have no client string (IDA `strings` finds
none) and no schedule row that names them, so they are not assigned to a season. The seven new rows ship `area_ids: []`
with that said in their `source`.

## Halloween: kept, with an open question

The shipped row (Sep 20 to Nov 11) is the Sanctuary reference's, and its own source calls the dates (R). The schedule
evidence disagrees: every dated Halloween run starts Oct 1 or later (2009 Oct 14, 2010 Oct 1, 2011 Oct 13, 2013 Oct 1)
and ends Oct 31 (2010), Nov 11 (2009), Nov 12 (2013) or Nov 23 (2011). Oct 1 to Nov 23 would cover all of them; the
current row starts 11 days before any dated run and cuts off the last 12 days of 2011. Not changed here: the brief
asks Halloween's behaviour to stay, and the dance-off tests and the 14-area count pin it. A window change is one data
edit plus those tests; the lead decides.
