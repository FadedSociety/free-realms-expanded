# T021 - Route soccer through the activity portal

## Result

Soccer is reachable through the activity portal's Play request. The bundled
`data/content/soccer.json` publishes the three shipped Soccer minigame rows
(`42` Sacred Grove, `43` Snowhill, `44` Briarwood). Portal Play (`167/1/2`)
zones the player into the captured-candidate `sg_soccer` scene; after Loaded,
the native family-76 roster and the shared type-12 `39/16` offer are sent.
Family-76 input is validated by the existing server authority, server ticks
own the clock and score, and full time settles through `MinigameRuns` and
`rewards::apply_grant`.

The prior patch's rejects were caused by the new typed activity catalogue:
the tip changed portal rows from a bare vector into `Activity.kind`. The port
adds `Kind::Soccer` and routes all admission/category/list behavior through
that one model instead of the old parallel soccer-id set. The tree now also
filters soccer through `warp_blocker`, ride restrictions, gameplay ticks and
gateway tick eligibility, and returns to the pre-match world on lifecycle
close/cancel.

## Reconstructed rules (R)

- Solo portal entry fills the other seven 4v4 slots with passive bots; there
  is no cross-session match registry, invitation flow or bot AI.
- Soccer ignores the combat-job gate and uses the configured level floor
  (`0` bundled). The original Soccer Star/level gate is not recovered.
- Pitch bounds, physics, kickoff, half length, side switching, automatic
  restart/second-half transitions and reward amounts are tunable content
  values. The bundled match is 180 seconds per half and pays 10 coins.
- A win pays once through the stable `minigame-run:<instance>` key; a tie or
  loss pays nothing. Matches are session-local and a reconnect drops them
  without a grant.
- The shared type-12 result sequence is a compatibility adapter. The original
  Soccer launch and result packet exchange is not recovered.

## Open gaps

- No original-client Soccer run was executed. Launch/result acceptance and
  `sg_soccer` scene values remain unverified natively.
- Multi-human roster, team invitations, score relay and bot AI are absent.
- Pass states remain refused because the family-76 payload does not establish
  a server-verified teammate target.
- NPC Soccer Gate and matchmaking (`141`) entry are not wired; the implemented
  client-facing entry is activity-portal Play.
- `39/16` uses shared empty metadata defaults; Soccer-specific name/icon and
  result fields need native verification.
