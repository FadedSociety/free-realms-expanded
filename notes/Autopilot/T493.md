# T493: Bruce's concert on the Snowhill and Wugachug stages

Parts 1-3 of the world events (T458, T460, T462) and T464 (the Halloween dance-off) are on the tip, so the fleet
research pack's "unmerged prerequisites" claim was stale. This task is content: two scheduled events in
`data/content/world_events.json` (joined with T464's `halloween_dance_off` row, whose `skip_if_slot_matches` names
the two Bruce keys and was dropped at startup until they existed), plus one loader relaxation and the gate tests.
Attempt 2: ported onto the T464 tip; the patch's file hunks conflicted only on the shared JSON and two assertions.

## What changed

- `world_events.json` now holds `bruce_snowhill` and `bruce_wugachug` (before the dance-off row). Each has one actor (model 20
  `human_rockstar_m.adr`, name locale 201, stand animation 1) and a schedule of 240-minute slots, offsets 0 and
  120, so the stages alternate every two hours and each stage repeats every four. Timeline of one run:
  - 0 s: spawn with poof 21, a zone-wide broadcast, and an idle `cycle_anim` (3333, 3354, 3363, 3319, 3347, 3346,
    every 15 s plus up to 10 s of jitter, until 300 s).
  - 300 s: area on (17 or 4285) and an air-guitar `cycle_anim` (3350 every 8 s for 181 s).
  - 481 s: area off, remove with poof 21.
- `src/world_events.rs`: a `broadcast` step's `locale_id` is now optional (a given id must still be > 0). Snowhill
  has no client line, and the loader forced an id, so the data would otherwise have to name a wrong one.
- `tests/bruce_concert.rs` (new, 4 tests; they use only the `bruce_` rows, because the dance-off shares the file and
  runs every 30 minutes in season). `schedule.rs`'s shipped-file assertion now lists the three scheduled keys.
  `tests/halloween_dance_off.rs` lost its helper that fabricated Bruce's two slot rows when absent (now they ship).

## (R) choices

- The 240-minute slots with offsets 0 and 120 (Sanctuary alternates stages every 2-hour slot of the server's local
  day: Snowhill on even slots).
- Local time is unix time plus `utc_offset_minutes` of `client_areas.json` (0 as shipped, T462's rule). Sanctuary
  reads the host's local clock, so set the offset to the host's if the times should match.
- The Snowhill broadcast text, "A concert is starting at the Snowhill Stage!", worded after client locale 5142
  (locale 94141 is only the place name). Sanctuary's own English line is not used.
- Stand animation 1 (as `enemies.rs`); the AddNpc body reader `0x00921a10` reads the stand field from every
  spawn (it follows Walk/Run in the string-delimited body; `tests/piece_052.rs` pins the empty-template offsets
  137/141/145). The content gate checks id 1 resolves as `loc_stand` in both animation tables.
- Poof 21 is Sanctuary's `StageChangeCompositeEffectId`; the client's `ActorCompositeEffectDefinitions.xml` has id 21
  as `PFX_smoke_black_explosion` (the test checks it). Whether it looks right on the stage is a native check.
- Wugachug's heading is the script's -1.715594 (the brief rounds it to -1.7156).
- The broadcast reaches the whole open-world instance (no radius), as the brief says "the zone".
- The concert grants nothing: the runner touches no inventory, coins or player state.

## Threat note

Nothing here parses client input: the rows come from the operator's content file, bounded at load (T458/T460/T462
bounds), and a player cannot start, stop or alter the concert. The loader change only lets a field be absent; a given
`locale_id` of 0 or less is still refused (test `the_loader_refuses_bad_part_two_ops`, case "no locale").
