# Oasis of Peril

Part of [[Content]] › [[Dungeons]]. Tags: **[client]** = game locale text (`loc <id>`), **[video]** = player recordings (frame key), **[wiki]** = fan wiki. Precedence client > video > wiki.

> Oasis of Peril is a **node battle**, not an Atlas dungeon. The wiki files it under *Battle*, *Combat Mini-game* and *Special Spawn*, difficulty 4. It is started by clicking the boss NPC **Surfmonger Arthur**. [wiki: Oasis of Peril!] No video of the fight itself was found.
