## Conflicts

1. **Star payout.** The wiki's "242 Stars or less" is a cap; one video result shows "Stars x119". Both are kept. The video figure is an actual payout.
2. **Coin figures.** The wiki wheel slot is 80 Coins. The video result shows "Coins x82". The extra 2 is not explained (score bonus, level bonus, or a different build).
3. **Prize name.** The lobby frame appears to read "Archer's Treasure Shard of Vitality II", which is not in the client. The client and wiki item is "Archer's Power Shard of Vitality II". The frame text is marked medium confidence.
4. **Goal spelling.** Video OCR reads "Save the Serpentail", "Save the S?perilla" and "Big Hungry Beast". The client string is "Save the Sasparilla!" and "Defeat the Big Hungry Bear!". Client wins.
