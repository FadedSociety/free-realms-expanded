  level-gate payout Postman level 10 (3130017, T663), Chef, Miner and Warrior gates already pay.
- `live` is "unsure" but the record is final and the text is the client's own. The Postman guide marks
  Brin as giver with a dagger (placement uncertain); the NPC row lists the quest.
