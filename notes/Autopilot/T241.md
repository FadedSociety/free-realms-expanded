## Security threat note (secure-code-guardian)

- Attack surface: a hostile client can send 26/8, 26/10, 26/6, 49/2, 49/14,
  forged 39/x type8 payloads and forged field-attack selections at any time.
  No parser, selector, packet family or client-supplied amount was added.
- Offers are server-gated: `offer_denial` checks giver, enabled/objectives,
  pending offer, prerequisites, the active+unlocked job level and the quest
  limit; `accept` rechecks the requirement. A client cannot choose a job for
  the gate: `character_eligibility` builds `job_levels` from the saved active
  profile only (`gameplay/npc_quests.rs`), never from a packet.
- Kills and gathers are server-authoritative: the kill event comes from the
  encounter's defeat result, the gather event from the Type8 engine's staged
  transaction/`grant` (no client score or win report reaches it). Forging a win
  payload after the run closes changes nothing (asserted).
- Rewards are content rows paid once through the existing
  `quest:<character>:<quest>` grant key and the staged `RewardState`; a forged
  or replayed 49/14 with no server-opened end screen is denied and leaves the
  save bytes equal. No new free item is granted, so there is nothing new to
  sell or re-grant; the mining ore is the normal node payout.
- Refusals are fail-closed: wrong job, disabled predecessor, no end screen,
  out-of-range interaction and replayed wins all assert the complete
  `GameplayState` is unchanged; the loader rejects a job profile or `job_xp`
  profile that does not exist in `jobs.json` (`InvalidData`, no unwrap).
- Residual, pre-existing gap (not introduced here, `src/gameplay/npc.rs` is
  S15/S16 for this task): once a 49/13 end screen has been opened legitimately,
  the 49/14 handler does not recheck interaction range or NPC before settling
  the pending turn-in. The slice cannot add that check without editing
  `npc.rs`; the test asserts the 49/14 attack with **no** server-opened screen
  is inert, and out-of-range 26/8 is refused. Lead follow-up.
