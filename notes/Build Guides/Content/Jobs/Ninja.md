## How to read this page

- Tags: **[client]** is the game's locale text (`en_us_data.dat`), **[video]** is player recordings, **[wiki]** is the fan wiki. Precedence is client, then video, then wiki. Where they disagree, the higher source is in the table and the clash is in **Conflicts**.
- Numbers in parentheses, for example `(3658895198)`, are client locale ids. The "Title ids" column gives both title entries for a quest as `ucdt` / `ugdt`; the two tags carry the same text.
- Video frame keys look like `(WhXTB5BvjG8/00000)`.
- **Matching caveat.** The locale file stores quest titles, descriptions, objectives and dialog as separate entries with no link between them. Every description or objective below was matched to its quest by wording (names, places, enemies), not by a data link. Matches that rest on wording alone are marked with a dagger (†). A dagger means "fits this quest, not proven". A match with no dagger either repeats the quest title in its own words, or is shown on screen in a video frame. Strings that fit no listed quest are in **Unmatched client text**.
- **The wiki has no pages for the Ninja quests.** The wiki archive holds the `Ninja` job page, the NPC pages for Ty, Frostpetal and Ree Peatpants, and weapon pages. The quest pages (`Quest:Ninja: ...`) are linked but not archived. Wiki facts about a single quest therefore come only from the job page's quest list, the NPC pages, or a weapon page that names its reward quest.
- Rewards (coins, XP, items) do not appear in the locale file and no video frame shows a Ninja quest reward. The only rewards known are four weapons that wiki weapon pages credit to a named quest. Every other reward cell reads "unknown".
- The client spells "Handle with Care" with a lower-case "with"; the wiki page has "Handle With Care". The client spelling is used.

## Overview

Ninja is a close-combat job that fights with swords and shadow skills, specialising in sneak attacks and confusion. [wiki] The client's own short description is "Ninjas are deadly Brawlers with dual swords and shadow skills." (2724607274) [client]. Experience comes from Ninja quests, battles in the Wilds, and dungeons. [wiki] The client calls Ninja one of the "Advanced Jobs like Ninja, Wizard, and Warrior ... that you can earn by being a master Archer, Medic, or Brawler" (1073904650). [client]

**Members only.** The wiki says the job is locked for members. As of 5 December 2009 it is free to sample through level 4: a free player can change into Ninja but cannot use it in combat until they buy a membership. [wiki] The client has a matching prompt: "Oh, no! Want to keep playing as a Ninja? Upgrade to unlock more fun!" (1381742725). [client] The client's free-to-level-20 blurb names only Brawler, Miner and Blacksmith (2640708938), so Ninja is not part of it. [client] See **Conflicts**.

**Unlocking.** The wiki says to speak to Master Ty in southern Lakeshore. [wiki] The client agrees on the place and the first step: "Learn how to be a ninja by finding Ty in Lakeshore." (1238810418), "Speak with Ty in Lakeshore to learn how to become a Ninja" (2792566015). [client] The client carries a second, different gate: Ninja as an advanced job that needs a level 20 combat job. A job-board notice reads "Wanted: Skilled Archers for Ninja Training ... Semi-retired Lakeshore Ninja seeks experienced archers to learn the way of the silent sword ... Requires: Level 20 Archer" (1592607723), and a trainer line says "The Way of the Ninja is only for those fleet of foot, still of mind, and swift of blade - and you are not ready ... Luguabow's Path of the Archer is open to you! Achieve level 20 as an Archer and return to me to begin your Ninja training!" (2334013303). [client] The wiki does not mention an Archer prerequisite. See **Conflicts**.

**Story hook (wiki order, client wording).** Ty is a retired master of the Flying Dragon Clan whose dojo fell. He sends a newcomer to the Forest Troll Village over the pixie kidnappings, then sets the stealth lessons, then hands the player to Chloe in Blackspore. The line then runs through Snowhill (Frostpetal), back to Ty, then to Sakka at the Pixie Nursery and Michi in Snowhill. [client][wiki]

**Levels.** The job runs from level 1 to 20 (**max_level 20**). [wiki] The wiki and client both give four gates at level 5, 10, 15 and 20, each with its own "Achieve Level" quest and its own trainer: Chloe at 5, Ty at 10, Sakka at 15, Michi at 20. [client][wiki]

2. **Sakka's location.** Wiki: southwest of Wugachug. Client: Pixie Nursery (2053496107; the Nursery is "west of Snowhill", 889138964). The client location is used. The wiki phrase is probably the Floren Forest area, but that is a guess.
3. **Giver of Troubled Waters.** The wiki job list puts it in the run of quests after Chloe's Achieve Level 5 and the first contract, with Chloe as the last named giver; the wiki Frostpetal page lists it among her appearances. The page keeps Chloe as the listed giver and notes Frostpetal.

One client wording (1599642277) says "Before you return to Lakeshore ... strike stealthily, from the shadows", pointing at Ty. Two stages of one quest, or an older version; not settled.

7. **Wiki NPC list vs. quest list.** The wiki Ninja page's quest list stops at Achieve Level 20 and one contract, but the wiki Ty page lists Walking the Path, Gathered Shadows and Stilling the Waters, and the wiki weapon page for Molten Dragon Blade names Restoring Honor.
