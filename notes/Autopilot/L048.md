## What changed

- **AddPc referee byte from the staff role.** `Gateway::bind_account_role` (the host-only binding
  made at login, after the handoff ticket and character owner are verified) now fixes
  `Gateway.referee` from the role it just read: true for Moderator and Admin (R, OSFR policy from
  #110 62b44b7 and 71e294b, evidence only). `Gateway::player_presentation` copies it into
  `PlayerPresentation.referee`, which the existing AddPc encoder writes (`players.rs:792`).
  Display only: nothing on the server reads the flag back.
- **Role change mid-session: the flag follows at the next login** (the brief's first option).
  Authority still follows the role at once (`refresh_role` on every message, L042, unchanged);
  only the display waits. A kick (L042) forces the relog when a demoted staffer's flag must go now.
- **189 encoder fixed to the client's 4-byte layout.** `encode_membership_service_flags(kind,
  flag_a, flag_b)` is now `encode_membership_subscription_info(lifetime_member, referee)` and
  writes exactly `[189, 0, l, r]`. The old extra i16 did not just shift the flags: `008f7300`
  refuses a packet with unread bytes, so the client dropped the whole packet.
- **189 is not sent.** See "Brief premise" below: the client evidence contradicts the brief's
  meaning of byte 1, and byte 2 has no reader. Sending it needs a decision first.

## Brief premise (for the lead)

The brief (after OSFR) calls 189 `{IsMember, IsReferee}`. The client disagrees:
- Byte 1 (+0x468) is the **lifetime-member** flag, not membership. Sending `is_member` there would
  show every member as a lifetime member in the Marketplace and answer a membership purchase
  with "You are already a lifetime member". Membership itself is +0x464 (opcode 100 and the
  self-data path).
- Byte 2 (+0x469) changes nothing on screen in this client.

So the encoder is fixed and named after the client, and the "send it once after the world loads"
step is left out: with lifetime false (the client default) and a byte nobody reads, the packet
would change nothing, and with lifetime = membership it makes a product decision. Question for
the owner (the lead adds it to `questions-for-the-owner.md`; builders may not write `lab/autopilot/`):
"Should members be shown as lifetime members in the Marketplace (189 byte 1)?"

Second question the brief asked to file: "Should admins show as referees too, or only
moderators?" (today both do, R).

## (R) choices

1. Moderator and Admin show as referees; Builder and Player do not (OSFR policy, display only).
2. The flag is fixed at login; a role change shows at the next login.
3. 189's second byte is named `referee` after OSFR; no client code reads it.

## Security

Trust boundary: the flag comes only from the account store role keyed by the host-verified owner
account (`bind_account_role`). No client packet is parsed into it, and no server code reads
`PlayerPresentation.referee` except the AddPc encoder (`rg '\.referee'`).

Threat note (what a hostile client could do):
- Claim the flag: impossible; there is no client field for it, and AddPc is built server-side.
- Keep the flag after demotion: yes until the next login (chosen semantics). It grants nothing:
  every staff command re-reads the role per message (L042). An admin kick ends it at once.
- Learn who is staff: by design, every viewer in range sees the referee flag of staff
  characters. It does not depend on the viewer or any request, so it is no new oracle beyond the
  display OSFR intends. Whether admins should be visible goes to the owner.
- Unreadable store at login: role Player, flag false (fails closed, existing `refresh_role`).
- Nothing logged, nothing saved; 189 is not sent.
