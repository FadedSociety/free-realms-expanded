## How to read this page

- Tags: **[client]** is the game's locale text (`en_us_data.dat`), **[video]** is player recordings, **[wiki]** is the fan wiki. Precedence is client, then video, then wiki. Where they disagree, the higher source is in the table and the clash is in **Conflicts**.
- Numbers in parentheses, for example `(1496851134)`, are client locale ids. The "Title ids" column gives both title entries for a quest as `ucdt` / `ugdt`; the two tags carry the same text.
- Video frame keys look like `(hmx3WC_T-V8/00106)`.
- **Matching caveat.** The locale file stores quest titles, descriptions, objectives and dialog as separate entries with no link between them. Every description or objective below was matched to its quest by wording (names, places, enemies), not by a data link. Matches that rest on wording alone are marked with a dagger (†). A dagger means "fits this quest, not proven". A match with no dagger either repeats the quest title in its own words, or is shown on screen in a video frame. Strings that fit no listed quest are in **Unmatched client text**.
- **The wiki has no pages for the Warrior quests.** The wiki archive holds the `Warrior` job page (a trainer-by-trainer quest list with no quest text), NPC pages for Steele, General Glowlight and Doctor Gordon, the `Warrior Power Attacks` page, and weapon pages. Wiki facts about a single quest therefore come only from the job page's list, an NPC page, or a weapon page that names its reward quest.
- Rewards (coins, XP, items) do not appear in the locale file. The only rewards known are four weapons that wiki weapon pages credit to a named quest, one wiki line for the job-unlock weapon, and one partly readable video reward banner. Every other reward cell reads "unknown".
- The wiki job page spells one title short ("Knocking Some Sense") and loses the dash in the last contract ("Defending Snowhill from ..."). The client spelling is used throughout.
- Quest counts: **26 client quest titles** for this job: 19 `Warrior:` titles in the wiki list, "Becoming a Warrior" (no prefix), 4 repeatable `Contract:` titles, and 2 client-only `Warrior:` titles (Rescue and Reclaim, A Skilled Smith). The wiki list has 24 of them.

## Overview

Warrior is a members-only close-combat job built on heavy weapons and armor: cudgels, axes and hammers, and the abilities Kick, Spinning Blade, Quake and Commanding Shout. [wiki][client] The client's own short description is "Warriors are hardy Brawlers that can take and dish out damage." (3971267496). [client] Experience comes from Warrior quests and from battles in the Wilds and in dungeons. [wiki] The client intro tip lists Warrior with Ninja and Wizard as "Advanced Jobs ... that you can earn by being a master Archer, Medic, or Brawler" (1073904650). [client]

**Members only.** The wiki marks Warrior "Members Only" on both the job page and the Jobs list. [wiki] The client has a matching upgrade prompt, "Oh, no! Want to keep playing as a Warrior? Upgrade to unlock more fun!" (3572684679), and a new-member offer, "If you join now, you'll receive this special new member pack of warrior items!" (4186282497). [client] The client's free-to-level-20 blurb names only Brawler, Miner and Blacksmith (2640708938), so Warrior is not part of it. [client] The wiki Jobs page says most jobs top out at level 4 for non-members. [wiki] The details of the free tier are not mapped for Warrior; see **Gaps**.

**Unlocking.** The wiki says to speak with Drill Sergeant Dewey in Snowhill, "in front of the Town Hall". [wiki] The client agrees on the first step: "Speak with Drill Sergeant Dewey in Snowhill to learn how to become a Warrior" (2140994752), "Learn how to be a warrior by finding Drill Sergeant Dewey in Snowhill." (1374599761), "Drill Sergeant Dewey can set you on the path of the warrior." (241604555). [client] A recruiter line from an unnamed speaker reads "If you think you can hang, you should talk to Drill Sergeant Dewey in Snowhill" (3377274837). [client] The video shows Dewey's nameplate with the title "Warrior Trainer" (Dsk5NVq5YMU/00302). [video]

**Prerequisite (client only).** The client gates the job behind a level-20 combat job. A job-board notice reads "Wanted: Skilled Brawlers for Warrior Training ... Sanctuary Warrior School of Tanking is looking for a few experienced brawlers to learn the ways of the Warrior. Perks include wielding giant axes, spinning like crazy, and being the toughest fighter around! ... Requires: Level 20 Brawler" (79965512). A trainer line says "I can't allow people that aren't battle hardened to start down the path of the Warrior. It's a dangerous profession! Come back after you've reach level 20 as a Brawler." (190929883) †. [client] The wiki does not mention a prerequisite. See **Conflicts**.

**Story hook.** The line is a boot-camp story that turns into the legend of Fastvi Frostflutter. Dewey tests a recruit's battle cry and fitness, Morninglory holds the initiation at the Hot Springs Haven, and Steele gives weapon training. The middle of the line sends the player around Merry Vale, the Roadhouse and Sanctuary for weapon crates, an injured trainer and the Royal Guard. The last third chases the lost hero Fastvi Frostflutter through an old tome, Chillblaine in Frostfang Caverns and the repair of his axe. [client][wiki]

**Levels.** The job runs from level 1 to 20 (**max_level 20**). [wiki] Four gates stop the line at a named trainer until the level is reached: Steele at 5, Aster at 10, Sir Whittington at 15, Fastvi Frostflutter at 20. [client][wiki] See **Trainers**.

## Conflicts

1. **Unlock prerequisite.** Wiki: speak to Dewey, no prerequisite. Client: a job-board notice and a trainer line require **level 20 Brawler** (79965512, 190929883), and the intro tip lists Warrior as an advanced job earned by mastering Archer, Medic or Brawler (1073904650). This page records level 20 Brawler as a client-only requirement. Whether the live game enforced it is not proven.
2. **Dewey's location.** Wiki: Snowhill, in front of the Town Hall. Client: Snowhill "near the town hall" (3983863291), "Speak to Drill Sergeant Dewey in Snowhill" (334997347), but also an older Sanctuary version: "Welcome to the Sanctuary barracks. I am Drill Sergeant Dewey, the warrior trainer." (1388742978), "Welcome to the Sanctuary barracks! Would you like to become a warrior?" (1315591681), "Drill Sergeant Dewey can be found at the barracks in Sanctuary." (3026159686), "Dewey is behing the barracks in Sanctuary." (2141078721). The page uses Snowhill, as the wiki and most client lines do. The Sanctuary lines belong to the old boot-camp line.
3. **Reminder for W1 names Glowlight.** "Still need some time to come up with a fierce battle cry to show to General Glowlight?" (3792909267) fits the battle-cry task but names Glowlight, not Dewey, again pointing to the old Sanctuary version.
4. **Steele's place.** Wiki NPC page: a pixie in the Snowhill Town Hall. Client: the Snowhill warrior barracks (1569616597), the Clocktower (3222909153), barracks to the southeast (973369277). Client wording is used.
5. **Aster's and Caleb's place.** Wiki: both in the Northern Wilds. Client: Aster on the road south out of Merry Vale (857744776); Caleb south of the Roadhouse (1804566446). The Roadhouse is north of Merry Vale (2396340712), so these are not clearly different places.
6. **Title spelling.** Wiki job page: "Knocking Some Sense" and "Contract: Defending Snowhill from ...". Client and the wiki weapon page: "Knocking Some Sense In"; client "Contract: Defending Snowhill". The wiki "Warrior: Hero in the Making Coins" has a stray word. Client titles are used.
7. **Enemy name in "Knocking Some Sense In".** Video tracker: "Defeat Thugarnig" (hmx3WC_T-V8/00106), and "Defeat Thugamug?" in a second frame (hmx3WC_T-V8/00014). Client: "Thugamug" (several strings, 1472926074, 2448940401) and no "Thugarnig". The page treats "Thugarnig" as a misread of Thugamug. The video frame reading "Thugarnig" is the only on-screen evidence of this objective.
8. **Snarler direction.** Dewey's description says the Frostfang Snarlers are "to the western side of Snowhill" (2218766120). Other client lines say south-east (937190059, 1962735708, 3693529699). Both are client text; south-east has three lines.
9. **Fastvi's Frozen Fire reward.** Wiki weapon page: from "Warrior: A Skilled Smith". Wiki job page: lists "A Magical Boost" under Fastvi and has no "A Skilled Smith". Client: both titles exist (3329033861, 4178272706). The page lists the weapon under A Magical Boost with the conflict noted, and A Skilled Smith as client-only.
10. **Ability numbers.** Wiki: Spinning Blade "50-68", Commanding Shout "50-68", Quake "83-126". Client rank text: 50 to 75, 50 to 75 and 83 to 125. The wiki looks like a rounded rank-4 view; client numbers are used.
11. **Weapon item levels (wiki internal).** The job page puts Pro Warrior Hammer at level 10 but its own page says level 8; Warlord Axe 19 vs 16; Dual Smasher 11 vs 12; Smasher 17 vs 12; Cudgel 3 and Axe 3 vs 1 and 4. The client carries no weapon levels in this pass. Weapon pages are used for the quest-weapon table.
12. **Number of target dummies.** The Dewey race text says "six target dummies" (1019274433, 1987379260) but the client also holds "Touch the seventh target dummy!" (1503282211), "Touch the eighth target dummy!" (430407344) and a "Target Dummy Nine" (452830231). Those probably belong to other jobs' dummy minigames or an earlier version.
13. **Members only.** Wiki and client both mark Warrior members-only. The wiki Jobs page says most jobs stop at level 4 without membership; no source says how far a free player can play Warrior.
14. **Fastvi's place.** Wiki: Snowhill. Client: "Fastvi Frostflutter in Snowhill" (1885943680), but also "outside of Frostfang Caverns" (4099574863, 2344779759) and "Fastvi's Retreat" (3573835553). Probably one fixed place near Frostfang Caverns.

## Gaps

- **Rewards.** No coin, XP or item rewards for any quest are known except the weapon credits in the quest-weapon table and one partly readable banner ("11 stars and 4?3 coins", hmx3WC_T-V8/00106). Per-quest rewards likely live in server quest data that is not in the locale or the video.
- **Quest-to-text link.** All description, objective and dialog matches are by wording. W2, W7, W8, W11, W14, W15, W20, W21, W23 and W24 are the weakest; their dagger clusters could shift between neighbouring quests. W7 and W20 share the Roadhouse weapon-crate strings.
- **Quest order.** Order is the wiki's trainer-by-trainer order. The client suggests a different story order in the Fastvi arc: Whittington's trio (W18, W19) and Steele's memory (W20) before the Arestol and Iris chase (W15), then Fastvi's summoning, then W21. The wiki puts W15 before the level-15 gate.
- **Level requirements** for quests other than the four "Achieve Level" gates are not stated. The contracts' level is unknown.
- **Contract objectives.** W11 has no matched title text. Counts, enemy lists and rewards for all four contracts are unknown.
- **Prerequisite.** Whether the level-20 Brawler gate was enforced is unknown. The Sanctuary-barracks Dewey and the boot-camp line are not placed in time.
- **Traits.** The wiki lists no Warrior traits. The client names Warrior's Spirit, Warrior's Boon and Crushing Blows but gives no unlock level or tie to the job level.
- **Ability details.** Star cost per rank is unknown. The unlock levels (1, 5, 10, 15) come from the wiki only; the client confirms the order through the gate text.
- **Video coverage.** One frame set for a Warrior quest ("Knocking Some Sense In"). No frames for Morninglory, Steele, Tumbolina, Aster, Glowlight, Whittington or Fastvi. Dewey is seen once, without a quest. The Warrior-level frames in the table above show combat dungeons only.
- **Locations** inside Snowhill and Sanctuary for Dewey, Morninglory, Steele, Fastvi and Whittington need the NPC zone pages. Milo has no location. Michelle's role is only the wiki's "warrior guild helper".
- **Free tier.** What a non-member Warrior can do (level 4 cap, no combat) is not mapped for this job.
- **Old boot-camp line.** Titles and strings are present but give no order, giver or rewards.
