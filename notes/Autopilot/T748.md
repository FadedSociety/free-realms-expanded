## Security note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client can send 26/8 with any actor GUID, 41/108 with arbitrary encounter/instance
words, 36/10 with arbitrary target/ability, 41/109 or 26/42 at any time, 45/7 objective completion
reports, 41/103 invite replies, 41/122 revives, and 39/46 wheel-stop reports. The design refuses
all of them: the marker is resolved from server content with range/level/busy gates; Go uses only
the server-held pending screen; attacks and rewards derive from server-owned HP and range; Close
pays only its own current run, once; 45/7 and 39/46 grant nothing on their own; invites must match
a server-held unexpired row and recheck every gate; the revive body carries no price or place;
saves use `character_store::replace_bytes`. The full threat note is in the research note.
