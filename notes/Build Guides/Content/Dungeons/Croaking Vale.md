## Conflicts

1. **Hannah's spelling.** The panel description spells "Hanna" (`loc 933164626`), every other client string spells "Hannah". The NPC name strings read "Smuggler Hannah". "Hannah" is used here.
2. **Members-only coin figure.** The wiki says 308 coins. A video results screen reads 583 coins at score 1,454,750 (another run's score 1,427,000 had unreadable counts). The Robgoblin Treasure Trove screen reads 105, which matches its wiki figure, so the bonus may be fixed per dungeon and one of these Croaking Vale numbers wrong, or it may scale with score. Unresolved; the video number is recorded.
3. **Location.** The wiki dungeon page says Nettleseed, Briarwood. The wiki Briarheart page says the Croaking Vale is in the front half of Briarheart, beyond Tanglewood Fort's back door. Client quests say only "in Briarwood". The Nettleseed placement is used.
4. **Level hint in a video title.** One video overlay says "A LEVEL 12+ COMBAT JOB" for this dungeon. It is the uploader's caption, not game text, and the in-game panel says level 9. Not used.
