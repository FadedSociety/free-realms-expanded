### Contradiction with the brief's literal-name fallback

The brief allowed a literal gate `name` with no locale id. That does not reach the client:
`world::add_static_actor` sets `NpcSpawn { ..content_spawn_template() }` and never assigns
`spawn.npc_name`, while `build_add_npc` serializes `spawn.npc_name`. A battle spawner therefore
sends an empty name string, and the nameplate has to come from `NameStringId`. The gate uses the
recovered battle title `24276` ("King of the Ring!") as its locale name and keeps "Brawling Ring"
as the server-side tool label. Making the literal label visible would need a production change in
`src/world.rs` (`spawn.npc_name = name`), which is outside this content slice.

## Threat note (hostile client)

What a hostile client can send here and why it changes nothing:

- **Forged quest replies (49/2, 49/14)** for C1/C2 before the offer or turn-in: the quest engine
  keeps a single `pending_offer`/`pending_turnin`; a non-matching reply is denied and the save
  bytes stay identical (`refusals_and_replay_change_nothing`).
- **Replayed 49/14 / 26/42** after the one payout: `pending_turnin` is cleared and the battle is
  no longer active, so the replay pays nothing (tests 3, 4, 5, 7).
- **Wrong NPC / wrong actor 26/8 and 26/10**: the NPC router and the marker radius check refuse;
  the snapshot is unchanged (tests 2, 5).
- **A non-Brawler asking for C1**: the offer gate reads the server-side active job profile, not a
  client claim, so no offer is produced (test 1).
- **Client-reported kills or wins**: the battle objective advances only from the engine's
  authoritative `completed` on the won run's close, never from a client result packet; the wheel
  and coins are granted by the engine's own win handler (test 3).
- **Quantity/item ids**: the client sends none for this content. Prizes are fixed server data and
  the loader refuses a non-positive id or over-stack quantity; the bag preflight refuses a start
  whose prizes cannot be granted (existing `battle_prize_bag_preflight`).
- **Level/job skips**: `Go` re-checks the pending screen's configured floor and the combat-job
  basic-ability support; the portal shares the same floor and busy gate. Battle 12400 sets
  `required_combat_level: 0` by the guide's own "no requirement" row, so the level gate is
  deliberately open; the quest offer stays Brawler-gated.
- **Economy**: 19 coins / 5 coins / 25 XP are bounded by the checked coin/XP paths; the quest XP
  is paid to profile 43 only when it is unlocked (`rewards::locked_job_profile`), and the reward
  row's job is explicit, not the active job (test 3's job-switch variant).
