## What changed

1. **BLOCKER 1, the lead-only rate change.** `data/content/pets.json` `follow.send_ms` is
   back to 500 (T827 had changed it to 100). The T455 sign-off (lead register, 2026-10-26)
   accepts this 125 source as rate-bounded by `pets.json` `follow.send_ms` at 500 ms, at
   most 2 frames/s per pet, and the gateway's `outcome.nearby` fanout is outside the F179
   budget, so 10/s was five times the accepted envelope. A faster cadence is now a lead
   rate decision;
   nothing in this diff needs one. The follow code itself is unchanged: `walk_if_due`
   already sends only while the pet moves, and reaching the trailing point between two
   owner reports sends nothing.
2. **BLOCKER 2, the vacuous rate test.** `walking_sends_at_most_two_frames_a_second` now
   drives the real 50 ms world tick with the owner reporting every 500 ms
   (`walk_at_client_cadence`), pins the accepted 500 ms in the test (so a data change
   cannot relax its own test), and asserts at most 21 frames in 10 s (10 000/500 + the
   t = 0 frame), at least 10, and no Stop. The old helper called `step` once per 100 ms,
   so its ceiling could never fail. `the_pet_walks_runs_and_stands_with_its_movement` now
   asserts the real sequence `[Walk, Run, Walk, Stand]`; the old `windows(2)` check was
   true by construction because `Move.gait` is `Some` only on a change.
3. **MINOR 3, the shortened walk assertion.** `a_follow_click_wakes_a_lying_pet...` runs
   the old six seconds again. It checks every tick before the 20 m click arrives for no
   idle base play (`Seen.plays` is the care tick; the follow step's gait is in `Seen.gait`),
   asserts that some walking ticks were checked, and asserts the click walk reaches its
   spot. The literal "all six seconds are walking" assertion would now fail on correct
   behaviour: at the pet's 5 m/s pace the 18 m walk arrives inside the window and a pet
   waiting at the clicked spot may rest again (the old 2 m/s clicked walk did not arrive).
4. **MINOR 4, the duplicated content reader.** `tests/support/json.rs::pets_json` is the
   one reader; `tests/pet_follow.rs::shipped` uses it and the now-unused
   `npc_visibility` include is deleted. `tests/support/pet_care.rs::pets_json` delegates
   to it, so its many callers are unchanged.
5. **MINOR 5, the threat note.** `notes/Autopilot/T827.md` now says a gait change may add
   one frame between due frames (and a settle/pause/arrival one Stop), instead of claiming
   the whole frame rate is `send_ms`-paced. At the shipped 500 ms a steady walk is at
   most 2 frames/s; a gait change resets the cadence, so it does not stack.
6. **MINOR 6, the content schema break.** `FollowConfig` accepts the removed
   `min_speed`/`max_speed` as optional, ignored private fields (documented on the struct),
   so a content package cut before T827 still loads whole. `a_pre_t827_content_package_still_loads`
   deserializes the shipped document with both old keys through the real
   `PetContent::from_json` path. Drop the shim after one release once private packages
   are regenerated.

T827 note edits: the data bullet, the (R) interpolation gap, checklist item 19, the
threat note and the native open gap now state 500 ms and the gait-change exception.

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

- What a hostile client could send: the owner position (already validated and clamped by
  `movement.rs` before `on_location`) and the 53/26 pet follow click (existing path,
  checked for finiteness, the client's 8-40 m band, the 10 m step gate, the 200 ms
  interval and walkability). There is no new opcode, field, item id, quantity, chat text,
  role or save input.
- Why it is refused or harmless: a non-finite owner or ground value skips the step and
  keeps the pose; walk frames are paced by the server clock (500 ms, at most 2/s per pet),
  a gait change adds one frame between due ones, and a settle/pause/arrival adds one Stop,
  so a client cannot demand a frame stream. An accepted click is bounded by the client's
  own 200 ms interval and 10 m step gate, and its walk takes server time. A warp needs a
  validated owner jump over `warp_owner_jump_m` (30 m), a blocked walk for `warp_far_ms`,
  a climb over `warp_climb_m`, or the 100 m / 5 s leash; a refused click answers flag 0 and
  changes nothing (tested). The pet is session-only and a follow step never saves, so no
  persistence, economy or privilege state can be forged. The accepted legacy content keys
  are server-authored content, never client input. No secret, key, name, chat text or
  absolute path is added.
