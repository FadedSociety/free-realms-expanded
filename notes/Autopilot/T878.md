# T878: placements from the video archive, first pass

Source: the video archive placement index (2026-10-29): world x/z per named thing, solved from about
6,300 original-game frames (minimap fixes the player, on-screen markers give distances). Lab-only; the
repo carries coordinates, counts and a short provenance string only. Row filter used: confidence medium
or high, at least 4 sightings from at least 2 videos, era gate not post-shutdown-only, zone FabledRealms
(the open world). Heights are not in the index: each y was sampled from the shipped zone-0 terrain grid
at the solved x/z (the brief's rule). Wiki and client text were not used for these positions.

## What changed

1. **Dungeon entrances (17 files).** `dungeon_data.entrance.position` is new, in the same
   `{value, reason, source}` shape as its siblings. The typed `Dungeon` record has no position slot; the
   `dungeon_data` detail envelope is the documented home for guide claims the typed schema cannot hold
   (`notes/Research/Content Batch Dungeons.md`), and nothing parses it, so the field is data only. (R) that
   this counts as "a slot": if the lead wants a typed field instead, the 17 values move with it.
   - 13 gate/barrier/door dungeons: Tanglewood Fort, Bone Bog Cemetery, Darvon's Descent, Forest Troll
     Fort, Briar Patch, Misty Mountain, The Snarling Hedges, Hot Springs Haven, Haunted Mines, Sheep
     Watch, Snowy Canyon (high) and Bixie Hive, Forgotten Caves (medium, tagged (R)).
   - 4 dungeons started by clicking an enemy: Prince of Low Tide, Snatching Snappers, Robgoblin Creek
     (Robgoblin Fishmonger), Snakes in a Maze (Amazing Asp). The position is the initiator's standing
     point; these are the "missing enemies" of brief item 2, so the enemy rows land in the one slot that
     says where the battle starts. Prince of Low Tide roams, so its point is a cluster centre (R).
2. **Regional NPC rows (12).** Each stood at a reconstructed POI/zone-centre spiral offset
   (`poi_anchor`/`unanchored`, "not a recovered NPC transform"). They now carry the solved point with a
   new placement class `video_index` / source `video_archive_index` / confidence from the index
   (all 12 are high), no POI candidates, and a `video` provenance tag with the counts in
   `field_sources.position` and `position_reason` (the old position is kept in the reason text).
