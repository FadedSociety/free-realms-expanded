## How to read this page

- Tags: **[client]** is the game's locale text (`en_us_data.dat`), **[video]** is player recordings (frame key `video/frame`), **[wiki]** is the fan wiki. Precedence is client, then video, then wiki. Where they disagree, the higher source is in the tables and the clash is in **Conflicts**.
- Numbers such as `(2790790419)` are client locale ids. The "Title ids" column gives the `ucdt` and `ugdt` title entries (the two tags carry the same text). Most Chapter 1 quests also have a second, un-prefixed title entry (for example "Rumors of Briarwood" 3003828547); those are not repeated.
- **Text match column.** The locale file stores quest titles, descriptions, objectives and dialog as separate entries with no link between them. Every description or objective in the tables was matched to its quest by wording. Three levels are used: **exact** (the string repeats the title or a title-specific objective, or the video shows it on screen under that quest), **direct** (the string names the same NPC, place and enemy as the quest), **inferred** (†, "fits this quest, not proven"). Anything unmarked in a text cell is exact. Strings that fit no quest are in **Unmatched client text**.
- Rewards (coins, XP, items) are not in the locale file. They are "unknown" unless a video frame or the wiki shows them. Several quest-complete frames do show coin amounts; those are cited.
- Quest order inside the opening arc comes from the order the recorded video plays them (A1 to A6), the NPC dialog in the client ("Before you leave", "Go talk with Cleetus"), and the vault note [[Starting Area Content Audit]] (client facts only). Chapter 1 order is the wiki's Sacred Grove page. Neither the client nor the video gives a prerequisite table.
- A second nameplate the video recorder read on the bearded farm NPC (frames `3caQP-As968/00410`, `/00424`, `/00426`, `/00428`) is **not listed as an NPC**: its first name reads three different ways across those frames, it appears in no client string and no wiki page, and the same frames' objectives name Pappy Farnum. It may be a nearby player nameplate, so it is not repeated here. See Conflicts.

## Overview

- **What it is.** Adventurer is the default freestyle job: no weapon, no abilities, no combat. It levels by exploring: "Completing exploration collections levels up your adventurer job." (927528277, 1778699671, 4057355580) [client]. The wiki says experience "is mainly gained by completing collections" and lists 35 collections whose common reward is "Adventurer Exp." (see Exploration collections). [wiki] Video level-up frames show the same collection banners beside "Adventurer Level Up!" (for example `mu9Y82K62nk/00012`, `I4VncbC3xYw/00030`). [video]
- **Max level 20.** Level-up trophies exist at 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 (see Level trophies). [client][wiki] Video shows Adventurer 20 in many frames (for example `9rPMuvwc728/00244`, `-itStdznbPo/00200`). [video]
- **Rank titles.** The client has "Novice Adventurer" (3634212502), "Intermediate Adventurer" (2541638791) and "Advanced Adventurer" (2111446639), the same pattern as "Novice Brawler" and "Novice Chef". Which levels map to them is unknown. [client]
- **How to unlock.**
  - 2009 to 2012 tutorial: the player finished the first Sacred Glade tutorial quest and spoke to Michael Tallstrider; skipping the tutorial gave the job automatically. [wiki] The client agrees: "Perfect! ... put the poor, petrified, potbellied pet Wilbur in the pig pen. ... really earned the Job of Adventurer!" (394934962); "Talk to Michael after you've saved Wilbur from the robgoblins." (3169726252); "Since you have just completed your first adventuring quest, you are an official 'Adventurer' now. Congratulations!" (1250445224). The wiki now says "This job is no longer going to be unlocking". [client][wiki]
  - Trophy: "Obtain the Adventurer job. Hey, everybody gets this trophy! Welcome to Free Realms!" (907127144). [client]
  - Opening arc (the supplied build): the job is simply the starting job. The video shows the HUD at **Brawler 1** during the two combat tutorials and the Hero's Journal, then **Adventurer 1** from the moment the player reaches Farnum Farmstead (`3caQP-As968/00278` to `/00346` Brawler 1; `/00404` onward Adventurer 1). The job then flips to **Chef 1** when the Cooking Table goal starts (`3caQP-As968/00644`). Job changes follow the minigame or encounter, like the Kart tutorial text "Your job has been switched to one that matches the minigame you are about to join." [video]
  - Client tips: "You got the adventurer job when you started the game, but you can also be a chef, a miner, or even a ninja! To change your job and equipment click the Main Menu icon in the game dock." (341617696); "You just got the adventurer job, but you can also be a brawler, an archer, or even a soccer star!" (4272033315). [client]
- **Quests do not require the job.** Chapter 1 frames appear under Adventurer 14, 15 and 18 and under Wizard 1 (`I4VncbC3xYw/00000`, `OG-MnE_soUk/00038`, `fEsimyxtCIg/00134`); Lucky's quests appear under Adventurer 1 and Card Duelist 13 and 1 (`TlGcKj-058s/00018`, `NPGZyfxQUrE/00008`, `myQyxfTSRpM/00006`); Local Hero appears under Medic 19 and Adventurer 20. [video]
- **Quest XP and coins.** Quest completion gives "coins, a new shirt, or even some experience" (1250445224). [client] Video quest-complete banners show coin amounts for some quests (see tables).
- **Story arc shape.** One continuous story: Briarwood Caverns tutorial (Darkthorne, the Gloam, the Hero's Journal) leads to Farnum Farmstead, then Cobblestone Village, then the Royal Palace in Sanctuary. From there the client and wiki carry a 22-quest **Chapter 1** campaign, ending with "Chapter 1: The Queen's Last Hope"; completing it gives the achievement "Realm Defender" (title "Story Teller"). There is no Chapter 2 string anywhere in the client (a search for "Chapter 2" returns nothing). [client][wiki]

1. **"Thugamug's Thievery" spelling.** The client (1685133772), the video (`db53WZqLX58/00048`) and the wiki all read "Chapter 1: Thugamug's Thievery". The assignment brief wrote "Thugawug's Thievery". "Thugamug" is the bandit boss's name; "Thugawug" is the species of the minions. This page uses Thugamug.
2. **Hero's Journal grouping.** The vault note [[Starting Area Content Audit]] lists "Hero's Journal" with the combat micro-steps inside it. The video shows three separate quest titles: Basic Combat Tutorial, Advanced Combat Tutorial, Hero's Journal. The client has all three titles. This page uses three quests.
3. **"Fighting the Pack" vs "Fighting off the Pack".** The client has both titles (188245996 with a ugdt twin, and 46289336 ucdt only). The wiki uses "Fighting off the Pack"; the audit note says the shipped locale uses "Fighting the Pack". Row A9 uses the title that has the twin entry.
4. **"Funky" vs "Spunky" Scrapper Hammer.** Wiki goal: "Forge a Funky Scrapper Hammer". Wiki dialogue and client: "Spunky" (342264351, 1041615162). Spunky is used.

The audit note calls the step "Cooking101". The client holds a "Cooking 101" title (192446581 / 443560929) with no matched text. This page uses Farming 101 and treats Cooking 101 as unplaced.
