# T490 - Pet navigation: terrain step checks and 53/26 send-the-pet-to-a-clicked-spot (2026-10-27)

Third pass. The two earlier patches no longer applied because the tip had moved: F833 already owned
`FollowConfig::click_max_m`/`admits_click` and T455/T531 owned the live follow pose, so the earlier
replacement of `follow_location` conflicted. The rejected hunks were ported by hand: F833's field and
helper stay (the helper now enforces the client's 8 m minimum too), and every constant this code
relies on was read from the client again in IDA. No hub file changed.

Skills applied: ponytail (full, including its review pass on this diff) and secure-code-guardian
(threat note below).

## Client evidence (IDA, this pass)

Sender `00b5fcf0` (`SendPetFollowLocation`, caller `00b60770` on message kind `0x1000`) was
decompiled and disassembled again:

- It copies the four incoming floats to `this+0x12d0..0x12df`; the cached vector is the next
  `__m128` at `+0x12e0`.
- Gate A: the 4D distance from the new vector to the cache must be at least `0x01b5d878` =
  `0x41200000` = **10.0 m** (`disasm` of the data address).
- It clears the can-go byte at `+0x12f9`, then `008a7d50` fills four owner floats.
- Gate B: the 4D distance from the owner vector to the click must be in **[8.0, 40.0]** (inlined by
  the compiler; the operands are `0x0176b1b0` = 8.0 and `0x017ed93c` = `0x42200000` = 40.0).
- Gate C: `a3[2] <= 0.5` (`0x017649d4` = 0.5). The vector `a3` is not the click and is not sent to
  the server; there is no server counterpart.
- Gate D: the delta from `007027f0` (`timeGetTime`, milliseconds) through `008b0e20` must be greater
  than `0x01b5d874` = `0xC8` = **200 ms**.
- Packet ctor `00b51b20` zeroes the byte at +32; serializer `00b5a530` writes the 16 bytes at +16
  through `sub_91A640`, then that byte (`sub_7CC400`). Request body: four f32, one u8 0 (17 bytes).
- Reply reader `00b578d0`: `sub_B55280` base header, `sub_8E2410` reads the u64 into +16, and the
  byte at +32 becomes the can-go flag at client `+0x12f9`. `pet_can_go_packet` matches.

The fourth float's meaning is not recovered (R). All four take part in the client's own 4D gates;
the server requires it finite, uses words 0 and 2 for the horizontal band, and never uses the
clicked y as the pet's height (the heightfield does). IDA wins over
`src/restoration/piece_091.rs:42-46` and the fleet research pack, which both said these constants
were unresolved.

## Behaviour summary

- Follow: the pet never takes a step over a 1 m rise, a 3 m drop or unknown ground; after 5 s of
  refused steps it warps beside the owner. Teleport, far-away and 6 m climb warps are otherwise the
  T455 rules, and every warp clears the clicked destination and the no-progress clock.
- 53/26: a click 8-40 m from the owner, within one `click_max_m` path of the pet and walkable from it
  answers 1, and the pet walks there at 2 m/s with the existing 125 pacing (owner and viewers get the
  same frames), then waits until the owner moves 3 m or 10 s pass, when following resumes.
  Everything else answers 0 and changes nothing.
- Dismiss, a replacing summon, zoning, disconnect and every warp clear the session-only go state.

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client can send 53/26 with any bytes. The decoder checks the length before indexing,
requires the one flag value the client writes, and rejects non-finite or >1e6 coordinates with `Err`
(the session logs a refused pet request; no panic and no state). A well-formed click is still refused
with flag 0 unless it is 8-40 m from the server's live owner position, at most one `click_max_m` path
from the pet, and walkable on the server's heightfield. The cost is bounded by 80 ground samples
(0.5 m spacing over a 40 m path) and one 12-byte reply; the 200 ms interval rate-limits accepted
moves, and the cheap band/interval refusals run before any sampling. A flood of refused clicks
allocates one small packet each and touches no state (the interval is not consumed by a refusal). No
client y, normal or flag decides anything; the actor guid and owner position come from the session;
nothing is granted (no coins, items or XP). A gridless zone cannot be used to move the pet
arbitrarily because every click there answers 0.

## Checklist

1. A refusal changes nothing: both refusal branches return before any write; tests assert the reply
   and `(active_follow_state, view)` equality for over-limit and malformed bodies, and the interval
   test compares the stored state plus a later accepted click. Canary 2 broke the interval rule and
   failed the follow-up click.
2. Rollback covers all state: go, clock and pose live in `ActivePet`, one session copy; a click
   writes no save, roster or shared `Rc`/`RefCell`/`Arc`.
3. Apply deltas: an accepted click records a destination and the step moves from the current pose;
   the live owner position is an input, never restored over newer state.
4. Busy rules: a click is a movement command, not a new activity; the existing `blocks(pets)` gate
   still decides whether a pet can be out, and the pet-window mode still pauses the step. No new
   `Occupation`.
5. Entry paths: 53/26 arrives only through `Gameplay::handle_pets` -> `PetService::handle_at_ms` ->
   `follow_location`; the no-clock `handle` refuses it and there is no test-only path.
6. Reachable in production, tested through it: the last `pet_navigation` test drives
   `Gameplay::handle_in_world` (click) and `Gameplay::pet_tick` (walk and resume) with a real
   `Terrain`; `app_fuzz` routes 53/26 through the gateway.
7. Reconnect: nothing here is saved (pose, go and clock are session state); `reset_session` drops the
   active pet, and an accepted click leaves the saved document equal (asserted). No persisted rule to
   test.
8. Every exit restores: arrival + timeout, owner-moved resume, warp (blocked/climb/teleport/far),
   dismiss, replacing summon and zoning all clear or drop the go; tests cover the timeout, owner
   resume and blocked warp.
9. Economy: no coin, item or XP is touched; no client-reported result is read; the target is
   validated and only the pet's own pose moves.
10. Wire: request `00b5fcf0` (ctor `00b51b20`, serializer `00b5a530`) is 4 x f32 + u8 0, 17 body
    bytes, asserted at the byte level with truncation, trailing-byte, flag, NaN/inf and 1e7 cases;
    reply `00b578d0` (u64 actor, u8 flag) is asserted as exact bytes in five tests.
11. Unique ids: none added; the reply and frames use the existing owner-derived pet actor guid.
12. Others see changes: every go frame goes through the existing `follow_owner` private+shared push;
    the reachable test asserts `private == shared` for every 125, and the reply is private only.
13. End to end: a real `Gameplay` summons, accepts the click, walks the pet to the spot within 0.2 m
    and resumes on the owner move; the service test also asserts the wait and the timeout.
14. Values in data: the seven new numbers (plus F833's `click_max_m`) are in `pets.json` `follow`
    with load-time bounds in `FollowConfig::valid`; only the 1e6 parser bound, the 4096-sample

15. Canaries break production: the two above each broke a tested production rule and were reverted.
16. Session plumbing: untouched (`gateway.rs`, `bin/osfr-gateway.rs`, saves and teardown unchanged).
17. Tick safety: `step` returns a pose and never errors; all time math is saturating; a bad ground

    pet arms; the isolation net is unchanged.
18. Type rules: `FollowConfig` documents and checks every new value; `GoTarget`/`PetPose` document

    persisted.
19. Hot path: a settled pet returns before sampling ground; while walking it samples at most
    `reach / 0.5 + 1` (2 samples at a 20 Hz tick and 12 m/s, 24 at a 1 s gap) with no allocation
    beyond the existing 125 frame. A 53/26 costs at most 80 samples and one small packet; the cheap
    band and interval refusals run before any sampling.

## Open (R) and gaps

- Not driven by a native client. The client's can-go flag (`+0x12f9`) and the pet's walk animation
  are the native check: summon a pet, click terrain 8-40 m away, watch the reply 1, the walk and the
  wait; click a cliff and watch 0.
- The fourth float's meaning is not recovered beyond "the same 4-float vector's fourth component";
  the server only requires it finite (R). All four words take part in the client's own 4D gates,
  while the server uses the horizontal (x, z) band (R).
- The client's 10 m minimum displacement between sends is not enforced server-side (the server's
  cache is not the client's); only the 8-40 m band and the 200 ms interval are.
- `step_up_m`/`step_down_m`/`sample_m`/`resume_m`/`go_timeout_ms` are (R) (OSFR PR #152 for the
  first three, first choices for the last two). The walk is a straight-line height check, not a
  pathfinder: an obstacle blocks the pet and the no-progress warp brings it back after
  `warp_far_ms`.
- The pet walks a click at `min_speed` (2 m/s, R); no run pace and no arrive animation beyond the
  stop frame.
- The client's third gate (`a3[2] <= 0.5`) has no server counterpart: the vector it tests is not
  sent, and its slot/axis meaning is unresolved. The server's heightfield check substitutes.
- In a zone with a grid, a step whose two ends are both unknown still falls back to the T455
  owner-height rule (the closure cannot distinguish a hole from a missing grid); a known start or
  end is checked and refused.
