## Conflicts

1. **Tavil.** The client puts Tavil, Shadow Talon leader, in the Deep Mines for the Ninja job quest, but the wiki lists only Tuga and Taraz as bosses. Whether Tavil is a separate quest battle or another boss in this dungeon is unknown.
2. **Boss count.** The wiki counter "(2)" and the client string "Defeat the Robgoblin Leaders, Tuga and Taraz!" agree, but the client also has a "hidden objective" for Taraz, so Taraz may be optional or secondary in one version.
3. **Robgoblin Wizard Hildegarde.** A client campaign line has a wizard named Hildegarde guarding her treasure trove right after a "bruiser" boss falls (`loc 3902306687`). That is the [[Robgoblin Treasure Trove]] boss, not a Deep Mines enemy.
