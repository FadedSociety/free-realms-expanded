# Kart Driver

Part of [[Content]] > Jobs. Engine guide for how the client runs the races: [[Kart Racing]]. Related: [[Demolition Derby Driver]] (shares the speedways and trainers' towns), [[NPCs - Briarwood]], [[NPCs - Snowhill]], [[NPCs - Lakeshore]], [[NPCs - South Sacred Grove]], [[NPCs - Seaside]].

Source tags: **[client]** locale text, **[video]** frame records (frame key `video/frame`), **[wiki]** fan-wiki archive. Precedence is client, then video, then wiki. Locale ids are the numeric ids from the client locale file; each title has a `ucdt` entry and, usually, a `ugdt` twin.

**How the quest data was matched.** The client locale stores quest titles and quest text as separate entries with no id link between them. Every objective or description below was matched to a title by one of three methods, shown in the "Text match" column: **exact** (the string names the race or minigame that is also in the title), **direct** (the string names the same NPC and place as the title), or **inferred** (the title and string fit but nothing names the title). Order within a speedway tier is read from the NPC dialog in the strings ("the next race is at Frost Ridge"); the client's quest prerequisite table is not decoded, so treat order as likely, not confirmed.

## Overview

- Kart Driver is a **freestyle, members-only** job whose gameplay is the Kart Racing minigame (see [[Kart Racing]]). [wiki Kart Driver] [wiki Jobs] [video: Kart Driver 20 seen on the job window, `0IaICGnTWWQ/00010`]
- **Job name.** The client uses "Kart Driver" for the job and quest titles; it also contains the older name "Kart Racer" (ids 1112672742, 3488061757, 3506512645) and prefixes the race minigame names with it, for example "Kart Racer: Wildwood Sprint Challenge" (2422977370). [client] One video frame shows "Kart Racer" on the job bar (`dMhAD7bReEk/00026`).
- **Max level 20.** Level-up achievements are at 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 (see Level achievements). [client] [wiki Achievements] [video: Kart Driver levels 1, 4, 5, 10, 16 and 20 seen]. The wiki says most jobs cap at level 4 for non-members. [wiki Jobs]
- **How to unlock.** Talk to any Kart Driver trainer at a speedway; the job is gained by speaking to them. [wiki Kart Driver] The client confirms with "Get the Kart Driver or Demolition Derby Driver job from any of the trainers at any of the speedways around the world." [client 514462904]
- **Town referrals.** Several town quests send new players to a trainer: Shakey sends the player to Johnny Thunder (id 4211297395), Laurin sends the player to Dane Blueshift (3535310333) and Raina Rush's pen hunt (The Burden of Fame) runs through Mustang Alli. The wiki lists the "Introduce Yourself" quests for Ricky Danger (Cobblestone Village) and Francis Panic (Stillwater Crossing) as town quests that introduce Kart Drivers. [client] [wiki Quest:Introduce Yourself]
- **Quest line shape.** Four speedway tiers (Wildwood, Frost Ridge, Thunder Falls, Tangletrack). Each tier has ranked rival races, one sponsor with a Sprint Challenge (fast lap), an Endurance Challenge (multi-lap) and a "Dominating at" (win several races) quest, then the four-leg SG Tour 400 against the champion Kade Carmichael for the Lightning Cup. Completing the Kart Driver questline earns the achievement "Rageracer" and the title "Racer". [client 3824178493, 3752292726, 1325221059] [wiki Achievements]
- **Rewards are largely unknown.** No quest-complete frame in the video set shows coin or XP numbers for these quests. The only reward seen anywhere in the kart content is 1 Blast Speedster from Sandstorm's Grand Opening, plus the Lightning Cup from the wiki. See Gaps.
