# Chugawump

Part of [[Content]] › [[Dungeons]]. Tags: **[client]** = game locale text (`loc <id>`), **[video]** = player recordings (frame key), **[wiki]** = fan wiki. Precedence client > video > wiki.

> Chugawump is a **boss duel battle**, not an Atlas dungeon. The wiki files it under *Battle* and *Briarwood* (it is not tagged Special Spawn). The player clicks the Chugawump boss NPC in Briarheart. [wiki]

## Conflicts

1. **Quest-run reward versus wiki.** The wiki says 13 Stars; the quest-run chat line says "11 stars and 13 coins". These measure different things (battle prize versus quest reward) or the wiki figures are mis-transcribed. The numbers 13 and 11 do not obviously match. Unresolved. The identical line "11 stars and 13 coins" is also read in the [[Feisty Floren]] recording, so it may be a frame-reading artifact.
2. **Title reuse.** "Test Your Mettle" (`loc 1858118349`) also fits a copper-ore quest (`loc 948067489`). The Chugawump text is used only where video shows the Chugawump objective.
