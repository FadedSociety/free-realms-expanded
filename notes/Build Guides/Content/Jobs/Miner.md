## How to read this page

- Tags: **[client]** is the game's locale text (`en_us_data.dat`) and the client data tables built from the game packs (`MiniGameData.txt`), plus the recovered mining and smelting movies as read in our research notes. **[video]** is player recordings. **[wiki]** is the fan wiki. Precedence is client, then video, then wiki. Where they disagree, the higher source is in the main text and the clash is in **Conflicts**.
- Numbers in parentheses, for example `(1726071120)`, are client locale ids. The "Title ids" column gives both title entries for a quest as `ucdt` / `ugdt`; the two tags carry the same text.
- Video frame keys look like `ct2JrtOiO4g/00080`.
- **Matching caveat.** The locale file stores quest titles, descriptions, objectives and dialog as separate entries with no link between them. Every description or objective below was matched to its quest by wording (names, places, items), not by a data link. Matches that rest on wording alone are marked with a dagger (†). A dagger means "fits this quest, not proven". The Miner line shares NPCs and places with the Blacksmith line (Derp Axemaker, Mili Ironcall, Silverray, Smitty), so some strings that name those NPCs belong to Blacksmith quests; those were left out unless they say "as a miner".
- **No wiki quest pages.** The wiki archive has no `Quest:Miner: ...` page for any title on this line (checked with and without the prefix). The wiki facts come from the Miner job page, NPC and location pages, and the Collections, Achievements and Titles pages. The wiki gives the quest list, the giver of each quest and the level gates, and nothing about objectives or rewards.
- **Rewards.** Coins, XP and items are not in the locale file. Every reward cell reads "unknown" unless a source names one.
- **Spellings.** The client spells the first quest "So, You Want To Be A Miner?" with no "Miner:" prefix; the wiki list has no prefix on any title and the video prints "Miner: ..." or "Miners ..." (a reading artefact). The client spelling is used. Client typos are kept (for example "Turqoise").
- **Research-note facts.** Facts about the recovered mining and smelting movies come from our own analysis notes ([[Research/Mining Restoration]], [[Research/Mining Original Powerups]], [[Research/Original Smelting]], [[Research/Native Smelting Controls]]). They are tagged [client] when they come from the original movie scripts or data tables, and labelled "reconstructed" when they are our own stand-ins for server data that was never recovered.

## Overview

Miner is a **crafting** job. It gathers ore and gems from mining nodes and smelts ore into bars that [[Blacksmith]] players forge into weapons and armour. The wiki calls it a harvesting job that "specializ[es] in the mining of ore and gems and can also smelt the ore into ingots", levelled by Miner quests, mining and the Smelting minigame. [wiki] The client minigame text says the same: "Gather ore to use in crafting!" (15743342) and "Convert your ore into useable metal bars through the smelting process. There are four stages: breaking down the ore, measuring the ore mix, heating the furnace, and finally filling the molds with molten metal." (3742443769). [client]

- **Max level 20.** The line runs from level 1 to 20; the four level gates are 5, 10, 15 and 20. [client] [wiki]
- **How to unlock.** Speak to **Therin**, guildmaster of the Singing Crystal Mines in Snowhill. [wiki] The client says the same many times ("Learn how to be a miner by finding Therin at the Singing Crystal mines in Snowhill." 1852781420; "Talk to Therin at Snowhill to become a miner." 2776526370), then makes the player pass a practice test: "do you even know what a pickaxe looks like? Use the set of mining practice tools to my left and prove to me that you've got the skills needed to be part of the guild." (1726071120). [client] The wiki adds that interacting with any ore node or forge offers an optional quest to become a Miner. [wiki] The client has a quest titled "Become a Miner" (see row 0a). A second Miner trainer, **Zoren Orecrusher**, stands at Highroad Junction beside a "Mining Practice" station and sends new miners to Therin. [client] [video]
- **Joining the guild.** The line ends with the player being "named" a member of the Free Realms Mining Guild by Therin after Snorg approves them. [client] [wiki]
- **Members.** The wiki marks seven quests as members only: Reach Level 20, Growing Up So Quickly, Biggest is Best, Speed Smelting, Mother Lode Lift, Rare Rock Collection and Part of the Guild. [wiki] The client says "This rich mining vein is for Free Realms members only. Become a member today!" (2292639008) for the Rich nodes, and has a Miner upgrade prompt ("Oh, no! Want to keep playing as a Miner? Upgrade to unlock more fun!" 347663181) and a "Miner Membership Upgrade Bundle" (3640818089). [client] But the Sunstone Valley blurb says "Play for FREE as a Brawler, Miner, and Blacksmith all the way to level 20!" (2640708938), and every Mining and Smelting row in the client minigame table has `MEMBERS_ONLY = 0`. [client] Frontmatter therefore says `members_only: false`; the members-only parts are the Rich nodes and (per the wiki) the last seven quests. See Conflicts.
- **Where it plays.** Five mines, one per ore, in this order of level: Singing Crystal Mines (Snowhill), Dartmoor Quarry, Southvale Quarry, Quagmire Quarry (Blackspore Swamp) and Mother Lode Quarry (Briarwood). [wiki] The quest line walks the player through them in that order.
- **Stars and levelling.** Miner stars come from Miner quests and the Mining and Smelting minigames. [wiki] The client has "Miner Bonus Stars" (1144530859) and tools that raise the stars earned (2136155588, 1043035138, 2358953104). [client]

**Recovered movie rules (`game_mining.gfx`, original).** From [[Research/Mining Restoration]] and [[Research/Mining Original Powerups]]. [client]

- It is the shared **TYPE 8** MatchThree engine: 78 of 106 scripts are byte-identical to the Forging movie. Board shape codes: `0` empty, `1` regular random, `2` any random, `3` blocker, `4` dropzone; tile value families: regular tiles `400` to `499` (400 plus type), wildcard `200`, collectable (ore and gems) `600` to `699` (600 plus type), bonus `700` plus type, loot drop `1000` plus type, locked tiles that unlock instead of exploding.
- **Chains.** At least three tiles, each consecutive pair orthogonally adjacent, no repeated coordinate, one selectable value; the wildcard matches and adopts the chain value. Ore tiles are never chained; they fall off the bottom into **bins**, one per goal. A bin label comes from the game's "Value" string for that ore.
- **Goals.** `OnQuotaInitMsg` carries the goal index, tile value, quota and the per-reward step; a goal completes when `collected` reaches the quota. A level ends when every goal is met or the timer runs out.
- **Powerups (seven).** Collected when they fall off the bottom: `900` Time (freezes the countdown), `901` Score x2, `902` Score x5 (each also +250 score). Clickable ("press me"): `950` Dynamite (shatters a neighbourhood), `951` Wild tile (turns selectable tiles into wildcards), `952` Destruct (removes N tiles), `953` Shuffle (rotates tiles). The client text matches: "Randomly shuffles tiles on the board." (1856424360), "Destroys a bunch of selectable tiles." (2961762447), "Destroys several nearby tiles." (2332965408), "Creates several star tiles on the board." (2128289937), "Click on these special tiles to activate them!" (3117721735). The spawn chance, durations and powers are server table values that were never recovered.
- **Dead boards.** When no legal chain exists the server reshuffles (`OnLevelEventMsg 11`); a clickable powerup counts as a legal move.
- **Protocol (for the Rust server).** Messages ride route `39`: client RequestStartGame (sub 5), Quit (6), ACK (7), payload (14, tab-delimited); server Info (16), start ACK (17), game over (18), removal (19), score rows (47); the movie launcher is `26/12 MiniGameFlash`. Handshake: `OnConnectMsg` to `OnServerReadyMsg`, `OnStartGameMsg` to `OnNextLevelMsg`, `OnStartLevelMsg` with the tile queue, build map and random queue, `OnLevelInitMsg`, `OnQuotaInitMsg` per goal. Chains arrive as `OnChainMsg x,y,x,y,...`; falls as `OnLevelEventMsg 50 x y`. Queue rules: 50 minimum, 20 per batch, 70 target.

**What is not recovered.** The server **level tables**: every board layout, quota, spawn percentage, timer, seed, score formula and per-node reward. Our reconstruction uses a 14 by 14 map with an 8 by 8 playable rectangle, two goals and 180 seconds, all marked reconstructed; they are not original facts. The reward for a mining game (how many ore and which gems the player receives) is **unknown**. [client] (reconstruction)

- **Quests** (rows 0a to 28): the main source of Miner stars; each gate needs a level, so the player must also level elsewhere. [client] [wiki]
- **Mining minigame** at the five quarries and the wilderness nodes: "Stars from Miner quests and Mining minigames go straight to the Miner job." [wiki]
- **Smelting minigame** at forges in Snowhill, Sanctuary, Lakeshore, Dartmoor and Blackspore, plus the Smelting daily activity. [client] [wiki]
- **Collections:** the six Miner collections spawn job materials in mines only while the player is a Miner. [wiki] [client]
- **Dungeons tied to the Snowhill mine:** [[Deep Mines]], [[The Bat Cave]] and, in Blackspore, [[Haunted Mines]] (near Quagmire Quarry). These are combat battles; the miners' lost tools and trapped miners are quest hooks, not Miner XP sources. [client] [wiki]
- **Daily activity:** Mining and Smelting both appear on the wiki Daily Activity page. [wiki]

## Gaps

- **Rewards.** No coin, XP or item rewards for any quest, minigame, collection step or node. Only guild-hall lines ("Collect a reward from ...") and wiki collection rewards are known.
- **Quest-to-text link.** Every objective and dialog match is by wording. Rows 2, 7, 23 and 25 are weak. A real quest table would settle every dagger.
- **Counts and amounts.** Gem Appraisal (three, five or more turquoise), Dartmoor Mining Camp (client five, video 25), Simple Surveying, Suspicious Eyes and the Mother Lode quests have unclear amounts. Known: Perfect Pour 5 tin bars in two minutes, Smelting Secrets 10 silver bars, Mother Lode Lift 15 moonstone, Arrowheads 300 arrowheads (15 iron ore), Rare Rock Collection five of each gem type, Biggest is Best 40 gold ore.
- **Original mining level tables.** Board layouts, quotas, timers, scores, powerup spawn rates, node rarity and rewards per node were never recovered. Our default ("reconstructed") values must not be treated as original.
- **Original smelting phase table.** Needed for the pass marks (57 to 73 percent window is reconstructed), time limits, the bonus-ingot rule and output counts per bar type.
- **Node data.** Node positions, respawn times, which gems each node can drop and with what chance, the Rich versus Rare rules, the exact traditional-node mining action (cast time, animation, repeat count) and the member gate per node.
- **Minigame video.** No frame shows Mining or Smelting being played, a node being used, a results panel, or any reward. Only node labels, quest trackers and two Games-menu frames exist.
- **Zoren and Becoming a Miner.** Which quest Zoren gives (row 0b is client-only), and what the optional "Become a Miner" prompt looks like on screen.
- **All I Got.** The identity of the quest is a guess (row 2).
- **Growing Up So Quickly and Speed Smelting.** No matching text (row 23) and no live client title (row 25).
- **Vendor stock and prices.** Only wiki item names for the Miner Gear vendors; no prices, no required levels.
- **Tennin, Spruce Waynehammer, Cronyn, Tince Hammerdudley.** Roles beyond vendor or "Miner" occupation are not known.
- **Ability or title levels.** The levels for Novice, Apprentice, Intermediate, Advanced Miner and Mining Master.
- **Video coverage.** Only three Miner quests appear in video: Rush Order, Gem Appraisal and Dartmoor Mining Camp (all as trackers). None of the other 25 line quests, nor Therin's exam, appears as a quest-giver scene.
