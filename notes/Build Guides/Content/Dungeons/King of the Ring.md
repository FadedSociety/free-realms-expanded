## Conflicts

1. **Location.** The wiki infobox says "Crossroads" while its categories say Cobblestone Village. The client puts the ring, Carlos and Mongo in Cobblestone Village every time. The client wins.
2. **Brawler Gloves name.** The wiki wheel says "Bell Brawler Gloves". The client item is "Saved by the Bell Brawler Gloves" (`loc 3699014784`). The client name is used above.
3. **In-game status.** The wiki says "No longer in-game". The client text and the video of the Chatty quest still describe it, so it existed at least at some point in the recorded builds.
4. **Wiki wording.** The wiki infobox calls the entry a "Battle area", but its category lists it with Dungeons. It is kept here as a dungeon page because it was assigned to this index.
