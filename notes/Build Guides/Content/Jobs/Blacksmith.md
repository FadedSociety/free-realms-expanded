Precedence is client, then video, then wiki. Where they disagree, the higher source is in the table and the clash is in **Conflicts**.
- Numbers in parentheses, for example `(3857116118)`, are client locale ids. In the quest tables the "Title ids" column gives `ucdt` / `ugdt` for the prefixed title ("Blacksmith: ..."). Where the client also has a bare title with no prefix, it is listed as "bare". The two tags carry the same text.
- Video frame keys look like `(2fhn8kePbKQ/00636)`.
- **Matching caveat.** The locale file stores quest titles, descriptions, objectives and dialog as separate entries with no link between them. Every description or objective below was matched to its quest by wording (names, items, places), not by a data link. A match that rests on wording alone is marked with a dagger (†). A dagger means "fits this quest, not proven". Two daggers (††) mean "fits one of several neighbouring quests". Strings that fit no quest are in **Unmatched client text**.
- **No wiki quest pages.** The wiki archive has no `Quest:Blacksmith: ...` pages for this job. The wiki facts come from the Blacksmith job page, NPC pages, a few place pages and about 40 weapon pages. The wiki gives the quest list per trainer and nothing about objectives. The one exception is the Sunstone Valley page, which gives level bands for six Sunstone quests.
- **Rewards.** Coins, XP and items are not in the locale file. No Blacksmith quest reward is known from any source, except recipes that the quest text says the quest teaches. Every reward cell reads "unknown" unless it says otherwise. Crafting jobs earn "stars" rather than XP in the minigame text (see **Crafting minigame**).

Client text for those jobs reads very like the Blacksmith text. Only strings that name forging, crafting, a recipe or the Blacksmith job are placed here.

## Overview

Blacksmith is a crafting job that makes weapons, gear and housing items from metal bars and other materials. The wiki calls it "a Members-only crafting job, specializing in the creation of weapons" and says experience comes from Blacksmith quests and from the forge minigames. [wiki] The client job blurb (as quoted on the wiki) is: "Blacksmiths use metal ore and other natural materials to create ingenious tools and dangerous weapons. You can craft items for your own jobs or sell them to other players to earn coins. Work with other professions to help you harvest the raw materials you need to craft additional items." [wiki] Blacksmith has no combat abilities and no combat level; it works through the anvil minigame (see **Crafting minigame**). [wiki][client]

**Unlocking.** The wiki says Blacksmith is unlocked by speaking with Smitty in Snowhill, and that Steelbloom in Sanctuary sends new players to him ("Give my Regards to Smitty"). [wiki] The client agrees. Steelbloom says: "If you want to study the art of a Blacksmith, you should go bother someone less talented... say like, Smitty in Snowhill." (1840944402) [client]. Unlock objectives read "Learn how to be a blacksmith by finding Smitty in Snowhill." (1260192132) †, "Speak with Smitty in Snowhill to learn how to become a Blacksmith" (4098523496) †, and "Choose Blacksmith in the Jobs menu." (260200902) †. [client] Smitty's intro line is "So you want to be a Blacksmith? I'll teach you a few tricks to get you started. And you'll need a recipe for you to practice with." (657026900) †, with the player option "I want to be a Blacksmith." (363147461). [client] The Smitty offer panel for "Blacksmith: Smithy Merchandise" is on screen in the video. [video]

**Using the anvil needs the job.** The client has "Sorry, you need to be a Blacksmith to use the anvil!" (1535610448). [client] The opening quest "Your First Weapon" (Cleetus Farnum, Farnum's Farm) has the player forge a Spunky Scrapper Hammer at a Blacksmith Anvil before choosing a combat job. [wiki] The vault research notes record that the opening arc treats that forge as a Blacksmith profile action. [client]

**Levels.** The job runs from level 1 to 20 (**max_level 20**). The client has Blacksmith achievement and level strings for levels 4, 5, 8, 10, 12, 15, 16 and 20, and "Reach Level 20 as a Blacksmith" (3210960050). [client] The wiki Achievements page lists levels 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20. [wiki] The video job-select panel shows "Blacksmith 11" on one account and "Blacksmith 1" on another (0IaICGnTWWQ/00010, ns0rlvCjXtU/00138). [video] The wardrobe screens in the video have a "Blacksmith" tab among the class tabs (for example Qg7_4br2H80/00460). [video]

**Members only.** The wiki puts Blacksmith in "Members Only". [wiki] The client has "Oh, no! Want to keep playing as a Blacksmith? Upgrade to unlock more fun!" (1251417193) and a "Blacksmith Membership Upgrade Bundle" (2708416031). [client] The client's Sunstone Valley blurb says "Play for FREE as a Brawler, Miner, and Blacksmith all the way to level 20!" (2640708938). [client] So non-members hit an upgrade prompt, but the Sunstone Valley line is advertised as free. See **Conflicts**.

**Gating by level.** The line stops at four trainers until the player reaches a level: Brody Sparfist at 5, Derp Axemaker at 10, Mili Ironcall at 15, Silverray at 20. The wiki lists only the 10 and 20 gates; the client has all four. [client][wiki] The Sunstone Valley line has its own gates (level 4, 8, 16). [client][wiki]

### How recipes work

- **Every minigame item needs a recipe.** "All items made through Mini Games require a recipe. To view available recipes, click on your My Character icon in your Game Dock." (528172005) [client]
- **Choosing a recipe.** The crafting window text is "Select a Recipe below to begin crafting" (4064703409); "These are recipes. Each recipe requires ingredients and creates an item you can use. Click on a recipe to see what ingredients it needs, or to select that recipe for the minigame." (3020645530; controller variants 573650363, 3403357374); a header "Components Required:" (171574001); and a prompt "Click Here to Select a Recipe." (118038548). The prompt is seen on a Cooking screen in the video (3caQP-As968/00654); the Forging screen is not captured. [client][video]
- **Learning.** A recipe is an item of its own, named "[Output] Recipe" (for example "Alley Fighter Hammer Recipe", 3415260283), with a description of the form "This recipe teaches you how to create a Biting Mongoose Blade." (754546045). A message reads `"%s" recipe learned` (2450323). [client] Quests can teach a recipe outright (rows 6, 7, 13, 18). [client]
- **Two kinds of ingredient.** "There are two types of Material Components, Primary Ingredients and Secondary Ingredients." (3707123132). "If the item your [sic] working on is created successfully, both the Primary and Secondary Ingredients are consumed, but you get a sweet creation in exchange!" (479456085). "These Material Components are items that are required to forge, cook, smelt, or otherwise make the item you're attempting to create." (2951876331). [client] For Blacksmith, the text fits bars as the primary ingredient and merchant stock as the secondary: Smitty says "Blacksmith supply merchants, like Bruno Battlerock, sell secondary components" (3152259161), and the forge reminder says copper bars come from a Miner and "Anything else you can buy from a merchant" (3617525660). The mapping of Primary to bars is an inference. [client] †
- **Failure.** Ingredients are consumed only on success. No text says what a failed forge returns. [client]

## Crafting minigame

Blacksmith crafting is a tile-matching minigame, the "Forging" game. It is separate from Smelting, which is the Miner's furnace minigame. The Games menu tile list in the video names both: "Cooking, Fishing, Forging, Harvesting, Micro-Games, Mining, ... Smelting, ..." (wI2eLQKFFHg/00078; UFsVcZ3X5iA/00014 lists Forging and Smelting). [video] The wiki groups Forging, Smelting and Cooking under "Crafting Games" and says they take place at a "Crafting Station". [wiki]

### Entry

- **Where.** The forging anvil stands at crafting stations and some quarries. The client has world objects "Anvil" (3132531851), "Sunstone Anvil" (134615320), "Scrapsmith Anvil" (1727712514), "Sunstone Forge" (1839029274) and "Scrapsmith Forge" (2111621048). [client] Wiki places: outside the Singing Crystal Mines ("the welding anvil for Blacksmith's", Snowhill), the Dartmoor Quarry (amenities "Smelting Furnace, Anvil"), Farnum's Farm, and the crafting stations of Lakeshore, Blackspore and Briarwood (from the merchant pages). [wiki] The video map labels show an "Anvil" marker about 21 m from Bruno Battlerock at the Snowhill forge (qtN6Kohe7zc/00014, /00060) and "Anvil? 13m" beside Smitty (tX6ZMrJ_nqo/00010). [video]
- **Job check.** "Sorry, you need to be a Blacksmith to use the anvil!" (1535610448). [client]
- **Prompt.** Interacting with the anvil opens the recipe window ("Click Here to Select a Recipe.", 118038548; "Interact with Here to Select a Recipe.", 1802874369). The client text warns "Make sure you have the right components or you won't be able to forge the Hammers." (3063575603). [client]

### How the board plays

- **Goal text (client).** "Craft your item by filling the item mold before time runs out! Drop item tiles into the mold by creating chains of three or more adjacent tiles of the same kind." (200487894). "Collect item tiles by making them fall to the bottom of the grid! Each item you collect will fill the item mold by a small amount. - **Controls in the shipped movie (vault analysis of the original client movie `game_forging`).** The player holds the left mouse button on a coloured tile and drags through at least three orthogonally adjacent tiles of the same colour, then releases; there is no submit button. Diagonal moves are not valid. Going back over an earlier tile in the chain removes the later selection. A quick single click enters an alternate "click mode". A tile must be idle (not falling or exploding) to be selected. The hammer tile is not matchable: the player clears tiles beneath it until gravity carries it to the bottom drop zone, which fills the mold. Gravity and refill run right to left, bottom to top. A board with no legal chain is reshuffled by the original reshuffle protocol. The movie's stage is 650 by 768 units, tiles are 40 units apart, and the movie's tile map is 14 by 14 cells. [client]
- **End of a run.** When the mold is full the grid pauses, the bin and mold animation plays, the movie sends the next-level message itself, and the "game won" message schedules the game-over callback about three seconds later; the results screen follows and the player closes it. Blacksmith has no dungeon and no wandering combat. It levels through quests and the forge minigame; the wiki says "experience is gained by completing Blacksmith quests and by playing the forge minigames". [wiki] Stars are the crafting reward (see **Crafting minigame**). [client] Level-up lines: "You've spent a lot of time forging! You're ready to move forward." (2765595242) fits a level-up message. ## Conflicts

1. **Members only versus free to level 20.** Wiki: Blacksmith is Members Only. Client: a Blacksmith-specific upgrade prompt (1251417193) and a membership bundle (2708416031), but the Sunstone Valley blurb says non-members can "Play for FREE as a Brawler, Miner, and Blacksmith all the way to level 20!" (2640708938). The generic client lines set the trial range for every job: "You can get every job in the game! Anyone can play up to level 4, but every job goes up to level 20! Use the Marketplace to purchase any job you want, or pay for a membership to unlock all of them!" (348625404) and "If you're a new player, you can try any job up to at least level five! Buy a Membership to unlock them all!" (2065313732). So a job can also be bought on its own in the Marketplace, which the wiki's "Members Only" label does not mention. A likely reading is that the base-game Blacksmith line is members-only (or a Marketplace purchase) past a trial level of 4 or 5, and the Sunstone Valley line is free. The `members_only: true` frontmatter follows the wiki.
2. **Level gates.** The wiki lists only the level 10 (Derp) and 20 (Silverray) gate quests. The client has all four (5 with Brody Sparfist, 10, 15 with Mili Ironcall, 20). The client wins; rows 8 and 19 are client-only.
3. **Gate text says "mining career".** The wait lines for levels 5, 10 and 15 read "Your mining career will continue once you have reached level N as a Blacksmith..." (70974171, 3824680113, 1964428081) while the level 20 line says "blacksmithing career" (888400885). These are copy-paste errors from the Miner text. The server should use the blacksmithing wording for all four.
4. **Title spelling.** Client "Curious Cancelation"; wiki "Curious Cancellation". Client spelling used. The wiki Sunstone page also has the typo "Fom Scraps to Iron". Client bare titles have no "Blacksmith:" prefix (for example "Hammer Training", "Impressing the Axemaker"); the wiki prefixes every title.
5. **Warrior Weapon item.** Client text has both "warrior copper axe" (417266560, 1566664832, 2361849858) and "Valiant Guardian Cudgel" (312940089, 3250596704, 3494730201). The wiki recipe list has the Cudgel as the level 1 Warrior recipe and no copper axe recipe. Two versions of the quest, or an old name; the Cudgel is used in the recipe table.
6. **Helping the Hospital item.** Wiki recipe "Trauma Support Shockrod" and client objectives (710678765, 2957731681, 1610195923) agree; a second set of client objectives says "Luminous Shockrods" (2492835863, 2180429893, 856867751) and the client has an item "Luminous Shockrod" (142897061). Likely an older name for the same weapon; unresolved.
7. **Recipe item names.** Client recipe items "Field Support Hammer Recipe" (2563189162), "First Aid Scalpel Recipe" (4131157841) and "Rowdy Rumbler Recipe" (301238581) do not match the "Forging: Field Support Scalpel", "Forging: First Aid Hammer" and "Forging: Rowdy Rumbler Hammer" titles, and the wiki spells the weapons as the Forging titles do. The recipe items' names are probably typos or swaps.
8. **Wiki recipe list is short.** The wiki job page lists 25 recipes; the client has 65 Forging titles and the wiki has 37 item pages under "Blacksmith Recipe Weapons" in total. Brutal Blast Bow, Frenzy Fighter Hammer, Void Woven Wand, Striking Serpent Sword, the Flair Shards and the six level 16 "Forged" weapons are in the wiki's item pages but not in its job-page recipe list.
9. **Derp's place.** Client: Dartmoor, southwest of Snowhill (2103091327, 3884621074). One Miner-era line says "Head on down to Southvale and talk to Derp Axemaker" (1357831956). The wiki NPC and place data put Derp at Dartmoor Quarry (Northern Wilds, southeast of Wugachug). Southvale is Mili Ironcall's place; the 1357831956 line looks like another version. Dartmoor is used.
10. **Anata Caliburn's place.** Client: north of Blackspore at the Awespring Tributary (440186893, 2937539212, 112150114). Wiki NPC page: Stillwater Crossing. The client is used; whether the Tributary lies within Stillwater Crossing is not known.
11. **Mili Ironcall's title.** "foreman" (100203861), "director" (4135704477) and "informant" (3527847883). Different flavour lines, not different roles.
12. **Silver Appearance item.** Rowdy Rumbler Hammer in most lines (row 20), but 671336603 says "the Glistening Scythes you forged" for Billie, which fits Scythes of Stalking (row 23). Probably an older version of Silver Appearance or a mis-copied line.
13. **Smithy Merchandise reward.** The video shows "You receive 100 coins" while the quest is still active (2fhn8kePbKQ/00648, with leaf pickups in the same frame). It is a loot or pickup line and is not recorded as the quest reward.
14. **Bruno's title.** The video shows "Crafting Supplies" (2fhn8kePbKQ/00612) and "Crafting Supplies & Recipes" (XPZ5uQEZSp4/00006). Both strings are in the client; they are probably two builds of the same NPC. "& Recipes" implies he also sells recipes; the video shows no recipes in his shop.
15. **Forging board features.** The vault description of the original `game_forging` movie records tiles, hammer tiles, a mold and the drag mechanic, and does not mention Dynamite, Shuffle, locked tiles or blockers. The video's "Forging: Motorsmith Tank Top" result shows all four (24 and 18 uses, 3 unlocked, 4 removed). The video wins. The vault analysis may simply cover the opening recipe (row 139) and a simpler board, or may not have decoded the power-up code; unresolved.
16. **Level 5, 10 and 15 achievements.** The wiki Achievements page lists levels 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 only. The client has titles for 5, 10 and 15 too ("Blacksmith: Level 5", "Level 10", "Level 15"). They probably support the gate quests and are not trophies.

## Gaps

- **Rewards.** No coin, XP, star or item reward is known for any quest. Recipe teaching is known for rows 6, 7, 13 and 18 only.
- **Quest-to-text link.** All description and objective matches are by wording. A real quest table would settle every dagger-marked entry. The shared NPCs (Smitty, Trixi, Derp, Mili, Silverray, Brody) also run Miner and Warrior quests, so some of the placed lines may belong to those jobs.
- **Quest order and prerequisites.** No source gives a quest-by-quest prerequisite chain. The order is the wiki's trainer order plus client hints; "Supply Delivery" and "Do Work!" placements are by text only.
- **Per-recipe ingredients.** Quantities and types are known for three items. The full recipe table, including the primary and secondary split, is unknown.
- **Recipe levels.** Whether a recipe needs a Blacksmith level is unknown. The wiki gives weapon levels (the level of the player who can equip the output), not crafting levels. Several quests teach a recipe with no level shown.
- **Forging balance tables.** Board size, colours, hammer rate, quota, time limit, scoring, goal sets per recipe and difficulty per recipe are not recovered. The reconstructed values in the restoration must not be treated as original.
- **Forging minigame ids.** Only row 139 is tied to a native run; the other 60 type-8 rows have no tested data. Which recipe maps to which row is not listed anywhere.
- **Hammer Quality minigame.** Which recipe or board it uses, and what "IWMG:" means, are not shown.
- **Smitty's other quest lines.** Smitty also runs Miner tasks ("Miner: Rush Order" is seen in the video, ct2JrtOiO4g). They are not Blacksmith content; they are listed only so they are not mistaken for it.
- **Sunstone placement.** Rows 39, 40 and 42 have no placed text. The wiki's level 12 band is empty. Crackin' Skullz versus Can We Build It? and the Motorsmith item order (Boots, Pants, Tank Top, Tool Belt, Gloves, Welding Mask) are by wording only.
- **Level-up details.** XP or star thresholds, unlocks per level, and what the rank titles are called are not known. Level 4, 8, 12 and 16 beyond the achievement titles carry no known content.
- **Members trial cap.** The level where non-members stop is not stated (see **Conflicts** 1).
- **Video coverage.** Only one Blacksmith quest ("Smithy Merchandise") and one Forging result screen appear in the video archive; the other Blacksmith-NPC frames are map markers, nameplates and a Miner quest tracker. There are no frames for Brody, Trixi, Derp, Mili, Billie, Silverray, Anata, Steelbloom, Red Stonehammer or any Blacksmith quest past the first. No frame shows the recipe window, the Forging board in play or the Blacksmith gear vendor's list.
- **Wiki quest pages.** The archive has none, so objectives and rewards from the wiki are not available. The wiki Blacksmith page is a 2022 revision; the NPC pages run to 2026.
- **Anvil and craft stations.** The full list of anvils and their zones is not in one source; zone pages should carry it.
