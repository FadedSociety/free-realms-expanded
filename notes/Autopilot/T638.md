# T638: Station Cash only Marketplace items leave the coin price

## What changed

- `scripts/build_wiki_prices.py` gets one rule (the T634 generator, same pinned inputs, plus the live
  `vendors.json`). A matched `stores.json` row with a Legacy coin price **moves to Station Cash** when all hold:
  its wiki article is in `SC Marketplace` and not in `Coin Shop`; no coin vendor stocks the item
  (`vendors.json` `item_ids`); the existing ambiguity rule passes (one item Class under the name); the wiki price
  list reads as regular price then optional sale price. The row gets `sc_price` (wiki regular price), `earn_only:
  false`, `price_provenance` (wiki name, URL, revision, listed prices, **`legacy_coin_price`** = the cleared
  Cost), the `T638` tag (legend entry added); `purchase_price_coins`, `member_price_coins`, `sell_price_coins` are
  cleared and `no_sale` is true. That last part is what the ride rows do and what `StoreCatalogue::load` requires of
  a Station Cash row (a row with `sc_price` and any coin price, or with `no_sale` false, is refused).
- A rerun reproduces the same rows (moved rows carry `T638`; the cleared price is read back from the provenance),
  and `--check` is clean. Rows in both categories or in vendor stock keep their coin price and are listed in the
  report as `sc:kept_dual` / `sc:kept_vendor`.
- `stores.json`: 663 rows move (plus one legend line); no Rust source changed.
- `tests/station_cash.rs`: two new tests and the store-packet count (145 to 808) plus a size bound (see below).
- `tests/test_wiki_prices.py`: the new rule on synthetic inputs; the two T634 tests now pass the vendor stock of
  their coin-priced sibling so they keep meaning "a stocked coin row keeps its price".

## (R) choices

- Station Cash only is decided from the wiki categories plus "no coin vendor stocks it" (the lead's rule, coded as
  given). A wiki article without a coin price is read as "not sold for coins".
- A moved row becomes `no_sale` with no resale price (loader rule), so a Marketplace item cannot be flipped to a
  vendor for coins. The owner's "Station Cash items can be traded" is the item catalogue's NoTrade value, unchanged here.
- The first wiki price is regular, a second is a sale (T634's rule, reused).
- The member discount percent is kept on the row (the ride rows keep it); `member_price_coins` is cleared.

## Threat note

A hostile client can send any bundle id, store id, quantity, tint, gift recipient or coupon in 66/1 and 66/3, and any
item id to the coin buy and sell paths. The listing is built only from `stores.json` rows with a server-side
`sc_price`; the price comes from the row, never the packet. A moved row has no coin price and `no_sale`, so the coin
buy path refuses it (`RESULT_SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE`, snapshot unchanged, tested) and a vendor never buys it back for
coins, so no coin arbitrage opens. The Station Cash order path is unchanged (debit and grant commit together on
clones, replay window, quantity 1, no gifts or coupons). The generator reads pinned lab files and the project's
`vendors.json` and writes only `stores.json`.
