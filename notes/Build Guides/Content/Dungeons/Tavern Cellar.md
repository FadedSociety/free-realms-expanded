| Internal bonus group | "Tavern Cellar Bonus Objective Set" (`loc 1035823821`) | [client] |

The four bonus objects are "Brawl Club Rule" props (`loc 2276216439`, game data `4140767304`), each carrying a rule from the Fight Club joke:

1. "First rule of Brawl Club: Do not talk about Brawl Club." (`loc 3097094835`)
2. "Second rule of Brawl Club: No, seriously, do not talk about Brawl Club!" (`loc 3303574665`)
3. "Third rule of Brawl Club: If someone says "stop" the brawl is over." (`loc 809940740`)
4. "Fourth rule of Brawl Club: Only two brawlers to a fight." (`loc 3598668745`)

The mapping of these four strings to the four objects is by number only. Whether they are read when destroyed is not shown in the recordings.

The client says "south of Wugachug" (`loc 3324003218`). These are all compatible with a single door at the Roadhouse. Exact coordinates are unknown.
