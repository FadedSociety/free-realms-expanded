## Delayed asset loading

The range contains a complete DelayedAssetLoader cluster. Constructor 00ae8360 installs the asset-delivery and job-queue bases, then initializes the request deque, tracked-asset list, pools, string state, and hash table. Destructor 00ae8460 drains those members and unregisters its callback. The list and map types are explicit in vtables at 00ae82a0, 00ae8300, 00ae7880, 00ae69b0, and 00ae6db0.

FileCreateJob is a ref-counted pooled job: 00ae56f0 constructs it, 00ae57b0 is its virtual work method, 00ae51e0 destroys it, and 00ae5790 provides scalar deletion. Pool-node creation/release appears at 00ae5d60, 00ae6990, and 00ae6320. The job method searches a stored path, copies a matching suffix through a fixed 8192-character string, calls further path/file helpers, and writes a result byte. Completion at 00ae8100 reports success/failure and removes the tracked request.

The manifest path is independently visible in strings. Entry points 00ae7950, 00ae79b0, and 00ae7a10 select loader states 3, 1, and 2 before starting a file-list request through 00ae6ab0. 00ae7ae0 parses lines, skips # comments, strips a leading +, hashes the basename, filters duplicates/paths, and queues accepted requests. The + marker is passed onward as the logged treat as list flag. 00ae6b90 pumps queued asset requests, allocates request IDs, enforces an active-request limit, and reports failed submissions. 00ae7f00 handles manifest or asset responses; 00ae6870 handles request errors. 00ae6d00 and 00ae6d80 repeatedly pump while sleeping in 25 ms intervals.

The surrounding pool/container helpers include chunked records at 00ae4fa0, 00ae5070, 00ae5560, and 00ae5a00; deque consumption and cleanup at 00ae6380, 00ae6420, 00ae6540, and 00ae69e0; and generic tree/pool management at 00ae5e00–00ae6180 and 00ae6ee0–00ae77d0. The red-black tree helpers fall into two node layouts. The 00ae5e00–00ae5fd0 and 00ae6f60–00ae7430 routines use parent/children/color fields at +4, +8, +0xc, and +0x10. The 00ae9a20–00ae9b00 helpers use +8, +0xc, +0x10, and +0x14. Insert, rebalance, and remove operations for the first layout include 00ae5e40, 00ae5fd0, 00ae7140, 00ae7200, 00ae73d0, and 00ae7430. The second layout includes a splice helper, left/right rotations, and insertion rebalance. No game feature is assigned to the untyped tree helpers.
