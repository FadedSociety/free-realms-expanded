## Conflicts

1. **Bonus clothing set.** The wiki says the bonus clothing for a Brawler is Barnburner or Stick and Move. The video shows a Brawler 9 receiving "Saved by the Bell Brawler Shirt", the level-1 Brawler set that the wiki lists for [[Bixie Hive]], [[Tavern Cellar]] and [[Cracked Claw Caverns]]. The set may scale with player job level rather than the dungeon. Unresolved; video wins for the observed case.
2. **Weak sourcing for the level.** Only the wiki ties "level 9" to this dungeon by name. The client strings are generic and the Brawler 9 recording is merely consistent with it.

Note: the wiki infobox calls Muggenstomp a chugawug while the page text calls her a thugawug leader. The client explains it: "We refer to chugawugs that have turned to a life of crime as Thugawugs" (`loc 149924372`). Both are right.
