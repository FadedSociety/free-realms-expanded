## Range and subsystems

This range centers on the client pet subsystem. `00b5b470` clears collection links and registers named pet inventory, equipped-item, and trick data sources (`BaseClient.PetInventory.*`, `BaseClient.PetTricks.*`); its mechanical role is UI data-source initialization (`ui.datasource`). `00b5ce60` is the large pet message handler: it dispatches subtype cases, updates pet and trick state, emits Lua-facing events and notifications, and contains pet utility and trial-expiration paths (`game.pet`).

`00b5bc20` through `00b5cd50` are payload wrappers. Each serializes a stack payload and constructs a `ClientGateway::BasePacket`/`PacketTunneledClientPacket` envelope. The callers may invoke the wrapper only when their transport field is non-null. Direct callers tie individual wrappers to PetUiMode (`00b5bd40` / `00b5f5d0`), feed (`00b5bf60` / `00b5f720`), groom (`00b5c070` / `00b5f8c0`), toy (`00b5c180` / `00b5fa50`, `00b5fbc0`), follow location (`00b5c290` / `00b5fcf0`), perform trick (`00b5c3a0` / `00b5ff50`), summon/recall (`00b5c5c0` / `00b60080`, `00b601a0`, `00b60610`), summon by item (`00b5c6d0` / `00b60110`), equip (`00b5c7e0` / `00b60220`, `00b602c0`), in-water (`00b5c8f0` / `00b60350`, `00b60420`), target (`00b5ca00` / `00b604b0`), and equip by item record (`00b5cd50` / `00b607e0`). Names based on callers are marked likely; wrapper bodies remain generic serializers.
