# T023 Demolition derby routing

## What changed

- Added `derby_gateway.rs`: strict opt-in `derby_entry.json` loading, the optional
  `derby_gates.json` NPC list, a single-human derby session, the native 39/16 offer, the
  39/5 Go acknowledgement plus the type-11 player-status activation, 34/* dispatch, tick
  handling, and the final 26/42 payout through the existing `rewards::apply_grant` path.
- Routed family 34 as `derby` in `gateway.rs` and added W90 seeds for every implemented client
  record plus the wildcard subtype.
- Added the NPC menu choice (`NpcAction::Derby` / `NpcSelection::Derby`) and set the gate from
  content. The option label defaults to the recovered lobby label 3053 (R for menu use).
- `Gameplay` now loads an optional derby package, switches to Demo Derby Driver (profile 49)
  for entry, restores the prior job on Close or reconnect, consumes the results tick, and applies
  the Close payout atomically. The save retains only the prior job marker, never the live match.
- Added `tests/derby_route.rs`: direct gateway entry-to-payout, a full `Gameplay` NPC-menu test,
  reconnect/job restoration, refusal atomicity, and reward staging checks.

## Reconstructed rules (R)

- Gate NPCs and the menu label are content policy in `derby_gates.json`; absent content or an
  absent gate list leaves the derby unoffered.
- Entry records a private instance starting at 49,000,001 and switches to Demo Derby Driver.
  The prior job is restored on Close, reconnect reset, or the saved reconnect marker path.
- The type-11 status carrier is sent as 35/20 with `0x801`; the status bit is recovered
  (`DERBY_ACTIVE_STATUS_BIT` is 11), while the derby carrier itself is reconstructed.
- This binding creates one human plus the service's deterministic NPC fill. It does not route
  actions to additional sessions.
- Payouts are data-driven coins and optional job XP. Reward items are not accepted in this
  content shape.
- Match completion uses the configured score target or time limit; a content package with
  neither is rejected.

transforms, and does not trust client reward values or identities. No dependent package was
added.
