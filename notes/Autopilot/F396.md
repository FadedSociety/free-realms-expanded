## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

No production parser, save, reward, role or session rule changed. The suite
drives the existing attack surface and asserts refusals. A hostile client
could send: a forged 41/108 Go with no screen (ignored; snapshot/save
byte-identical), a Go with an old screen naming another encounter/instance
(inert; test 3 cross-Go), an out-of-range Go (range gate drops it; canary 4),
five malformed 26/8, 36/10, 26/42, 41/109 and header-only bodies (refused for
that session alone, empty reply, snapshot/save byte-identical; B keeps
fighting), a replayed Close/Go (no pay, no start), or enter the field hoping
to see/join another player's arena (sees only the static marker; its own click
starts its own instance). Client-reported results and reward quantities are
never used: only server-verified 36/10 kills and the arena tick pay or credit.
Instance ids stay per character save and are never aliased to the public-world
instance.
