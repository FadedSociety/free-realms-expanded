## Conflicts

1. **Enemy names.** The wiki lists "Savage" and "Howling Fateeater". Neither is a client enemy string. The client has "Corrupted Hound", "Greater Corrupted Hound" and "Gloam Hound".
2. **Boss name.** Wiki and client agree on "Mysterious Stranger". The panel text uses "her".
3. **Name form.** "Vale of Ancients" in two quest lines against "Vale of the Ancients" everywhere else. The latter is used.
