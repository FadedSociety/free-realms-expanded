## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client could send a forged 49/2 accept, a forged 49/14 turn-in before the goal is done, a
duplicate 49/14, or a level claim. The rank is read only from the server's profile state (never from a
client field), the turn-in needs the server-held `pending_turnin`, and the payout is data-read and paid
once (status Completed). The tests assert byte-equal saves for the forged accept (other job), the early
49/14 and the duplicate 49/14. Free grants: coins only, once; nothing sellable.
