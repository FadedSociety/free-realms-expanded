deleting-destructor shape even though its owning class is unknown. 00a22a30 likewise leaves entry
ECX unchanged for the cleanup call. 00a23580 calls only the return stub 01062790, then conditionally
frees; its record uses a mechanical name and does not assign a class. Other cookie expressions in
the shard were checked against their code paths; record descriptions do not treat
DAT_01b839d8 ^ &local as a functional argument.

Remaining questions: what the serialized check byte means; why 129/30 result 5 triggers another
check; which Pet packet classes correspond to the indirect calls; what the game-list virtual slot
and returned value represent; what owns 00a23fd0; and who calls the three ClientPcData senders.
