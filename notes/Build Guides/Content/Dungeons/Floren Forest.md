## Conflicts

1. **Location.** The wiki says the gate is in the north west (infobox "Northern Wilds"). The client says the forest is east of Seaside next to the Pixie Nursery. Both are kept; the client is the higher source, but "Wilds" as the zone could still be correct if the nursery is in the Wilds. The gate's real position is unconfirmed.
2. **Boss lore.** Wiki "Ozymandias" is a boss. The client calls him a "grinning artifact" and "evil artifact", and the quest says the player "battled" him as an idol, not as a Floren.
