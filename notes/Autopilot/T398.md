# T398: field enemy damage matches the data (starter budget on the live scale)

Opus lane, Sonnet builder, tip a924701 (T639 and T415 merged). Skills: ponytail (full), lean-structure.

## Where the tip stands against the brief

- **46 / 141 are gone, and were never a hidden constant.** The lead's amendment holds: they were MID-H1 (level 7) and
  ELITE-H2 (level 20) enemies (row damage 15 x the old weapon-tier factor 1.75^(tier-1)) stacked on the W102-H1 camp.
  T415 moved those bands off the starter camps. T639 then put the whole path in `combat_scale.json`: a field enemy's hit is
  `damage x tier_factor(level) x enemy_damage_scale` (`encounter/field_content.rs` load, `combat_scale.rs` `scale_enemy`),
  its health `max_health x health_factor(level) x enemy_health_scale`. Today the level-3 Hooligan hits for 4, MID-H1
  (level 7) for 13 and ELITE-H2 (level 20) for 70. Brief steps 1 and 2 are met by T639 (a row edit moves the hit; new
  test below proves it).
- **Player health baseline.** The amendment asked for 2081 x 1.75^((L-10)/4) (591 at level 1). T639 chose the video fit
  500 x 1.75^((L-1)/4) (`player_base_health` 500, Damage Scale.md Q3: HUD frames read 500 / 4069 / 7116 at levels 1 / 16 /
  20), tunable in `combat_scale.json`. I kept T639's number: it carries the lead's choices and a sourced fit, and the lead
  told this task to build on that file. The two differ by 15%; changing `player_base_health` to 591 moves every test that
  reads it, nothing else.
- **What was left: step 3, the starter budget.** Measured on the tip with the real engine formulas (table below), two
  starter groups broke the brief's rule ("a lone level-matched enemy takes the player well over a minute solo-idle, a camp is
  survivable"):
  - the level-5 MID-F wolves (authored 25): hit 22, a lone wolf knocked out a level-5 Wizard in 34 s, the pair in 17 s;
  - the four quest Hooligans (authored 8, hit 4 each): 16 hit/s, 27 s for a level-1 Wizard.
  Authored damage is the only lever (level scaling stays, as the lead said), so the change is data.

## Budget (R)

Rule (the same as the earlier attempts): at its own level a lone starter enemy needs over 90 s to knock out an idle player
of the weakest job (Wizard, health x0.85), and a whole camp swinging together over 30 s. A camp is a spawn group, or the four
quest Hooligans (they appear together). Starter = authored level 5 or less. Field enemies swing every 1,000 ms
(`behaviours.json` `field_roamer`). The test checks both bounds; the numbers below are the shipped values now.

Player health = 500 x 1.75^((L-1)/4) x job multiplier (Wizard 0.85, Medic and Ninja 0.9, Brawler 1.15); hit = authored
damage x tier factor x 0.5 rounded once; enemy health = authored x 1.75^((L-1)/4) x 3.72.

the elite bands. Retuning bands 6-20 (or moving enemy damage onto the health growth) is a lead call and is left alone.
