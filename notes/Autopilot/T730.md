## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client can send 49/2 for a quest it was not offered (before 3000009 or 3000010), 49/14 early
or for an unaccepted quest, a repeated 49/14, 26/8-26/10 Attack on the creature without the quest, or
a Talk at the wrong NPC. All go through the unchanged `QuestEngine` gates and the quest-gated field;
the tests assert each refusal leaves the serialized save equal. The kill comes only from server HP (no
client field says "dead"). The creature pays 0 coins/0 XP and drops nothing, so its 60 s respawn cannot be
farmed; the reward is fixed content paid once on the server-judged turn-in (duplicate 49/14 tested).
