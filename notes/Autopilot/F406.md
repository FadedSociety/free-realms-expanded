   that its instance members contain guids 2 and 3 (the pair's own instance, so
   distance is the only thing keeping the AddPc rows apart) and that the far

   including `other(&second_login, 4).is_empty()` for B. **Canary F** (the

   really is in range of the policy check. Restored, SHA-256 `393a6b06...b8cb5b5`
   matches the original.

   captures `a_start`/`b_start` before the first kill, settles and asserts
   `saved_coins(3) == before_b + T335_FIELD_COINS` after B's shared kill,

   ("B's saved balance changed exactly once for the shared kill", left 9, right
   7). With the same canary the **pre-fix** test 4 (HEAD's `multiplayer.rs`,
   swapped in temporarily) **passed**, which proves the review's gap was real
   and is now closed. Restored, SHA-256 `8c2634d8...1bcbc581` matches.

   `rank = (1 + total / 100).min(20)`, progress 0 at the cap, matching
   `Profiles::rank` (`src/profiles.rs:228`) and `rewards::experience_packets`
