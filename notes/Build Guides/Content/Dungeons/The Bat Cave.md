## Conflicts

1. **Medic wheel item.** The wiki lists "Medic's Necklace of Regeneration II". The client has "Medic's Ring of Regeneration II" (`loc 211760624`, `2912687230`) and "Medic's Ring of Energy II" (`loc 3794736706`, `4173213135`) but no such necklace. Other jobs on this wheel have a Ring of Energy II, so the slot is probably a Ring, but which ring is unconfirmed.
2. **"The The Bat Cave!"** The client strings repeat "The" because the dungeon name already begins with it. This is a client artifact, not a typo of ours.
3. **Name forms.** The client writes "The Bat Cave!" (dungeon) but "Bat Cave" and "Bat Caves" in quest text.
