## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client can send a 49/2 accept for a prerequisite-locked quest (Serenity, Birthday
Surprises, The Perfect Present), a duplicate accept, a 49/14 before the ordered steps, twice, for a
never-accepted quest, or during another quest; it can click 26/8/26/10 on an out-of-order Talk target
or on a giver whose quest is not eligible, and send 26/6 to try to release an end screen. Every path
still goes through the unchanged production gates: `QuestEngine::can_offer`/`offer` (job, level,
prerequisite and limit checks), the ordered objective list, `begin_turnin` completeness and
`finish_turnin` once-only. The tests assert each refusal leaves the serialized save byte-equal. The
rewards are fixed content values (at most 30 coins and 30 XP here, no items); nothing is taken from a
client-reported quantity. The two new `content_key`s are NPC ids, never spawn guids, and the loader
binds givers/turn-ins by row id, not by name. No new packet, handler or trust boundary.
