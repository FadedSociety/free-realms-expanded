## Overview

**Name.** The client calls the zone **Cobblestone Village** (`ugdt` 836851972; `ucdt` 2415390857, 3733684809; warpstone "Cobblestone Village Warpstone" `ugdt` 118491817, `ucdt` 4132532572). The wiki says the village was "formerly known as Crossroads" and still writes "Crossroads" in several pages (Kibler, Mongo, King of the Ring!, Cooking and Chef text, collection names). The client carries no "Crossroads" string except one internal note, "Do NOT leave crossroads pet tutorial" (3741022042). Video shows the old name once, in a Treasure War event panel, where the screen reads "Robgoblins have stormed the Crossroads" and the client text for the same event reads "Robgoblins have stormed Cobblestone Village" (1254669846; `JYoSoRzyLrI/00042`). [client] [video] [wiki Cobblestone Village]

**Where it is.** The wiki puts the village at a crossroads at the centre of Free Realms, where the road from Sanctuary to Wildwood Speedway meets the road from Blackspore Swamp to Lavender Coast. The part west of the main road is "southwest Wilds" and the part east is "southeast Wilds"; the infobox says "Southern Wilds". [wiki Cobblestone Village] The client agrees on the neighbours: Wildwood Speedway is "south of Cobblestone Village" (144865826, 2929942543, 435936839); Farnum's Farmstead is "southwest of Cobblestone Village" (120228216, 636961791); Queensfields is north ("Head North from Cobblestone Village to Queensfields", 510664415, 3577341758); Jonelle is met "on the road north of Cobblestone Village, leading to Sanctuary" (177505531); "the entrance to Blackspore is a large cave directly east of Cobblestone Village" (3456908047); Jones's camp is on the road "leading east from Cobblestone Village toward Blackspore" (18137765); a cave "shaped like a monster's head" lies just east of the village (1171384635, 2447998130); Greenwood Camp is "to the southwest of Cobblestone Village" (2504933996). [client]

**Landmarks the sources name.**
- **Warpstone.** Mentioned in the warpstone tutorial strings: "Open the map and Interact with the purple warpstone icon and hold it down for 3 seconds to teleport to the Cobblestone Village warpstone." (2081958103) and "Teleport to the Cobblestone Village warpstone." (1574399668). The wiki calls the village "most notable for having a warpstone" that makes Farnum's Farm and Wildwood Speedway easy to reach. [client] [wiki]
- **Cafe and cooking table.** Simone's cafe: "Cobblestone Village Cafe" (name `ucdt` 3311670746; `ugdt` and `ucdt` "NOT LIVE - Cobblestone Village Cafe" 1335065899, 179901773). Simone is "near the cooking table to the north" (1322711683), "to the Northwest of here" (609555893) and "north of Samantha" (3602865719). Wiki collection spot: "Crossroads Cafe: Northwest of the center of town." Bartle is "just down the road somewhere behind the cafe" (800284964). [client] [wiki Exploration (Collections)]
- **Brawling Ring / Fighter Pit / Brawler Pit.** The client calls it "the Brawling Ring" (623292891, 2737772893), "the Cobblestone Village Brawler Pit" (1429650934) and "the Fighter Pit" (3636618686); "Carlos Brazenfist is by the fighting pit" (3776526926). Carlos is "to the east of here" (1285671500, 1021160902). Wiki collection spot: "The Brawler Pit: Southeast of the center of town." Dungeon: [[King of the Ring]]. Ricky Danger's crashed car is "next to the Brawling Ring" (2437808087) and the lamppost he destroyed stands there (3754646142). [client] [wiki]
- **Pet Adoption Center and Animal Park.** "Welcome to the Cobblestone Village Pet Adoption Center! ... Click on one of the pets inside the adoption center fence to try it or buy it. You can also buy outfits and other accessories for your pet from our pet vendors, Jennifer and Gloria." (13993651; "Interact with" variant 2092778366; short form 335112892). Majorie is "across the street, in front of the Pet Adoption Center" (2418930055). The client has a "Cobblestone Village Animal Park" entry (4118410604); the wiki says the Animal Park is "behind the pet center". Tutorial instance "Pet Tutorial - Cobblestone Village" (985862161). [client] [wiki]
- **Tavern.** The client holds "Cobblestone Village Tavern" (`ugdt` 918382506, `ucdt` 2840537779) and Amara Hearthsong's quest about "her tavern's cellar" (1466150632). The pie-theft strings send the player into "the cellar behind the cafe" (1204868364). See Conflicts 4. [client]
- **Job training center.** Olivia is at "the job training center in Cobblestone Village" (1223755301); no other string says which building that is. The wiki lists Majorie, Carlos Brazenfist and Simone as the village's "Job Trainers". [client] [wiki]
- **Shops without NPCs.** The wiki lists Potions (health, energy, replenishing), Pet Collars and Pet Supplies and a "Minigames" point of interest, and names no NPC for them. [wiki Cobblestone Village]
- **Cave east of the village.** Two uses: the "monster's head" cave of Ivan's Missing Satchel (2050890848) and the cave to Blackspore where Mac's gang retreated (2441990229). Whether these are the same cave is unproven. [client]

**Starter-town flow.** The wiki says "New players leaving the tutorial will enter Free Realms here instead of Highroad Junction." [wiki Cobblestone Village] The client teleports a new player here: "I can help teleport you to a friend of mine, Samantha. She's in Cobblestone Village in Sacred Grove." (417833941), "Find Samantha after Ashley Lightwings teleports you to Cobblestone Village." (2727718658, 2884693019) and "Find Samantha in Cobblestone Village to begin your adventures in Sacred Grove!" (1377412632). Samantha greets and points the player at Carlos for the Brawler line (1591220599, 2831550575, 3965578265); a longer welcome quest sends the player to Simone, Carlos Brazenfist, Aunt Daisy and Sam Potts (459280103); the wiki's shorter "Introduce Yourself" sends them to Ricky Danger. Ricky's crash leads to Shakey and Wildwood Speedway, where Johnny Thunder and Mad Jack train Kart Drivers and Demolition Derby Drivers, and then back to Samantha ("Samantha was looking for you while you were learning about racing. Ricky thinks she has more quests for you", 943045307). The order of the later hand-offs is [inferred]. [client] [wiki Quest:Introduce Yourself]

**Story role.** The Adventurer story reaches the village after Farnum Farmstead: Pappy Farnum sends the player to Bartle, who says Mac's Hooligans have blockaded the village; the player destroys three Gloam Artifacts and defeats Mac in "Cobblestone Showdown", then is sent to Queen Valerian ("Everyone here in Cobblestone Village thanks you", 588705107). The journal lore page reads "The Cobblestone Village was once a great crossroads of commerce, but has recently been barricaded by a thieving band of hooligans!" (1045243864). [client] [video `5ZDRXl-DES8/00254`, `3caQP-As968/00348`: Hero's Journal lore page]. Full rows: [[Adventurer]] A11 to A14.

**How to read the tables.** One row per NPC. A client name entry proves the name exists, not where the NPC stands; standing place comes from a client sentence that names the village, a video frame, or the wiki, and the row says which. Quest links go to the job page that holds the full row; quests with no job-page row are in [[#Quest index]]. The video archive holds almost nothing for this village (see Gaps): the only frames are a lore page, a Treasure War panel, Simone's Shopping List and Samantha as a distance marker. Player names, player chat and player-chosen names are never recorded.

## Notes on important NPCs

- **Samantha and Ricky Danger** are the village's own start. Samantha greets the player, sends them on to Ricky (wiki "Introduce Yourself"), to Carlos for the Brawler line, and to Simone, Aunt Daisy and Sam Potts in the longer client version. Ricky's crash chains into "Call The Crew!", which sends the player south to Shakey at Wildwood Speedway and the Kart and Derby trainers, then back to Samantha for more quests. Her "Samantha's Mail" quest teaches the warpstone. [client] [wiki Quest:Introduce Yourself]
- **Carlos Brazenfist and Kibler** run the Brawler start. Carlos is the washed-up champion; Kibler brought the troll Mongo into the ring. The player takes the job from Carlos, fights Mongo behind him ([[King of the Ring]]), then is sent to Jones's camp on the Blackspore road for the hooligan quests and onward to Sabastian Cabet and Petra. [client] [wiki Brawler]
- **Simone** starts the Chef line and has seven client-only titles on the Chef page, built around a "Battle of the Chefs" that no source explains. Jonelle's lost packages and Honey at Queensfields complete the Cobblestone Chef series. [client] [wiki Chef]
- **Majorie** with Spot runs the three pet tutorials in their own instance; Olivia and Lulani give the village's two non-tutorial Pet Trainer errands. [client]
- **Bartle** is the Adventurer story's contact: he explains the blockade, sets the three-artifact task and the Mac fight, and thanks the player at the end. [client]
- **Amara Hearthsong** is a tavern keeper with a hooligan-cellar quest; her cellar may be a village tavern or the Roadhouse (Conflicts 4).
- **Jennifer, Gloria and Roosey** are the village's vendors: two pet vendors in the adoption center and a Brawler gear merchant by the cooking table (wiki). [client] [wiki]

## Conflicts

1. **Samantha's race.** Wiki page: category Pixie. Client: asset tag `human_f_samantha` (3342010238), which reads human female. Client used. The same pattern holds for Simone (`human_f_simone` and the line "some human named Simone", 3614772560; no wiki NPC page).
2. **Crossroads against Cobblestone Village.** The wiki (rev 2026-06-27) says the village was formerly Crossroads and keeps "Crossroads" in the Kibler, Mongo, King of the Ring!, Chef and collection text. The client uses Cobblestone Village everywhere. A video frame of the Treasure War panel reads "the Crossroads" where the client text reads Cobblestone Village (`JYoSoRzyLrI/00042`), so a build exists with the old name. Client used; "Crossroads" is recorded as a former name.
3. **Aunt Daisy's place.** "At Farnum Farmstead" (1094445753, 2676810818, 3130839664) against "She should be in Cobblestone Village" (2345385226); the long welcome quest lists her among the village locals (459280103). Treated as one NPC; both places recorded.
4. **Amara's tavern and the Tavern Cellar.** The [[Tavern Cellar]] dungeon page puts the dungeon under the Roadhouse in the northern Wilds (wiki, and the Lucy quest summary "The hooligan hideout is in the Tavern Cellar behind the Roadhouse." 174198601), and lists Amara as one of its quest givers. The client puts Amara Hearthsong in Cobblestone Village (3378386281) with a "Cobblestone Village Tavern" place name, and her follow-up line says "I heard you were able to beat Mongo in the fight ring" (1574737981), which fits the village. The pie-theft text puts a hooligan "cellar behind the cafe" in the village (1204868364), while the sibling pie text for Terrence says "behind the inn" (2526291482). Whether the village had its own tavern cellar, or the same dungeon was entered from two places, is unproven.
5. **Brawling Ring direction.** Client: Carlos is "to the east" (1285671500, 1021160902, 1534164790). Wiki collection spot: the Brawler Pit is "Southeast of the center of town". Compatible; client used for the quest text, wiki for the collection spot.
6. **Where Simone stands.** Client: "in Cobblestone Village" in every line but one, which says "Speaking with Simone in Sanctuary is a great start to becoming a Chef" (293476933). The Chef page treats that as a probable typo. Client Cobblestone placement used.
7. **Kibler and Mongo availability.** The wiki marks both and the King of the Ring! dungeon "No longer in-game" (rev 2025-09-07 and 2026-05-31). The client still holds all their strings and a video of the Chatty "Battles" quest shows the same gate text. See [[King of the Ring]].
8. **Starter town.** Wiki Cobblestone Village: new players now start here instead of Highroad Junction. The Highroad page notes that the client and video still run the Highroad flow. The Chatty "Battles" quest sends a new player to "the Battle gate in Highroad Vale" but its bubble says "face the king of the ring" (3292324407), which is Mongo's gate. The same text may be shared between starter towns.
9. **"Bell Brawler" gear.** Wiki Roosey: "Bell Brawler X". Client: "Saved by the Bell Brawler X" (630718380, 3699014784). Client used.
10. **Eddie Eagle Eyes's place.** Wiki Archer page: just south of Cobblestone Village. Client: no place, only "Eddie's Bounty Hunter Service" (2308037803). Kept in Part C on one weak source.
11. **Jones's page.** The client puts his camp on the road east of the village toward Blackspore; the [[NPCs - Blackspore Swamp]] page lists him as a Blackspore-side row. Both pages carry him; this page keeps the client's wording.
12. **Introduce Yourself wording.** Wiki (rev 2013-06-02): "You meet the coolest people here in Cobblestone Village!" Client: same sentence (3023896759). A second client version names four villagers (459280103). Whether the long version replaced the short one is unknown ([[Adventurer]] conflict 13).
