# Content

This section covers the game's content: who gives what quest, what each dungeon requires and rewards, and where every NPC stands. The other [[Build Guides]] cover how the client code works.

Every fact is tagged with its source. Precedence: **[client]** (the game's own text and code) beats **[video]** (recordings of the live game), which beats **[wiki]** (the fan wiki). Where the client has better information, it replaces the wiki's, and the disagreement is recorded on the page.
