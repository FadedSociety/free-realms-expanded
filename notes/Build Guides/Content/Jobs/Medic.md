## How to read this page

- Tags: **[client]** is the game's locale text (`en_us_data.dat`), **[video]** is player recordings, **[wiki]** is the fan wiki. Precedence is client, then video, then wiki. Where they disagree, the higher source is in the table and the clash is in **Conflicts**.
- Numbers in parentheses, for example `(3096711074)`, are client locale ids. The "Title ids" column gives both title entries for a quest as `ucdt` / `ugdt`; the two tags carry the same text.
- Video frame keys look like `(qCG_K2piQgE/00030)`.
- **Matching caveat.** The locale file stores quest titles, descriptions, objectives and dialog as separate entries with no link between them. Every description or objective below was matched to its quest by wording (names, places, enemies), not by a data link. Matches that rest on wording alone are marked with a dagger (†). A dagger means "fits this quest, not proven". Strings that fit no listed quest are in **Unmatched client text**.
- **No wiki quest pages.** The wiki archive has no `Quest:Medic: ...` pages. `cq.py wiki` on every Medic quest title returns "no page". The wiki facts below come from the Medic job page, the Doctor Gordon, Doctor Callahan, Mayor Crystalline and dungeon pages, and a few item pages. The wiki gives the quest list, the giver of each quest and the level gates, and nothing about objectives.
- **Rewards.** Coins, XP and items are not in the locale file. Only four quest rewards are named anywhere (all in wiki item pages, all weapons). Every other reward cell reads "unknown".
- The client spells "So You Want to be a Medic?" without the "Medic:" prefix that the wiki uses; the client spelling is used.

## Overview

Medic is a combat job that heals allies and weakens enemies with medicine. The wiki calls it long-distance combat that uses medicine to heal allies. [wiki] The client says "Medics use medicine to keep allies healthy as well as debilitate opponents." (1016140721) [client]. Experience comes from Medic quests and from battles in the Wilds and in dungeons. [wiki] The wiki says Medic is trained by Doctor Gordon. [wiki]

**Unlocking.** The wiki says Medic is unlocked by visiting Nurse Naia at the hospital in Sanctuary. [wiki] The client agrees on Naia ("Learn how to be a medic by finding Nurse Naia in Sanctuary." 3443798543; "If you've always wanted to take care of others, Nurse Naia will get you started." 4165224478) [client]. The client also names Doctor Gordon as a starting point ("Doctor Gordon can help you start your career as a medic." 788404651; "Poor Doctor Gordon and his staff at Ayani's Pride Hospital are always spread so thin! ... You should go talk to Doctor Gordon in Sanctuary if you're interested in becoming a medic." 2968234539; Amara's referral 4109358917). [client] See **Conflicts**.

- **Entrance exam.** Unlocking is a three-question test run by Nurse Naia. Question one: "You see someone passed out on the side of the road. What do you do in this situation?" (1223254944). Question two: "A man walks into the hospital and is very sick. He reveals he has no money to pay for treatment, but he needs it badly. What would you do?" (2136427033). Question three: "Why do you want to become a medic?" (2523135785). Failing sends the player back to study: "I think you have some more studying to do before you can become a medic. Come back later and take the test again." (3993954191); "That is incorrect. I am sorry but you do not qualify to become a medic at this time." (1302975110); "I don't think you are ready to become a medic. Study up a bit and take the test again later." (437707728). A perfect score gives "A perfect score, wow! You definitely have what it takes to begin with us here!" (836266468). The answer options and which answers are correct are not in the locale text found. [client]
- **Starter gear.** The Student Medic Hammer (level 1 weapon) was "previously acquired by unlocking the Medic job". [wiki]

**Levels.** The job runs from level 1 to 20 (**max_level 20**). [client][wiki] Achievements exist for Medic levels 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 (see **Quest line**). [client][wiki]

**Members only.** The wiki puts Medic in "Members Only". [wiki] The client has a Medic-specific upgrade prompt ("Oh, no! Want to keep playing as a Medic? Upgrade to unlock more fun!" 3220787906) and a "Medic Membership Upgrade Bundle" (249744385). General client text says "Anyone can play up to level 4, but every job goes up to level 20!" (348625404) and "you can try any job up to at least level five" (2065313732). [client] The client's Sunstone Valley blurb names Brawler, Miner and Blacksmith as free to level 20 (2640708938, quoted on [[Brawler]]); Medic is not in that list. [client] So: non-members can try Medic to about level 4 or 5, and the job is members only beyond that. This is an inference from the client and wiki together; see **Conflicts**. The video shows a Medic at level 10 in the Robgoblin Treasure Trove with a "Members Only Bonus! Coins x105" line on the results panel (qCG_K2piQgE/00178), which shows the dungeon bonus, not the job gate. [video]

**Level gates.** The line stops at four trainers until the player reaches a level: Doctor Gordon at 5, Clover at 10, Docaloc at 15, Doctor Callahan at 20. [client][wiki] Between each gate the player is sent to Beth Callahan for a repeatable contract (see **Quest line**). [client][wiki]

## Where the job levels

Combat jobs level through battles, which are the dungeons and the wandering fights in the Wilds. [client][wiki] The client tip says combat jobs level by playing "battles" (4024725827, quoted on [[Brawler]]). [client] Dungeon entry needs a minimum combat level that grows with difficulty (wiki dungeon pages: difficulty 3 needs level 5, difficulty 4 needs level 9, difficulty 5 needs level 13). [wiki] Every dungeon has a "Medic Prize Wheel" with Medic-only items (for example the Trove wheel: Antibiotic Medic Shirt, Battle Item Mystery Pack, Medic's Power Shard of Vitality II, Medic's Ring of Regeneration II, Medic's Scalpel of Triage, 49 coins).
