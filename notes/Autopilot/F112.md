# F112: T013 review blockers

## What changed

- `src/gameplay.rs`: added `npc_location_event`; `initialize_world` now emits
  `QuestEvent::VisitPosition` after NPC quest login synchronization in both
  the opening and no-opening branches. The no-opening branch previously
  returned before any regional location event.
- Reworked the T013 tests around real request dispatch:
  - mining and harvesting click ordinary `26/8` node actors, then drive the
    server-owned Type8 lifecycle and completion frames through
    `Gameplay::handle_in_world`;
  - cooking starts with recipe-book `26/24`, plays the movie through its
    readiness, round and win reports, and completes two recipes;
  - kart registers/goes/finishes through gameplay frames and checks the
    regional quest only after a paid claim; an active non-Kart job forces a
    refused payout, and changing back retries the still-unpaid finish;
  - fishing installs both collect and minigame objectives, completes three
    catches, and compares inventory, coins, profiles and quest state across a
    replayed reel while the objectives are still active;
  - the collect refusal now runs while its objective is at 1 of 2 and compares
    the whole gameplay state.
- No production behavior outside T013's location-event path changed. No
  launcher, login, account, session, transport, trade, store, studio or
  manifest file was touched.

## Review resolution

- BLOCKER 1 confirmed and fixed: both `initialize_world` branches now emit the
  location event after quest synchronization. The new no-opening test loads a
  copied package without `opening.json` and asserts area and reach both count.
- BLOCKER 2 confirmed and fixed: gather, craft, mining and kart regressions now
  use the request/lifecycle paths described above rather than calling payout
  helpers. Existing service-level kart/fishing tests remain as narrower checks.
- BLOCKER 3 confirmed and fixed: refusal tests run before objective completion;
  fishing uses two objectives with three required completions and compares the
  full economy plus quest state and `changed`.

## Reconstructed rules

- (R) Scene entry is an authoritative regional location event, including when
  the package has no `opening.json`.
- (R) One paid fishing catch is one collect event and one minigame completion;
  a replay is refused without economy or quest mutation.
- (R) A kart speedway id is the minigame completion identity, but only a
  successful paid claim may forward it to the quest engine.
