## Security note (secure-code-guardian)

Threat surface: crafted client combat packets (`26/8`, `26/10`, `36/10`) and
reconnect timing. A hostile client cannot report a kill, a damage amount, a
definition id, an actor level, a coin amount or an XP amount: the payout is
derived only from the server's own alive-to-dead HP transition on a
server-owned field actor row (`actor.definition_id`, `actor.level`) and the
loader-validated table. A player cannot claim another player's credit: credits
are keyed by the session's character guid, never by a packet field.
Refused/ignored: replayed lethal damage and corpse hits (`health == 0` guard),
out-of-range/cooldown/evading/returning attacks, unknown or dead targets, a
bystander with no participation credit, a second claim, an expired credit, a
malformed/overlapping/duplicate/out-of-bounds table (load error), and an
unrepresentable payout (checked against the live wallet/active job, logged,
answered with a fixed server string, `Ok` returned so the shared tick
continues).

The disconnect retention is bounded by `CREDIT_TTL_MS` and `MAX_CREDITS`,
computed from the server clock only, and never retains the field-owned drop. A
client cannot extend it, and a permanently absent player's credits are pruned
at the next field tick after expiry (asserted). The new content read is bounded
before allocation. The refusal line contains no client text and no player data.
No item, trade or save surface was added.
