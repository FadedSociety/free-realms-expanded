## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

The text documents ticket and role security, so the risk is the runbook misleading the host, not
code. A hostile VPN peer can no longer enter as 1/1 with the public fixed ticket (refused, logged).
What it can still do: replay a ticket it obtained (a leaked log, screenshot or packet capture of
another player's handoff) for as long as the handoff key is unchanged, because tickets carry no
expiry or nonce; the duplicate-session check stops it only while the owner is online. The runbook
now says this and leaves the go/no-go to the lead, as the review asked. No new policy written.
