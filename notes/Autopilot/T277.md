## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

Hostile client input for this slice is limited to the existing NPC and quest
packets. A client can send a forged 49/2 accept, a 49/14 with no end screen, a
repeated 49/2 decline, an out-of-range 26/8/26/10, or replay a Talk. Every one is
refused or ignored by the existing engine/session gates: `QuestEngine::offer`
requires a pending offer and an eligible, server-authored definition;
`can_offer` rejects active/offered/completed records; `finish_turnin` requires
the saved `pending_turnin`; the NPC router proves range/zone and that the exact
menu option was offered; the reward ledger (`quest:<character>:<quest>`) pays
once. Kills are server-generated from the shared-field attack path only; the
client cannot report a kill, and `930060` is a different `definition_id` so it is
not counted. A near-cap coin balance is refused per player by `grant_coins`
(`> i32::MAX`) without a commit, and the job XP profile must be unlocked
(`rewards::locked_job_profile`). No new parser, secret, SQL or network surface
was added. Refusals change nothing is proven in
`a_refused_or_out_of_range_request_changes_nothing`.
