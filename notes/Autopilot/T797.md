# T797: real pet care items - the client's type-4 kit, named, stocked and fed

Brief (native 2026-10-27): find every client pet kit item (item type 4), record it in `pets.json` with its
slot and name, stock the pet merchants at the client Cost, include the bowls in the stock and the care path,
and make a feed with a real bowl + food play the eat animation and restore the need. The ids the pet tests
used (11, 1835, 1836, 10521, 912) are real client ids, but only those five were ever wired; everything else
in the kit was absent from the content and the shops.

## What changed

- `data/content/pets.json`: `care_items` is now the whole client kit table: **all 170 client item definitions
  with `Type` 4** (bowls 27, food 45, shampoo 29, conditioner 25, brush 15, vitamins 29), each row
  `{definition_id, name_id, label, slot, restore, src}`. `label` is the client locale text at `name_id`
  verbatim (52 names carry the client's `{n}Name{pl="Plural"}` markup); `slot` and `restore` are the client
  definition `Slot` and `Param2`. `care` gains `feed_animation` 5507.
- `src/pets.rs`: `PetCareItem` lost the hand-typed `action`/`need`/`consumes` flags; `KitRole` derives the
  panel tab, action and keep/spend rule from the client `Slot` (200 shampoo, 201 conditioner, 203 brush,
  204 bowl, 205 food, 206 vitamins). A feed now needs a recorded bowl in slot 5 and a recorded food in slot 6
  (`feedable`), grooming a recorded shampoo, conditioner and brush (`groom_kit`), and `care()` spends one
  recorded food. `care_tick` orders the client `pet_feed` animation (35/8, id 5507) at the apply step to the
  owner and to the pet's viewers. `PetCareItem` validation checks every field at load.
- `src/pets/grooming.rs`: the shampoo/conditioner/brush checks use `KitRole`.
- `data/content/stores.json`: 146 one-line rows for the kit ids that had no price; client `Cost` where it is
  positive, 5 coins where the client Cost is 0 (R). The 17 kit ids that already had a row keep it; F841
  repriced 37016 (Massage Brush) from its older R118 5 coins to the client Cost 15.
- `data/content/vendors.json`: Gloria (9500000246, Cobblestone Village) and Florina (9500000482, Sanctuary
  "Pet supplies") stock the 163 kit items that are not client test rows and not Bait, 316 new item rows, at
  the store price. Coverage counters follow (`items_resolved` 1063 -> 1379, `items_with_stores_price`
  1061 -> 1377); a `t797_pet_kit` provenance note and a `t797_pet_kit_rows` count are added.
- `tests/pet_kit_items.rs`: four tests (data, stock, real feed/eat animation, refused non-bowl) - below.
- Deleted: `PetCareItem.action`/`.need`/`.consumes`/`.source`, `PetService::is_care`, `parse_need`. Every
  pet care fact now has one owner (the client slot) instead of four fields that had to agree.

## (R) choices

- (R) The eat animation is ordered at the apply step (`feed_effect_ms` after the begin), not at the begin:
  the client's kind 1 only raises BeginInteraction. The original ordering is not recovered. If a native run
  shows the eat starting too late, the `feed_animation` send moves to `start_feed`'s begin (one line).
- (R) Feed restore: `Param2` is applied as need points on the 0..100 bar and clamps at 100 (the previous
  T485 rule, kept; foods run 50..400 on a scale the client does not expose).
- (R) A zero client `Cost` is not a sale price. The shop price is 5 coins, the lowest price any store row
  carries; member price is 10% off (min 1). Where the client row is not `NoSale`, resale is
  `max(1, Cost // 10)` (the T633 floor policy); the two stocked rows whose client row says `NoSale`
  (62536, 68931 Confetti Shampoo) keep `no_sale: true` and no resale price, and 57012 Bait (also `NoSale`)
  is not stocked.
- (R) The client defines the kit items and their Cost; that Gloria and Florina stock every one of them is a
  reconstructed stocking decision (the client never names these two NPCs as sellers). The vendor rows carry
  `stock_source.kind: reconstructed` and an `(R)` claim, and the kit table's client Cost (15) replaces the
  older R118 Massage Brush price of 5 so the row is sold at the client Cost.
- (R) Vitamins are recorded and fittable, and their `Param2` is kept, but no action spends them yet: the
  client's vitamin tab's effect (cleanliness, transformation, treasure chance) has no recovered request.
- (R) Bowl and brush are kept, shampoo/conditioner/food/vitamins are consumed (client locale and the Pets
  care section of the build guide).
- (R) The client's kit class is per species (73 generic, 31 dog, 61 cat); the fit gate's class check is the
  existing T485 rule.

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

- A hostile client can send 53/16 fit requests with any slot/item words. The fit path (unchanged) requires
  slots 1..=7, an owned instance, type 4, definition slot == index + 199 and a class the pet model lists;
  a slot outside 1..=7 echoes nothing (the client's 53/16 handler writes the echoed slot unbounded, F778).
- 53/19 (feed) carries only the pet id; no quantity, item id or restore value crosses the trust boundary.
  The server picks the fitted bowl (slot 5) and food (slot 6), requires both to be recorded client kit rows
  in those slots, and spends exactly one owned unit through `Inventory`'s staged transaction. A stale save
  with a non-kit item in slot 5 is refused and changes nothing (test above).
- 53/18 (groom) likewise requires recorded shampoo/conditioner/brush instances and spends one shampoo (and
  one optional conditioner); stroke points are server-bounded.
- The new animation id comes from content (validated 1..=4_000_000 at load), never from the client. No new
  network surface, no path or id from a packet reaches the file system, no new persisted state.
- The shop stock/prices are server content; the existing buy path checks the vendor's own stock list and the
  player's coins before granting. No item is made free and no price is client-controlled.
