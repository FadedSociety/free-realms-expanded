## Overview

The wiki calls Wugachug "the hometown of the Chugawugs", a town whose "several quests related to the Sasparilla Brewery are started here", with "slight signs of snow", "right next to Snowhill, and Seaside", "Below is Merry Vale", and a carnival look (a tent, a stage, shops). Its infobox says "NE of Seaside, N of Merry Vale". Points of interest: Chugaroot Farm, the Carnival, the Party Tent, the Sasparilla Brewery, the Thoroughfare. Shops: Chef Clothing & Tools (level 9 tools, level 20 clothing), Chef Recipes & Supplies, Shards & Accessories, Potions. Local dungeon: [[Sweetwater Climb]]. Resources: choychoy, chugaroot, gummyfruit, rainleaf, spiralmint, stainberry. Trivia: it was "originally called Chugawug Village back in the early development". [wiki Wugachug]

## Part A. The entrance, the Sasparilla Brewery and the Party Tent

## Notes: quest givers and trainers

- **Wugachug is a sasparilla town, and almost all its quests are about the drink.** Wugawump is the Brewmaster who gets the player started; Brockoom, Oggy, Tugaboom, Gooplemoop and Guard Thoomlug each hold a small chain around the Brewery, the bixies, the Party Tent and the Climb. The one wiki-documented quest here is A Sweetwater Climb, which needs A Brewing Darkness first and leads to Chug-a-Mug Challenge. [client] [wiki]
- **The Heroes story is the main fixed content.** Chugzy starts A Sweetwater Climb and Chug-a-Mug Challenge, then Countering the Cavalry, Elf Ex Machina and The Bugawug Queen run the Ninjawug mountainside and the hunt for Raven. The wiki puts the whole chain in the Heroes Journal and says Chugzy, Raven and the story arrived on 13 August 2013; the client has "Region 2 ... Hero Rises" sticker strings. Which of the six titles holds which description is partly inferred. [client] [wiki]
- **Three job lines run through here.** Postchug Runalug is the [[Postman]] level 15 gate. Cook Yumtumlot is the [[Chef]] stop before Lucca. Shifty, Bud Grimyfoot, Grollgroll and Wugawump host the [[Card Duelist]] tournament steps, and Twingnbor hosts [[Kart Driver]] Endorsement Deal. [client] [wiki]
- **Shops.** Five wiki-era shops (Fraz, Vere, Zamzani, Bev, Cliff) sell job gear for Brawler, Archer, Chef, Medic and Postman; Tatounbor is the Chef clothing and tools vendor. All are from 2013 updates, so a launch-era rebuild would not have them. [wiki]
- **Sweetwater Climb.** The dungeon page covers the fight; this page lists who sends players there (Thoomlug, Tugaboom, Wugawump, Terrence, Kazuo).

## Conflicts

1. **Where Wugachug is.** Wiki: "NE of Seaside, N of Merry Vale", "right next to Snowhill, and Seaside"; the wiki Seaside page says Seaside is "SW of Wugachug". Client: "northwestern Wilds" (1918156750), "North of Merry Vale" (1639677413, 3768554252), "west of Snowhill" (708435957), and the Roadhouse "on the road from Wugachug to Snowhill" (3585554671) beside "north of Merry Vale" (see [[NPCs - Wilds]] Conflicts 1). Not resolved; all are recorded.
2. **Sweetwater Climb's place.** Wiki Quest:A Sweetwater Climb: "behind the Wugachug Brewery"; the dungeon page: "a gate in Wugachug". Client: "Sweetwater Climb, North of Wugachug" (58657603). Both kept.
3. **Burnetta.** Wiki infobox: location Wugachug, occupation Chef. Client: the Greenwood Camp cook (1320192863, 1472974034). Client used; the row is on [[NPCs - Greenwood Forest]]. The wiki Chugawugs list also names her under Wugachug.
4. **Chugzy's name spelling.** Client: "Chugo'Zytok'Rylari the Banished" (101226827). Wiki infobox: "Chugo'Zytok'Rylary the Banished". Client used. The wiki quote itself spells it "Rylari".
5. **Quest title spellings.** Client: "Countering the Cavalry" (3171712014) and "Elf Ex Machina" (2723116348). Wiki General Wugashima page: "Countering the Calvary", and "Ex Elf Machina" once beside "Elf Ex Machina". Client used. The wiki also writes "Flying Pig Dogo" once; the client has "Flying Pig Dojo!" (2574423346).
6. **The disguise and the queen.** Wiki Raven and General Wugashima pages: "Bixie Queen". Client: "Bugawug Queen" (2742387075, 2951834333, 4226350966), "Wugachug Queen" (639327255, 2640171443) and "Queen Raven" (name 3105531472). 16. **Chug-a-Mug Challenge is more than one thing.** The client uses the name for a quest ("Chug-a-Mug Challenge", 6276459), a drinking contest in the Kart line ("Drink a mug of sasparilla at the Chug-a-Mug Challenge!", 3676252454) and the wiki's combat battle with Glugalug, Rumple and the manguanas. The wiki battle is not in the client text; the Kart contest is a different minigame.

## Gaps

- **No coordinates.** The sources give directions only. The video carries distance markers and the "Wugachug" banners but no map positions.
- **Video coverage is thin.** The recordings show Boranug, Durple, Wugawump, Gooplemoop, Wootmoot, Twingnbor, Farmer Murglot, Chugzy, Postchug Runalug (quest panel), Pollentine and Shifty (interaction bar) and a handful of blue-marker names. No recording shows Thoomlug, Brockoom, Oggy, Tugaboom, Loopug, Sal, Delilah, Happalot, Chuggoom, Yumtumlot, Lumlot (a marker only), Mumpalot, Nomnomalug, Woomplot, Grollgroll, any Part C merchant, General Wugashima or the Ninjawugs, or the Chug-a-Mug battle. Most rows rest on [client] and [wiki].
- **Quest titles.** Titles with no matched description: A Brewing Darkness (only wording), The Bugawug Queen (only wording), A Hero Rises!. Descriptions with no title: Brockoom's flavour chain, the Murglot errands, Lumlot's, Oggy's gathering lead, Tugaboom's bixie chain, the party-tent chain, the Virtalot chain, the Wootmoot chain, the sasparilla raid, the revelers' rescue, the Floren and Geomancer contracts. Possible matching titles that were not proven: A Light Brewing, Sour Sasparilla, Feeding the Kittens, Found Kitten, Bears on the Loose!, Can You Bear It?, Fetch Me a Sasparilla.
- **Rewards and levels.** No source states a reward or level for any Wugachug-only quest except A Sweetwater Climb (wiki: 88 coins and a job-specific power shard; level unknown) and the collection rewards. The Heroes chain's levels are unknown; the wiki lists Chug-a-Mug Challenge as the quest after it.
- **The order of the Heroes chain.** Only A Brewing Darkness, then A Sweetwater Climb, then Chug-a-Mug Challenge is documented. Where Countering the Cavalry, Elf Ex Machina and The Bugawug Queen fall, and who gives A Brewing Darkness and The Bugawug Queen, is unknown.
- **Standing place not stated.** Tugaboom, Loopug, Virtalot, Mumpalot, Gertrude's usual spot, Lumlot's stall, and the stands of the four revelers have no usable place line.
- **Name-only chugawug entries.** Durple, Glugalot and Sparkolot have client name entries and a few video sightings; they have no text. Other chugawug-style name entries with no zone evidence at all (Borgug, Bogug, Glot, Olberug, Jugwump, Pipagug, Chugamug, Glugamug, Gertrug) are not placed here.
- **Wiki-only content.** Shifty's tent position, the five merchants' places and stock, the Chug-a-Mug battle opponents and the Ninjawug Outpost difficulty are wiki-only. Sweetwater Climb is covered on its own page.
- **Retired content.** The client has a "NOT LIVE - Wugachug's Stock" pair (ucdt 1723559829; ugdt 2779398236), so a Wugachug shop was cut. The wiki lists Bruce on the Wugachug Stage only from 5 June to 14 Aug 2009 and for the July 2010 Rock Out. No NPC here is marked "no longer in-game" by the wiki.
- **Events.** The Sasparilla Festival, Birthday Bash and Rock Out events are client strings with no NPC rows; the spawn rules of Cakenstein and the pet and pink-and-purple dress parties ("Wear something pink or purple and join us on the Main Stage in Wugachug!", 574938037) are not in the text found.
