# T028 Kart racing end-to-end audit

## Verdict

The server path is complete for the local reconstructed solo race: an NPC menu choice is
admission-checked, starts the entry state machine, runs the ordered lap authority, sends the final
progress frame, pays through the atomic reward seam, persists the paid marker and cooldown, and
returns through the normal close or timeout path. This is source and synthetic-wire evidence only.
No native run was available or attempted here.

Three gaps were fixed: race content without a countdown-zero step was accepted, a gate claim could
reach the finish just after the race window before the next tick, and the lower-level session could
turn a finished result into an abandoned run.

## Changes

- `src/kart_gateway.rs`: refuses race content whose schedule does not reach `Countdown(0)`. This
  prevents a gate NPC from offering a track that can never leave preparation/countdown.
- `src/kart_service.rs`: checks `race_elapsed` before accepting a gate claim. The refusal retains
  the existing bounded-attempt behavior and moves no race, reward, position or lap state.
- `src/kart_racing.rs`: `abandon()` is a no-op after `Phase::Finished`, so a late teardown cannot
  erase a validated result and its pending reward.
- Tests: `tests/kart_npc_entry.rs`, `tests/kart_service.rs` and `tests/kart_racing.rs` cover all
  three fixes. The cooldown test now also includes the cooldown message and asserts refusal purity.

## Reconstructed rules

- (R) A deployed kart package must contain an emitted countdown zero; otherwise it is refused at
  load rather than offering a stuck race.
- (R) The packet path and tick path share one whole-race time window. A late claim spends one
  attempt-budget slot but cannot advance or finish the race.
- (R) A validated finish is terminal for reward purposes. Abandoning or closing a finished race
  does not invalidate the result.
- Existing (R) policy remains: the two gate NPC picks, the menu label, the 300 s shipped cooldown,
  auto-unlocking Kart Driver, solo-only racing, and the starter payout.
