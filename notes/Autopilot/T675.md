## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client could forge a 49/2 accept for a quest it was never offered (job or prerequisite wrong), a 49/14 turn-in
before the goals are done, a Talk to a target out of order, or replay a 49/14. The production paths refuse each
(`QuestEngine::can_offer`, `begin_turnin`, ordered goals, the pending-turn-in state); the tests assert every such refusal leaves
the serialized save byte-equal (`assert_packet_changes_nothing`, `assert_no_offer`). The level comes from the server's own
`Profiles::rank` (`job_level`), never from a client claim. Rewards are fixed values in the record (at most 15 coins and 25 XP):
no item, no overflow path, no client quantity.
