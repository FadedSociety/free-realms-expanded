## Security threat note

- A hostile client can replay a recorded winning line, send `OnMoveComputerMsg` with chosen bytes,
  forge `FRServer_GameOver`, or send movie traffic outside `Playing`. The computer reply is still
  chosen only from the server's scoring and its own seeded stream; `OnMoveComputerMsg` stays inert;
  only a server-verified terminal position plus the client acknowledgement reaches `settle`; movie
  traffic outside `Playing` is inert; every refusal is staged on a clone and leaves the service
  untouched.
- The offline-fixture setters (`Gameplay::seed_chess` → `ChessService::reseed` → `ChessTables::reseed`)
  are in-process test hooks only; production `ChessService::with_state` seeds from `getrandom` and
  returns `Err` (never a predictable fallback) if the OS source is unavailable.
- The persisted ledger is per character and validated on restore: an over-bound `paid_coins` is
  refused, never clamped, so a tampered save cannot mint coins or widen its own cap. The daily cap
  is checked at the only grant site and counts the grant actually applied.
- A zero near-equal window (which would make every reply position-determined) is refused by content
  validation and by the table constructor.
