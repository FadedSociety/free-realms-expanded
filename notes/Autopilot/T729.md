# T729: zone quests round 3, Southvale (data + tests)

Of the seven rows in `lab/references/quest-lines/wiring/round3/southvale.json`, **two are enabled** ("Finding
Southvale" 3100013, "Reach Level 15" 3100019) plus **two neighbours the chain needs** (3100012 "Reach Level 10",
3100013's prerequisite, and 3100020 "See For Yourself", which enabling 3100019 makes reachable). The other five
rows are not playable, each for a reason below: 3020014 is default-enabled but its prerequisite 3020013 is an empty
stub, and the other four are disabled. Data and tests only: no `src/` file, no wire shape, no new NPC row.

## Tests (`tests/zone_quests_southvale.rs`)

All five drive `Gameplay::handle_in_world` through the real NPC menu (26/8, 26/10, 26/6, 49/2, 49/14) and the real
`realm level` command; rewards and text ids are read from the record. The two level gates are pinned to the client
text's 10 and 15 (F746), so an editor changing a gate fails the suite.

1. `a_miner_walks_the_level_ten_gate_then_finds_mili_and_is_paid_once_each`: forged 49/2 for 3100013 before the gate and
   a forged early 49/14 change nothing; Derp offers 3100012 (49/1 card ids equal the record), the rank completes the
   goal, the turn-in pays the data row; Derp then offers 3100013, a Talk at Derp holds nothing, Mili's Talk opens the end
   screen at Mili (actor, end text and 49/13 reward bundle checked) and the 49/14 pays coins and XP; a duplicate 49/14
   and a second offer change nothing.
2. `grumpore_holds_the_level_fifteen_gate_until_the_rank_is_real`: one rank short never credits, Grumpore offers and hands
   in nothing, an early 49/14 changes nothing; the real rank completes it; paid once; a duplicate 49/14 is a no-op;
   Grumpore then offers 3100020, whose Talk at Silverray opens the end screen there and pays the data row.
3. `only_the_active_miner_is_offered_the_gates`: an active Adventurer gets no offer at Derp or Grumpore and forged 49/2
   packets for both quests leave the save byte-equal.
4. `progress_survives_a_reconnect_and_players_are_independent`: the real `game_store` save envelope after accept and
   again with the end screen held; a second player on the same quest is untouched.
5. `finding_southvale_reopens_at_mili_after_the_real_save_envelope`: completes 3100012, accepts 3100013, holds the Talk
   at Mili, saves through `game_store`, reloads, and asserts the released 49/13 names Mili (not the Derp fallback) and

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client could forge a 49/2 for a quest it was not offered (wrong job, prerequisite open), a 49/14 before the
goal is done, a duplicate 49/14, or claim a level. The rank is read only from the server's profile state (`realm level` is
admin-only dev tooling in the test; production ranks come from XP), the turn-in needs the server-held `pending_turnin`,
and the payout is data-read and paid once (status Completed). The tests assert byte-equal saves for the forged accepts
and the early and duplicate 49/14. Free grants: coins and Miner XP only, once; no item, so nothing sellable.
