## Result

T240 pays every credited participant of a shared-field kill (a hitter inside
the participation window, the enemy's engaged target, and the killer). T256
pushed `EncounterOutcome::defeated` only from the engine that landed the lethal
hit. T302 carries the killed actor's server-authored `definition_id` on T240's
`KillCredit` and makes `claim_kill_credits` push it into that claimant's own
outcome, exactly once, on the claimant's next shared tick. The same `who` set
now drives the reward and the quest event; a future contributor-set change must
change both together (module doc).

Unchanged: the killer's T256 push in `strike_damage` (the killer is excluded
from its own credit list), the solo-engine path (no credits are queued), the
reward amount, the contributor set, the wire, the save schema, and the
`CREDIT_TTL_MS`/`MAX_CREDITS` values. No new coins, XP, items, save state,
packet family, ledger or handler.

A zero-reward, quest-bearing field enemy now leaves a credit for every
participant: the producer returns early only when the kill has no drop table,
no reward **and** no positive definition (`definition <= 0`).

## Security note (secure-code-guardian)

Threat surface: a hostile client can send `36/10` abilities, `26/8`/`26/10`
interactions and `26/13` job switches on a shared field. It cannot report a
kill, a damage value, an actor definition, an actor health, a quest id, an
objective id, a count or a reward. The carried `definition` is the server-owned
field actor row's `definition_id` from trusted content; the credit is keyed by
the authenticated sender stored by `with_shared_fields` (`character.guid`), and
the kill is the server's own alive-to-dead HP transition. The claim event is
server-authored with `count: 1`, and `QuestEngine` caps the counter at the
objective's required value.

Refused/ignored: a non-lethal hit; a corpse hit (`s.health == 0`); an
out-of-range/cooldown/evading/unknown target; a negative definition (refused at
content load); a definition of `0` (carried only when the kill already has a
drop table or reward, and never pushed as an event); an expired credit; a
credit dropped by the 16-entry cap; a request from a player whose interest set
does not contain the actor (the production membership gate); a session with no
bound NPC quest catalogue (the event is skipped, the credit is consumed, no
error). No coin, item, trade, save-ordering or fanout code was touched; a
claim's reward and event are private replies to the claimant.

The only per-player error path is the pre-existing `?` on
`gateway.tick_shared_effects(...)` in the shared tick
(`src/bin/osfr-gateway.rs:3087`, lead-owned). The claim event cannot fail for a
positive definition: `OpeningEngine::on_event(Defeat)` refuses only a
non-positive definition and a defeat event never carries an opening reward;
`QuestEvent::KillActorKind` validates a positive id with count 1 and a bound
engine only emits objective packets. The reachable absent-engine case returns
`Ok` and is tested. No new `?` was added.

## Decisions and invariants (each with its test)

1. **Which profile receives XP.** No XP or coins are added here. The
   contributor's T240 reward still goes to the active job at payout time; the
   new event advances only the claimant's active quests and opening `Defeat`
   goals. `the_credit_pays_the_contributor_reward_and_quest_event_once` snaps
   coins/XP before and after the claimant's tick: 2 coins and 5 Brawler XP once,
   one 38/19/38/14, quest counter once, and no second reward on the next tick.
2. **Where the credit lives; reconnect/expiry.** The credit stays in the
   world-owned `KillLedger.credits` (in memory). Expiry (`CREDIT_TTL_MS`) and
   the leave prune (`retain_casters`) drop it; nothing is saved.
   `an_expired_or_left_field_credit_advances_nothing` proves an expired credit
   and a quest-only credit dropped on leaving change neither the counter nor the
   serialized state. **Stale brief corrected:** F276 already keeps an unexpired
   *reward-bearing* credit for a departed player and drops only the field-owned
   drop; T302's event rides that same credit, proven by
   `a_reward_bearing_credit_advances_a_reconnected_contributor_once` (drop the
   session, run the real `retain_casters`, reconnect through
   `Fixture::game_as`). A quest-only credit is still forfeited on leave, as the
   brief says.
3. **Arithmetic/caps from data; no server error.** `definition` is the actor
   row's `i32`; the event push is guarded by `> 0`, so no `unwrap` conversion
   is needed and a zero definition can ride a credit that exists for its
   drop/reward but is never pushed as an event. A full list drops the oldest
   credit (existing `MAX_CREDITS` rule).
   `a_zero_reward_event_credits_a_contributor_quest` uses a content copy with no
   `field_kill_rewards.json` and actors with no `power_up_table`, so both the
   table and reward are `None` while the definition is positive.
   `a_zero_or_invalid_definition_is_ignored_without_an_error` proves a zero
   definition with no table/reward queues no credit and pushes no event, and a
   negative definition is refused at load.
   `a_full_credit_list_drops_the_oldest_and_other_players_continue` runs 17
   kills inside the TTL: the 17th drops the first (the only quest target) from
   B's 16-entry list, B advances nothing, and C's identical first-kill credit
   still advances C and pays.
4. **What a refusal leaves unchanged.** A malformed/absent quest state and a
   refused event leave the claimant's serialized state equal and do not stop
   another contributor. `a_failing_quest_event_for_one_contributor_does_not_
   change_or_stop_the_other`: B has no bound catalogue and no matching opening
   goal, its claim returns `Ok` with no change and byte-identical state, and C's
   credit still pays. The pre-existing tick `?` is noted for the lead (above).
5. **Concurrent players.** `the_killer_and_two_contributors_each_advance_once`:
   A kills, B and C each wound; A advances at once, B and C once on their ticks;
   the killer has no credit and a second tick advances nothing.
6. **Who counts.** `a_group_member_who_never_hit_or_engaged_gets_no_credit`:
   mere co-presence gives no credit; the `who` set is T240's hitters + engaged
   target + killer, unchanged.
7. **Opening and quest consumers.** `a_contributors_opening_defeat_goal_
   advances_once`: the same `defeated` entry advances opening stage 910007 once
   and does not double on a second tick; a positive non-target definition
   advances neither the quest nor the opening.
8. **Busy/eligibility gates.** No new entry path. `a_player_outside_the_field_
   instance_gets_no_credit` drives the production seam with the outside
   player's (empty) interest set: no damage, no ledger hit, no credit, no tick
   change.
9. **Hub budget.** `src/encounter.rs` net -18 lines; `gameplay.rs`,
   `gateway.rs` and `bin/osfr-gateway.rs` net 0. Spine rules pass.
