## Overview

**Name.** The client calls the zone **Highroad Junction** (56287863, `ugdt` 3154303472, warpstone "Highroad Junction Warpstone" 3280258149). "Jumpin' Junction" does not appear in the client locale, the video records or the wiki, so it is not used. [client] [video `UmmdRi-z-zY/00088`, `MJkBn2nwbVU/00140`]

**Where it is.** The wiki calls it a small town in the Northern Wilds, south of Snowhill, and says new players now start in Cobblestone Village instead (rev 2023-04-15). [wiki Highroad Junction] The client treats it as one of three starter towns: Ashley Lightwings in the Sacred Glade tutorial teleports a new player to Merigold here (1317780521, 1706608391), the others being Samantha (Cobblestone Village) and Valerie (Stillwater Crossing). [client] [wiki Sacred Glade] Roads: Ranger Ricardo's station is "near the road leading to Snowhill" (828381427); Perry's Pastures lie "to the north of Highroad Junction" (895262302, 3734802194) and Frost Ridge Speedway is "to the north" of Raina Rush (4128013034); the PCA Camp is reached by heading "southwest from Highroad Junction" (2236162202); the Roadhouse and Dartmoor Quarry are "west of Highroad Junction" (wiki). [client] [wiki]

**Landmarks the sources name.**
- **Pet Adoption Center and pet park.** "Welcome to the Highroad Junction Pet Adoption Center. Pick out your pet, then take it for a walk or buy it!" (2235627259); Mercy Merrywings and Verill Shiverwings stand near it (670827856, 2472251404, 170901141). Wiki: "Pet Center: Northernmost building in the village center." [client] [wiki Exploration (Collections)]
- **Highroad Haven Inn / Dance Party.** The client names the party at the "Highroad Haven Inn" (138234926, 540793855, 2349307100; "Highroad Haven" 1868746656) and a collection spot "Dance Party" (2169611153); the wiki puts the Dance Party in the "picnic area near Auguste Frydaze". Auguste's cooking table is "behind the large building" (365352132) and "west of Merigold" (540793855). [client] [wiki]
- **Bear Cave.** "Interact with the gate to the Bear Cave east of Harold to start the battle!" (3216721027); wiki: "East along a dirt path from the center of the village". [client] [wiki] Dungeon: [[Hewey's Escape]].
- **Ranger Station.** Ranger Ricardo's post, with a Crate of Fireworks beside him (1924426577). Direction is given three ways (see Conflicts). [client]
- **Mining Practice station.** Beside Zoren Orecrusher: "use the mining practice tools to his left" (2629181298). [client] [video `fktAs_GRrjM/00318`]
- **Spiralmint Patch.** A map warp (name 3427521022 `ucdt`, 4078246999 `ugdt`) described as "right next to Highroad Junction" (1970389585) and "to the north, just outside of Highroad Junction" (2835914802). [client]
- **Highroad Vale.** The wiki says the Vale was where newly created characters were introduced, on a path "diagonally across the intersection from the Highroad Junction warpstone". The client's tutorial quest texts point at a "Battle gate", a "Housing door" and a "Pet Adventures pet house" in Highroad Vale (399259867, 2409582345, 1950344286) and place Alena Goldenheart's trial-pet adoption centre there. [wiki Highroad Vale] [client]
- **Highroad Junction Playground** (client 3307636519; a playground minigame entry seen in `lAqC9DygfEk/00016`). Wiki: added May 2010. No NPC is tied to it. [client] [video] [wiki]

**Starter-town flow.** The client's new-character script strings run in this order: new character validation, click to move, housing (take me there, click to interact, completed), battles (same three steps), pets (same three steps), then "merigold: take me there" and "merigold: completed" (98529078, 3804523519, 1371708138, 1292522208, 1489488524, 3969271301, 3686181865, 44897978, 2478105415, 1490694527, 2413910799, 455794293, 1950654005, 1577998139, 567885175). The three station quests are given by Chatty (video, below). After them the player is sent to Merigold, who points them at Harold (the Brawler line), Auguste (the Chef line), Raina Rush (the Kart Driver line) and the pet trainers. [client] [video `yJ2KHN9I7EI/00014`, `/00018`, `/00800`] [inferred for the order of the later hand-offs]

## Notes on important NPCs

- **Merigold, Chatty and Reggie Skylight** make up the greeter side. The dev strings show the Highroad Vale script (housing, battles, pets) ending in "merigold: take me there". After that the town's three trainers (Harold, Auguste, Raina) and the pet trainers are reached by pointer text, and the Chef, Brawler and Kart Driver hand-backs all say "head back to Merigold" (3404995492, 3562100712, 2622336658). Reading Merigold as the hub and Harold, Auguste and Raina as three parallel starts is my inference [inferred]; no source states the order.
- **Harold and Assistant Archy** run the Brawler start. In game the player talks to Archy, enters the Bear Cave gate east of Harold, knocks out Hewey, and is paid at Harold's wagon. Harold also teaches the job and coaches inside the cave; the wiki gives Harold the quest, the client and video give Archy. See [[Hewey's Escape]] and [[Brawler]].
- **Auguste Frydaze** starts a four-quest [[Chef]] series that uses Ranger Ricardo and ends at Perry's Pastures. Ricardo's Fireworks Frenzy is a collect-in-time minigame around the town. The "Highroad Haven Inn" party gives Auguste's repeating "rush order" text.
- **Mercy Merrywings and Verill Shiverwings** are the two Pet Trainer stops in this town: Mercy gives the three tutorials, Verill gives the level 15 to 20 tricks. Both stand around the Pet Adoption Center and pet park. Alena Goldenheart's trial-pet chain sits at a "Highroad Vale" adoption centre, which may be the same building or an earlier one.
- **Raina Rush** is the Kart Driver link: a pen hunt that sends the player to Snowhill's Frost Ridge Speedway and back, plus the Introduce Yourself quest.
- **Zoren Orecrusher** is a practice-station trainer for [[Miner]] who sends the player north.
- **Raven** and the Bugawug Queen are the only boss content here besides Hewey. The client says the boss fight is a group-of-20 world battle reached through a secret passage south of town.

## Conflicts

1. **Zone name.** The task note mentions "Jumpin' Junction". No client string, video record or wiki page uses it; the client and wiki use "Highroad Junction". Client used.
2. **Who gives Brawler: Hewey's Escape!** Wiki: Harold. Client text and video tracker: Assistant Archy (1605633744, `aveqYa4AVk8/00000`). Client used; Harold is the hand-in and trainer.
3. **Verill / Veril and Mercy Merrywing(s).** The wiki Highroad Junction page writes "Veril Shiverwings"; the Pet Trainer wiki page writes both "Verill" and "Veril", and "Mercy Merrywing" once. The client has "Verill Shiverwings" and "Mercy Merrywings". Client used.
4. **Ranger Station direction.** Auguste's text says "to the north" (2134813413), another line says "to the east" (418841157), and a third says "near the road leading to Snowhill" (828381427). All could describe the same station from different spots; unresolved.
5. **Highroad Hijinx is not in this town.** The client says the hooligans are "on the road west of Highroad Junction" (3240677); the wiki puts the entrance at a fence in the south west Wilds near Farnum's Farm, with location "Southern Wilds". The dungeon is left to [[Highroad Hijinx]] and the Wilds page.
6. **Highroad Vale against Highroad Junction.** The client uses both names: the station quests and Alena Goldenheart use "Highroad Vale"; Mercy, Verill and the adoption-centre greeting (2235627259) use "Highroad Junction". The wiki lists the Vale as a place under Highroad Junction. Whether Alena's centre and Mercy's centre are one building is unknown.
7. **Raven's disguise and place.** Wiki: Raven posed as the "Bixie Queen" and is fought "in the old tutorial area". Client: the disguise was the "Bugawug Queen" (1844096292, 4226350966) and the fight is in a grove south of the town. Client used for the names; the place agreement with the Vale is not proven.
8. **Starter town.** The wiki says new players now start in Cobblestone Village. The client and video still run the Highroad start (Merigold, Hewey's Escape, Highroad Cooking), so the Highroad flow existed in the recorded build. [[Hewey's Escape]] records the same point.
9. **Hewey's Escape availability.** The wiki says "removed" and "no longer in-game"; the client and video show it working. See the dungeon page.
10. **Raina against "Raine".** Video OCR reads "Return to Raine in Highroad Junction" (`fktAs_GRrjM/00308`) and "Raina Ruth" (`fktAs_GRrjM/00336`). The client strings for this hand-in read "Return to Raina in Highroad Junction" (1246157085) and "Return to Raina Rush in Highroad Junction" (873401195). "Raine" in the client is a different NPC with a weapon-testing dummy (487581788); not merged. Client used.
11. **Goldwupp / Goldwrupp.** Video reads "Coin Farmer Goldwrupp"; the client name is "Coin Farmer Goldwupp" (1647494841). Client used.
12. **Battles tutorial text.** The video quest log says the Battle gate is in Highroad Vale (399259867), but the dialog in the same recording says "face the king of the ring" (3292324407), which is the Cobblestone Village fight against Mongo. The client may share one dialog line across starter towns; the Highroad Vale gate's battle is not named.
13. **Sandstorm riddle numbers.** A Highroad Junction warpstone frame (`MJkBn2nwbVU/00140`) carries a "Goal Complete: Talk to the person in Riddle #3" line, but an earlier frame shows Pablo Gable completing Riddle #3 (`MJkBn2nwbVU/00124`) and Raina's frames sit beside Riddle #1 (`/00168`, `/00172`). The line is probably a chat-log echo. Raina's riddle number is not stated.
14. **Bowawow availability.** The wiki marks him "No longer in-game" (rev 2026-05-31); the client still has a gift pick-up objective for him. Kept as a Highroad NPC.

## Gaps

- **Quest givers by inference.** Merigold's quests, Zoren's quest, Auguste's first three quests and all of Alena's quests are linked from wording only. The title that goes with Chatty's Housing and Pets descriptions is not proven.
- **Title text.** Mercy's town introduction, the Bowawow gift, the Bugawug Queen objective, Cillian's quest and the pirate treasure chapters have text but no title.
- **Positions.** The sources give only phrases ("west of Merigold", "behind the large building", "east of Harold"). There are no coordinates. Video distance markers exist but the player's position is unknown.
- **Vendor stock.** Gaston's prices, Bowawow's stock, Cillian's clothing list and the potion and collar shops have no data. The adoption-centre greeter (2235627259) is unnamed.
- **Video coverage.** Only two long recordings cover the town (`fktAs_GRrjM`, `yJ2KHN9I7EI`), plus short appearances. There is no frame of Auguste's dialog, Mercy, Verill, Zoren's offer, Alena or Gaston with a zone banner, and none of a shop panel.

- **Raven's quest.** Which quest sends the player to Raven's secret passage, and whether the Raven battles named "Raven Unleashed" and "Raven, Lunatic" are in this zone, is unknown. The wiki says the Hero Rises line; the client titles were not matched.
