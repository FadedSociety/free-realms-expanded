## Conflicts

1. **Zone.** The wiki text says Forest Troll Village (north end of the forest) while the infobox says "Blackspore Swamp" and the category is Wilds. The index uses Blackspore Swamp. The Forest Troll Village is in the northern Wilds per the client and the [[Forest Troll Fort]] page, so Wilds is used here.
2. **Enemy names.** Wiki plurals "Troll Bone Shamans" and "Troll Initiates"; the client has the singular forms above.
3. **Wheel.** Only one of six wheels is on the wiki page; the weapon list for the others comes from item pages and may be partial.
