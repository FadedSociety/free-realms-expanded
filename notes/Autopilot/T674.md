## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client can send a forged 49/2 accept (wrong job, before Tucker's Troubles, twice), a 49/14 before the goals, a
repeated 49/14, a Talk to Kanan before the derby, or a mid-match quit. The derby finish comes only from the server's Results
phase (a cancelled match credits nothing), Kanan's Talk is ordered after it, the turn-in needs the server-held
`pending_turnin`, and the payout is data-read and paid once. The tests assert byte-equal saves for every one of those refusals.
Free grants: coins and job XP only, once; nothing sellable.
