## Conflicts

1. **Spinnerette is not Arachnia.** The client has two spider queens: Spinnerette (south Bristlewood) and her sister Arachnia (this dungeon). Some text with "Spider Queen" and "Necrosis" (`loc 186544471`, `2252878950`) belongs to another storyline and is not used here.
2. **"Cave Queen".** One Spider Web Spit ability is tagged "Cave Queen", which is not the same name as Arachnia.
3. **Wiki mob names.** The wiki lists four enemy types. The client also has "Baby Black Widow", which the wiki does not list.
