## Client evidence (IDA, read-only `ida_query.py`)

- `decompile 0x008e5940`: the 36-byte body is a 2-byte value (the standalone
  opcode 125 the encoder writes), u64 actor, three floats position with W
  written 1.0 at +28, three floats direction with W written 0 at +44, u8 state,
  u8 unknown. There is no subtype between the opcode and the GUID.
- `decompile 0x0095dae0`: masks the direction with `[1,0,1,0]`, requires its
  horizontal squared length above `0.010000001` before normalizing and deriving
  `atan2(X, Z)`. Below that threshold the client ignores the facing, which is
  why the notice direction must keep its sub-centimetre magnitude.
- No wire byte, opcode or field changed; all reply bytes come from the unchanged
  `movement()`, `remove()`, `spawn()`, `enemy_hp()` and `relevance()` encoders.

## Security note (secure-code-guardian)

Attack surface: the field AI consumes player positions (untrusted, validated by
`tick_encounter` before this code) and the reloads read an operator content
file. The restored notice divisor is clamped at 1 cm, so no client position can
divide by zero or produce a non-finite direction; `heading` retains its 1 mm
guard; `advance`'s caller guards `left > 0.001`. No new parsing, `?`, panic,
per-player state or allocation enters the shared tick. The reloads are
developer/role actions: both refuse a shared-field world and an active
encounter, the file is bounded at 128 KiB and schema/behaviour-checked on a
staged clone, and the per-GUID cleanup now provably runs only after the load
succeeds. No client id or quantity reaches them, no save/coin/item path changes,
and no new selector is added.
