Seven tests:

1. `every_shipped_field_battle_loads_with_a_unique_drawable_marker`: per-row
   production-loader probe (the row id is in every failure), then the whole
   catalogue in `Gameplay::load` order; cross-catalogue GUID uniqueness;
   `battle_spawners` equals the authored marker; the portal row carries the
   authored difficulty and level gate; the open-world set (NPC content minus
   field enemies plus `add_static_actor`) draws exactly one actor per marker at
   the authored position; the 35/2 AddNpc carries the authored model, name id
   and nameplate image; the arena zone is not the open world.
2. `every_battle_has_a_valid_non_empty_roster`: every row begins through
   `EncounterEngine::begin` (level 20) and the arena state keeps the roster
   size/order/GUIDs/positions, marks exactly the summon-threshold actors
   dormant, scales difficulty 1 at x1 and higher difficulties by one uniform
   factor; every enemy GUID is catalogue-unique and aliases no field enemy or
   open-world NPC.
3. `every_battle_prize_and_coin_reward_resolves`: every prize id resolves in
   the merged runtime catalogue and the production preflight accepts the
   previewed bundle; the portal Play on a full bag is refused with the
   unavailable-prize line and a byte-equal snapshot.
4. `every_battle_is_published_or_documented_guide_only`: the emitted 167/1/1
   list (decoded with the fixture's reader) contains every shipped row with its
   authored title/description/difficulty, and `Catalogue::admit` accepts each
   row for a qualified combat job. A row neither published nor in `GUIDE_ONLY`
   fails with its id.
5. `one_marker_battle_and_one_guide_battle_start_win_pay_once_and_return`: two
   independent in-process gateways (Sheep Watch 12350 at its marker, Troll
   Summoner Madness 96 at its start screen). Both start through the real
   41/114 -> 41/108 path, win through server-observed 36/10 damage, pay the
   row's coins + exactly one previewed prize + 50 job XP once, return to the
   pre-entry zone/position/rotation on 26/42, and a replayed Close/Go/attack
   changes nothing. Winning the first battle changes nothing in the second
   player's wallet, bag, XP or live encounter.
6. `a_malformed_battle_row_is_refused_atomically`: a scratch copy (in
   `CARGO_TARGET_TMPDIR`) of the shipped file is mutated in turn (duplicate
   GUID, empty roster, difficulty 6, unknown prize id, zero title) and
   `reload_field_battles` proves the loader refuses without replacing the
   loaded battles, encounter state or prize preflight. The unknown prize is
   refused by the production preflight (the loader owns no item catalogue) and
   the zero title by the audit's own rule. The scratch copy is restored
   byte-identically and the shipped file's SHA-256 is re-checked.
7. `the_audit_uses_the_shipped_content_and_no_dev_commands`: prints the row
   count and the shipped file's SHA-256 (`11 rows, sha256 fb93c7b5...d3ed` at
   this tip), proves the loader yields exactly those rows, and asserts the
   route's packet builders are ordinary client messages (none is the 15/x
   local-chat family that carries a realm order or developer teleport).

## Findings that contradict the brief (code wins)

- **Battle 96 is portal-published.** `activity_portal::Catalogue::load` turns
  every `battles[]` row into a 167 row, and
  `tests/activity_portal.rs::shipped_battle_content_publishes_every_battle`
  already asserts 96 is listed. All 11 shipped rows are published, so
  `GUIDE_ONLY` is empty. The table is kept (the brief requires it) and the test
  asserts an entry can never name a row that is in fact published, so it cannot
  hide a broken row.
- **One spawner legitimately reuses an open-world NPC GUID.** Battle 12303's
  marker `100000004750` is the same Stone Heart's Hideaway entrance as
  `npc_spawns_extra.json` spawn `100000004750` (legacy NPC 4750, same position,
  model 2056, name 426971). `Gameplay::load` adds every spawner through
  `NpcVisibility::add_static_actor`, whose `retain` removes the NPC with the
  same GUID (`src/world.rs` `add_static_actor`), so exactly one actor is drawn.
  The audit keeps the strict rule with a documented `MARKER_REPLACEMENTS` table
  and asserts the replaced NPC stands at the authored marker position; any other
  collision fails.
- **Zero title / required level / coin cap / empty prize wheel are not loader
  rules.** `load_field_battles` (`src/encounter/field.rs`) bounds difficulty
  1..=5, 1..=16 enemies, at most 4 prizes with positive ids and quantity
  1..=`MAX_PRIZE_QUANTITY`, actor bounds and GUID uniqueness, but not
  `title_id`/`description_id`/`objective_id`, `reward_coins`,
  `required_combat_level` or a non-empty wheel, although the dungeon loader
  (`EncounterEngine::load`, `src/encounter.rs:961`) checks the 1,000,000 coin
  bound. The loader is unchanged (brief: test-only); the audit enforces those
  content rules and names the row/field. Moving them into the loader so a bad
  row fails at startup is a lead decision. Of the brief's five mutations, three
  are loader refusals (duplicate GUID, empty roster, difficulty), one is the
  prize preflight (unknown prize) and one is audit-only (zero title).
- **Battle 96 cannot be won with the shared `fight_to_win`.** The Troll Summoner
  is shielded while a summoned minion stands (`src/encounter/skills.rs`
  `shielded`; battle 96's 75/50/25 summon thresholds), and `fight_to_win`
  always attacks the first living actor (the boss). The route test uses a local
  `fight_summoner` loop: the same fixture `walk`/`melee`/`send` calls, targeting
  standing summoned minions first and the summoner second. This is the real
  encounter rule, not a change to the production helper.

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

The audit adds no parser and no trust in client input. It drives the existing
surface with hostile-shaped values and asserts refusal semantics:

- A hostile client can send a forged 41/108 Go, a forged 167/1/2 Play id, a
  36/10 attack naming any GUID, or a 26/42 Close at any time. Production
  refuses these: Go re-checks the pending screen and the marker radius, the
  portal only admits a listed id and re-runs the prize/level/busy preflight,
  attacks only hit a living, non-dormant, in-range actor of the active
  encounter, and a Close with no finished run is inert (test 5 replays Close,
  Go and attack; `field_battle_roster` and T319 own the other negatives).
- Prize ids/quantities are checked against the merged catalogue rather than
  trusting the preview: the full-bag Play and unknown-prize cases are refused
  before the encounter, bag, coins or save can change (snapshot equality). The
  win is server-observed through 36/10, never client-reported.

## (R) rules used

- Enemy `max_health`/`damage` are pre-scale authored values; the loader scales
  them by the difficulty's level factor (R in `load_field_battles`; difficulty
  1 = x1). The audit asserts the x1 identity and one uniform factor above 1,
  not a hard-coded factor.
- Battle 96's summon thresholds 75/50/25, its skittish summoner and the
  summoner shield are local reconstructions (T243/T319, `Battle Template`); the
  audit reads the rows and the existing runtime rule.
- `required_combat_level <= 20` comes from the code cap (`Profiles::rank`
  `.min(20)`, `set_player_job` `clamp(1, 20)`).
- Audit-only content rules (not loader rules): positive text ids that resolve
  in the client locale, a non-empty prize wheel, `reward_coins <= 1,000,000`
  (the dungeon loader's bound), `summon_below_percent` in 1..=99 and a summoned
  minion is neither boss nor skittish, at least one non-summoned enemy.
- The portal publishes every battle row; the 167 list is the client's only way
  to ask for one (policy documented in `src/activity_portal.rs`).
