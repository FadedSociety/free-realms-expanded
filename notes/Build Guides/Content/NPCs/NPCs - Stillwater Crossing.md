## Overview

**Name.** The client calls the zone **Stillwater Crossing** (title 626542722; `ugdt` 1682986521; warpstone "Stillwater Crossing Warpstone" `ucdt` 3455611328, `ugdt` 2382130703). Its welcome window reads "Welcome to Stillwater Crossing! If you're looking for fun and adventure, you've come to the right place!" (3453817544). [client] Collection names: "Stillwater Crossing Newbie" (3011311369) and "Stillwater Party" (1496518601). [client] The video archive has one frame of the warpstone discovery notice (`-itStdznbPo/00036`). [video]

**Where it is.** The wiki puts it "southeast of Sanctuary, northeast of Blackspore", a mainly fishing town like Lakeshore, which lies directly north of it. [wiki Stillwater Crossing] The client calls it one of three starter towns: a new player is teleported to Valerie "at Stillwater Crossing in Sacred Grove" (570656378, 2389679865, 4288777475); the other two are Highroad Junction (Merigold) and Cobblestone Village (Samantha). [client] [wiki Sacred Glade] The wiki says Blackspore is entered by tunnels "near Crossroads or Stillwater Crossing" and puts the unreleased Black Forest "east of Stillwater Crossing". [wiki Blackspore Swamp, Black Forest] Client directions seen in the quest text: Caitlyn Gravefog is "to the north" (3927973780, 2504663190); the Cursed Graveyard gate is "north west of Caitlyn Gravefog" (357349257) and the graveyard is "north west of Stillwater Crossing" (1241556474); Gavin Greenpetal is on "the road heading south out of Stillwater Crossing" (2661208112); Seth McGrobie went "southeast" (3422963869); Queensfields is "southwest of Stillwater Crossing" (997589004); Helena's cooking table is "east of Valerie" (289368182); Francis Panic stands by his kart "around the corner to the north east" (3439454587); Thunder Falls Speedway is "to the north" of Francis (1232089011). [client]

**Landmarks the sources name.**
- **Stillwater Inn.** Helena's cooking table: "Business at Stillwater Inn has never been better!" (2532624881). [client]
- **Fishing job center.** Reed Stillwater's post, with the boat to Sacred Grove Shallows "behind" him (2989176545, 3475562700); a client line calls Reed's post "the fishing job center" (690281943, 845805624). [client]
- **Pet Adoption Center.** "Zachery is near the Pet Adoption Center in Stillwater Crossing." (1114196202) Wiki: "Pet Center: South side of the town center." [client] [wiki Exploration (Collections)]
- **Cursed Graveyard.** A combat gate north-west of Caitlyn: [[Cursed Graveyard]]. Wiki route: "Take the north-west path and the right fork and follow the road past Gravedigger Dustin and Caitlyn Gravefog." [client] [wiki Exploration (Collections)]
- **Stillwater Party.** A collection spot: "Follow the northeast road before turning fully east along a path, past a group of benches until you reaching the party." No NPC is named for it. [wiki Exploration (Collections)] [client name 1496518601]
- **Sacred Grove Shallows.** A fishing area entered by boat from the town. The wiki files it under the Stillwater Crossing category and lists it as the town's fishing resource. Its NPCs are in [[#Part E. Sacred Grove Shallows]]. [client] [wiki Stillwater Crossing, Sacred Grove Shallows]
- **Warpstone.** Merchants Glenda and Kimble stand near it; Ingrid and Newman stand near the town's Cooking Table (all wiki). [wiki Merchants]
- **Shops without NPCs.** The wiki lists Pet Collars, Pet Supplies and Potions (health, energy, replenishing) for the town and names no NPC. It also lists Checkers & Chess as a resource, with no opponent named. [wiki Stillwater Crossing]

**Starter-town flow (inferred from the client).** Ashley Lightwings in the Sacred Glade tutorial teleports a new player to Valerie. Valerie starts the town's "Introduce Yourself" quest, which sends the player to Francis Panic; his kart-power errand then points at Thunder Falls Speedway. Trainers' hand-back lines say "Valerie was looking for you ... she has more quests for you" (2585187751, 4277860925, 2505291937). Helena, Caitlyn and Zachery are three parallel job starts (Chef, Brawler, Pet Trainer); Reed Stillwater starts Fisherman. The order is [inferred]; no source states it. [client] [wiki Quest:Introduce Yourself, Sacred Glade]

**How to read the tables.** One row per NPC. A client name entry proves the name exists, not where the NPC stands; standing place comes from a client sentence, a video frame or the wiki, and the row says which. Quest links go to the job page that holds the full row; quests with no job page are in [[#Quest index]]. Player names, player chat and player-chosen pet or lot names are never recorded. The video archive holds no frame of any town NPC with a zone banner; the only video evidence for this zone is the Shallows fishing recording, the Cursed Graveyard fight and a Brawler quest tracker (see Gaps).

## Notes on important NPCs

- **Valerie and Francis Panic** are the town's own start. Valerie is the greeter that the Sacred Glade tutorial teleports a new player to; her "Introduce Yourself" quest leads to Francis Panic, and Francis's kart-power errand ("Power Problems") sends the player to Laurin at Thunder Falls Speedway, who in turn points at the Kart and Derby trainers there. [client] [wiki Quest:Introduce Yourself]
- **Caitlyn Gravefog** is the Brawler start. She sends the player into the [[Cursed Graveyard]] to kill the wraith, then sends them after Seth McGrobie, who sold her the cursed headstone. Seth's camp is outside town to the south-east, with Robgoblin Pondblasters east of it; the line finishes at Virgil Veilmont in Blackspore and goes on to Petra. [client] [wiki Brawler] [video]
- **Fernando Flexsteel** gives the four repeatable Brawler Contracts, one for each level gate (5, 10, 15, 20) in the wiki order; his place is a wiki claim only. [wiki Brawler] [client]
- **Helena and Gavin Greenpetal** run the Chef start: Helena teaches Spiralmint Steak at the inn's Cooking Table, then sends the player down the south road for Gavin, who needs help picking up scattered ingredients and then points to Honey at Queensfields. [client] [wiki Chef]
- **Reed Stillwater** is the only Fisherman trainer the client names for the town; he owns the boat to the Shallows, identifies odd catches and sends anglers on to Brambleback's Bayou. The Shallows NPCs (Anna, Muggup, Jonah, Guppie, Vick, Callie, Melanie, Dwight, Derek) give the beginner and bait quests. [client] [wiki Fisherman]
- **Zachery and Vince** are the Pet Trainer start: Vince is the object the player clicks to begin each tutorial; Zachery completes it. [client]
- **Kanan** is a Stillwater sponsor for the Demolition Derby line; the sponsorship fights happen at Thunder Falls and Frost Ridge, and the charity flier run is back in town. [client]
- **Merchants** (Glenda, Kimble, Ingrid, Newman) are wiki-era gear shops added August to October 2013. [wiki]
