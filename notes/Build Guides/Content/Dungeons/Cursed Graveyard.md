## Conflicts

1. **Brawler Gloves name.** The wiki wheel says "Bell Brawler Gloves". The client item is "Saved by the Bell Brawler Gloves" (`loc 3699014784`, `3683936112`). The client name is used above.
2. **Zone.** The wiki file puts the dungeon in Stillwater Crossing. One video map frame has the Sanctuary label under the cursor, but it is a hover area, so the client and wiki location stands.
3. **Achievements.** The index says the wiki has no trophy for this dungeon, and no trophy strings were found in the client. The other difficulty 1 dungeons all have them.
