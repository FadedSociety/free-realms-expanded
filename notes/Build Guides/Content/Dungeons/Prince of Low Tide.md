# Prince of Low Tide

Part of [[Content]] › [[Dungeons]]. Tags: **[client]** = game locale text (`loc <id>`), **[video]** = player recordings (frame key), **[wiki]** = fan wiki. Precedence client > video > wiki.

> Prince of Low Tide is a **wandering boss battle**, not an Atlas dungeon. The wiki files it under *Battle*, *Special Spawn* and *Seaside*. The player clicks the Prince; after a battle is accepted he "disappears for an unknown duration of time". [wiki]

## Conflicts

1. **Coin figures.** The wiki page text shows blank coins; the figures come from the page's infobox template and are read by order. Unproven.
2. **Spelling.** The client uses "Prince of Low Tides" in some quest text (`loc 932872288`) and "Prince of Low Tide" in names. The name form is used. The client text "toursits" is a client typo and is not reproduced.
3. **Servants.** The wiki lists no minions. The video shows Cray Royal Servants, but only in the Cook-off Catastrophe run.
