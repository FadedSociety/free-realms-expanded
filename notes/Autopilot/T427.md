# T427 — quest pin for dungeon and battle objectives, re-synced after movement and kill credit

Attempt 2. Attempt 1 (`lab/autopilot/patches/T427-opus.patch`, base `56db232`) was READY but no
longer applied on tip `598531a`: T410 had moved `load_field_enemies` and the battle loaders out of
`encounter/field.rs` into `encounter/field_content.rs`. This attempt applied that patch, ported its
one rejected hunk (the `field_sites` fill in `load_field_enemies`) by hand, and, as the brief asks
once T410 has landed, put the pin lookups in `encounter/field_content.rs`. Everything else is
attempt 1's diff unchanged. All gates and canaries were run again in this worktree and build dir
(`lab/build/ap-slot11`); the canaries invalidated by the deleted shared crate (lead note) were

## What changed

- `src/quest_journal.rs`: `Places` gains `battle` (battle id -> spawner guid and position)
  and `battle_enemy` (actor kind -> marker of the lowest-id battle that fields it, (R)).
  `locate` pins `CompleteDungeon` at the battle marker (zone 0) and lets `KillActorKind`
  fall back to `battle_enemy` when no field enemy of the kind exists. A field enemy still
  wins. Unknown ids stay `None`; gather, minigame, item, craft, job and client-reported
  steps still get no pin.
- `src/encounter/field_content.rs` (T410's content module): `battle_marker(id)` and
  `battle_marker_for_enemy(def)`, read-only over `self.battles` (the rows `battle_spawners`
  reads; no new battle state). `field_enemy_site` moved here from `field.rs` beside them, so
  the three pin lookups sit next to the loader that fills their data.
- **`field_enemy_site` now reads the loaded field rows (`Content::field_sites`, filled in
  `load_field_enemies`) instead of the live `field_sim`.** The brief does not ask for this, but its
  "field enemy wins" rule depends on it. Every production session hands its live field rows to
  the shared field at login (`osfr-gateway.rs:956` `load_gateway_from`, `:1027`
  `Session::new`, `:3396` the world seed; `encounter/shared.rs:296` `take_shared_fields`
  `mem::take`s `field_sim.enemies`). So the old lookup returned `None` in every production
  session. Kill pins (W161) worked only in tests that never share the field. With the new
  fallback, a 930050 kill quest (3030007) would have pinned Sheep Watch (battle 12350) instead of
  the field home. The index lives in the `Arc<Content>` the engine already shares, so clones stay
  cheap; `src/encounter.rs` gains the one `#[serde(skip)]` field. The rule is unchanged: the
  lowest guid of the kind, present or not, at its home position.
- `src/gameplay.rs` (hub, +9 net): `sync_tracked_target` passes both closures, and the pin
  re-syncs:
  - on both non-gated returns of `on_location` (not the `owned_movement` early return),
  - on `tick_world`'s plain world path after `apply_encounter` and `refresh_power_up_slot`
    (not soccer, derby, kart, movie or the kart claim),
  - in `handle_shared_field`, for the killer's own lethal-hit credit. The gateway runs it after
    `handle_in_world` has already synced (`osfr-gateway.rs:1718`, inside `commit_outcome`),
  - in `tick_shared_effects`, for a contributor's T240/T302 kill credit
    (`osfr-gateway.rs:3109`; staged gateways are always committed, `:3152`).

  The last two go beyond the brief's list. The brief named `tick_world`'s `sync_field_quests` as
  the place where shared-field kills are credited. That function only gates quest enemies
  (`gameplay.rs:2147`). The real credit lands in `handle_shared_field` and
  `tick_shared_effects`. `Tracker::update` skips an unchanged pin, so every call is
  unconditional and idle calls send nothing.
- `src/gameplay/w64_regional_quest_tests.rs`: `an_objective_without_a_known_place_sends_no_pin`
  used 3110002, which now correctly pins battle 96. It moves to 3000016, which starts with a
  gather step (the quest is ordered). The new `a_battle_only_kill_objective_pins_its_battle_marker`
  pins 3110002 at 100000096000, (-440, 0.8, 1290), zone 0.
- `notes/Design/Build Ledger.md:90`: the stale "47/14 tracked-target pin not built" is
  replaced with W161 plus this slice (native check pending).

## (R) rules

- `battle_marker_for_enemy`: a kind that fights only in battles pins the **lowest-id**
  battle whose spawner or roster has that definition id. No shipped kind appears in two
  battles today (checked all 11 rows of `field_battles.json`).
  `the_battle_kind_fallback_takes_the_lowest_battle_id` pins the tie-break (lowest id, not file
  order) on a synthetic second battle.
- The pin guid for a battle step is the spawner's guid. Client `0x00a8e660` uses the guid only to
  highlight an existing minimap indicator with that guid; if none exists it clears the highlight
  (`0x00a88280`). The waypoint, distance and route come from the position, so a guid with no
  indicator is harmless.

## Threat note

No new client input is parsed. The pin reflects server state only: the selected quest
(47/12 already refuses ids the character does not own, W145), the server's objective
counts, and content positions. A hostile client cannot choose the pinned guid or position.
Another player's kill reaches my pin only through the server's T240/T302 credit list. The pin is
per-session and never saved.

## Checklist

1. Refusal changes nothing: there is no new refusal path. The pin is computed from committed
   state after each handler. The gateway's staged clones (world tick, shared tick, shared-field
   request) carry the tracker with the outcome they commit or drop.
2. Rollback covers all state: the tracker lives on `Gameplay`, which is cloned with every staged
   gateway. `field_sites` is load-time content in the shared `Arc`. Nothing changes it at run time
   except the staged field reload (`EncounterEngine::staged`), which rolls back with the engine.
3. Deltas, not snapshots: not applicable (no inventory, coin or position writes).
4. Busy rules: not applicable (no activity added).
5. Every entry path: the pin re-syncs on every path that can change quest state. Existing:
   in-world packets, login and zone ready. New: movement, the killer's shared-field request,
   the shared tick and the world tick. Tested by tests 1-4.
6. Reachable in production, tested through it: the tests use the NPC menu, 47/12, `on_location`,
   `handle_shared_field`, `tick_shared_effects`, `tick`, the marker/Go/Close path and zone
   ready. Test 4 reproduces the production shared-field handoff.
7. Survives reconnect: the tracker is deliberately not saved. A new session resends the pin on
   login (existing `a_new_session_sends_the_pin_again`). Nothing new to persist.
8. Exit paths: the arena entry clears the pin and the return re-pins it (test 2).
9. Economy: not applicable (no rewards).
10. Wire format: the 47/14 layout is unchanged. The tests compare whole packets built from the
    client body (`0x00a88080`/`0x00a8fab0`, re-decompiled this run).
11. Unique ids: the pin guid is the battle's existing spawner guid; no ids are created.
12. Others see changes: not applicable (the pin is private to the session).
13. Content end to end: shipped 3000061/12350, 3030005/12400, 3000029, 3110002/96 and 3000009
    resolve to real markers, NPCs and field homes in the tests.
14. Values in data: positions and ids come from `field_battles.json`/`field_enemies.json`;
    no new tunable.
15. Canaries break production code: A-E edit `field_content.rs`, `quest_journal.rs` and
    `gameplay.rs`, not the tests.
16. Session plumbing: untouched.
17. One player's error: `sync_tracked_target` cannot fail (no `?`, no arithmetic). A missing
    engine or place yields no pin.
18. Rules on the type: the `Places` fields and `Content::field_sites` carry doc comments naming
    their rule; `Tracker` keeps its send rules.
19. Hot path cost: one `sync_tracked_target` per world tick and per shared tick. With no selected
    quest it allocates nothing. With one, `current_objectives` builds a one-element Vec (the split
    quests are ordered) and `locate` scans at most 64 field sites or the battle rows (11 battles,
    rosters of at most 16). No state clone.
