   `check_bundle.py` already passed `--resources` to the gateway at the tip, so no change there.

   `PlayerRegistry::update_pose` (same position, `instance_id + 1`). It gets no 35/39; a viewer in the player's instance
   gets exactly one.
4. **Finding 5, own refusal for "no look yet".** `handle_style_card` now separates "no tables" ("Style cards are not
   available on this server.") from "no identity applied and no saved look" ("Your character's look is not ready yet.").
   Both refuse before any change.
