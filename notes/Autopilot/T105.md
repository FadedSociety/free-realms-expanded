# T105: authored interaction and dungeon-completion objectives

## Changed

- `src/content.rs`: added source-bearing `interact_actor` (`id`, positive `actor_guid`) and
  `complete_dungeon` (`id`, positive `encounter_id`) `ContentGoal` variants. Flat content
  validation rejects zero targets.
- `src/quest_engine.rs`: maps the new goals to the existing `ObjectiveKind::InteractNpc` and
  `ObjectiveKind::CompleteDungeon`, each requiring one authoritative event. Split content
  loading rejects zero targets and empty provenance.
- `src/gameplay.rs`: emits `QuestEvent::InteractNpc` only after an accepted regional menu
  interaction or opening-object interaction; Talk remains a separate 26/10 action. Emits
  `QuestEvent::CompleteDungeon` only from `EncounterOutcome::completed`, which is populated by
  a won encounter at terminal exit, not by entry, loss, retry, duplicate results, or a client
  completion claim.
- `tests/quest_content_goals.rs`: synthetic split-content fixture against the preserved opening
  package. It exercises the real NPC menu and encounter packets, including loss, terminal win,
  duplicate interaction/result, forged 45/7 claim, wrong actor, wrong zone, out-of-range, and
  malformed ID/provenance refusals.
- `scripts/content_check.py`: recognizes the two new quest objective kinds and validates their
  GUID/encounter targets.

All added behavior is marked `(R)` reconstructed policy. No shipped quests were enabled or edited.

## Open gaps

- No queued regional quest content is authored against these goals yet.
- The historical server's exact interaction-vs-menu acceptance rule remains unrecovered; this
  implementation accepts a range- and content-validated 26/8 interaction as the event and keeps
  Talk on its separate action.

  registry does not require every GUID to appear in a regional NPC file.
