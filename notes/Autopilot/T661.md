  Five wireable rows were already live on the tip: `3000022`, `3000059`, `3000060`, `3030007`, `3050011`
  (and their dialog ids are already bound on the records: intro/description/end where the answer names one).
  Their answers carry no new id the records lack, so they are untouched.
- Of the 15 wireable rows that really are disabled, only `3060024` has all of: a complete objective spec, a
  mechanic the server runs, no disabled predecessor and a drawn giver.
