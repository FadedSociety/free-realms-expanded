## Conflicts

1. **Level gate versus video.** The wiki and client tier strings require a level 9 combat job. A Medic shown at level 8 is in a battle frame with the same goal text. The HUD reading may be wrong, or the gate may differ. Unresolved; client tier strings are generic and not tied to this battle.
2. **Goal text in video.** One frame reads "...stealthy ninja." (no final s); the client string is "...stealthy ninjas." (`loc 2099651034`). Client used.
3. **Client typo.** `loc 686974377` spells "Despoliers". Other client text uses "Despoilers".
4. No wiki item name lacked a client match, so no item corrections apply.
