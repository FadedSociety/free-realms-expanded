## Overview

"The Wilds" is everything in Sacred Grove that is not a town. [wiki Wilds] The wiki splits it into three sectors: the **Northern Wilds** (between Wugachug, Snowhill and Shrouded Glade), the **Central Wilds** (between Merry Vale, Sanctuary and Briarwood) and the **Southern Wilds** (between Seaside, Merry Vale, Lavender Coast and Blackspore Swamp). [wiki Wilds] The wiki then lists places per sector:

- Northern: the Roadhouse, Dartmoor Quarry, Pixie Nursery, PCA Camp, Robgoblin Camp, Highroad Junction, Frost Ridge Speedway, plus areas south of Snowhill, Shrouded Glade and Wugachug. [wiki Northern Wilds]
- Central: Lakeshore, Forest Troll Village, Thunder Falls Speedway, Stillwater Crossing, and the land around Sanctuary. [wiki Central Wilds]
- Southern: Cobblestone Village, Wildwood Speedway, Farnum's Farm, Lavender Coast Pass, Southvale Quarry, Greenwood Camp and Forest, Ambrose's Dig Site. [wiki Southern Wilds]

The client uses the name loosely: "The city of Sanctuary borders on an undeveloped area known as The Wilds. The Southern Wilds are home to a wide variety of groups including the Queensfields Bixies, Greenwood Archers, Shadowtalon Ninjas, and Fairy Cultists." (3629363041). It also has "North Wilds" for the Sidekicks soccer camp (781135410, 3612474566), "Win a Duel in North or South Wilds" (36598439) and a video zone label **South Sacred Grove** that sits on the Wildwood Speedway frames (`FkCPRbskFVU/00008`). [client] [video]

**What this page covers.** The sub-areas the sources group under the Wilds that have no page of their own in [[NPCs]]: the Roadhouse and Tavern Cellar, Dartmoor Quarry, the Pixie Nursery, the Robgoblin Camp and Treasure Trove, the PCA Camp, Forest Troll Village and Fort, the three speedways, and the wilderness NPCs the job pages send the player to. **What it leaves out**, because [[NPCs]] already has or plans a page: Greenwood Forest (Camp Chatty, Camp Robbie, Greenwood Camp, Bandit Hideout, Bigfoot's Lair, and Southvale Quarry; the wiki files Camp Chatty and Southvale Quarry under Greenwood Forest), Farnum Farmstead, Cobblestone Village, Highroad Junction, Lakeshore, Wugachug, Merry Vale, Stillwater Crossing, Shrouded Glade and Sunstone Valley. NPCs that the dungeon or job pages tie to the Wilds but who the client puts in one of those places are in [[#Tied to the Wilds by another page, standing elsewhere]]. The "Wilds Farm" and "Wilds Condo" are player housing plots (Farmer Chug, Melanie Lane), not world places, and are not listed.

**Two roadhouses.** The client has **The Roadhouse** (Terrence's inn, covered here) and **Wheelie Pete's Roadhouse** (1885145003), which the wiki puts in Sunstone Valley ("Red's Haven behind Wheelie Pete's Roadhouse", wiki Fry). Wade Shatterslag and Fry belong to the second one and are not on this page. [client] [wiki]

## Notes: quest givers and trainers

- **Terrence and the Roadhouse** supply the early Medic and Warrior lines: Terrence is the contact for Supply Recovery and Medic on a Mission and holds the Warrior weapon crates; Caleb Sleepyswing gives the repeatable Warrior contracts and every Warrior level-gate trainer sends under-level players to him. The Tavern Cellar is the combat hook: Serge's mail, Desi's dog Lucy and Cal. See [[Medic]], [[Warrior]], [[Tavern Cellar]].
- **Dartmoor** is the Blacksmith and Miner mid-level camp: Derp Axemaker gates Blacksmith at level 10 and Miner at level 10, Shimmer Shinyaxe takes the Sorin delivery and gives Simple Surveying and Suspicious Eyes. See [[Blacksmith]] and [[Miner]].
- **Pixie Nursery** is the Ninja level-15 stop (Sakka) and a cluster of client-only errand NPCs (Flax, Zinnea, Funnel, Greenthumb, Peacepetal). Its quests mostly have no recoverable titles.
- **Robgoblin Camp** starts the Wizard line (Fizzlesticks) and holds the Chef level-20 gate (Glerga). Nurble serves Brawler, Wizard and Kart Driver (Squee) errands.
- **PCA Camp** ends the Postman line (Emahl) and houses the Return to Sender boss.
- **Forest Troll Village** is a client-only quest hub (Grosh, Voggun, Vergosh, Nog, Burgl, Troggers, Bluz, Hogosh) feeding the Fort and the Ninja "Stolen Away" quest.
- **Speedways** carry the whole Kart Driver and Demolition Derby lines. Wildwood has both job trainers (Johnny Thunder, Mad Jack), the SG Tour 400 start (Kade Carmichael) and the film-set derby series (Sydney Spellmerg, Jace Wildwheel, Sir Horace). Frost Ridge has the sponsor Petal Oceanmist and the Paws 'n Claws arena. Thunder Falls has the third kart tier (Shooblemoot) and the first derby series (Crash Davis).
- **Coach "Golden Leg" Eddie** is the Soccer Star coach; his camp is the only Soccer Star location in the Wilds.

## Gaps

- **No coordinates.** The sources give directions only. Distance markers in video do not give a stable position, so no row has a map position.
- **Quest titles for client-only quests.** Roadhouse, Pixie Nursery and Forest Troll Village errands (Pear Jam, Snarf's pies, Desi and Lucy, Serge's mail, Flax, Zinnea, Funnel, Greenthumb, Peacepetal, Ginger, Greta, Hogosh, Gulsh, Burgl, Grosh, Voggun, Vergosh, Mungo) have descriptions but no title match, because the client stores titles and descriptions apart. Titles shown are exact name-bearing strings or wiki-backed job titles.
- **Rewards and levels.** Almost no source states a reward for a Wilds town quest. Wiki prize-wheel contents for the dungeons are on the dungeon pages.
- **Merchant stock.** Bodkin, Sleeping Panther, Redsden, Seppa, Witts, Hex Webdancer, Nightshade, Coiled Viper, Broogle, Keren and River are wiki-only for place and stock; the lists are not copied here.
- **Standing place not stated.** Blazing Star, Ginger Gold, Dahlia, Wuga's race, Burgl's exact camp, Jace Wildwheel and Makayla Azureeyes have no usable place line beyond the area.
- **Video coverage is thin.** The zone labels that match the Wilds are South Sacred Grove (62 frames, nearly all Wildwood), Wilds (5 frames, none with an NPC), North Sacred Grove (7 frames, none with an NPC), Thunder Falls (2 frames) and Frost Ridge (1 frame). Most rows have no video tag. There is no video of Terrence in the Roadhouse, Fizzlesticks inside the camp, Glerga, Derp Axemaker with a label, or any Pixie Nursery NPC with a zone label.
- **Strings with a name but no NPC.** "Tavern Keeper" (title string 1941856817), "Rocco" (chased bixies out of the cellar, 150172332; name ucdt 3872896647), "Shorty up in Dartmoor Quarry" (1071561278, no name entry), "The Handy Boys" (stagehands, 68299626), "Robgoblin Camp Cook" (934499965) and "Troll Villager" (ucdt 2077544781) are named in client text but nothing places them.
- **Thunder Falls ownership.** Whether Thunder Falls (and with it Crash Davis, Dane Blueshift, Laurin and the merchants Keren and River) belongs to this page or to Lakeshore is not settled by the sources.
- **Southvale, Camp Chatty, Farnum and Cobblestone NPCs** are only cross-referenced above. If their pages are not written, those rows are missing from the content set.
- **Retired content.** No NPC on this page is marked "no longer in-game" by the wiki. Job-specific merchants were added on 6 August 2013 per the wiki Merchants page, so the merchant rows reflect the 2013 game, not launch.
- **Enemies and bosses** have their abilities and health on the dungeon pages, not here.
