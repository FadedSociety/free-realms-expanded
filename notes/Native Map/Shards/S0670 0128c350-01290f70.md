## Tentative wire and server-contract links

The classes `AccountOnReceiveFriendConfirmRequestUC`, `AccountOnReceiveFriendConfirmResponseUC`, `AccountOnReceiveFriendLoginUC`, and `AccountOnReceiveFriendLogoutUC` suggest incoming friend-request/response/presence events. The community catalogue and [[Research/Pieces/087 Friends and presence]] associate them with `CommandPacketConfirmFriendRequest` (26/16), `CommandPacketConfirmFriendResponse` (26/17), `FriendOnlinePacket` (74/2), and `FriendOfflinePacket` (74/3), respectively. The corresponding native handlers (`0128fda0`, `01290210`, `01290c90`, `01290f70`) only consume display-action arguments and update UI/commands; they do not parse or identify those wire packets. These are tentative associations, not native opcode evidence.

Hypothesis, dependent on those class/catalogue/note links: a server may send a friend-confirm request, receive an accept/decline response through a separate command path, and send presence changes that prompt the client to refresh its roster. The original server is unavailable, and the supplied emulator does not implement friend persistence or roster behavior. The exact packet-to-handler dispatch, response fields, and effects of the friend command objects remain unresolved.
