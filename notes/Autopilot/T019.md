  store catalogue behind an `Arc`. The shop inventory and transaction ledgers now

- `prototype/osfr-tools/tests/vendor_sell.rs`: six gateway-level tests cover a
  successful buy, insufficient coins, a full bag, malformed, unknown-item,
  invalid-quantity and closed-shop requests, stale inventory after a later
  reward, and bundle rollback. All refusal tests assert the gateway snapshot is
  unchanged and that the client receives a 66/44 refusal.

## Recovered facts and provenance

- 66/43 is encoded and decoded by `npc_interaction::{encode,decode}_merchant_buy_66_43`.
  The request layout comment cites the client serializer (`00b3ab20`); the
  selected item, quantity and transaction fields are reconstructed policy (R).
- 66/44 is the client-readable merchant result. Its reader is `00b32430`, and
  the status field at wire offset 48 is compared with 1 by dispatcher `00b48a10`.
- Prices come from `data/content/stores.json`; the client-supplied price is not
  trusted. Existing store rows and the open-shop/stock/range checks remain the
  source of admission.

## Reconstructed rules (R)

- The client transaction id is the vendor idempotency key for successful buys.
  Refusals are retryable and are not cached.
- Bag capacity is 100 occupied stacks, checked after staging the grant. Refusal
  leaves coins, items, equipment and the transaction cache unchanged.
- The live inventory is synced immediately before each 66/43 transaction so
  rewards earned after login are not overwritten by the shop's login snapshot.
- A successful buy deducts the checked total, grants through `Inventory::grant`,
  and commits only after all checks pass. The 66/44 status and the leading
  values are server policy where the client reader does not name them.
