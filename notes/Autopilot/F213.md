## Security threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client can send arbitrary `26/8`, `26/10`, `26/6`, `49/2`, `49/13`, `49/14` and
`35/x` bytes, replay a completed turn-in, name another quest or NPC, or disconnect and
reconnect at any point. The change trusts none of it:

- The replay reads only server-saved `pending_turnin` / `pending_turnin_actor` and the
  content definition; no client packet selects the quest, actor or timing. A stale or broken
  definition refuses the per-session reply (`pending_end_screen_refused`) and the shared
  wrapper logs it for that viewer without faulting the tick.
- The actor written into `49/13` is the runtime GUID of a server-emitted `35/2` in the same
  batch, resolved from the saved content key through `content_key_for_turnin`; a client
  cannot inject an actor.
- The replay grants nothing; `49/14` settlement still requires the exact pending quest and
  the existing idempotent `quest:<character>:<quest>` grant key, so a replay cannot pay twice.
- Packets stay per-viewer (interest-filtered shared view and per-session reliable queue); no
  other player sees or can trigger the screen.
- The synthetic gateway fixture and the bin test are test-only; they write under the process
  temp directory and change no production validation.
