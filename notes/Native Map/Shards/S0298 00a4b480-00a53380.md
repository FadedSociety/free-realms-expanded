## Main clusters

- **SoeUtil arrays and containers.** 00a4b480..00a4d1c0 contains array destructors, swap/append/access helpers and overhead-related specializations. At 00a4b520, slot 3 transfers elements through a temporary and frees its backing allocation; it does not invoke a temporary-array destructor. Fixed-stride buffer replacement helpers at 00a51090, 00a51260, 00a512f0, 00a51380, 00a51400 and 00a514c0 allocate replacement storage, copy existing elements and release the old buffer. They do not hash or relink nodes.
- **OverHead UI/rendering.** 00a4c490 registers 26 chat-bubble and health-bar resources under IDs 42000?42025. The OverHeadBitmapElement and OverHeadNameIconElement RTTI classes are anchored by their vtables and constructors (00a4c260, 00a4c330). 00a4c780 performs visibility/distance checks and sorts accepted elements. 00a4bc10 releases several referenced members and embedded arrays; the owning class is not directly established here.
- **Options UI.** 00a4d650 stores its supplied argument at +0x30 and creates data sources named BaseClient.Options, BaseClient.ControllerOptions and BaseClient.HelpCredits at +0xc/+0x1c/+0x2c. 00a4e450 loads the corresponding element files. 00a4dd70 removes one node through 00a4da30 before opening a file, then parses ^-delimited lines, skips # comments and links 0xc0-byte rows at the passed list's tail. 00a4dc90 is described mechanically as three reference releases plus three list-helper calls; direct evidence here does not establish that those references are the three option windows.
- **Pirates ship-combat code.** RTTI and strings anchor the packet and processor family. 00a4fb70 is ShipCombatProcessor vtable slot 1. 00a4f880 has Pirates readiness strings. Kind-9 lookup/forwarding helpers (00a4f9b0, 00a4fa70), HUD field forwarding (00a4f7f0), and geometry (00a529b0) are tied to concrete caller paths. Unowned field setters and pair checks remain in unknown, without a Pirates class assertion.

## Container and list corrections

- 00a50780 and 00a507c0 initialize RTTI-named maps with twenty inline bucket slots. This shows their layout, not how many maps the processor owns.
- 00a51710 and 00a517a0 use fixed modulo-20 buckets, with distinct node key/next offsets (+0x78/+0x7c and +0x64/+0x68).
- 00a50840 uses a runtime bucket count at map+4 and a mask of count?1; its key and next offsets are +0x360/+0x364. It is not the fixed-20 implementation.
- 00a51c20 starts at +0x184 and follows +0x200 links only while the step count is positive. Zero or negative steps return the original head; a null head or encountered null link returns zero.
- 00a51ce0 tests only the low nibble of the first dword. The decoded bytes show the second-dword load is dead.
- 00a531a0 and 00a532e0 have a force flag that changes reuse and zero-size behavior. With force clear they use the 5/4 growth and 6/5 shrink hysteresis; with force set they reuse only on equal capacity, return null for a differing zero request, and otherwise allocate the exact requested byte count (4-byte or 8-byte stride).

## Existing-note comparison and open questions

The Research folder has no ship-combat or OverHead research note matching these addresses. Existing notes/Native Map/Classes pages repeat RTTI names and constructor summaries; they do not establish packet direction. The prior S0298 findings text incorrectly said the family was entirely inbound, that no opcode-94 community evidence existed, and that the six fixed-stride copies were hash-list rehashers. The caller review and the pinned local community sources contradict those claims; this findings file records the narrower supported conclusions. The older claim that 00a50780 proves one map per ship is also removed.

Open questions:

1. What payload fields the receive handlers outside this shard decode, and what the original server did with outbound subcodes 3 and 5.
2. The concrete packet class/sub-opcode paired with destructor 00a4f320; its constructor is outside this range.
3. The owning class and semantics of the three references released by 00a4dc90, and of the unowned field helpers at 00a4f670, 00a4f700, 00a4f740, 00a4fb20..00a4fb60.
4. The meanings of the overhead configuration rows and nested element slots; current evidence establishes resource IDs, layouts and control flow, not the content schema.
5. The behavior of the indirect branch in 00a53260; its slot-3 path is not identified by this shard.
