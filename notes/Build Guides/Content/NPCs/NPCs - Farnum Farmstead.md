## Overview

**Name.** The client uses three spellings for the place: "Farnum Farmstead" (`ucdt` 1604148638; `ugdt` 3676046583), "Farnum's Farmstead" (`ucdt` 1299973687) and "Farnum's Farm" (`ucdt` 1092093905; `ugdt` 1226612565). Region-entry text: "Welcome to Farnum Farmstead. I hope you enjoy your visit!" (1177536037). Activity names carry the same spellings: "Farnum's Farm Harvesting" (`ucdt` 1994585129; `ugdt` 2230579324), "Farnum's Farm Harvesting Practice" (3690285639), "Harvesting: Farnum: Game Guide" (2766548474). The wiki title is "Farnum's Farm", "also commonly called Farnum Farmstead". [client] [wiki Farnum's Farm]

**Where it is.** Client: "Farnum's Farmstead is southwest of Cobblestone Village." (120228216); "...southwest of Cobblestone Village, which is south of here" (636961791, a harvesting hint spoken north of the village); "Pappy Farnum can be found at Farnum's Farmstead to the south of Sanctuary." (1390303341); Southvale Outpost is "in the far south, west of Farnum's Farmstead" (803481107); a Gloam creature roams "our land north of Wildwood Speedway" (157225278). One line says "southwest of Queensfields" (514372645), which does not fit (Conflicts 5). Wiki: "in the far southern reaches of Sacred Grove ... heading south from the Cobblestone Village warpstone"; infobox "South of the Cobblestone Village warpstone"; the Southern Wilds page lists it with Cobblestone Village, Wildwood Speedway, Southvale Quarry and Greenwood. There is no warpstone at the farm in any source. [client] [wiki Farnum's Farm, Southern Wilds]

Hartley Harrison is "on the other side of the barn. Just go past the Cooking Table to the Forge and Anvil area." (3747418871). Wiki: Rocky stands "near the Cooking Table". [client] [video] [wiki Rocky]

(1041615162); "Use the Smelting Furnace to make copper bars from the ore" (2350895608, text match is in [[Adventurer]] A7). Wiki: Estus and Vela stand "at the forging station" or "crafting station". [client] [wiki Estus, Vela, Merchants]
- **Harvesting Practice and the crop plants.** Video landmark "Harvesting Practice" at 16 to 45 m (`3caQP-As968/00478`, `/00506`, `/00552`, `/00560`); client: "Gummyfruit or Rainleaf you can get from the plants behind me or the Harvesting Practice bushels" (1665907717). Crops: client says Stickfruit and Tumtumnip grow at the farm, Spiralmint and Stainberry "are found easily", Gummyfruit and Rainleaf come "by doing a good job harvesting" (636961791); wiki lists Spiralmint, Stainberry, Gummyfruit, Rainleaf, Stickfruit, Tumtumnip; the video map tooltip reads "Tumtumnip?, Stickfruit, Spiralmint, Rainberry" (`3xN9pspaqbw/00016`, the last word is probably Rainleaf). [client] [video] [wiki]
- **Water Well, Copper Vein, Checkers & Chess.** Video markers "Water Well", "Copper Vein (19 m, 39 m, 41 m)" and "Checkers & Chess (14 m to 46 m)" during the opening quests (`3caQP-As968/00442`, `/00468`, `/00478`, `/00514`, `/00542`, `/00562`). The Gear Up! text sends the player to "these rocks nearby" for copper ore (1096004507). [video] [client]
- **Sheep pen and Sheep Watch.** The video shows a "Sheep Watch" marker 5 to 6 m from the burning-barn area (`3caQP-As968/00468`, `/00478`, `/00482`, `/00492`) and a red "Sheep Watch?" marker at 19 m (`/00404`). Wiki: the dungeon is "started by clicking on the gate in the southwest Wilds, near Farnum's Farm". Full dungeon: [[Sheep Watch]]. [video] [wiki]
- **Garden Defense tables.** "The Garden Defense tables can be found at Farnum's Farmstead." (3972257612); the easy table is "near Hartley Harrison on the western side of the farmstead" (2164323471). [client]
- **Highroad Hijinx fence.** Hooligans "up on the highroad" keep the farm awake and kick soccer balls "down here, breaking windows and hitting sheep!" (925272355, 2154570430). Wiki: the dungeon is "started by clicking on the fence in the southwest Wilds, near Farnum's Farm". Dungeon: [[Highroad Hijinx]]. [client] [wiki]
- **Vending machine, Farmer's Market, Tool Shed, Farm-O-Matic.** These appear in video frames with the banner "Wilds Farm", which is the player farm lot, not this zone (Conflicts 12). They are not landmarks of the Farmstead.

**Story role.** The Adventurer story in the client text, and the first-time-player video, put the player here right after the Briarwood Caverns tutorial: Darkthorne's Magic Door "will take you to Farnum Farmstead. I have asked the Farnums to aid your journey the Royal Palace." (1398765326). The farm quests then run Pappy Farnum (turn-in and fire-fighting), Mae Farnum (harvest and cook), Cleetus Farnum (mine, forge, wolves) and Pappy again, who sends the player on to Bartle in Cobblestone Village. See [[Adventurer]] A4 to A11. The wiki also makes Henry the farm's entry quest for Chapter 1 ("Farnum's Free Advice"). [client] [video] [wiki Sacred Grove]

**How to read the tables.** One row per NPC. A client name entry proves the name exists, not where the NPC stands; standing place comes from a client sentence that names the farm, a video frame, or the wiki, and the row says which. Quest links go to the job page that holds the full row; quests with no job-page row are in [[#Quest index]]. The video archive holds only the first-time-player opening (Pappy, Mae, Tamara, Uncle Irving as markers) and the Peep's Missing Bowl hand-in; every other NPC here rests on client text and the wiki. Player names, player chat and player-chosen names are never recorded.

## Notes on important NPCs

- **Pappy Farnum** is the quest anchor of the whole zone: he greets the hero, sets the fire-fighting task, offers Farming 101, asks for one last Gloam kill and then hands the player to Bartle. Several other quests end at him (food baskets) and one starts with him (the BBQ invitations). Reward coins for the first two are known (3 and 4, video). [client] [video]
- **Mae Farnum and Cleetus Farnum** run the two skill lessons of the opening arc: Mae teaches harvesting and Strong Arm Stew at the Cooking Table; Cleetus teaches mining, smelting, forging and the first hammer fight at the Furnace and Anvil. Both also give repeats ("Second Helping of Strongarm Stew", "Cleetus' Copper Collection", "Forging for Reinforcements"). Mae sells Spice, Bat Meat and Burnetta's recipe. [client] [video] [wiki]
- **Henry, Hartley Harrison, Wompugg and the Shady Chugawugs** form the farm's hooligan-and-thief storyline: hooligans and soccer balls from the highroad (Henry, Highroad Hijinx), a crate thief and shady chugawugs after the wool (Hartley), and a traitor guard in Wompugg whose mask quest takes the player to the Bandit Hideout. [client]
- **Anne and Bo** run the sheep. Anne's two wolf quests feed [[Sheep Watch]]; Bo's lost-sheep quest is a shearing minigame, and Peep's lost bowl is the Adventurer's "Peep's Missing Bowl". [client] [wiki]
- **Houston, Rucks and Papa Salt** are mini-game NPCs: three tiers of Garden Defense (tower defense) and a Harvesting minigame challenge. [client]
- **Maple Sugarleaf and Riley** are the end of the [[Card Duelist]] line (Maple) and the Player's Club (Riley) and both duel at the farm. [client] [wiki]
- **Vicki** is the Archer recruit who practises on the sheep pen; her three Archer quests send the player into the Sheep Watch and Highroad Hijinx. [client] [wiki]
- **Rocky, Estus, Vela and Shiny Straightshot** are the wiki's merchants; stock lists come from the wiki (2013), places are wiki-only. [wiki]

## Conflicts

1. **Name of the place.** The client uses "Farnum Farmstead", "Farnum's Farmstead" and "Farnum's Farm" in different entries; the wiki title is "Farnum's Farm" and says it is "also commonly called Farnum Farmstead". This page titles the zone "Farnum Farmstead", the form the zone-entry text and most quest text use. [client] [wiki]
2. **Papa Salt's town.** The wiki puts Papa Salt in the Cobblestone Village category with location "Farnum's Farm, Cobblestone Village". The client gives no place; its Garden Defense tables are at the farm, so the farm is used and the village category is treated as a loose region label. The wiki also files Gloamling under Cobblestone Village. [client] [wiki Papa Salt, Gloamling]
3. **Aunt Daisy's place.** "At Farnum Farmstead" (1094445753, 2676810818, 3130839664, 3567812532) against "She should be in Cobblestone Village" (2345385226); Kibler's checkers quest in Cobblestone also says "There be a game table over near Aunt Daisy" (3412900257), and the long Cobblestone welcome lists her among the village locals (459280103). Video shows a Checkers & Chess marker at the farm (`3caQP-As968/00478`, `/00562`) and none is documented in Cobblestone. Treated as one NPC; the farm is the most-attested place; the village mentions are recorded on [[NPCs - Cobblestone Village]].
4. **Place of the sheep and hooligan tales.** The wiki files Bo, Rocky, Vela and Estus under the Wilds category (Rocky's, Estus's and Vela's infoboxes say "Southern Wilds", Bo's says "Farnum's Farm, Wilds") while their text says Farnum's Farm and the client calls the place a farmstead. Vicki's wiki categories say Bristlewood while her text says she starts at the farm. Client wording used.
5. **Direction.** Client "southwest of Cobblestone Village" (120228216, 636961791, 67472592), "to the south of Sanctuary" (1390303341) and wiki "south of the Cobblestone Village warpstone" agree; the client line "southwest of Queensfields" (514372645) does not, because Queensfields is north of Cobblestone Village (510664415). The Cobblestone-relative direction is used.
6. **Hammer name.** Wiki goal "Forge a Funky Scrapper Hammer"; wiki dialogue and client "Spunky Scrapper Hammer" (342264351, 1041615162). Spunky is used.
7. **Title of the wolf quest.** Wiki "Fighting off the Pack" (client `ucdt` 46289336); client also has "Fighting the Pack" (188245996 / 1289042976). Both are real client strings; the count of five is wiki-only.
8. **"Peep" against "Pozy" or "Posy".** The video reads the title as "Pozy's/Posy's Missing Bowl" (`3JXpHghLOTs/00000`, `/00014`); the client says Peep (384690558) and the dog is named Peep (1416865463). Peep used.
9. **Who stands at the farm in the opening.** The frames name Pappy Farnum in every objective; the nameplate on the bearded NPC who speaks in the offer and turn-in frames is read with a different first name in each frame and matches no client or wiki NPC. The Adventurer page treats it as probably a neighbouring player nameplate or an OCR error and does not list a second NPC. That reading is not recorded here. Note for the Soccer Star page: it lists a "Farnum's farm quest giver" under a name that appears in no client or wiki entry and comes from this same nameplate reading; it should not be treated as an NPC. [video] [client]
10. **Gear Up! against "Learn to Mine & Smelt Ore".** The wiki makes "Learn to Mine & Smelt Ore" the prerequisite for "Your First Weapon"; the client has no such title but has the copper-ore and smelting text, and the unique title "Gear Up!" matches it by wording ([[Adventurer]] A7, [[Miner]]). They are probably the same quest renamed [inferred].

## Gaps

- **Video.** Only the opening quests (Pappy, Mae, Tamara and Uncle Irving as markers) and the Peep's Missing Bowl hand-in are on video. No frame shows Cleetus, Henry, Anne, Bo, Hartley, Wompugg, Houston, Rucks, Mandy, Peavey, Maple, Riley, Vicki, Rocky, Estus, Vela or Shiny Straightshot with a farm banner; only client text and wiki lines apply to them.
- **Positions.** The sources give only phrases ("past the Cooking Table to the Forge and Anvil area", "western side of the farmstead", "outside of Farnum Farmstead", "next to the Sheep Watch battle"). There are no coordinates and no map of the farm; relative positions are from video distance labels in three frames.
- **Rewards and levels.** No source gives coins, XP or items for any farm quest except the video's 3, 4 and 1-shard rewards and the wiki's 16 and 18 coins. No level gates are known for Garden Defense, the hooligan thread or the wool thread.
- **Titles without text and text without titles.** The Henry hooligan quest, the shady chugawugs, the BBQ invitations, Tamara's harvest, the chick roundup, Riley's delivery and Maple's stolen cards have text but no title. "Finding Farnum's Farmstead", "Seeking Maple" and the three Garden Defense tiers have titles whose pairing with text rests on wording alone. Anne's two quests, Woolen Woes, Chasing Down the Crate Thief, Hartley's Deliveries, Lost Sheep and Cal's tent are by wording.
- **Job-page rows.** The Ninja wolf quest at Sheep Watch (3801319221) is row X1 on [[Ninja]] and the Brawler Club Hooligan Bullies quest is row BC1 on [[Brawler]]; both are untitled or title-unproven there, with no giver level or order for X1 and no kill count for BC1. The Medic contract "wolves at Farnum Farmstead" (3291191374) is a guess on [[Medic]]. The Wanderwings riddle answers near the farm are on [[NPCs - Wilds]].
- **Vendors.** Rocky's, Estus's, Vela's and Mae's stock and prices are wiki or unknown; Shiny Straightshot's whole existence in the live game is a retired wiki detail.
- **Dev flags.** "Sheep Watch - Ravenous wolf teleport A to D" (67158606, 296209523, 786943527, 1784479680), "Lost Sheep Despawn" (60818986), "Special profile for Sheep in Sheep Watch" (1222012348) and similar are internal; what they drive is unknown.
- **Opening content.** The repo's reconstruction of the opening farm is not evidence for this page. How the original build ordered Gear Up!, Your First Weapon, Fighting the Pack, One More Gloam and Where's the Help? is from client dialog and wiki prerequisites only, with no video after the Cooking minigame.
- **Dates.** The wiki dates Rocky and Estus to 6 August 2013 and Vela to 15 October 2013; the quest pages date to July 2013. No source dates the Garden Defense, wool-theft, Rucks or Peavey content or says whether they were removed.
