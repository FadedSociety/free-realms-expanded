# Farm Actor Asset Repairs

Run 090058 cannot accept the well and fire visuals. Its original logs show missing housing-well mesh/textures and an unavailable seasonal campfire material. The accepted farm quest remains at four zero counters; packet presence does not establish visible, reachable quest objects.

The repair manifest in `../lab/reports/opening-actor-asset-repairs.json` is restricted to a new forging variant. It replaces well model 3097 with registered original model 3519 (`farming_well_01.adr`) and three seasonal model 1939 fires with registered original model 204 (`bw_campfire_01.adr`). These are explicit reconstructions using genuine original models. Exact historical quest bindings and Gloam-colored fire visuals remain unrecovered. GUIDs, actions, radii, profiles, goals and ordering are preserved. The research agent did not edit opening content, saves or the client.

Unmodified `fr-adr-toolkit` readers at revision `5acfdf8d6006fa6f43d1d3605f076a6be7393e72` accept the ADR, DMA and DME records. The well has six named dependencies; the campfire has ten, including its flame particle XML and four particle textures. Every raw CRC matches both the original pack catalog and native manifest. Every native compressed envelope length also matches. Exact paths, hashes, model-table rows and original failure-log lines are in `../lab/reports/farm-actor-asset-closure.json`. The campfire's global audio event is named in the ADR; this audit does not independently prove every indirect audio-bank reference.

The original static scene contains `sg_well_01.adr` at `[-2196.748, -31.475924, 324.771]`, near Pappy. Its dependencies are preserved, but the native numeric model table has no corresponding dynamic model ID and no ADR-path override has been proved. Inventing a numeric ID would not load this model. The registered farming well is therefore an explicit alternate visual at the existing local quest position, rather than a duplicate over the static well.

Unmodified ForgeLightToolkit readers at revision `9c06714c5d79d9152c322814bc904abad944e06e` read four original FabledRealms terrain chunks. The well floor is -56.6594975; fire floors are -56.5658735, -56.7464775 and -55.703051. Fire 3's old Y of -56.5 would bury the entire replacement mesh, whose top is 0.70243 above its origin. Only that fire moves vertically, to -55.65. The other actor positions remain unchanged.

The well-to-fire terrain samples have no missing floor and gentle slopes. A straight Pappy-to-well line crosses a steep hill, so it is not a proved walking route. The later native approach near `[-2154.1, 286.5]` has gentler terrain toward the well. These are render-mesh floor checks, not collision or path-clearance acceptance. Native appearance, target selection and extinguishing still need actual play after the separately reviewed migration.
