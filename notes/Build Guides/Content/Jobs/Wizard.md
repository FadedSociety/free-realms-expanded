## How to read this page

- Tags: **[client]** is the game's locale text (`en_us_data.dat`), **[video]** is player recordings, **[wiki]** is the fan wiki. Precedence is client, then video, then wiki. Where they disagree, the higher source is in the table and the clash is in **Conflicts**.
- Numbers in parentheses, for example `(1692259777)`, are client locale ids. The "Title ids" column gives both title entries for a quest as `ucdt` / `ugdt`; the two tags carry the same text.
- Video frame keys look like `(CW9hNMMNV8w/00004)`.
- **Matching caveat.** The locale file stores quest titles, descriptions, objectives and dialog as separate entries with no link between them. Every description or objective below was matched to its quest by wording (names, places, enemies), not by a data link. Matches that rest on wording alone are marked with a dagger (†). A dagger means "fits this quest, not proven". Strings that fit no listed quest are in **Unmatched client text**.
- **Video coverage of the quest line is almost nil.** The only Wizard-line quest title seen on screen is the entry quest **Being a Better Wizard** (frames `CW9hNMMNV8w/00000` to `/00074`). Video also shows the boss Wizard Hildegarde in the [[Robgoblin Treasure Trove]], players on the Wizard job at levels 1, 2, 3 and 20, and the job-window and members-upsell screens. No frame shows a Wizard trainer, a Wizard quest reward or any other Wizard-titled quest. Every quest's "video" evidence is "none" unless a cell says otherwise.
- **Quest wiki pages are not in the archive.** The wiki archive holds the `Wizard` page, which lists the quests by giver, plus NPC and item pages. There is no per-quest wiki page, so quest order, givers and the four weapon rewards come from those pages only. Coins, XP and most rewards are unknown.
- Rewards (coins, XP, items) do not appear in the locale file. The only quest rewards known are four wands named on wiki item pages. Every other reward cell reads "unknown".
- The client spells some titles differently from the wiki. The client spelling is used, with the wiki spelling noted in the quest cell where it differs. The client stores most titles with the prefix "Wizard: ". **Sticky Spell Book** has no prefix in the client (ucdt 690898156).

## Overview

Wizard is a members-only ranged combat job that fights with wands and elemental spells. [wiki] The wiki states "Wizard is a Members Only ranged combat job, specializing in long-distance combat using magical skills and wands. This experience is gained by completing Wizard quests and by battling in combat instances in the Wilds and in dungeons." [wiki] The wiki job quote: "Wizards summon the elements to attack their foes from a distance. They use magical wands to channel elemental energy into spells that can confuse enemies. They can even shoot lightning! Those who wish to learn the mystical ways of Wizardry can speak to Fizzlesticks in the Robgoblin Camp for training." [wiki] The client's short description is "Wizards are skilled at calling on the elements to damage their foes." (1749735770). [client]

**Unlocking.** Speak to Fizzlesticks in the Robgoblin Camp. [wiki][client] The client says "Fizzlesticks can be found at the Robgoblin Camp to the north of Lakeshore." (3122558389), "Learn how to be a wizard by finding Fizzlesticks in the Robgoblin camp." (2002641289) and "Speak with Fizzlesticks at the Robgoblin camp near Lakeshore to learn how to become a Wizard" (1250342927). [client] The wiki says the job is "unlocked by talking to Fizzlesticks in the Robgoblin Camp and completing his quests." [wiki] The objective "Obtain the Wizard job" (303623269) exists. [client] The first Fizzlesticks quests are a spell-book errand, a pair of glasses and a cleaning job (quests 1 to 3), and the job-teaching speech "the first rule of being a wizard is..." (3200727836 †) comes after them, so the exact quest that grants the job is unproven. [client][wiki]

- **Entry quest.** **Being a Better Wizard** (objective "Learn how to advance your skill as a Wizard by speaking with Fizzlesticks near Lakeshore" 639925547) is seen in video with the objective "Speak to Fizzlesticks near Lakeshore" (CW9hNMMNV8w/00004). It is the same pattern as the other jobs' entry quests (compare [[Archer]]). [client][video]
- **Advanced-job gate.** The client calls Wizard an "Advanced Job" earned by mastering another job: "There are even Advanced Jobs like Ninja, Wizard, and Warrior (like me) that you can earn by being a master Archer, Medic, or Brawler" (1073904650). [client] A job-board advert reads "Wanted: Skilled Medics for Wizard Training ... Really, really, really old Wizard seeks an experienced Medic to learn the path of arcane magics ... Requires: Level 20 Medic" (1780716543), and its title is "Advanced Wizard" (ucdt 3943816485). [client] A refusal speech (speaker not named; it fits the trainer) sends the player to the Medic job: "I only teach the Way of the Wizard to those who are ready. You have the gift, I'll give you that, but you're as likely to blow yourself up as you are to zap your opponent! Learn more about becoming a Medic from Nurse Naia." (2494908436) †. [client] The wiki and the video do not show this gate. See **Conflicts** and [[Medic]].
- **Opening at the Wizard Job Center.** The client also has a separate opening led by Xoren Fellspark ("Meet Xoren at the Wizard Job Center" 1228043655), the Wizard counterpart of the Kazuo opening on the [[Ninja]] page. It is client-only and is not placed in the line. See **Xoren Fellspark opening**. [client]

**Members.** The wiki puts Wizard in the Members Only category. [wiki] The client has the upsell "Oh, no! Want to keep playing as a Wizard? Upgrade to unlock more fun!" (1164096267) and a membership blurb "Access to exclusive jobs like the Wizard?" (2166516018). [client] The same blurb, "Access to exclusive jobs like the Wizard!", is on the Marketplace Portal screen (wZTgpVsMqTk/00026). [video] No source gives a date on which Wizard stopped being members-only (compare [[Archer]]). The page is set to `members_only: true`.

**Levels.** The job runs from level 1 to 20 (**max_level 20**). [wiki][client][video] Level gates are Reach Level 5, 10, 15 and 20 (see the quest tables). Client goals exist for 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 (see **Gaps**). The wiki level 20 achievement is "Wizard: Level 20" with title "Epic Enchanter" (client 104270161, ugdt 591733015). [wiki][client] Completing the whole line grants the achievement "Complete the Wizard Questline." (1698015607) with title **Arcane Master** (wiki Achievements; client 397812778, ugdt 2968745268 / ucdt 3527689136). [wiki][client] Player job bars at **Wizard 20** are seen in `FsCYVYzjnpg/00018`, `R0F3breqLUc/00030` and `LgrhYq2Hs1I/00040`. [video]

**What the line is about.** The line runs through six regions: the Robgoblin Camp (Fizzlesticks), Snowhill (Yaren, Morgulg), Merry Vale (Yvelle), Sanctuary (Osgood, Sandbur, Karin), Blackspore (Iliya) and Briarwood (Sven). Its arc, as the strings read: recover a stolen spell book, learn Zap and Fireball, help a careless student (Morgulg) and a forgetful researcher (Sandbur), then help Sven (a wand-making student) collect an Azure crystal, a Treeleaf twig, an Arachnia web and a Haunted Mines gem, rescue him from the Briarheart Caverns, and have Osgood build the **Wand of Spectral Fire**. [client][wiki]
