## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client can send a forged 49/2 accept for an excluded quest (refused: no pending offer, or
`requirement_denial` at accept), a 49/15 breadcrumb or Talk for it (refused by `offer_denial`), or
abandon to reopen the others (allowed by design (R); abandoning pays nothing, and finishing a second
version after abandoning the first is impossible because the first was never completed). Refusals
change nothing (tests assert byte-equal saves). The rule is per character state; nothing is shared.
Bad content fails at load, never at runtime.

## (R) choices

- Three-way group: the 3000020/3000022 edge is a transitive inference.
- Offered and Active block, not only Completed (wiki says "complete"; the forks block while active).
  Offered adds nothing beyond the existing pending-offer check, but keeps the rule stated in one place.
- Abandoning reopens the others (Lxzxrus `QUESTS.md`).
- `MAX_EXCLUSIONS = 16`.
- A refused accept keeps the pending offer (the brief's "changing nothing", like the prerequisite
  refusal in the same function). T418 instead withdraws a closed live event's offer because the client
  drops its offer on accept (`0x00a97d70` sets it to -1). For an older save holding a stale excluded
  offer, the kept offer blocks other offers until declined. Lead's call; see Open gaps.
