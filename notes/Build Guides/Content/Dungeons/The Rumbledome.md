# The Rumbledome

Part of [[Content]] › [[Dungeons]]. Tags: **[client]** = game locale text (`loc <id>`), **[video]** = player recordings (frame key), **[wiki]** = fan wiki. Precedence client > video > wiki.

**What this is.** The Rumbledome is a real combat instance: a Skullz-run arena where the player fights a fixed sequence of Skullz gladiators and three named champions. It is also (a) the venue for a separate player-versus-player "Rumbledome Duel!" mode, and (b) the setting for the Card Duelist "Rumbledome" quest line. The card duels themselves are NPC dialog games held next to the arena, not this instance (see Related quests). The wiki files it as a difficulty 1 **battle**, not as an Atlas dungeon, and it is not in the wiki's "Combat Mini-game" category. [client] [wiki] [video]

## Conflicts

1. **Dungeon or battle.** The wiki infobox says Arena and the page says "Battle", the Sunstone Valley page lists it among "Dungeons", the Battles index page omits it, and the category list has it under Battle and Difficulty 1 but not "Combat Mini-game". It has an entrance and a goal panel, so it is treated as a combat instance here. [wiki]
2. **Item spellings.** Wiki "Tattered Short Sleeve Shirt" and "Knee High Combat Boots" against client "Tattered Short-sleeve Shirt" and "Knee-high Combat Boots". Client used. [wiki] [client]
3. **Boss line-up.** The wiki and the video agree on Bronzeknuckles, Silverfist and Goldcrusher. The client also has Kraig and Krissy with announcer lines, which neither the wiki nor the video shows.
4. **Level.** Wiki "Varied" (difficulty 1, open to all). Players of level 13 and 20 are the only ones seen. No client level line was read.
5. **Two modes under one name.** "The Rumbledome" (PvE battle, one goal) and "Rumbledome Duel!" (PvP, "Win the duel!") are separate client entries. Descriptions of traps and power-ups come from the duel string and from dev notes, so not every hazard is proven for the PvE battle.
