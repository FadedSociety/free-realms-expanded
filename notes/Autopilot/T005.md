## Reconstructed rules

- (R) Titles are granted only through a trusted achievement-completion hook.
  The client cannot name or grant a title.
- (R) Claim rewards are server-owned. A client selects queued claim ids; the
  server merges and applies its own grants once, then records the ids.
- (R) A successful claim commits the claim ledger and reward snapshot in the
  same character save. Pending claim rows are not saved.
- (R) A malformed/forged title request is a silent no-op. A well-formed claim
  request with unknown or replayed ids gets a result-0 `108/4` and changes
  nothing.
- (R) Local safety limits are 256 title rows, 256 pending claim rows/trackers,
  4096 durable claim ids, and 1 MiB per message/aggregate record set. These are
  local bounds, not recovered historical values.
- (R) `152/7` China SNS title updates remain deferred.
