Source tags: **[client]** locale text (`en_us_data.dat`, numeric ids), **[video]** frame records (frame key `video/frame`), **[wiki]** fan-wiki archive (page named). Precedence is client, then video, then wiki. Each quest title has a `ucdt` entry and, usually, a `ugdt` twin with the same text.

**How the quest data was matched.** The client stores quest titles and quest text as separate entries with no id link between them, and the quest prerequisite and reward tables are not decoded. Every objective, description or news line below was tied to a title by one of these methods, shown in the "Text match" column. **exact**: a string carries the title's own words or names the same minigame as the title, or a video frame shows the pair. **direct**: a string names the same NPC, place and task as the title. **†**: the string fits but nothing proves the link. Nothing is invented: rewards, givers and levels read "unknown" unless a source names them. Quest order comes from the wiki list, the client level-gate dialog ("Return to X when you are a level N postman"), and one video banner; it is a reading, not a decoded prerequisite chain.

**Evidence rules for this page.** Only the client locale, the video records and the wiki archive were used. No data from OpenSourceFreeRealms or FreeRealms-Legacy, and no older vault note that drew on them, was used. No player names, player chat or player-chosen names are recorded.

## Overview

- Postman is a **freestyle** job about delivering mail. The wiki calls it "a delivery job, specializing in delivering the mail. A freestyle class job, experience is gained by completing quests and by playing the Postman minigames." [wiki Postman] The job-window text uses the display name "Postman" everywhere; there is no alternate job spelling in the client. [client 1053596881, 2191725644, 2447683035, 3191064423 ucdt "Postman"; 2699880323 ugdt]
- **Max level 20.** Level achievements sit at 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 and the client also has a "Postman: Level 5" title (see Level achievements). [client] [wiki Achievements] [video: Postman 10, 12 and 20 on the job bar, `VpHBPT_Mrvw/00012`, `Wh_6VeMIGCA/00002`, `ryw8Lsu-xv8/00000`]
- **Members only (after level 4).** The wiki Jobs list marks Postman members only. [wiki Jobs] The client rule for every job is "Anyone can play up to level 4, but every job goes up to level 20! Use the Marketplace to purchase any job you want, or pay for a membership to unlock all of them!" (348625404; Station Cash wording 1319279804). The level-cap prompt names the job: "Oh, no! Want to keep playing as a Postman? Upgrade to unlock more fun!" (1922489552). Postman sale items: "Postman Membership Upgrade Bundle" (1419490454), "If you join now, you'll receive this special new member pack of postman items!" (657669236), "Members Only Postman Shirt" (1705367394). [client] Which individual quests or minigames are members only is unknown.
- **How to unlock.** The wiki says: talk to Felipe in Sanctuary; or click a set of postboxes in a town before having the job, which adds a journal quest that sends the player to Felipe. [wiki Postman] [wiki Jobs] The client agrees on Felipe ("Find Felipe in Sanctuary and become a postman!" 4100717826) and has a "Postman Pointer - From any mailbox" entry (1931313050), but it also has several strings that send new players to **Flitter** instead (4071005094, 2655956484, 3408708112, 1188443967, 3789678095, 3631246417) and one that sends them to **Loryn** in Snowhill (4143221103). See Conflicts 2.
- **Job switching.** Entering a minigame switches the character to the matching job ("Your job has been switched to one that matches the minigame you are about to join." [video `fktAs_GRrjM/00178`, a Kart gate, shown on that page]). Postman quests were also seen active while the HUD job was Adventurer or Ninja (`zNMmxK6l5jI/00006`, `vcRIx8EficY/00000`), so quest progress does not need Postman as the current job. [video]
- **Quest line shape.** Six town bands, each ending in a level gate: Sanctuary (Felipe and Flitter), Merry Vale (Postmaster Vixie), Seaside (Enve LeStamp), Wugachug (Postchug Runalug), Snowhill (Loryn), then Shrouded Glade and the PCA Camp (Brin Galeglow, Postmaster Lane, Emahl). The client has **30** quest titles that start "Postman:" (26 story quests, 4 "Achieve Level" gates) plus one unprefixed title ("Can You Deliver?") and an intro ("Become a Postman"). The wiki list has 30 entries: it lacks "Achieve Level 20" and "Beautiful Bouquet" and adds "It's all in the Training", which has no live client title. The story is the **Postal Chaos Alliance (PCA)** trying to wreck the postal service. Finishing the line earns the achievement and title "Postmaster". [client 2740930807, 342849470, 1785154931] [wiki Achievements, Titles]
- **Rewards are largely unknown.** No quest-complete frame in the video set shows coin or XP numbers for any Postman quest. The only reward text found is the starting uniform line "Welcome, postman [*player*]! Here's a starting uniform. Wear it well!" (2051750787) and the collection rewards listed under Gear. See Gaps.

## Return to Sender and the Postal Chaos Alliance

The [[Return to Sender]] page asks whether its Postal Chaos Alliance client lines belong to the Postman line. Findings, strongest first:

- **Direct link to the PCA world.** "PCA Operatives are evil postmen that can be found wandering the wilderness." (11589577) equates PCA members with evil postmen, which ties the battle's Evil Postman enemy (4062420947) to the same faction the Postman line fights. The wiki PCA Camp page also says the Postman series "eventually culminat[es]" at the PCA Camp. [client] [wiki PCA Camp]
- **Not proven: the three lines on the battle page.** "Stop the PCA postmen from destroying the mail!" (1607767424), "Stop the PCA from destroying the mail." (2465017856) and "You sure showed those PCA postmen a thing or two!" (1923872681). No string ties them to Return to Sender or to a titled Postman quest. This page places 1923872681 under Biting Back (†) because Biting Back is the only Postman quest whose strings are about chasing off PCA postmen, and the other two stay unplaced. The battle's own goal is "Knock out all of the evil postmen!" (3300949067) and its story line is "There is mail scattered everywhere! The evil postmen are collecting it to throw into the bon fires! Don't let them destroy more than 15 letters or packages." (347866273). [client]
- **PCA Takedown is a different activity.** The Outpost Ordeal minigame uses PCA Campers, Magic Mail and "escape" limits (see the activities table); it is not the battle, which has Evil Postmen, Mailbit and fires. The news line "Armed with letters and an excellent throwing arm, [*player*] kept the PCA from destroying the mail!" (3931963797) could belong to either (†).
- **Conclusion.** Return to Sender is PCA-themed content that sits next to the Postman line but is not part of it. The battle's start node is an Evil Postman in the Wilds [wiki Return to Sender!], and the Postman line ends at the PCA Camp in the Northern Wilds [wiki PCA Camp]; they share a region, not a quest chain.
- **PCA enemy names across the content (all client).** PCA Operative (1323460666; wandering in the wilds), PCA Camper (3331448332 ugdt; PCA Takedown), PCA Trickster (2142513758; Snowhill Mail Morph), Evil Postman (4062420947; Return to Sender), Mailbit (3167520901; boss).

## Conflicts

1. **Who gives the Seaside quests.** The wiki gives It's a Breeze!, Invitation Only and Where's the Sasparilla? to "Enaine in Seaside" and writes the level-10 quest as "Enve LeStampin Seaside" (a garbled name). The client names **Enve LeStamp** for the level-10 gate (2557899825), for the invitations ("Deliver the party invitations for Enve LeStamp." 631359764; "Enve needs that Sweetwater Sasparilla for the party" 3334530306) and for the Merry Vale hand-off (3470990346). The client Enaine is a shopkeeper who collects seashells and has a treasure-chest game (3794148203, 1474017691), and her wiki page (spelled "Enanie") lists no job. Video shows the Invitation Only dialog under a speaker whose name reads "Pace Lothamp?" and is not in the client. Enve LeStamp is used as the Seaside trainer; Enaine and the video name are kept as unresolved.
2. **Who unlocks the job.** Wiki: Felipe. Client: Felipe in 4100717826, 2020875599, 802297762, 1846718813; Flitter in 4071005094, 2655956484, 3408708112, 1188443967, 3789678095, 3631246417 and in the long route-learning dialog 403291592; Loryn in 4143221103; "NOT LIVE - Finding Flitter" (1992057393) and "NOT LIVE - Fit to be a Postman?" (219782705) mark the Flitter intro as an older design. The wiki's Felipe is used; all three are listed as trainers.
3. **Felipe's location.** Wiki and video put Felipe in Sanctuary (video: nameplate beside Sam Potts, `zIwAzf-mdqY/00066`, zone not read). Two client strings say "at Pixiewood" (1114413807, 2920310904). The same client text also sends Card Duelist players to Sam Potts "at Pixiewood" (1155984690) while the wiki and video put him in Sanctuary, so "Pixiewood" probably names the Sanctuary downtown area in older text; unproven.
4. **Ability names.** Wiki: Mail Throw and Toss Bone. Client: Mail Toss (210595591), "Throw Mail" in help text (2845390713), Bone Toss (1851662272). Client spelling is used.
5. **Quest title forms.** Wiki "Sorting it Out" against client "Postman: Sorting It Out" (3333907631); wiki "Where's the Sasparilla" against client with a question mark (1960073937); wiki "Achieve level 15" against client "Achieve Level 15"; wiki "Postman: Can You Deliver?" against a client title with no prefix (1000315976 ugdt only); wiki "Postman: It's all in the Training" against a client "NOT LIVE" unprefixed entry only (582052115). (1328272967). Trophy tier for level 12 is Bronze on the wiki for Postman, Silver for the same level of Kart Driver.

The wiki Postman page names the Sanctuary route quests under Flitter while the client says the three mail minigames report back to Felipe (983271315); givers for those rows follow the wiki.

Whether the numeral or the flavour decides the stats is unknown. Cliff and Reba are listed with the same III accessories.
13. **Highroad Junction versus wiki directions.** The client says the PCA Camp lies southwest of Highroad Junction (2236162202); the wiki says south of the Dartmoor Quarry in the Northern Wilds, and the Return to Sender wiki text says northeast Wilds. Not directly contradictory, but the three descriptions have not been reconciled.
14. **"Speedy Postman".** The client has three "Speedy Postman" entries (2364662491, 3286344294, 3747958693), but the wiki lists "Speedy Postman" as a Card Duelist game-time achievement ("Won a game in less than 3 minutes"). It is not a Postman-job level title.
15. **Rank titles.** The client has "Novice Postman" (2487792989), "Intermediate Postman" (582540773) and "Advanced Postman" (3156577627). Their level ranges are unknown; they are not placed in the tables above.
