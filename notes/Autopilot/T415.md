  (GUIDs 131000102013-024 and 131000102031-036) moved off the W102 starter centres. Only `position`,

## Why (verified at tip 4b409c7)

Before T415, W102-H1 at (-1779.455, -28.88, 562.833) carried W102-H1 (level 3, hit 8), MID-H1 (level 7,
15 x 1.75^2 = 46) and ELITE-H2 (level 20, 15 x 1.75^4 = 141); every starter camp had the same stacking.
Scaling: `encounter/field.rs` `load_field_enemies` (`1.75^(weapon_tier(level)-1)`, `weapon_tier` in
`encounter.rs`); aggro is distance-only (`tick_field`, 4 m chase hysteresis). The canary run below
reproduces it: against the pre-T415 file a level-3 player at W102-F1 takes a 77 hit from MID-F1
(25 x 1.75^2).

## Placement method (R)

Scratch wrapper `%TEMP%\op-T415\verify_t415.py` (not committed) loads
`lab/reports/field-enemies-w102/check_placements.py` read-only by absolute path
(`importlib.util.spec_from_file_location`) and reuses `height()` and `validate()` over
`lab/terrain/zone_0.hgt` and `lab/reports/field-enemies-w102/safe_zones.json`. Rows are passed merged with
their group's `level_band`, `leash_radius` (12), `respawn_seconds`, `aggro_range` (8), `zone_id` and the
`field_roamer` reach (2.5). NPC anchors: every `npcs/*.json` `legacy_data` non-enemy record, all 3,301

4675, 23625 and the static Legacy wolf/Hooligan copies) and the 13 `safe_zones.json` extras. On top of the
W102 limits (height error <= 0.051 m, slope <= 25 deg over every 2 m node of the leash disc,
>= 25 + leash + reach from NPC centres, >= 20 + leash + reach from town rectangles and paths):

- far-apart bands (`max(B.lo - A.hi, A.lo - B.hi) > 2`): >= leash_A + leash_B + max(aggro) + 4 m; the
  quest-gated Hooligans count as band [1,1], aggro 10, leash 12;
- bands starting above 4: >= leash + aggro + 20 = 40 m from every difficulty 1-2 battle spawner
  (96, 12370, 12301, 12302, 12350, 12310, 12311, 12400);
- >= leash + Flame Wave radius (10, `power_ups.json`) + 4 = 26 m from `power_up_use` CLEAR_AT;

  rejected the first ELITE-T choice (-320, 1210) beside Legacy 3149: a 644-node plateau with no walkable
  way down, so the trolls went to (-408, 1362).

Search: per camp a seed cluster (R) near the same-kind Legacy rows the brief names, 1 m grid within 20 m of
the seed, first member = nearest passing point, second member 12-25 m from the first; y = terrain height
rounded to 0.01. Seeds put higher bands further from Sanctuary, Cobblestone and Farnum (R): wolves MID 65 m
-> HIGH 156 m from Farnum; Hooligans MID 53 m from Cobblestone -> ELITE 253 m from Farnum; shamans
MID 727 -> HIGH 803 -> ELITE 977 m from Sanctuary; bixies MID 252 -> ELITE 288 m from Sanctuary.

Legacy anchors (FreeRealms-Legacy 9e08202 `src/Scripts/Zone/FabledRealms.lua`, positions only): every
brief candidate (3156-3158, 2529, 4667-4675, 23623-23626) is already shipped as a static
`npc_spawns_extra.json` NPC, and 3095-3150 sit among shipped troll NPCs, so no centre reuses one. Each
centre is an audited terrain point and `reference_spawn_guid` names the nearest same-kind row ("Hooligan

## New tests (`tests/field_camp_separation.rs`)

- `no_field_camp_shares_ground_with_a_band_more_than_two_levels_away`: every pair of shipped actors whose
  bands are more than 2 apart is >= leash + leash + max(aggro) + 4 m apart. Band, leash and aggro are what
  the loader applies: `load_field_enemies` copies the group's aggro and leash onto each member. (The brief
  said a per-row aggro overrides the group; the loader does the opposite. No grouped row has one, so the
  result is the same today.) Ungrouped rows use their own level, `aggro_range` and resolved behaviour leash.
- `low_difficulty_battle_markers_are_clear_of_higher_bands`: bands starting above 4 keep leash + aggro +
  20 m from every difficulty 1-2 spawner in `field_battles.json`.
- `a_level_three_player_at_each_starter_camp_is_hit_only_by_its_own_band`: one production-shaped root
  (`RewardFixture::new` + the shipped field file), a Brawler with 200 XP (level 3), god mode off (the
  participant's mitigation must be > 0), 80 x 250 ms of `Gameplay::tick_shared_motion` (the gateway's
  shared-field path) at each W102 centre, damage applied through `Gameplay::receive_field_damage`. Every
  32/7 (client reader `0x00A2A910`, re-read with `ida_query.py decompile` this run: attacker u64 +4,
  target u64 +20, damage i32 +28, remaining HP +46) must come from a W102 row and equal round(authored
  damage x the participant's mitigation) (starter rows are levels 1-3, weapon tier 1, asserted), or be
  smaller only when it leaves 0 HP. Each centre must see at least one hit.
