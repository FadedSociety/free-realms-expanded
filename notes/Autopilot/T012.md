# T012: Quest offer hygiene

## Result

Verified the existing production path end to end:

- `QuestEngine::offer_denial` rejects quests with no objectives, even when their
  content entry is enabled.
- `NpcInteraction::offerable_quest_for_talk` goes through `can_offer`, so a
  hollow quest is skipped and cannot consume the talk choice.
- `Gameplay::character_eligibility` derives `player_level` from the active
  job's current profile XP, not from a fixed value. A job requirement also
  requires that same active job to meet the level.
- Eligibility is refreshed before NPC packet handling and after job/XP changes,
  so a stale level cannot keep a quest offerable.

No production behavior change was needed. The change adds a regression suite
for the three requested gates and tightens the existing live-level test to
assert the computed `player_level` directly.

## Files changed

- `prototype/osfr-tools/tests/quest_offer_gates.rs` (new): hollow-quest talk
  selection, atomic under-level refusal, correct offer, and active-job gate.
- `prototype/osfr-tools/src/gameplay.rs`: test-only assertions that the real
  active-job XP maps to player levels 1 and 3 across a job switch.
