## Overview

Snowhill is the snow town north of Sanctuary (wiki, rev 2026-05). A huge pine, the Gifting Tree, stands in the middle; the road loops around it. [wiki] The client names these places: the town hall and Clocktower, the Post Office, the Singing Crystal Mines with the forge beside them, the Hot Springs (Snowhill Hot Springs), Big J's Ice Skating and Bait Shop, the Snowhill Stage, the Crystal Barrier, the Penguin Training Institute, the Warrior barracks, Perry's Pastures just south, the Snowhill Soccer Field past Perry's Pastures, and Frost Ridge Speedway just outside town. [client] (location ids are in the rows below)

How to read this page:
- **Exact NPC names are the client's `ucdt` name strings.** The number after each name is its `ucdt` id. Where the client has a second `ucdt` row for the same name, both are shown.
- Tags: `[client]` locale text, `[video]` Haiku frame reads (frame key shown), `[wiki]` fan wiki. Client beats video beats wiki. A name that only the wiki gives is marked **wiki-only**.
- A client name entry proves the name exists, **not** that the NPC stands in Snowhill. Standing place comes from a client sentence ("in Snowhill"), a video frame, or the wiki; each row says which.
- Quest links go to the job page that holds the full row. Quests that belong to no job are in [[#Town and event quests]] below.
- Video coverage of Snowhill is thin: only about 17 NPC names on screen near Snowhill, and most frames have no zone label. Rows with no `[video]` tag have never been seen on screen in our records.

## Notes: quest givers and trainers

- **Mayor Crystalline and Tevin** run the town's problem-solving. Crystalline is the level gate for Brawler 10 and sends the Medic line into the Bristlewood; Tevin is the Brawler hand-off and the start of the town-problems chain. Client strings put Tevin first ("Mayor Crystalline has bigger fish to fry", 2639833286) where the wiki puts the quests under her.
- **Frostpetal** is the greeter. Newcomers meet her at the gates, she points to the Mayor ([[#Town and event quests|Meet the Mayor!]]) and, for Ninjas, to the kidnappings ([[Ninja]]).
- **Mines and forge (Therin, Sorin, Trixi, Smitty, Brody Sparfist, Bruno Battlerock)** serve [[Miner]] and [[Blacksmith]]. Therin starts Miner; Smitty starts Blacksmith; Sorin gates Miner at level 5; Brody Sparfist gates Blacksmith at level 5; Bruno sells the planks and Singing Coal both lines need; Trixi gives rush orders. The Bat Cave is entered from the mines ([[The Bat Cave]]).
- **Warrior line** is Snowhill-heavy: Dewey (start), Morninglory (initiation at the Hot Springs Haven), Steele (level 5 gate), Fastvi (level 20 gate), plus the old folks Silversnow, Milo and Steele for [[Warrior|Asking Old Folks]].
- **Ninja** uses Michi (level 20 gate) and Ree Peatpants' four repeatable Contracts; the Masterless Ninja story runs through Annabelle and Nenjai.
- **Chef** reaches Lucca at level 10 to 15; Assistant Chef Edward sells recipes.
- **Card Duelist**: Garrison Gold is the early opponent; Mayor Crystalline, Senari and Jonathon Forkpath appear in the Baron packs.
- **Snow Days**: Candi Ivy starts the series; Trina Turtledove gives the daily quests; Everett and Calvin Coldcastle run repeatable activities; Downey, Annabelle and the Mayor give the repeatables after the series. See [[Snow Days]].
