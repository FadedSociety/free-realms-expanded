# T464: the Halloween Vampires vs Werewolves dance-off (content + one op)

The first shipped world event: `halloween_dance_off` in `data/content/world_events.json`, scheduled every
30 minutes in the Halloween season, skipping Bruce's two-hour stage starts. Parts 1-3 (T458, T460, T462) are on
the tip; the fleet pack that said they were missing was stale. The event grants nothing (the cake is scenery).

## What changed

- `data/content/world_events.json`: one event, 12 actors (boombox, prince, Ed + 3 vampires, Jake + 3 werewolves,
  the two cakes), 205 timeline steps, a `schedule`. Generated once from the numbers below by a scratch script
  (not committed); the file is the source of truth. Every actor row carries its provenance in `source`.
- New op `face {actor, to}` (`src/world_events.rs`). The brief allowed an op only where a step cannot be
  expressed otherwise. `move` turns an actor along its walk, so a crew would arrive at the dance spot with its back
  to the prince and stand at home with its back to the rival, while the original script turns each side on arrival
  (Sanctuary `HalloweenDanceOff.lua` `turnArrived`, read as reference). Faking it with a short `move` would
  also play the walk animation and shift the position. `face` is the `move` arrival packet without the walk:
  opcode 125 at the actor's own position with the new heading (reader `0x008e5940`, re-read this run: i16 opcode,
  u64 guid, then the position and heading floats; T460 checked the fields). The shared turn maths moved into
  `facing()`, used by `advance` and `face`. The loader refuses a non-finite target or one beyond 1e6 m.
- `tests/halloween_dance_off.rs` (5 tests) and one `face` unit test; two existing assertions that the shipped file
  was empty now say "loads" (`world_events.rs`) and "holds the dance-off" (`schedule.rs`).

## Timeline (all times from the run's start; (R) unless a client fact is named)

- 0 s: spawn all actors but the cakes (no poof), broadcast 432431 (radius 120 m). Round r starts at the end of
  round r-1; the first at 0.
- Round: banter `say`s over 10 s, then the side `move`s at 1.5 m/s (leader to the dance spot, the crew into a row
  behind him), `face` toward the prince once the slowest walker has arrived (distance / speed, rounded up, plus
  500 ms for the tick). Then area on, tag 5120 on the boombox, a `cycle_anim` over the side's four actors (the round's
  moves, in sequence, every 8 s, no jitter, until the song's length) and the rival leader's taunts. At song end:
  area off, tag off, `base_anim` stand, 2 s, `move` home, `face` toward the rival. Rounds alternate V, W, V, W, V, W.
- Songs (area / seconds / moves): 4299/19/[3551], 4300/18/[3531-3534], 4302/29/[3521-3525],
  4303/27/[35010-35050], 4304/21/[3511-3514], 4305/26/[3501].
- Ending at E (the last `face`): E+2 s broadcast 432406; `one_of` at E: the prince says 432432 or 432407 at +4 s, the
  winner's leader his lines at +6 s (+8 s), the loser's leader his at +9 s (+11 s), then at +13 s the prince's 432433

## (R) choices and open points

- 30 minutes (the Blackspore guide says the wiki has 15), 2-minute kickoff lead, fixed crew spots (Sanctuary scatters
  them at random), walk waits computed from the distances, `Halloween` not `halloween` (the shipped season name).
- Broadcast text keeps Sanctuary's "Pumpkin Prince: " prefix (a broadcast has no speaker); the client line is the rest.
- The broadcast centre: T460's radius is around the first actor, so the boombox is first (0.9 m from Sanctuary's fixed
  centre (-2384, 1211)), radius 120 m.
- The brief said Sanctuary leaves the winner lines out and everything leaves with a poof; the pack found both
  half wrong: Sanctuary does say per-side lines (we also use 432407 and 432432, which it lacks) and removes
  with no poof. We follow the brief's poof (21).
- `dance` `play_anim` is one-shot per 8 s (T460's `cycle_anim`); the original sets a looping base animation. If the
  clip is shorter than 8 s the dancers stand between moves. Native check.
- Whether the viewer needs to be within 30 m of a speaker to see the bubbles is T460's rule; the dance spot is 12-17 m
  from the prince.
- Coupling with T493 (Bruce's concert, running now): `skip_if_slot_matches` names `bruce_snowhill` and
  `bruce_wugachug`, and a schedule that skips an event without a schedule is dropped at startup with a log line
  (T462). So on this tip alone the dance-off cannot be scheduled (`realm event start` works); it schedules once T493
  lands. Test 5 adds those two slot rows (240 min, offsets 0 and 120) only when the shipped file lacks them, so it
  holds before and after. Both tasks edit `world_events.json` and the "shipped file" assertions: whichever merges
  second needs its two `events` rows joined.
- Combat markers: Models.txt describes the werewolf crew and Jake (3293-3296) as "Halloween Combat NPC"; we never
  derive roles from model descriptions (`tests/piece_051.rs` counts them as combat actors elsewhere).

## Threat note

Nothing here is reachable by a player: the event runs from the operator's content file (admin start or the clock).
The new op is bounded at load (finite target within 1e6 m, same as `move`); a hostile or careless file cannot make
`face` panic (no horizontal offset keeps the heading). No client message selects a text, id or target.
