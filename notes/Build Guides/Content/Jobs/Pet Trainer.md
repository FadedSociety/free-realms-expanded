# Pet Trainer

Part of [[Content]] > Jobs. Engine guide for how the client runs pets, the trick gesture round and the pet packets: [[Pets]] (the "Pets build guide", cited below as **Pets guide**). Related: [[Mounts]], [[NPCs - Cobblestone Village]], [[NPCs - Stillwater Crossing]], [[NPCs - Highroad Junction]], [[NPCs - Sanctuary]], [[NPCs - Shrouded Glade]], [[NPCs - Seaside]].

## How to read this page

- Tags: **[client]** is the game's locale text (`en_us_data.dat`; numbers in parentheses such as `(950749466)` are locale ids), plus the mapped client code in the Pets guide where a line says "Pets guide". **[video]** is player recordings (frame key `video/frame`). **[wiki]** is the fan wiki archive. Precedence is client, then video, then wiki. Each client title has a `ucdt` entry and, usually, a `ugdt` twin with the same text.
- **Matching caveat.** The locale stores quest titles and quest text as separate entries with no id link. Every objective or dialog line below was matched to a title by wording. The "Text match" column says how: **exact** (the string repeats the title's wording, or names the title's own NPC and task), **pun** (the title is wordplay on a trick name and the trick's strings fit), or **inferred** (the story fits but nothing names the title). A dagger (†) means "fits, not proven". The client has no quest prerequisite or reward table that we could read, so quest order comes from the wiki list and the dialog.
- **Rewards (coins, XP, items) for every quest are in no source** unless a cell names one. Pet Trainer XP is "stars"; no quest star value is known.
- **No older vault note, OpenSourceFreeRealms or FreeRealms-Legacy data was used.** From the Pets guide only lines labelled client code or client locale were used.
- **Names.** Only NPC names, and the names of NPC-owned pets that the client locale itself holds (for example the trainers' tutorial dogs), are recorded. Pet names seen in video are player-chosen and are left out.

## Overview

- **Job name.** The client spelling is "Pet Trainer" (132675296 ucdt, 1921948481 ugdt), the same as the wiki and the one video job bar. No variant spelling of the job name exists in the client. [client] [wiki Pet Trainer] [video `nsDhkn9N8K0/00796`]
- Pet Trainer is a **freestyle, members-only** job. Its experience comes from teaching tricks to a pet, using the trick gesture game (see Pet training activities). [wiki Pet Trainer] [wiki Jobs] [client 735151418: "You and your pet will only level up from tricks when you're in the pet trainer job. The higher level pet trainer you are, the faster you'll be able to teach your pet tricks."]
- **Max level 20.** The client has "Reach Level 20 as a Pet Trainer." (1473751301), "Pet Trainer: Level 20" (1733289007) and "Achieve Level 20 as a Pet Trainer" (2758884932), and the last trick unlocks at level 20. [client] The wiki Jobs page says the maximum is 20 (4 for non-members, for most jobs). [wiki Jobs]
- **How to unlock.** Talk to any of three junior trainers: Zachery (Stillwater Crossing), Majorie (Cobblestone Village) or Mercy Merrywings (Highroad Junction). The client has "Learn how to be a pet trainer by finding any trainer." (3542364000), "Speak with Majorie in Cobblestone Village to learn how to become a Pet Trainer" (1269757578) and the quest title "Become a Pet Trainer" (3702038637). [client] [wiki Pet Trainer]
- **Quest line shape.** Every new trainer first plays the three-part pet tutorial (Basics, Happiness, Training) with the trainer's own dog. Then the common series "Speak Up!" and "To Sanctuary!" send the player to Roscoe in Sanctuary. The main series moves through Roscoe (Sanctuary), Vidor Stormshroud (Shrouded Glade), Verill Shiverwings (Highroad Junction), Moanna (Seaside) and the Master Trainer Kirill Moonrunner (Sanctuary). Each stop teaches one or two tricks and is gated by a level check at 10, 15 and 20. [wiki Pet Trainer] [client]
- **Counts.** The client has 28 quest titles starting "Pet Trainer:" (19 are on the wiki list, 9 are client-only), 3 titles "Pet Tutorial: ..." and 5 level titles "Pet Trainer: Level N". [client]
- **Tricks are the progression.** Ten dog and cat tricks unlock at fixed job levels (client "unlocks at Pet Trainer Level N" strings): Speak 1, Sit Up 5, Jump 5, Peek-a-Boo 10, Roll Over 10, Box 10, Handstand 15, Spin 15, Dance 20, Play Dead 20. See Pet tricks.
- **Removed from the live game in 2009 per the wiki.** The wiki says the job was removed on 14 December 2009 but stayed usable for accounts that already had it, and that a 2013 SOE Live announcement note "Hints at a Pet Trainer job return, but in a slightly different form." [wiki Pet Trainer] [wiki Free Realms] [wiki SOE Live] The client still holds the whole job, and one video frame shows Pet Trainer level 2 on the job bar. [client] [video `nsDhkn9N8K0/00796`] For a revival the job should be built in full.
- **Quest rewards.** No coin, XP or item reward was found for any Pet Trainer quest. The only reward text is the trick "Play Dead" from Kirill (1939693444). See Gaps. [client]

### Pet tutorial (three quests, same text for each trainer)

The tutorial is played in its own instanced area with an exit door. Area titles "Pet Tutorial - Cobblestone Village" (985862161), "Pet Tutorial - Highroad Junction" (1812050759) and "Pet Tutorial - Stillwater Crossing" (3533441821) exist as `ucdt` only †. The exit text is "Thanks for learning about pets! You can use the magic door if you're ready to leave, or you can hang out and try those tutorials again." (2945515630). ### How a trick is learned (client flow)

1. The player owns a pet and has finished the pet tutorial; otherwise a trainer refuses: "You must have a pet in addition to completing the pet tutorial" (1129498571). [client]
2. Job level unlocks the trick: "You unlock more tricks as you level your pet trainer job." (766532816); "You will earn new tricks when you level up as a Pet Trainer." (3970059737). Server messages: "[*pet*] can learn [*trick*]!" (1531558413) and "[*pet*] has gotten better at [*trick*]!" (165527326). [client] In the mapped code these are the `PetTrickAdd` and `PetTrickSkillLevel` packets. [client; Pets guide "Tricks and the gesture round"]
3. The trick is equipped under the pet's play menu, then the paw icon next to the pet's name opens the trick list, and clicking the trick starts the trick game: "Make sure you have the "Jump" trick equipped under your pet's play menu, then return to the world and click the paw icon next to your pet's name. Click the "Jump" trick icon and you are ready to start the trick game!" (279712; one such string per trick). "When you click one of the tricks, a brief tutorial will walk you through the steps." (766532816). [client]
4. Practice raises the trick: "Keep practicing the same trick to gain stars for you and your pet." (366484894); "Great work! All pets will get better at their tricks the more they practice them." (3877778191). Tricks have ranks: "Have your pet attain Rank 4 in one or more pet tricks" (994768897). [client]
5. Progress only counts while in the Pet Trainer job, and the job level speeds it: (735151418). The trick sender also refuses while the owner is busy. [client; Pets guide]
6. A quest trainer lends a pet of the right species when the player has none (Rex, Buddy †, June Bug, Sparrow, Juniper, Tuffy). [client]
7. Tricks are used out in the world as pet actions: "You use tricks out in the game world." (2565945908).

## Pet training activities and minigames

- **The trick game (gesture round).** The player traces a shape on screen: "Teach your pet to do a trick by tracing the shape drawn on the screen with your mouse. The better and quicker you trace the shape, the better your pet will do the trick!" (2918893530). [client] In the mapped code, `PetGesture` loads `PetTrickGesturePoints.txt` (147 waypoint rows for 23 trick ids, unit-square coordinates) and `PetTrickGestureSettings.txt`; the round shows "Get Ready!" and "Draw!" (the same texts exist as 3327509820 and 1587594077, id link unproven), ends on completion, a 10 second timeout or a 20 second hard stop, and is scored locally from scale, centre error, direction and time into grades 0 to 3 that pick one of four result strings. The grade is not sent to the server. Which gesture shape belongs to which trick name is not in the client files. [client; Pets guide "Tricks and the gesture round" and "Data and formats"] The top grade is called "Amazing!" in quest text (row 17). [client]
- **Result feedback lines** (unmapped to grades †): "That was a good try, but we should probably practice that trick a few more times." (3986164062); "See? That was easy! Keep practicing and your pet will get better at the trick!" (1343778850); "Great job! Tricks are a good way to show off what a cool pet owner you are." (1120654161); "Let's try that trick again." (3408735010); "Ok, now do the trick over again." (2264085550). [client]
- **Pet care loop (Happiness tutorial).** Feeding, grooming (a scrubbing action) and playing keep the pet happy; a happy pet runs faster beside the owner. Ranks 1 to 5 of "Pet Happiness Speed Boost" exist (1807103391, 2960148279, 124481750, 1480008914, 213157072; "Speed boost given when your pet is happy", 2620923750).
