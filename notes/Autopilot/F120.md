# F120: T029 fishing hour-cap reconnect blocker

## Review finding

The single BLOCKER in `277c27a-T029.md` was correct: `FishingService::reset_all()` cleared
both the session count and the rolling-hour timestamps, and a fresh `Gameplay` started with
no history. A character could therefore reconnect and bypass an enabled hourly cap.

## What changed

- `src/fishing_service.rs`: reconnect now clears only `PaidCatches.session`. The rolling-hour
  timestamps survive `disconnect`, `reset` and `reset_all`. `hour_history` and
  `restore_hour_history` transfer the character ledger; restore rejects an oversized or
  out-of-order history and leaves the existing ledger unchanged on refusal.
- `src/gameplay.rs`, `src/gateway.rs`: narrow accessors connect the service ledger to a
  fresh loaded instance without adding it to `GameplayState`.
- `src/bin/osfr-gateway.rs`: `SharedWorld` owns `fishing_hours`, keyed by character. Each
  message restores the gateway's ledger from that state, and each committed outcome copies
  the updated history back. This survives reconnect and save reload for the server process
  lifetime. Committed updates also discard entries whose timestamps left the hour window.
- `data/content/fishing_spots.json`: the cap provenance text now states the hour ledger is
  keyed by character and is not refreshed by reconnect.
- `tests/fishing_entry.rs`: service-level regression proves reconnect clears only the
  session count, preserves hour history, refuses a malformed restore without mutation, and
  resumes payment after expiry.
- `src/bin/osfr-gateway.rs`: gateway regression drives two real sessions over one shared
  world: paid catch, save, fresh load from disk, capped refusal within the hour with unchanged
  snapshot/file bytes, then a paid catch after the hour expires. Inventory, coins and
  Fisherman XP are unchanged by the refusal.

## Reconstructed rules (R)

- The optional hour cap remains an operator policy, not recovered original behavior.
- Hour history is character-scoped and survives reconnect; session-window counts reset with
  the login session.
- A blocked catch takes the no-payout escape path and does not request a save.
- The process-lifetime ledger is not durable across a server restart.
