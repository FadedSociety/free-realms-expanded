## How to read this page

- Tags: **[client]** is the game's locale text (`en_us_data.dat`), **[video]** is player recordings, **[wiki]** is the fan wiki. Precedence is client, then video, then wiki. Where they disagree, the higher source is in the table and the clash is in **Conflicts**.
- Numbers in parentheses, for example `(1942984500)`, are client locale ids. The "Title ids" column gives both title entries for a quest as `ucdt` / `ugdt`; the two tags carry the same text.
- Video frame keys look like `(l-KJnbZShSk/00018)`.
- **Matching caveat.** The locale file stores quest titles, descriptions, objectives and dialog as separate entries with no link between them. Every description or objective below was matched to its quest by wording (names, places, enemies), not by a data link. Matches that rest on wording alone are marked with a dagger (†). A dagger means "fits this quest, not proven". Strings that fit no listed quest are in **Unmatched client text**.
- **Video coverage of the quest line is nil.** No frame in the video archive shows an Archer-titled quest, any Archer trainer, or a quest reward. The video is used only for where Archers were seen playing and for item and job-window facts. Every quest's "video" evidence is therefore "none" unless a cell says otherwise.
- Rewards (coins, XP, items) do not appear in the locale file. The only quest rewards known are four bows named on wiki item pages. Every other reward cell reads "unknown".
- The client spells some titles differently from the wiki. The client spelling is used, with the wiki spelling noted in the quest cell where it differs.

## Overview

Archer is a combat job that fights at range with bows. [wiki] The wiki quote for the job: "Archers specialize in long-distance attacks using a bow and arrow, so you can strike before your target even knows it's in your sight! You can attack a single creature or use explosive arrows to attack multiple enemies at the same time. Archers are trained by Lugabow, the defender of the Greenwood Forest." [wiki] Experience comes from Archer quests and battles in the Wilds and in dungeons. [wiki] The client's own wording of the job's start is "Learn how to be an archer by finding Lugabow in Greenwood Forest." (4230418877) [client].

**Unlocking.** Speak to Lugabow at the Greenwood Camp. [client][wiki] The wiki places the camp north of the [[Bandit Hideout]] and west of the Lavender Coast Pass warpstone. [wiki] The client says "Southwest of Sanctuary lives Lugabow, who trains those wishing to lug a bow." (2313781218) and "Speak with Lugabow at the archer camp near Lavender Coast Pass to learn how to become an Archer" (2858904238). [client] The first quest, **Path of the Archer**, makes the player talk to Lugabow's recruits; Lugabow then grants the job: "I hereby take you under my wing to teach you the ways of the archer. Congratulations. I have more tasks for you. Don your archer gear and then speak with me when you are ready." (2967121729) †. [client] The achievement text "[player] has successfully unlocked the Archer job, taking aim at mastering the profession!" (1934712498) and the objective "Obtain the Archer job" (609239670) also exist. [client]

- Two entry quests point the player at Lugabow: **Being a Better Archer** (objective "Learn how to advance your skill as an Archer by speaking with Lugabow in Greenwood Forest" 1598517969 †) and **Aiming For Archery** (no description matched). The pattern is confirmed by the Wizard version, "Being a Better Wizard", seen in video with objective "Speak to Fizzlesticks near Lakeshore" (CW9hNMMNV8w/00004). [client][video]
- No source shows alternate starting areas for Archer, unlike [[Brawler]]: the wiki and client both name only Lugabow. [client][wiki]

**Members.** The wiki says "As of March 16th, 2013, the Archer job is no longer a Members Only job" but the page keeps the Members Only category. [wiki] The client has an upsell "Oh, no! Want to keep playing as an Archer? Upgrade to unlock more fun!" (2768735996). [client] See **Conflicts**. The page is set to `members_only: false` on the wiki's dated statement.

**Levels.** The job runs from level 1 to 20 (**max_level 20**). [wiki][client] Client level goals exist for 4, 5, 8, 10, 12, 15, 16 and 20 (see **Level gates**). The level 20 achievement title is "The Sniper Extraordinaire" (wiki Achievements; client 3326340197). [wiki][client] Completing the whole line grants the achievement "Complete the Archer Questline." (768265311, 2488607550) with title "Marksman" (4237213119, ugdt 4068916014). [client][wiki]

**Level 20 unlocks Ninja.** Client text: "Requires: Level 20 Archer" in the Lakeshore Ninja advert (1592607723) and "Achieve level 20 as an Archer and return to me to begin your Ninja training!" (2334013303). [client] See [[Ninja]].

**Level gates.** The main line stops at four level checks: Hilforn at 5, Bullseye at 10, Bowman Bilter at 15, Bullseye again at 20. [client][wiki] Each has its own client lines (see **Trainers** and the quest tables).

## Conflicts

1. **A Stunning Effect vs A Piercing Attack.** Wiki lists "Archer: A Stunning Effect" between Paralyzing Poison and Suspicious Smuggler, and its item page says the quest gave the Pro Archer Bow. The client has **no** "A Stunning Effect" title but does have "Archer: A Piercing Attack" (ucdt 2811831921 / ugdt 1865438696) and cray marauder text with both "pierce their shells" (2707686906) and "try your stunning shot" (1689438919). Client title is used; the two are treated as one slot. Not proven.
2. **Title spelling.** Client: "No Arrows, No Work!", "No Bow, No Work!", "Suspicious Smugglers", "Attack From Above", "Can You Hit a Bullseye?". Wiki: no exclamation marks, "Suspicious Smuggler", "Attack from Above", "Can You Hit a Bullseye". Client titles are used. The wiki also writes "Archer: Prevention First - from Mr. Miles", which is a giver note, not part of the title.
