## Overview

Blackspore Swamp is the dark, gloomy region in the south-east corner of Sacred Grove. The only ways in are the two tunnels near Crossroads and Stillwater Crossing, or a warpstone. [wiki Blackspore Swamp] The client agrees that Blackspore is "to the southeast of Sanctuary" (744295510), that the entrance is "a large cave directly east of Cobblestone Village" (4283728578, 3456908047) and that Briarwood lies just north of it (30189753). The client uses the short name **Blackspore** nearly everywhere; the long name "Blackspore Swamp" is the zone title (895876724, 2321951309) and the quest text for the Brawler, Ninja and Card Duelist lines. [client] [wiki]

Places named by the client and used below: **Mire's Edge** (3872110846), the main outpost, which is "slowly sinking into the swamp" (1614606259); the **Ancient's Wall** (647520803); the **Sinking Graveyard** (138049231; quest text also says "sunken graveyard", "located south of Mire's Edge", 1402669240); the **Quagmire Quarry** (3617182465, "south of here" from the camp, 439252323); **Oberyn's Retreat** (4228056886); **Mucklewhump's Ferry** (3956739609); **Splortata's Camp** (2712869446) and **Splortata's Timeout Spot** (1090543211); the **Isle of Tears** (1186894833, 4159527668: "that island out there, covered in ruins"); **Ramirez's Dig Site** (1264169343); the **Blackspore Pet Adoption Center** (2662607998); the **Blackspore Warpstone** (3165592831); and the Bone Bog Cemetery gate (526698436). The wiki adds the Guild House near Mire's Edge, the Shadowtalon Dojo in the south-east corner and the Checkers & Chess table (no client string for the first two). [client] [wiki] The wiki says Mucklewhump's Ferry holds old man Mucklewhump and his dog and sits in the north-east. [wiki Mucklewhump's Ferry, Exploration (Collections)]

The region has three clusters of NPCs. **Mire's Edge**, a camp run by Donovan Shadeweaver, holds the Blackspore Swamp Series quest givers, the job trainers Chloe and Petra, the merchants and the Realmkeeper-era Card Duelist givers. **Quagmire Quarry** holds the miners. The **swamp and the graveyard** hold Eggert, Oberyn, the dig-site men and the Halloween event NPCs. Event and boss NPCs are in Parts F and G.

## Notes on quest givers and trainers

**Donovan Shadeweaver and Mire's Edge.** Donovan "runs the show around here" (912846612) and likes everyone in camp "working for him, or being too afraid to cross him" (4134681675). Players arrive from the warpstone or a tunnel, hear him named by an unnamed greeter, and are then walked through Rumor, Darci, Ramirez, the cray, the quarry and finally Iliya and Lukov. See the series table. [client] [wiki]

**Job trainers.** Chloe starts the Ninja Band 1 line (to level 5) and Band 2 pollen and spirit work before sending the player to Frostpetal ([[Ninja]]). Petra runs Brawler Band 1 and Band 2 (to level 10, then Snowhill) ([[Brawler]]). Silverray runs Miner Band 4 and Blacksmith Band 4 ([[Miner]], [[Blacksmith]]). Iliya is the Wizard link between Sandbur and Sven ([[Wizard]]). Madam Zelda and Tyrone Shadowshard are Card Duelist givers ([[Card Duelist]]). The Archers (Bullseye, Bowman Bilter) stand outside the gates (Part I).

**Eggert and Milford.** Eggert is a changeling alchemist who needs poisonous ingredients: poisonous purple toads, bogwart, strangleroot, Grave Blossoms. His apprentice Milford dances in the swamp (3731835062). The wiki says the player first finds Milford and is then offered a full series. The Adventurer storyline uses Eggert as a hand-in (E19 to E21) and the video shows the rescue fight "Save Eggert the Mad from the Grave Elementals." with Vittorio's charge attacks. [client] [video] [wiki Eggert the Mad]

**Quagmire Quarry.** Silverray is the foreman, Dalton runs the camp, Ramberry complains about the bars, Vrock researches the haunted mine and Hiram is trapped inside it. The quarry quests are also the third and fourth band of the Miner line. Quarry text sets the story as the old dwarven outpost that vanished and now has ghost dwarves (Block Loblog text 2717679151). [client] [wiki Quagmire Quarry]

**Super Spooktacular in Blackspore.** Lucinda and Lord William IV give the Sacred Grove Pumpkin Prince chain (A Ghostly Discovery, Guardians of the Tomb, Sealed with a Signet, The Pumpkin Prince). Madam Zelda gives A Dark Future and the two team chains; Drake and Ed Sullenfangs and Lon and Jake Blackforest give the dance-practice side quests. The wiki also has a Vampires vs. Werewolves dance-off battle every 15 minutes outside the Bone Bog gate. [client] [wiki Super Spooktacular]
