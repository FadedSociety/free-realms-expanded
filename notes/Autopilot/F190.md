The review (`lab/autopilot/reviews/be5091e-F163.md`) had one blocker and four
minors. All were addressed and no finding was wrong. One suggested fix could not
be applied literally: the review asked the quantity test to take a bounded prize

## Security threat note

A hostile client can replay `41/108` Go, forge encounter/instance values, send
arbitrary activity ids to portal Play, spam attacks, or replay results Close.
F190 adds no player-triggerable path and no wire parser: it deletes a test hook
and changes tests and notes. The F163 preflight remains server-side: the Go body
is not trusted, the battle id comes from the server-owned pending screen, and
prize ids/quantities come from operator content, never from the client. Both a
missing definition and a quantity above `max_stack` are refused before zoning,
instance allocation or any grant; the refusal restores the same screen and
leaves the saved state byte-identical (snapshot equality). Item ids and coin
grants stay checked (`grant_bundle` stages a clone; `grant_coins` refuses
overflow).

Residual (review minor 4): the preflight is catalogue-only. A full inventory
(`MAX_ITEMS` 4096) that needs a new stack, or a coin grant that would exceed
`i32::MAX`, can still pass entry and fail atomically at payout. Nothing is
granted, and the per-session `contain`/`end_session` boundary contains the
error (pinned by the F163 reward-failure test and this task's tick test), but
the battle was entered and won before the refusal. A future state-aware
preflight should take the coin component and the live item count into account.
