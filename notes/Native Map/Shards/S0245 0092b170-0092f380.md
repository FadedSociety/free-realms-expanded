## Functions and subsystems

- `0092b170` is `PlayClient::Initialize` (slot 18 in both `PlayClient` and `BaseClient` vtables). It is a large startup path: selects admin/play mode, configures crash reporting and settings, initializes asset delivery/display/UI resources, and constructs many managers. Its strings include `FreeRealms PlayClient`, `InitAssetDelivery failed`, and `The client will be connecting to a server`. It also sets up the `MissingWorld.eqg`/`TileSeamless` fallback when the world setting is absent. The exported pseudocode does not resolve every manager type.
- `0092e550` refreshes an unidentified two-part timing snapshot and associated state. Four position-update variants and the jump sender call it; exact timer semantics are unknown.
- `0092e5d0`, `0092e670`, `0092e860`, `0092ea40`, `0092ebf0`, and `0092eda0` construct and send player-update objects in connected mode. RTTI vtables identify `PlayerUpdatePacketFlourish`, `PlayerUpdatePacketJump`, and `PlayerUpdatePacketUpdatePosition`. The position variants differ in low flag bits (`2`, `2|mode-bit`, `0x82|mode-bit`, `0x42|mode-bit`) and copy related position/actor fields. Their packet classes are absent from the current packet catalogue, so no catalogue mapping or server interpretation is asserted.
- `0092ef50` and `0092f260` occupy PlayClient vtable slots 37 and 41 and construct profile-related command packets (`CommandPacketSetProfileByItemDefinitionId`, `CommandPacketActivateProfileFailed`). Both conditionally pass them to a sender. Their classes are absent from the current packet catalogue; no opcode or server contract is assigned.
- `0092efe0` is PlayClient slot 40. It checks client/target state, scans a target list, applies a repeated-sample/time gate, computes a relative vector, invokes a target interface callback, and sends a catalogue-identified `CommandPacketInteractRequest`. On a separate rejection branch it displays `Interactions.PendingGaqInvitationWarning` / “Your have been invited to a minigame. Please accept or reject the invitation first!”. This is evidence for a client-side gate and warning, not for server-side authorization policy.
- `0092f300` constructs and sends `CommandPacketClosedMinigameEndScreen`. Address-specific evidence in `Research/Native Crafting Results Close.md` says its dedicated sender serializes two u16 values, 26 and 42 (`1a002a00`). That class is missing from the current packet catalogue; the note supports packet identity and bytes but does not make the function's pseudocode contain those opcode constants.
- `0092f380` constructs/sends catalogue packet `CommandPacketSetChatBubbleColor` and copies the three supplied values to local client offsets `+0x71c/+0x720/+0x724`. Its caller `00c167f0` is the Lua binding `SetChatColorFgBgSz`.
