## Client evidence (checked in IDA this pass)

- 35/27 `PlayerUpdatePacketThoughtBubble`: ctor `008b4870` (calls the opcode-35 family ctor `0089fbb0(0x1b)`, vtable `0180230c`).
  The reader is `008d7880` (called by `008f80a0` at `00933164`; base words by `008d6830`, two u16): a u64 actor guid (+0x10/+0x14),
  an i32 at +0x18, an i32 at +0x1c and a u8 `!= 0` at +0x20. The dispatcher `0092f460` (jump table `0092f540`, case 27 at
  `00933124`) looks the actor up by guid (`00984960`, the table at client +0xc0) and calls `0096c120` at `009331cd` with
  (+0x18, +0x1c, +0x20). So the wire is u64 guid, i32 icon, i32 seconds, u8 clear, 21 bytes with the header.
- `0096c120` (the brief's `ShowThoughtBubbleAndTrack`): when the actor's owner handle (+0x738) resolves to an object whose guid
  (+0x88/+0x8c) equals the actor's owner field (+0x78/+0x7c) it raises the Lua event `PetsHandler:ThoughtBubble` (string
  `0180abd8`, `009797a0`) with the three values; `ScriptsBase.lua:22998` has `ThoughtBubble = function(self, iconId, time, clear) end`,
  empty. Independently of that it stops the actor's previous bubble (`00960300` on the id kept at +0x5f8) and, when the flag is 0,
  plays the icon id as an actor composite effect: `00960170(icon, 0, -1)` -> `00bf6770`, which looks the id up in the effect-definition
  hash (`01025390`, key at node +32; the same table `0092f460` uses for 35/16 and the effect tags) and returns without error when the id is
  missing (an effect not yet loaded is queued by `00960170`'s else branch and flushed by `009630e0`). It keeps icon, seconds and the start time
  (`00702670`) at actor +0x5f8, +0x5fc, +0x600. **The icon is therefore a composite-effect id**, shown on the actor itself; seconds > 0
  starts an expiry clock on the actor, and the server never needs the clear flag.

## Behaviour

- **Settled.** A pet is settled when the follow step has parked it beside a still owner (`PetPose::settled`) and it is not in water, and
  no feed, groom or trick runs. A walk, a warp, the pet window (53/24 mode), water or a care action clears it. Content without a follow
  block never settles, so such a pet never idles (bubbles still run).
- **Idle.** Settled for `idle_after_ms` (10 s, R): one random animation from the model's `animations` (or, with `bathroom_chance` 0.1, from
  `bathroom`), then the next after `idle_min_ms`..`idle_max_ms` (30..60 s). One animation per window; the next waits for the current one's run time.
- **Rest.** Settled for `rest_after_ms` (90 s, R): a random posture, its loop as the base animation and the enter transition over it; then a
  fidget every 30..60 s and no more idle animations. When the pet stops being settled it stands up: base `loc_stand`, then the exit transition.
  The timers start over when it settles again.
- **Shake-off.** Leaving water (53/28 false after true) arms one shake; the pet plays `shake_off` once it is settled (so after a walk out of
  the water). Entering water, or a "false" with no water before, arms nothing.
- **Bubbles.** `bubble_min_ms`..`bubble_max_ms` (45..60 s) after the first tick of a summon, then again after every bubble: the lowest of food,
  groom, play (food, then groom, then play on a tie) under `need_bubble_threshold` (25) shows its icon (F809: 5524 hunger, 5241 hygiene,
  10 play); else a mood under `mood_bubble_threshold` (25) shows 5099. A vanity pet shows none. 35/27 carries `bubble_seconds` (10, R:
  OSFR's ambient bubble). Needs are the live decayed view `care_tick` already sends as 53/3. Mood is the need average today, so a low mood
  with no low need cannot happen yet (the sad icon is reachable once mood decouples; the test splits the two thresholds to reach it).
- Everything goes to the owner (`private`) and the viewers (`shared`, forwarded as `nearby`), nothing is saved and `changed` stays false.
- Every choice comes from a per-summon RNG: `PetService::set_idle_seed(seed)`, mixed with the owner and the summon time, so tests replay any session.

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client controls two inputs here: 53/28 (water) and its position updates. 53/28 can arm one shake-off per real leave of water (a
"false" with no water before arms nothing: tested with 1,000 messages); toggling water every tick gives at most one 35/8 per shake run time
(1.4 s), the same order as the client's own movement traffic, and nothing is granted. Walking or jittering only clears the settled flag, which
sends at most one stand-up pair, once. Nothing the client sends reaches an icon, an animation id, a duration or a timer: all of them come from
validated content (ids > 0, run times 1 ms..60 s, list sizes bounded, gaps 1 s..1 h, threshold 0..100, chance 0..1) and the server clock
(`saturating_add`). A bubble is rebuilt from the decayed needs, so a clock that ran backwards (`decayed` returns `None`) shows none and never
errors the tick. No coins, items, XP or saves are touched.

## Checklist

1. A refusal changes nothing: the idle life has no request that can refuse; the only write from the wire is `IdleState::water`, which arms a
   flag on a pet that was in water. Hostile 53/28 spam is tested to change nothing (no save; repeated "out" while dry arms nothing, and
   F809 adds a rapid true/false toggle that still yields exactly one shake).
2. Rollback covers all state: no transaction; the state is one `Copy` field of `ActivePet`, rebuilt by every summon.
3. Deltas: nothing is restored from a snapshot; the roster is never written (asserted: `document_value` equal after five minutes of idling).
4. Busy rules: idle never runs during a feed, groom or trick (`ActivePet::interacting`, tested for a trick) and never in water; no new
   activity, so the `Occupation` table is untouched.
5. Entry paths: the tick is the only entry (`care_tick`, from `Gameplay::pet_tick`); the water arm is reached only by 53/28. Tested through
   `Gameplay::pet_tick` as well as the service.
6. Reachable and tested through production: `tests/pet_idle.rs` drives `follow_owner` + `care_tick` (what `pet_tick` runs), 53/4, 53/7, 53/28
   through `handle_at_ms`, and one test goes through `Gameplay::load`, `handle_in_world` and `pet_tick` with the owner's replies and the
   viewers' `nearby`.
7. Survives reconnect: session-only by design (a reload has no state to keep); a dismiss and a re-summon start over (tested).
8. Every exit: a pet that lies down stands up when the owner moves, enters water or starts a care action; dismiss, zoning and disconnect drop the
   actor (and with it the state), so no animation or timer is left running on a new pet (tested: dismiss while lying down, then re-summon).
9. Economy: nothing is granted, bought or saved.
10. Wire matches the client reader: 35/27 `008b4870`/`008d7880` (bytes asserted from the reader's field order, 21 bytes, clear 0); 35/8 is the
    existing writer (asserted by length, guid, slot, flag).
11. Unique ids: none added; every packet targets the existing pet actor guid.
12. Others see changes: every idle packet is in `shared` and `private` alike (asserted per tick by `Session::tick`, and through `nearby` in the
    Gameplay test). F809: a late viewer's spawn carries the active rest loop as a base 35/8 (`PetPresence::spawn_packets`, tested).
13. End to end: the Gameplay test asserts a dog's idle animations and a hungry dog's icon; the rest and stand-up paths and the shake-off are
    asserted as packets in the service-level tests, not through `Gameplay`.
14. Values in data: `care.idle` and each model's `idle`; only the sanity bounds, the client's subtype numbers and `ANIM_ID_STAND` are code.
15. Canaries break production code (each run against `tests/pet_idle.rs`, then restored): see below.
16. Session plumbing: untouched.
17. Tick safety: `idle_tick` returns nothing and never errors; every addition is `saturating_add`; a backwards clock shows no bubble; a model
    with no rows is disabled until the next settle, never a panic (tested).
18. Type rules: `PetIdleSet::valid` and `IdleConfig::valid` are on their types; `IdleState` documents each field and `water()` owns the arming rule.
19. Hot path: per tick one copy of the small `ActivePet`, a few comparisons and a write-back, no clone of content. A quiet tick allocates
    nothing; a tick where something is due allocates the one or two packet vectors it sends. The model's rows are read (a scan of at most
    165 models) only when a timer is due, once per 30 s or more; a model without rows is looked up once per settle.
