## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

Hostile input: a client can send `33/17` claims with any guid, NaN/teleport position and any replay
rate, timed before Go, after the window, or in-window; it can also flood `33/6` bulk frames on the
reliable path or the recovered direct `5/1` tunnel. Before F181 every evaluated pickup frame
incremented the persisted observation counter, so `KartGateway::handle` reported a change and the
gateway wrote a character save per frame (up to `max_observations` = 120,000 saves in one race);
accepted `33/6` frames did the same.

Why it is refused now: the guard is a plain `u32` inside `KartService`, covered by the staged
`Gameplay` clone but never serialized. After the ownership and phase checks, every evaluated
pickup frame spends one slot before the race-window check, so timing a claim after the window
does not dodge the bound. Exhaustion returns `ObservationBudget`; the frame changes no saved
state and produces no reply and no save. The unchanged authority checks (owned guid, finite
position, movement bound, queue cap, ordered gate, server clock) still decide every grant, and no
client-reported quantity reaches a reward. The gateway regression asserts the routed frames, the
empty reply list, `!state_changed && !gameplay_changed` and complete gateway-snapshot equality;
the service regressions assert complete `ServiceSnapshot` equality plus the live counter.

Reconnect: the per-session guard resets, so a hostile client can regain up to 120,000 observation
slots by reconnecting. That is the reviewer-authorized tradeoff; the persisted `attempts` cap, the
race window, the owned-guid/position checks and the ordered gate remain authoritative, and each
observation is a fixed small comparison. Two pre-existing per-frame saves remain and are not
caused by the observation guard: an in-window refused `33/7` gate claim spends the persisted,
lead-owned `attempts` budget (so it still marks a change), and an accepted `33/6` frame with
`weapon_effects` on moves the persisted weapon pose. Both are recorded under Open gaps.
