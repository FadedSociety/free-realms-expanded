## Classes and subsystems

Typed `SoeUtil::List`, `HashList`, and integer `Set` destructors appear at `009a2570`, `009a2ca0`, `009a2d90`–`009a2e10`, and `009a4cd0`.

Most remaining targets are UI-facing `InGamePurchaseDataSource` code (`ui.coinstore`).
