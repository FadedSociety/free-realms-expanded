## Conflicts

1. **Boss name.** The wiki and panel text say "Treeleaf Hedge". Quest text mostly says "Treeleaf". One entity.
2. **Wiki age.** The wiki revision is from 2013, ten years older than most dungeon pages (2025 revisions). Its stars, coin and member figures may predate later rebalancing. No newer source checks them.
3. **Wiki layout.** The wiki page has "Required: Level 9, Any Combat Job" in the older format. The client rule gives the same level.
4. **Accessory.** Difficulty 4 pages Briar Patch and others use Necklaces of Bark Skin IV. This page's wheel has Rings of Energy IV instead. That is what the wiki lists, and no other source checks it.
