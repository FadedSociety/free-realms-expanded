## Overview

Merry Vale is the fairy town: a spring-themed village of sunflowers around a big rainbow, with a stage show, a cooking table, mail boxes, launch pads and a warpstone. [wiki Merry Vale] The wiki says it is "a town south of Wugachug, west of Sanctuary, and east of Seaside", centred on the **Ever Rainbow** (a rainbow where the town's hit show is staged), and lists these points of interest: Ever Rainbow, the Merry Vale Mainstage, the Midway, the Sunflower Patch and the Rainbow Spire; shops for Chef clothing and tools (level 1 tools, level 20 clothing), Chef recipes and supplies, Potions and Warrior weapons (level 5); the local dungeon Den of Secrets; and the minigames Spot the Difference, Cooking Table and Checkers & Chess. [wiki] The wiki trivia says the town was called "Pixie Ring" early in development; no client string uses that name. [wiki]

The client agrees on the landmarks: "Merry Vale Warpstone" (327090672), "Ever Rainbow" (234121627), "The Merry Vale Mainstage" (2566314702), "The Midway" (2016009084), "Merry Vale Cooking Table" (1066410917), "Merry Vale Postboxes" (1099559043), "Merry Vale Training Hoop" (830292537) with a fiery hoop above the launch pad (738877338), the "Rainbow Fountain" (2782335200), a music stand near the stage (4098652010), "three visitor camps" (1102174313) and a "little chugawug encampment" (2689905545). It also has Rare and Elite Exploration collections for the town (309594979, 2013014340) and housing lots ("Merry Vale Lot", "Vale Stream Lot"). [client] A video frame shows the warpstone inside a fenced sunflower garden with the prompt "Merry Vale Warpstone" (`JMmHnklaj5Y/00000`, `Oa-JwM60kJQ/00000`). [video]

Where the town sits, per client sentences: Chef Jarvie is "west of the Queensfields" (2689703576); the Roadhouse is "north of Merry Vale" (2396340712, 4175888807); the Pixie Nursery is "north of Merry Vale" (2201482484); the Chugaroot Farm in Wugachug is "north of Merry Vale" (3768554252); Aster travels "the road leading south out of Merry Vale" (857744776); and three camps lie "south of Merry Vale" (Ari the Fish 616902065, the Cheetahs 3970292950, the ninja Jintaka 2916738083). The town has "three bridges" or "three exits" (north, west and south: 3112934980, 3841763330, 1311223139, 1600377261, 3244444649). [client]

Events held in the town: the **Festival of Hearts** (the wiki says February to March each year; Cudip, Stone Heart and the Heartbreakers), **Saint Patrick's** (Lucky Chance McXander; the wiki dates it from 14 March 2013), and the **Birthday Bash** (Chef Jarvie and Mayor McFairy have quests; Cakenstein "has risen in Merry Vale!", 38764221). [wiki Festival of Hearts, Saint Patrick's, Birthday Bash] [client]

## Notes: quest givers and trainers

- **Mayor McFairy** is the town's quest hub: the welcome tour, the flyer clean-up, the cake-clue and the kidnapping line all start or end with him. In the older story he is a villain; in series 4 of the Southern Wilds list the fight is titled after him and ends in the cavern with Liana. See Conflicts 2.
- **Shaggy Mysterychaser** starts the cave investigation; **Wugunbump** starts the missing-children line; **Cudip** starts the Festival of Hearts; **Luella** starts the ambassador chain.
- **Chef Jarvie** and **Postmaster Vixie** are the job trainers. **Tumbolina** and **Braldan Stoneaxe** carry the middle of the Warrior line, which the Den of Secrets crate quest finishes. **Talespinner Yvelle** is both a Wizard stop and the keeper of the fairy and heart-flower stories.
- **Joona** and the cast around him (Tyra, Joelle, Krissa, Tarvie, Brennion, Morrie) are one small story about a script, an audition and a missing set of props.
- **Rimpadun, Verna, Dubawug and Wugunbump** are the Chugawug visitors in the Chugawug camp.
- **Lucky Chance McXander** and the Festival people (Cudip, Arianna, Stone Heart, Joyce, Jecca) are seasonal: the wiki gives dates for the 2010, 2011 and 2014 festivals and for Saint Patrick's 2013.
- **Vendors** (Meridia, Miesha, Claudia, Nadia, Arel, Giggles, Braldan) sit around the warpstone and cooking table; the 2013 job-gear vendors were added on 6 August and 1 October 2013 (wiki).

## Conflicts

1. **Where the town sits.** Wiki page text: "south of Wugachug, west of Sanctuary, and east of Seaside". The same page's infobox: "East of Sanctuary". The Seaside wiki page says Seaside is "W of Merry Vale". Client: Chef Jarvie "west of the Queensfields" (2689703576), the Roadhouse and the Pixie Nursery "north of Merry Vale", the Chugaroot Farm in Wugachug "north of Merry Vale". The wiki Wilds page puts the Central Wilds "between Merry Vale, Sanctuary and Briarwood". Not resolved; no coordinates exist.
2. **Who is the boss of the Den of Secrets.** [[Den of Secrets]] uses Illusionist Liana (video-confirmed) and lists Mayor McFairy and Yuriah as an unresolved conflict. New here: the client's series 4 list has a step titled "Mayor McFairy!" (213723861) and the Den line "Mayor McFairy and his assistant Liana were involved?" (3448615816), so the Mayor fight and the Liana fight read as two parts of one story. The wiki says the Mayor is an enemy in the Den "during At the End of the Rainbow". The Den page's wording should be reconciled with this.
3. **Sammy.** [[Den of Secrets]] says only the wiki ties Sammy to the Den. The client has "Locate all of Meliee's friends! Find Sammy!" (2943354132), which ties him there.
4. **The cave north, west or northwest.** The Den of Secrets is "in the back of the cave in northern Merry Vale" (1819244747), "the cavern north of Merry Vale" (1876015264) and "the caves in northwest Merry Vale" (3742381212); Lugunwump was last near "the cave on the west side" (1467691), and Stone Heart's Hideaway is "on the western side" (3155888320). These may be different caves; not settled.
5. **Pixie Nursery ambassador giver.** [[NPCs - Wilds]] lists "Ambassador to Pixie Nursery" (1144527425) with Oxalis Shamrock as the probable giver. The client has cut twins "NOT LIVE - Ambassador to ..." for six legs and the Pixie Nursery leg targets Starshine, Peacepetal and Greenthumb (4146796987), all inside Luella's chain. The Oxalis link (1506221538) is unproven.
6. **Michelle.** The client has "Michelle Mundy" and "Michelle Oceanflutter" and a bare "Michelle" (ugdt 3519639981). The wiki's "Michelle" is the Merry Vale warrior guild helper. Video shows Michelle Mundy at Merry Vale. Whether they are one NPC is not proven.
7. **Wugunbump and Wugawump.** Wugunbump (Merry Vale visitor) and the Wugachug "Sasparilla Brewmaster Wugawump" are different NPCs. Video frames from Wugachug (`OHuwchqU5V0`) show "Wugawump" and one misread "?gawump".
8. **Tyra and Counselor Tyra.** The Merry Vale fairy Tyra (name 1740782212) is not the Camp Chatty counsellor (ugdt 1008997850); video frames that read "Counselor Tyra" are not Merry Vale.
9. **Ronnie.** Ronnie Britesmile (Merry Vale boy, name 1943692833) and "Iron Foot" Ronnie (Snowhill Snowspurs soccer opponent, 1029617479) are treated as separate; nothing links them.
10. **Necronomicus level.** Wiki: a level 13 combat job or above. Client: "recommended for a full group of level 20 players" (2156346441). Client used.
11. **Mayor McFairy's first name.** The wiki infobox and text give "Riffickitus"; the client has only "Mayor McFairy". Client used.
12. **The Mystery Cavern.** The wiki gives Shaggy Mysterychaser this quest; no client title has that wording. The closest client strings are "Secrets of the Den" and "Merry Vale Mysteries".
13. **Series 4 labels.** The client dev label "Combat the Cultists" (2034750990) has no matching title string; "Confront the Cultists" (ugdt 1084967951) is the nearest. Dev labels are not shipped titles.
14. **Roadhouse "north of Merry Vale".** Used by the client for Terrence, Herme and the Warrior line; [[NPCs - Wilds]] records the conflict with other client sentences.
15. **Postmaster Vixie spelling.** The name entries read "Postmaster Vixie"; one client typo reads "Postmaster Vixtie" (2252778316). Entry used.
16. **Unconfirmed video nameplates.** Two nameplates read in Merry Vale recordings match no client name entry and no wiki page, so they are not recorded as NPCs (they may be player-chosen names).
