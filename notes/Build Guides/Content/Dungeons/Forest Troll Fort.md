## Conflicts

1. **Panel level.** One frame of the lobby panel reads "Requires a level 11 combat job" while loading and the next reads "level 13" with a Go button. The client rule for difficulty 5 is 13 (`loc 1323029355`), so 13 is used. The "11" is most likely a loading-state or misread value. [video eko5OxPGNsM/00062, 00064]
2. **Queen's name.** Panel, wiki and most client text say "Verda". Older quest strings say "Varda" (`loc 350466328`) and "Vera" (`loc 1454027080`). Client panel spelling "Verda" used.
3. **Wizard cloak.** Wiki "Adept Wizard Long Cloak". The client has "Adept Wizard Robe" (`loc 2549405387`, `2004879944`) and "Adherent Wizard Long Cloak" (`loc 445814810`) but no "Adept ... Long Cloak". The Robe is used as the nearest same-set item. Unconfirmed.
4. **Set name.** Wiki bonus set "Enery Transfer" against client "Energy Transfer" (`loc 176942616`). Client used.
5. **Hidden Troll Fort.** One client objective calls the combat encounter "Hidden Troll Fort" (`loc 565095190`). The Atlas name and everything else say "Forest Troll Fort".
6. **Score weights.** The index lists the same weights (3,000, 50,000, 120,000, 25,000) for [[Briarheart Caverns]] and this fort. They agree here with the one score screen read.
