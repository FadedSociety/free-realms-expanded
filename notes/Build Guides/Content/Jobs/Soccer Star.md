## How to read this page

- Tags: **[client]** is the game's locale text (`en_us_data.dat`), **[client code]** is our mapped client code in [[Soccer]] (the Gameplay page; function addresses are quoted so they can be found), **[video]** is player recordings (frame key `video/frame`), **[wiki]** is the fan wiki. Precedence is client, then video, then wiki. Numbers in parentheses, for example `(2283833823)`, are client locale ids. Each quest title has a `ucdt` entry and usually a `ugdt` twin with the same text.
- **Matching caveat.** The locale file stores quest titles, descriptions, objectives and dialog as separate entries with no link between them, and the quest prerequisite and reward tables are not decoded. Every objective below was matched to a title by wording. **exact** means a string carries the title's own words; **direct** means a string names the same NPC, team and place as the title; a dagger (†) means "fits, not proven". Nothing is invented: rewards, givers and levels read "unknown" unless a source names them.
- **The wiki has no Soccer Star quest list.** The fan wiki has one short Soccer Star page, the achievement and title rows, three item pages and a few location mentions. It names no trainer, coach or team. Every quest row therefore rests on [client] text; [video] covers only the trainer, the gear vendor, one quest-log line and match screens.
- **Truncated dialog.** Long dialog strings in this locale dump stop at about 256 characters. Quoted dialog is cut where the file cuts it.
- **Order.** The quests are grouped into story arcs by the NPCs and teams their strings share. Order inside an arc is a reading of the dialog, not a decoded prerequisite chain (see Gaps).
- **Developer strings.** Strings starting "DNT -" and the title "NOT LIVE - Soccer Star: Part of the Team" are developer-marked; they are listed so the team does not rebuild them by mistake.

## Overview

- Soccer Star is a **freestyle** job whose gameplay is the team soccer minigame played at the Snowhill Soccer Field and two other fields (see Matches). Wiki: an athlete job whose minigames can be played solo or in teams. Players gain levels (stars) through the quest series and by playing soccer matches. [wiki Soccer Star] [client 1743754919 "Soccer Star and Freestyle"] [wiki Jobs lists Soccer Star under Freestyle]
- **Job name.** The client and the wiki use "Soccer Star". The wiki adds that the UK name is "Football Star". [client] [wiki Soccer Star] The client has no "Football Star" job string (only "Gold/Silver/Bronze Football Trophy" items).
- **Max level 20.** Level achievements are at 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 (`Soccer Star: Level N`); the level 20 title is "The Crowd Pleaser". [client 3990071702, 2963449982, 3913249450, 577132062, 1333761857; client 912727405] [wiki Achievements, Titles] Separately the questline contains seven "Achieve Level N" quests at 2, 5, 8, 10, 13, 15 and 19 (see Quest line).
- **Members status.** Treated as **free to start with non-member limits**. The wiki Jobs list marks Warrior, Wizard, Chef, Kart Driver, Pet Trainer and Postman as members only and does not mark Soccer Star. [wiki Jobs] The wiki Soccer Star page says "non-members can play a limited amount of games before upgrade is required". [wiki Soccer Star] The client has the upgrade prompt "Oh, no! Want to keep playing as a Soccer Star? Upgrade to unlock more fun!" (956608340; the same prompt exists for many jobs, for example Miner 347663181, Chef 581732194, Wizard 1164096267), a "Soccer Membership Upgrade Bundle" (2690287275), a new-member pack "special new member pack of soccer star items" (1795367220) and "Monthly Membership Reward - Soccer" (3371665972). The size of the non-member game limit and the non-member level cap are **unknown**. See Conflicts 1.
- **How to unlock.** Speak to Manager Landon at the Snowhill Soccer Field. Client strings: "Speak to Manager Landon in Snowhill for more information on how to become a Soccer Star." (1388210539), "Learn how to be a soccer star by finding Manager Landon in Snowhill." (3270318411), "Speak with Manager Landon at the soccer field near Perry's Pasture to learn how to become a Soccer Star" (1821460636) and the quest objective "Obtain the Soccer Star job" (614042897). [client] [video `WjpJl2U5OK0/00002`: nameplate "Manager Landon", title "Soccer Trainer"]
- **Referrals.** Adventurer tutorial text names the job: "...you can also be a brawler, an archer, or even a soccer star!" (4272033315, 1711441406). A town message board "points to Job Trainers that will teach you how to race karts, smash karts, play soccer, go fishing, or even play trading card games!" (2435323721). The job window lists Soccer Star in the job-switch panel (page 2 of 2, with Medic, Miner, Ninja and Postman). [client] [video `OK4E0QTjTQ8/00090`]
- **Introduced** in the Goal Time update on 2 October 2009 per the wiki; the client keeps a teaser "Over at Snowhill they have a soccer field where you can play by yourself or with others. Available in an upcoming release!" (3121265190) and a "Goal Time Soccer" label (3335246055). [wiki Soccer Star] [client]
- **Quest line shape.** One story season. The player joins a team of local players, the Sidekicks, coached by "Golden Leg" Eddie in the Northern Wilds, plays the Snowspurs, Black Widows, Goalbusters, Mud Hoppers, Twinkletoes and Cheetahs, solves the theft of the tournament trophy and wins the final against the Cheetahs for the "Premier Soccer Cup". The client has **20 live "Soccer Star:" story titles**, 7 "Achieve Level N" quests, 1 NOT LIVE title and 5 level-achievement titles. The line ends with the achievement "Complete the Soccer Star Questline" (882214261, 2212314839; no title string found) and, per the wiki, the title "Striker". [client] [wiki Achievements, Titles]
- **Rewards are largely unknown.** No quest-complete frame for a soccer quest was found. See Gaps.

1. **Members only.** The wiki Jobs list does not mark Soccer Star members only but marks Warrior, Wizard, Chef, Kart Driver, Pet Trainer and Postman; the wiki Soccer Star page says non-members get a limited number of games; the same Jobs page says most jobs cap at level 4 for non-members. The client has the upgrade prompt, membership bundle, new-member pack and monthly reward for the job. Treated as free to start with an unknown limit. [wiki Jobs, Soccer Star] [client]
2. **Level quests.** The brief expects "Achieve Level N" quests. The client has them at levels 2, 5, 8, 10, 13, 15 and 19 with `Soccer Star: Achieve Level N` titles, and separate level achievements at 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20. The wiki lists only the achievements. Level 8 appears in both families, and the quest has a second hyphen spelling (2684953835).

The wiki title "The Crowd Pleaser" matches the client. [wiki Achievements, Titles] [client]
