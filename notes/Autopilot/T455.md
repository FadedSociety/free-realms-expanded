# T455 - Pets follow their owner: server-driven 125 moves (lead build, 2026-10-26)

Built by the lead (the owner, 2026-10-26: "continue on with your pet work"). Stacked on T454 (`bd3a0710`, the pet
catalogue, filed first); this patch is only the follow work.

## What changed

- `src/pet_follow.rs` (new, pure: no I/O, clock or terrain of its own): `spawn`, `step(config, owner, pet, ground,
  paused)` and the `FollowConfig` bounds. Behaviour: walk to a spot `behind_m` behind and `side_m` beside the owner
  (on the side the pet is on) at the owner's observed pace clamped to [min_speed, max_speed]; past
  `catch_up_start_m` add `catch_up_gain` m/s per metre up to `max_speed_multiplier` until back near the owner; after
  `settle_delay_ms` of a still owner walk to a spot `settle_radius_m` away at the angle in [settle_min_deg,
  settle_max_deg] nearest the pet's bearing, stop and face the owner (one stop frame); warp beside the owner on an
  owner jump over `warp_owner_jump_m`, after `warp_far_ms` more than `warp_far_m` away, or a climb over
  `warp_climb_m`; while the 53/24 pet window is open stop once and face the owner. Height is `ground(x, z)`; a zone
  with no terrain grid keeps the owner's height (R). A non-finite owner pose or ground value skips the step and keeps
  the last pose. Frames go out at most once per `send_ms` while walking, plus the stop and warp frames.
- `data/content/pets.json`: new `follow` block, every value an (R) default from the brief; `PetContent::validate`
  rejects one outside the bounds in `FollowConfig::valid` (so a bad file fails at load). Content without a block
  keeps the pre-T455 behaviour (the pet stands where it was summoned).
- `src/pets.rs`: `ActivePet` carries the session-only `PetPose` (never saved, not in the roster); `summon` places the
  pet `side_m` beside the owner; `PetService::follow_owner(owner_guid, owner_position, ground, now_ms)` runs one step
  and puts the 125 frame in `private` (owner) and `shared` (viewers), `changed` false; the old `Follow` no-op now sends
  a 125 to the clicked spot (R). `view()` already returned the pet's own position.
- 125 encoder: the enemy writer `enemies::movement_packet` is now `pub(crate)` and shared (no new encoder).
- Hooks (thin): `Gameplay::on_location` calls `follow_pet` (+1 line in `gameplay.rs`); `gameplay/rides.rs` gains
  `follow_pet` and `pet_tick` (ground from `Gameplay.terrain`); `gateway.rs` forwards `on_location`'s `nearby` and its
  world tick returns `pet_tick` when no minigame tick runs (+2/-1 lines; the helper lives in `gateway/companions.rs`,
  on the live gameplay, no clone); `osfr-gateway.rs` `with_pets` no longer overrides the pet's position with the
  owner's (net -2 lines). spine_rules passes.

## Client facts

- Opcode 125 is the 36-byte actor move read at `008e5940`; state bit 4 is the quarter-speed selector and no other
  state bit is assigned a movement mode (notes/Research/Integration Round 5.md; `00934a20`, `0090e0f4`, `008b3180`).
  The pet uses the natively accepted enemy frame (state 1) for walk, stop and warp. I did not re-read the 125 handler
  `0090de90` in IDA in this build: whether the client interpolates or snaps between frames, and so whether 500 ms
  reads smoothly, is the native check below.

## Native check

Summon a pet, walk and run: it trails behind and beside you, catches up after a sprint or ride, settles 2-3 m away
facing you when you stop, warps after a warpstone or `realm goto`, and stands still while its pet window is open.
A second client sees it move.

## Checklist

1. A refusal changes nothing: following has no request to refuse; a step with no pet or a foreign owner returns
   an empty wire and leaves the pose.
2. Rollback: the pose is session-only on `ActivePet`; nothing in the roster or save changes.
3. Deltas: each step moves from the current pose toward the owner; no snapshot is written back.
4. Busy: a pet window open (`ui_mode`) holds the pet still; summoning keeps the existing busy gate.
5. Entry paths: steps come only from an accepted owner move (`on_location`) and the world tick (`pet_tick`).
6. Real path: gateway message and tick hooks -> gameplay -> `PetService::follow_owner`; the F762 binary test drives
   the session dispatch, the tick and `with_pets` with two viewers.
7. Reconnect: nothing persists; a reconnect starts without a summoned pet (unchanged).
8. Exits: dismiss and zoning remove the actor (unchanged); a dismissed pet sends no moves (test).
9. Economy: none.
10. Wire: 125 reuses the enemy writer, which matches client reader `008e5940` (36 bytes); the AddNpc is the existing
    pet spawn template, now at the pose.
11. Ids: actor guids stay owner-derived (`pet_guid`), unique per owner.
12. Others: frames go to viewers (`shared`/nearby) and late viewers get the AddNpc at the current pose (F762 test).
13. End to end: the binary test sees B receive moves and C the spawn at the pose.
14. Data: every follow number is in pets.json `follow` with load-time bounds; only the 0.05 m still and 0.1 m arrive
    epsilons are code constants in `pet_follow.rs`.
15. Canary: `catch_up_gain` 0 fails the catch-up test; the owner's position in the summon AddNpc fails F762.
16. Session plumbing: the two lead-only hooks and `with_pets` are signed off in lead-signoffs.md (T455 row).
17. Tick safety: a step cannot fail; no error leaves the tick.
18. Type rules: the follow model is one pure module (`pet_follow.rs`) with `FollowConfig::valid`.
19. Hot path: one O(1) pose step and at most one 36-byte packet per pet per `send_ms`; no `Gameplay` clone.
