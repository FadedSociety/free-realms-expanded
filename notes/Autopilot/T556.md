## (R) choices
- 60 s life, 5 s window, 1.5 m offset, 10-20 s one-shot cadence and its 3 s length are Sanctuary's `CakeItemDefinition` defaults (reference only, nothing copied); 5 m click range is ours. All live in the JSON with sanity bounds in the loader (brief bounds: lifetime 1..=600000, window 0..=60000, at most 8 outcomes, interval 10000..=20000).
- Boss roll is OSFR's (stoneheart 2055, snowmanboss 1944, pumpkinprince 1702). The item's own ability 286 is Cakenstein (model 22): question for the owner.
- Seaside's two models (750 seagull, 749 parrot) replace Sanctuary's synthetic ids 900100/900101.
- Buff icon 3843 (`icon_status_changeform_32`) follows the existing consumables convention.
- Guid range: high word `CAKE` (0x43414b45), low word a counter. Characters count up from 1, content NPCs are 9e9..1e10 and 1e11, pets `owner ^ 0x4000.. ^ pet<<32`, rides `owner ^ 0xc000..`, other tags 0x1000/0x0f00/0x4f50/0x5255/0x5057/0x4e4f/0x424f. Disjointness is asserted for the live set and sampled pets and rides.
- Cap: 64 live cakes (`max_live` in the data, 1..=256 sanity).
- One-shot and its return are 35/8 with the base flag set (what Sanctuary sends); whether the client replays `amb_oneshot_01` on that path is a native check.
- `encounter::composite_effect` is gone from the tip (moved to `effect_packets::encode_play_composite_effect`, T458); the registry uses that shared writer, `encounter::animation` for 35/8 and `encode_remove_gracefully(guid, 0)` for 35/3 (same bytes as `pets::pet_removal_packet`).
- Randomness: a pure hash of cake guid, clicker and time (`Rng::mix`, `power_ups.rs`), so the registry holds no random state and stays comparable.
