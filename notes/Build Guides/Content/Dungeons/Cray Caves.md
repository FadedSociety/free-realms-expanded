## Conflicts

1. **Descend wording.** Video "Descend into the lower cave." Client and wiki "Descend into the lower caves." Client used.
2. **Goal 2 name.** Video line reads "Defeat Tidewatcher ?" (blurred). The client and wiki name the boss "Tideweaver Oceana". Client used.
3. **Bonus wording.** "Defeat all enemies!" is wiki and video only. No exact client string.
4. **Hoodie spelling.** Wiki "Pinhole Archer Hoodie". Client "Pin Hole Archer Hoodie" (`loc 1574638063`). Client used.
5. **Set name.** Wiki "Enery Transfer" against client "Energy Transfer" (`loc 176942616`). Client used.
6. **Stars.** The Seaside collection panel shows "Rewards 275 stars", a collection reward unrelated to the wiki's "274 Stars or less" dungeon figure. Not a conflict, noted to avoid confusion.
