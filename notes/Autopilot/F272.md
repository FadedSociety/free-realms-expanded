## Security threat note (secure-code-guardian)

- **Attack surface:** unchanged from T247. A hostile client can send 26/8,
  26/10, 26/6, 49/2 and 49/14 at any time and can report movement through the
  normal movement path. F272 adds no parser, selector, packet family, field,
  reward amount, client-supplied quantity, role or transport change; it is test
  and content data.
- **Why hostile claims are refused:** 26/8/26/10 need an NPC content key, the
  12 m range/zone check and an open server menu; 49/2 needs the server's
  `pending_offer`; 49/14 needs `pending_turnin`, set only by a server Talk that
  completed every objective and the 49/13 release. The reward still comes from
  the content row through the existing per-character
  `quest:<character>:<quest>` grant key. Refusal tests snapshot the whole
  `GameplayState` and its serialized save bytes.
- **Pre-existing turn-in range gap:** after the final Talk, 49/14 is paid without
  rechecking the speaker or position. It cannot forge completion (no client
  quantity or result is trusted), but a completed player can walk away and pay.
  Pinned by a test; a range gate is a separate lead-approved 49/14 change.
- **Content fail-closed:** the split loader still rejects a malformed objective
  (0.0 radius) with `InvalidData` and rejects a quest no job lists; no `unwrap`
  on client data was added.
- **Economy:** 50 coins/25 Adventurer XP once, no items, no re-grant; duplicated
  accept/turn-in/reconnect tests change no save bytes.
