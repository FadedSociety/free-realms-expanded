## Reconstructed rules (R)

- Buy-back quantity zero means all units still outstanding; partial quantity is limited to the
  recorded remainder. Buy-back price is the sale's unit price times the restored quantity.
- A session retains the configured number of recent sales. The default remains ten and the
  configured maximum is 100.
- Uncapped catalogue delivery is not performed. Uncapped requested 35/37 payloads are split at
  60 KiB; the item decoder bounds one row to 65,536 bytes, so an oversized single row is sent alone.
- Station Cash, gifts, wallet funding and paid orders remain refused or empty.
