## Conflicts

1. **Goal count.** The wiki lists eight goals. The recording's panel shows seven at the final tower. Goal 8 ("Defeat Brutus, the betrayer!") is not seen on screen; the client string exists.
2. **Boss names.** The wiki lists "Sergeant Brutus" as boss. The client has a separate wandering enemy "Brutus the Brute". They are different entries.
3. **Item spelling.** Wiki "Soke Ninja Wristguard" against client "Soke Ninja Wristguards" (the item's name is plural, `loc 212567066`). Client used. Wiki set name "Enery Transfer" against client "Energy Transfer". Client used.
4. **Tower Captain vs Sentry Sergeant.** The wiki NPC list says "Chugawug Sergeant"; video shows the Tower Captain speaking at goal 1, and the goal says "Sentry Sergeant". Whether these are one NPC or two is unknown.
5. **Achievement.** The wiki's achievement list has no Trail of Betrayal trophy, but the client has both encounter strings.
