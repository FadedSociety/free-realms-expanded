# T014: Starting-region field spawn groups

## What changed

- `prototype/osfr-tools/src/encounter.rs`: `field_enemies.json` now accepts
  `spawn_groups`. A group owns the shared region, zone, behaviour preset,
  respawn timer, aggro radius, level band and home leash for its explicit
  member GUIDs. The loader rejects empty groups, duplicate group ids, duplicate
  or unknown members, a second group claiming the same actor, invalid source
  evidence and out-of-range policy before replacing the live field.
- `prototype/osfr-tools/src/gameplay.rs`: behaviour presets load before field
  actors at startup and before the field reload in `reload all`. This lets the
  grouped actors name a preset instead of relying on the old implicit base.
- `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/behaviours.json`: added `field_roamer`
  (melee, 3 m/s, 2.5 m reach, 1 s attack interval, 12 m leash).
- `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/field_enemies.json`: added four spawn
  groups covering the twelve W102 always-on actors. Group members no longer
  duplicate policy fields in their actor rows. The original four quest-gated
  Hooligans remain flat, unchanged actors.
- `prototype/osfr-tools/tests/field_enemy_spawn_groups.rs`: added a three-test
  suite for the manifest, spawn/roam/death/timed-respawn behavior, and atomic
  refusal of a malformed group reload.

## Reconstructed rules

- (R) The four starting groups are Farnum wolves, Highroad Hooligans,
  Queensfields Bixies and northeast Wilds shamans. Each contains three explicit
  level-1 or level-3 actors in the W102 placement table.
- (R) Wolves and Bixies respawn after 45 s; Highroad Hooligans and Wilds shamans
  respawn after 60 s. Each actor's timer starts on its own death.
- (R) Group members have an 8 m aggro radius, 2.5 m reach and 12 m home leash.
  Idle goals use a 6 m radius around the authored home.
- (R) Starter level bands are 1-2 except Highroad at 3-4. These remain
  chosen bands, not recovered 2014 overworld levels.
- (R) `field_roamer` uses the existing local melee pace/attack constants. No
  original overworld AI parameter table was recovered.

## Evidence and provenance

- The placements, community anchors and terrain checks are the W102 work
  recorded in `notes/Design/Field Enemies.md`. Each actor retains its original
  `client`, `reference`, `legacy-data` and `reconstructed` source entries.
- Each spawn group carries `reference` links to the named Build Guides and a
  separate `(R)` placement-policy entry. The runtime requires at least one
  reconstructed group source and requires it to start with `(R)`.
- The new test verifies all twelve group members retain client evidence, a
  legacy positional anchor, Build Guide context and reconstructed markings.
- No original spawn table, exact count or historical respawn interval is
  claimed. Community positions remain anchors, not native transforms.

## Security-relevant decisions

- No authentication, login, account, session, transport, trading, character
  store, game store, Studio or launcher code was touched.
- Spawn groups are bounded (64 groups, 64 members per group, 64 actors total),
  member GUIDs must be unique and known, and all numeric policy is range-checked
  before actor validation.
- Group provenance is validated separately from actor provenance. A malformed
  group cannot partially replace the field because the loader builds a new
  field locally and assigns it only after every check passes.
- No dependency or manifest changed.
