## Security threat note (secure-code-guardian)

This slice changes tests and notes only; no production parser, reward, selector or
save code changed, and the T303 content record is unchanged. The suite still drives
the hostile cases: forged `49/2` accept and `49/14` turn-in with no server-held
`pending_offer`/`pending_turnin`, premature turn-in, replayed accept/turn-in after
completion, an out-of-range `26/8`, a wrong-NPC conversation, out-of-order and
duplicate Talk events, and a busy (encounter) Talk. Each refusal asserts the whole
serialized `GameplayState` is byte-identical and no 49/1/49/13 leaks. The identity
fix (finding 2) is test-side: production `on_location` reads only zone/position
today, so a wrong guid could not forge progress; the corrected helper keeps future
guid-keyed location/warp work attributed to the right character.

The two unfixed pre-existing gaps are security-relevant and are deliberately left
to the lead: (a) the split loader never sets `members_only`, so a lapsed member who
still holds the Wizard job can accept and collect this member-only job chapter;
(b) the 49/1 offer card shows no coins/XP because its reward bundle is the empty
placeholder.

## Open gaps

- Finding 4 (members-only) not fixed. It is pre-existing (the loader line is older
  than T303), and the reviewer labels the fix a lead task. A correct fix needs the
  membership policy: where the membership flag is refreshed from (account/session
  owned), whether job-gated quests inherit the job's `members_only` for all split
  content, and how a lapsed member is represented. Applying it to every job-gated
  quest without that decision would change offers catalogue-wide; the fixtures
  default to member (`MembershipState::default` is member), so no existing test
  would detect the lapsed-member path.
- Finding 5 (offer-card reward) not fixed. It is T242's documented gap and a lead
  task. The 69-byte empty bundle is pinned byte-for-byte by the captured-packet
  test `tests/npc_interaction.rs:390`; populating it needs client evidence for the
  populated offer bundle (and the display identity fields) before accept, plus a
  decision on whether the offer card may advertise the payout.
- No black-box regression test is possible for finding 2's guid attribution: no
  production reader on the location path consumes the guid (`warp.visit` ignores
  `TravelContext.guid`; `VisitPosition` carries zone and position only). The
  two-wizard test now drives both guids through the corrected helper.
- T303's two existing native gaps still stand: no native client run (lead-only),
  and the spell book remains narrative (no item).
- `notes/Autopilot/T242.md:86` repeats the old 49/1 dword-order sentence. Left
