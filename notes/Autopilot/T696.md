## Security threat note (secure-code-guardian)

Nothing reads client bytes. The two rows are server content loaded from disk at world load; the
only reachable interaction is the existing 26/8 NPC click, range-checked against the row's
position. A hostile client cannot fabricate a placement, choose a model/name id or claim a
reward; the existing menu/quest/vendor gates still decide what the actor offers. Neither row
adds a vendor, price, item or coin path. A bad data row can only fail the loader or a test.
