## Wrapper and element behavior

This affects range/fill wrappers 0044808b–0044829f, 004483c2, 00448403, 00448443–00448554, 00448579, 004485a1, 0044a803, 0044a82b, 0044a86d, 0044a8af, 0044a8fa, and 0044a922. Their records describe the calls without promoting the partial local to a zero tag.

The loops at 004482d5 and 0044833f repeatedly assign wide- and narrow-string handles through 00446ecc and 00446eee; they are fill/assignment loops, not destructors. Separate release loops at 004483a9 and 004483ea traverse begin-to-end in 4-byte steps and release each handle.
