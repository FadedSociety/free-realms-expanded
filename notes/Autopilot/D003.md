## Security threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client controls the chat family/subtype, channel, claimed sender and
target identities, claimed names, message text and claimed position. Existing
decoders validate lengths and ignore the wire identity in favour of the
authenticated session; this change derives scope from the registry that
accepted the player's validated pose and from the server-owned social service,
never from packet identity, zone or position. A left session has no player row
and no chat member, so `chat_session` returns `None` the moment
`leave_player` runs; the t020 disconnect test asserts both absences. No item,
coin, reward or client-reported outcome is touched. Budget refusal stays
silent, spends nothing extra, and drops the whole bundle before staging.
