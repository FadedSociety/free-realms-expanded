# T011: Job abilities per level

## Result

Job bars are now resolved from authored per-job rows for all six combat jobs.
`OpeningAbilities::job_bar` builds seven fixed toolbar positions at the active
job's level. A locked or absent ability leaves its position empty, so later
skills never shift into an earlier key. `activation` consumes that same shape,
and `job_slot` uses it for cast admission.

The authored roster has 26 abilities across profiles 2, 11, 12, 32, 35 and 43.
The new Brawler entry is Rock Toss (local ability definition 62024) at level 5.
Its client name, description and icon ids are recorded in the row; its damage,
range, stun, energy and cooldown are reconstructed (R).

The tests cover every level from 1 through 20 for each combat job, the wire
definitions and variable-length toolbar, switching through every combat job and
the non-combat Adventurer job, and preservation of each job's experience when
switching away and back.
