## Threat note

A hostile client can send 41/103 at any time with any header words, GUID and accept byte. The decoder is strict
(exact 21 bytes, flag 0 or 1); the responder is the session's own character; the run, inviter and encounter come
from the server-held row; a repeated Accept while refused only repeats the same rechecks (bounded by the 30 s
deadline and one row per invitee); a committed join or a Decline consumes the row, so a replay is inert.
