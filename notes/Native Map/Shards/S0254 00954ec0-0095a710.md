## Systems and structure

- The range contains `ClientLoginManager` vtable methods at **009552e0, 00955460, 009566c0, 00957530**. - Two generic red-black-tree/free-node-pool families appear at **009556e0-00955f50** and **00956b40-009573b0**. The first allocates 0x74-byte-stride nodes (**00955d60**); the second allocates 0x1a0-byte-stride nodes (**009571c0**). The fixed-list constructors/vtables at **00955550** and **009569f0**, adjustor thunks **00955fc0-00955fd0 / 00957420-00957430**, and callback builder **00957530** connect those pools to the `NameSuggestion` and `AccountInfoCharacterDetails` list specializations.
