## The class registration anchor

interface `GFC_Draw_DrawDevice`, version "1.0.0") and **`GDisplayD3D9Impl`** ("Direct3D 9 Dependent
Display", interface `GFC_Interface`). It is the only confirmed record in the shard and it names the whole
translation unit. `00466d6e` is the draw-device constructor: it derives `GInterface` (vtable evidence),
stores the `IDirect3DDevice9*` at +0x10, vertex/index capacities 0x1000/0x2000 at +0x2c/+0x44, cached caps
at +0x14/+0x18, and creates a 0x200 x 0x200 `D3DFMT_A8R8G8B8` texture at +0x6c with CreateTexture pool value 2
(`D3DPOOL_SYSTEMMEM`), then gets its level-0 surface at +0x70. `00466eb3` is the per-frame begin (buffer init `004621ca`, state init,
`004624ac`), and `00466e97`/`00462140` (S0019) are its destructors.

## The 2-D batch pipeline (this shard is its backend)

- `004621ca` creates the dynamic vertex buffer (capacity*0x1c bytes, write-only|dynamic) and the
   16-bit index buffer, clamping both capacities to the D3DCAPS9 fields at +0xB4/+0xB8 (floor 0x80), and
   derives the dither flag +0x75 from the back-buffer format (0x14/0x15/0x16 = R8G8B8 family).
- `004622fe`/`004623b5` lock ring-buffer regions (wrap on overflow, D3DLOCK_DISCARD-style flags from
   +0x35/+0x49) for the submitter at **0047735b** (S0024), which is outside this shard but consumes the
   exact three-dword {pointer,count,cursor} contract produced here.
- Draw records are encoded by `004652aa/004652ed` (60-byte), `00465395/004653d8`,
   `00465480/004654c3` (20-byte), `0046556b/004655ae` (28-byte), `00465656/00465699` (36-byte). The
   encoder `004600c2` (S0019) picks one of seven compact vertex encodings per record (returned sizes
   0xc/0x10/0x14/0x18/0x20/0x28/0x38) and its inverse decoder is `00460459`; the arena allocator
   `00461ffa` caps allocations at 0xFFF0, i.e. the 16-bit index limit.
- `00462463` submits one textured record (SetTexture stage 0, draw-counter +0xa0), `004624ac` re-programs
   the fixed-function state block (identity transforms for states 2/0x10/3, stages 2-4 disabled, FVF
   0x144, index buffer bound, shaders cleared), and `0045adaa` (S0018) replays the cached 14 render
   states plus 6 texture-stage and 4 sampler states per stage from tables DAT_01af61e4/DAT_01af621c/
   DAT_01af6234.
- `00462f15`/`0046321e`/`0046333c`/`00462979` build the atlas grid: page table entries {index, u, v,
   1/u, 1/v}, 0x14-byte stride; cell table 0x3c-byte stride with integer and inset float UV quads; the
   row records are created lazily per grid row and linked into the device list at +0x54.

- `004652aa`: encoder 004600c2 returns encoded byte length and writes the compact tag through its second argument; append uses the returned length.

- `00466d6e`: CreateTexture pool argument is 2 (`D3DPOOL_SYSTEMMEM`), not managed pool.
- `00462eee`: 0045b544 returns the exact power of two unchanged, or rounds a non-power upward; it does not double exact powers.
- `00463dc9`: the first tag-5 collection uses page lookup and decode helpers; the second pointer-array collection copies fields and calls 00463d4f directly.
- `00463ef4`: the 64-byte local array holds 16 pointers; counts above 16 take the heap path. Both per-entry helpers receive the saved pointer in ECX. Their device-change purpose is unknown.
- `004659a8`, `00465bab`, and `00465c60`: source pointer receives slot +0x0c; old destination receives slot +0x10(0); then the source pointer is stored. `004657df`/`004658b3` perform the same slot roles with source at param_3 and destination at param_1.

- `004647d9`, `004648f1`, and `0046497f` preserve and return the iterator around verified successor/successor/predecessor helpers.
- The S0020 pseudocode scan found no `DAT_01b839d8` cookie use. At `00465ced`, `&stack0xfffffff0` is stored in the SEH frame, not passed as a call argument.
- Read `nmq bytes` for every function in this shard below 48 bytes. The wrappers and short helpers inspected are calls/field operations rather than jump thunks; records describe the verified targets/operations.
