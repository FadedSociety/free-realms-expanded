Built on T531 (`7723705b`, idle life), T489 (tricks), T488 (grooming) and T772 (adoption). Skills applied: ponytail (full, incl. the

## Client evidence (checked in IDA and the client tables this pass)

- **The click.** 26/8 is built by `008b6bc0` (u64 actor guid + u8 flag; the server decoder `decode_npc_interact_26_8` wants exactly 13
  bytes). The click handler `0093bff0` looks the actor up by guid (`00984960`, the table at client +0xc0); found, it builds 26/19
  SelectPlayer, sends it, and calls PlayClient slot +0xa0 (`0092efe0` `RequestInteraction`, 26/8) when the byte at client +0x659 is
  set; not found, it returns -1 and sends nothing. So the chest has to be a real AddNpc (35/2, reader `00921a10`) the client knows. The
  prompt radius is the AddNpc float `unknown_float_51` (T400: actor +0x1818, dispatch `0092f997`), which the chest sets to the open range.
- **The model.** `Models.txt` row 152 is `151^sg_treasure_chest_01.adr`. That ADR declares the texture aliases `Default1`, `Default2` and
  `Default3` (one chest texture per tier: `sg_treasure_chest_01/02/03.dds`) and the animation slots `com_spawn`, `com_stand`, `loc_stand`,
  `com_cast_01/02`, `com_death_01` and `com_death_static_01`. It has no open slot, so opening only removes the actor (35/3).
- **The names.** `lab/reports/locale-id-to-text.json`: 140227 Common, 140234 Rare, 140235 Elite Pet Treasure (the AddNpc `name_string_id`).
- **The animations (the brief and its research pack were wrong).** 5525 is `pet_digging` and 5520 is `pet_sniff` in `AnimationTypes.xml`
  (lines 1607 and 1603, type 4 = pet). `PetTrickGestureSettings.txt` has `PLAYER_DRAW_EFFECT^5525`, a second, unrelated consumer of the same
  number as an effect id. Only **21 models** declare both slots in their ADR (20 with a `Pets.json` row, plus the St. Bernard 4109): all
  dogs (`dog_large_*`, `dog_small_*`; one of them, Dukey 3793, is vanity). Cats, birds and the rest have no such slots, so the importer marks
  the 21 (`models[].digs`) and every other pet hunts without the two animations, the T489 and T531 rule (a slot the ADR does not declare is
  never sent).
- **The vitamins.** `Pet Trainer.md:248` lists text ids (2394385210, 1216125650, 3290017331), not item ids; the item ids are not
  recovered. The data hook is `find_chance` (see below).
- Not built: the thought bubble the video shows at a find (OSFR icon 158 is not in the client's effect table, see T531), the chest opening

## Data (all (R); OSFR PR 152's numbers, tunable in `pets.json` `treasure`)

`treasure` (new, top level; `PetContent.treasure`, validated at load): `min_mood` 25, `find_min_ms`/`find_max_ms` 15 s to 50 s,

(3 s, first choice) and `dig` 5525 (4 s), three `tiers` (common 80, rare 18, elite 2: name id, texture alias, coins 15/250/500, and a

`scripts/pets_import.py` (deterministic; the file is unchanged outside the 21 new flags and the new block, and the importer keeps the

loads and a pet that never dug saves the same bytes as before.

## Behaviour

- **Hunt.** The summoned pet hunts only while it is settled (T455) beside a still owner, out of water, not feeding, grooming or doing a
  trick, not lying down (T531's rest posture), not a vanity pet, mood at least `min_mood`, its cooldown over, no chest waiting, and the
  owner not in a minigame. Then: a random wait of `find_min_ms` to `find_max_ms`, a roll of `find_chance` (a failed roll waits again), the
  sniff, the dig, and the chest. Anything that stops being true cancels the hunt; nothing is saved until the chest appears, so an
  interrupted dig costs nothing. The sniff and the dig run as `ActivePet::interacting()`, so a feed, a groom or a trick is refused (53/20
  unlock) while the pet digs, and T531's idle animations hold; a feed that starts during the wait cancels it.
- **The chest.** Rolled when it appears: the tier by weight, the coins, and one item (the bonus with its chance, else the tier's table). The
  chest is one `Option` in the `PetService` (session only): the AddNpc goes to the owner's `replies` alone, never to `nearby`, so viewers
  and late arrivals never see it. Its guid is "PTCH" plus a process-wide counter (never reused, across tiers, owners and sessions). It
  appears `spawn_distance` from the pet toward the owner on the terrain. The pet's cooldown starts then (`changed`, so the roster is saved
  with it). It vanishes after `lifetime_ms` (35/3 to the owner), on a zone change (`settle_pets`, also with no pet out), and on
  `reset_session` (a reconnect has none).
- **Opening.** 26/8 on the chest, one line in the NPC router (`pet_treasure_26_8`, first of the 26/8 handlers). A click on anything else
  falls through unchanged. The handler: busy refusal (shared `activity_busy`), then `PetService::open_treasure` (read-only: live chest,
  its owner, lifetime, range), then `rewards::apply_grant` over `reward_snapshot()` with the key `pet-treasure-<guid>`, then, on a staged
  copy, `adopt_reward_state` and `close_treasure`. Every refusal is one chat line and changes nothing; a full bag (`GrantFailure::BagFull`)
  keeps the chest until it expires. The contents were rolled at the find, so opening rolls nothing and cannot be re-rolled.

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client controls: the 26/8 target and framing, the pet requests (summon, dismiss, water 53/28, care actions), and its movement.
- A forged 26/8 with any guid but the owner's live chest falls through to the existing NPC path; a garbled one (length, flag) is ignored
  by this handler and refused by the existing decoder as before. The chest lives only in its owner's session state, and its owner id is
  checked again at the open, so another player cannot open it even with its guid (tested through a second session and by sending a
  stranger's character to the owner's session).
- Range uses the server's position for the player and the chest, never a client value; a non-finite position is "too far".
- Rewards come from a chest the server rolled (OS entropy, `getrandom`); the client sends nothing the roll or the table reads. Spamming
  the click pays once (the chest is removed in the same staged commit as the grant), and a refused open (range, bag, busy) leaves the chest
  and the save as they were. The idempotency key per chest guid is the applier's; the chest itself is the one-shot record.
- No free re-roll: the cooldown is stamped at the find in the saved roster (wall clock); dismissing, re-summoning, zoning or
  reconnecting changes nothing. A stamp dated past a whole cooldown ahead of the clock (the server clock was set back) is ignored, so a
  clock error cannot freeze a pet for years; one dated just ahead still counts.
- A hostile client can cancel its own hunt (walk, enter water, feed): nothing is granted by that. The tick never errors: every addition is
  `saturating_add`, a clock that ran backwards hunts nothing, a missing pet or content block is a no-op.
- Economy (R, for the lead): the cooldown is per pet, so a player with several pets gets a chest per pet per 8 h, and a newly adopted pet
  has none on cooldown. The brief asks for no caps in code; the data carries `cooldown_ms`, `find_chance`, the tables and the coins.

## Checklist

1. A refusal changes nothing: `open_treasure` takes `&self`; the handler stages the commit on a clone and refuses before it. Tested for
   a stranger, too far, a late click, a full bag, garbled packets (state, roster and chest equal before and after).
2. Rollback covers all state: the chest, the cooldown and the grant are in the one cloned `Gameplay`; nothing is shared.
3. Deltas: the grant is applied as `apply_grant` over a fresh snapshot of the live inventory and adopted at once; no old snapshot is
   restored over live state.
4. Busy rules: the open uses the shared `activity_busy()`; the find is refused while the owner is in a minigame (`PetBusy`); the dig is
   a pet activity that excludes feed, groom and trick both ways (`ActivePet::interacting`). Not tested: the open while another
   activity runs (no cheap fixture; the code is the one shared check).
5. Every entry path is gated: the find is only automatic (the tick, behind `may_hunt`); the open is only the 26/8 route, behind busy, owner,
   lifetime, range and the bag.
6. Reachable and tested through production: `Gateway::tick_at` + `Gateway::handle_at` (login, summon, ticks, click), `Gameplay::pet_tick`,
   `handle_in_world`, `settle_pets`, `reset_session`, and the service-level tick that `pet_tick` runs.
7. Survives reconnect: the reward is in the saved inventory and the cooldown in the saved roster (tested: reload, no second chest and
   no second grant; an older save loads); the chest is session-only by design (brief edge case 4).
8. Every exit restores the player: nothing is held on the player; zone change, reconnect, expiry and open all remove the chest.
9. Economy safety: see the threat note; rewards only from a server roll; one grant per chest; every item is in the client catalogue.
10. Wire format: AddNpc through `startup_world::build_add_npc` (reader `00921a10`); the chest is parsed back by `parse_add_npc` in the
    tests (model, name id, texture, interactable, X-prompt radius); 35/8 is the existing writer; 35/3 the existing `pet_removal_packet`.
11. Unique ids: the chest guid is "PTCH" plus an atomic counter (tested: 40 chests, distinct from each other and the pet's guid).
12. Others see changes: the sniff and the dig go to `shared` as well (tested); the chest deliberately does not (owner only).
13. End to end: asserted as inventory (coins and one item from the table) and as packets (AddNpc, 35/3), not as flags.
14. Values in data: `treasure` in `pets.json` and `models[].digs`; only sanity bounds and the guid tag are code.
15. Canaries break production code: see below.
16. Session plumbing: untouched (`osfr-gateway.rs`, `gateway.rs` not edited; `settle_pets` and `pet_tick` are the existing `Gameplay` hooks).
17. One player's error never stops the server: `treasure_tick` returns a `PetWire`, never an `Err`; `saturating_add` throughout; a
    backwards clock, a missing pet, a missing block and a failed AddNpc build are no-ops (tested: the clock).
18. A type's rules live on the type: `TreasureConfig::valid`, `TreasureTier::valid`, `TreasureBonus::valid`; `Pet::treasure_found_at` and
    `Hunt` document who decides them; `ActivePet::interacting` owns what counts as busy.
19. Hot path: per tick one `Arc` clone, a roster scan of at most 50, and a handful of comparisons; the model rows are looked up only when
    the cheap conditions (settled, rested) already hold; no allocation unless a timer is due. A find allocates the chest once.

## Open (R) and gaps

- Every number and every reward row is OSFR's first choice and (R); the sniff run time is mine (3 s). Native check: a dog's sniff and dig,
  the chest's look per tier (`Default1` to `Default3`), the prompt at 5 m, the reward toast (it has no item row; the item arrives through
  the inventory packets).
- OSFR only lets a pet find treasure near seven fixed spots of the open world (the owner within 5 m of a point of interest); the brief
  has no such rule, so a settled pet digs anywhere. A `spots` list in the block is the small change if the design wants it.
- The bonus does not skip a Treasure Spring item the player already owns (OSFR does); a duplicate is possible.
- A pet that lay down (T531, after 90 s of standing still) hunts again only when its owner moves and it stands up; with `find_chance`
  under 1 that matters, with the shipped 1.0 the find comes first.
- Vitamins: `find_chance` is the place; the vitamin item ids are not recovered.
- No test opens a chest while another activity runs (see checklist 4).
