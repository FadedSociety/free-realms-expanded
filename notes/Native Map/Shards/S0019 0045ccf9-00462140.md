## Contradictions / cautions for the vault

- None of these geometry (de)serializers belong to `engine.gfc.serialization`
  (that bucket is `GSimpleClassIO`, address 00403ddf). This shard's stream is
  the display command/geometry channel; I filed it under `engine.gds`.
- The RTTI classes GDrawSurface/GDrawDevice/GBitmap etc. (strings at
  0176459c/0176xxxx, functions in the 004ebxxx-005xxxxx range) do **not**
  appear in this shard; nothing here may be labelled with those names.
- The retain/release slot survey is still ambiguous: 0045f753 uses +4 (retain)
  and +8 (release) like COM, while the GFC pointer copies use +0xc and the
  frame holder releases through +0x10(0). Do not assume one interface layout
  across all of them.
