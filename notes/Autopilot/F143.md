can send arbitrary item IDs, quantities, transaction IDs or a buy without an
open shop; the active-merchant/range/stock gate, bounded decoder, quantity and
bag checks, server price lookup and bounded idempotency cache refuse those
without committing state. Details are in the threat note in `T108.md`.
