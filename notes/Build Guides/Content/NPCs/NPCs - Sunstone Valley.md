## Overview

Sunstone Valley is the desert region added to Free Realms in the update of 19 September 2012; the wiki says it was called "Stormfront Mountains" in 2009 and is "about the size of Briarwood". It has open-world combat: "enemies roam the land and attack approaching players", and a defeated player may revive in a safe zone or pay 1,000 coins to revive on the spot. Its inhabitants are manguanas, robgoblins, feathered drakes, rocklars and "Biker Dwarves". The wiki lists these places: Glimmerdeep Hydropower Dam, Mystical Barrier, Red's Haven, Rough Rider Raceway, Rumble Ridge, Wheelie Pete's Roadhouse, Whirly's Scrap Camp, Drakewind Peak, Skullz Base Camp, Pony Vale and Rocklar Village; the dungeons Precursor Ruins, Sandscale Oasis and The Rumbledome; and the quest hubs Rumble Ridge, Wheelie Pete's Roadhouse, Whirly's Scrap Camp, Noggle's Retreat and The Dig. [wiki Sunstone Valley]

The client agrees on the landmarks and adds its own: a region panel in a video frame lists "Rough Rider Raceway, Sunstone Valley Approach, The Rumbledome, Precursor Ruins, Red's Haven, Sandscale Oasis, Whirly's Scrap Camp, Glimmerdeep Hydropower Dam" (`b6QfVC_2YpQ/00028`), and a Rare Exploration window lists "Wheelie Pete's Roadhouse, Abandoned Mine, Noggle's Retreat, The Dig, Scorch Stream Falls, Mystical Barrier, Sunstone Arch, The Great Rift" (`b6QfVC_2YpQ/00020`). [video] The locale holds a "Sunstone Valley Warpstone" (3646061469) and a "Rumble Ridge Warpstone" (926583046), Sunstone Valley Explorer, Rare Exploration and Elite Exploration collections, "Sunstone Valley Lot" and "Rumbledome Lot" housing plots, and the product blurb: "Journey to the desert homeland of the dwarves and help the valiant Freewheelers defeat the vile Skullz. Battle your way past all new monsters, like the manguana, drakes, and cactoids as you unlock the secrets of Sunstone Valley. Meet Red Stonehammer, the Master Blacksmith and learn tons of new recipes ... Play for FREE as a Brawler, Miner, and Blacksmith all the way to level 20!" (2640708938). [client]

**How a player gets here.** The story starts outside the valley: Arch Druid Camellia in Shrouded Glade gives **Aiding our Allies**, which sends the player to Headmaster Merk, and the client intro line says "Talk to Arch Druid Camellia in Shrouded Glade to begin the adventure!" (4138568321). During the Gloam invasion the dwarves are trapped in Shrouded Glade and plan a bridge home. [client] [wiki]

**The story in one paragraph.** While the Freewheelers were away fighting the Gloam in Shrouded Glade, the Skullz biker gang led by Zandro Rageracer took over Rumble Ridge. Dorn Geargrinder regroups the gang, wins the race against Lieutenant Brock, fights in the Rumbledome, loses Golmie to the Skullz, and with help from the manguana, robgoblins and drakes rescues him at the Skullz Base Camp. Alongside this, Wheelie Pete, Rizzle, Noggle and Sarkin run a chain of small valley quests; Red Stonehammer's Blacksmith line chases her renegade apprentice Jarvus; the Card Duelist line frees the four Realmkeepers from the Rumbledome; and a client-only hub at the "Sunstone Gateway" and the late Pony Vale region add more. [client] [wiki]

## Part I. Pony Vale (through the Mystical Barrier)

Enemies: the Palescale manguana tribe ("Palescale By Night", "Palescale Scout", "Palescale Gatekeeper" and others). No video frame in our set shows Pony Vale. [client] [wiki]

## Notes: quest givers and trainers

- **Two chains, one valley.** The wiki's "Sunstone Valley Quest Series" runs in two strands. The dwarf strand is Aiding our Allies, Sobering Homecoming, Bike Retrieval, A Deal with a Dust Devil, Fixing Up a Sweet Ride, then the Dorn, Golmie and Merk quests to Saving a Friend. The valley strand is Wheelie Pete's five (Like Scavengers in a Barrel to For Rizzle), then Rizzle's five, Noggle's five and Sarkin's five, each wiki page's "next" link leading into the next giver. The client confirms the combat-level gates in the valley strand: level 8 (Rizzle, 2365384286), level 12 (Noggle, 1208109993) and level 16 (Sarkin, 8598901, by wording). The dwarf strand has no stated gate. [wiki] [client]
- **Dorn, Golmie and Merk** run the story. Dorn sets the beats (regroup, bikes, the race, the Rumbledome, the rescue), Golmie builds the counter-gear (Better Treads, Better Threads) and Merk supplies the Gloam purge and the alliances with the Sandscale (Joe), the robgoblins (Whirly) and the drakes (the Elder). The Skullz boss chain is Brock (race), the Rumbledome bosses and Zandro. [client] [wiki]
- **Wheelie Pete's Roadhouse** is the town hub: Pete (sasparilla), Wade Shatterslag (diner quests), Red's Haven just behind it (Red, Fry, Kallie, Broheim) and the Rumbledome "just down the way". [wiki] [client]
- **Red Stonehammer** is the Blacksmith trainer for the whole Sunstone line; her quest rows, gates (4, 8, 16) and gear recipes are on [[Blacksmith]]. The client blurb says Brawler, Miner and Blacksmith can be played free to level 20 here; the Blacksmith page records the conflict with the wiki's Members Only flag. [client]
- **The Rumbledome** is shared by the dwarf strand (Enter the Rumbledome), Wade's diner fight, Finnerty's bottle collection and the Card Duelist line, which captures the four Realmkeepers and has the player duel Jort, Milo, Yucca and Ironteeth before returning to Rumble King Don. [client] [wiki]
- **The Sunstone Gateway** is a client-only hub with 21 named NPCs and 28 quest rows in the table above (the dev strings number stages up to "Quest 50"). It is invisible in the wiki. If the server team rebuilds from the wiki alone this whole hub is missed. [client]
- **Pony Vale** is a separate short line of 11 wiki quests plus two client-only titles (Flowers for Xeydia, Palescale By Night), entered through the Mystical Barrier. It was the last area added (wiki). [wiki] [client]
- **Shrouded Glade overlap.** The Freewheelers' captivity, the Gloamed Freewheelers, the bridge, the Freewheeler Cannon test and Gus's death belong to the Gloam invasion in Shrouded Glade. They are cross-referenced above and left for [[NPCs - Shrouded Glade]]. [client] [wiki]

## Conflicts

1. **"Committed to the Plan" / "Commited To The Plan".** The wiki spells the Noggle quest "Committed to the Plan". The only client title string is "Commited To The Plan" (ucdt 1951725499; ugdt 2682697247). Client spelling used as the exact title; the wiki spelling is also shown.
2. **Scrapsmith Crackle.** Client name entry "Scrapsmith Crackle" (1385019975); the wiki page title is "Scrapsmith Crackle" but its first line and infobox say "Scrapmaster Crackle". The Blacksmith line text also calls him "Robgoblin Scrap Master" (1268963) and "Robgoblin Scrapsmith" (4237999219). The client name is used. The wiki also has a different robgoblin boss, "Scrapmaster Fizzbang".
3. **Golmie Greasebeard in video.** One nameplate reads "Golmie Greaterbeard" (`Mgm7Ux7ee9k/00020`, under the banner Sunstone Valley Rare Exploration / Abandoned Mine). Client and wiki say "Greasebeard"; treated as a reader error.
4. **Flashbang.** Client "Scrap Collector Flashbang" (457339875); wiki Merchants list "Scrap Collecter Flashbang". Client spelling used. The wiki Sunstone page lists him as an NPC without a place.
5. **Headmaster Merk's place.** Client puts him first in Shrouded Glade at the druid refuge cave (384305230, 549050541) and says he "went to help the dwarves in Sunstone Valley" (658173465); the wiki Sunstone quests say he stands in Rumble Ridge. Both are recorded; no Sunstone video frame shows him.
6. **What the "Sunstone Gateway" is.** The wiki has no Gateway page. Wade Shatterslag's client text calls the diner "Wheelie Pete's diner" and the troublemakers "Sunstone Gateway ruffians" and "diner ditchers"; Garrick's scouting includes "the diner overlook"; and Garrick says the Gateway mesa is "on the far south end of Sunstone Valley" (1220354302). The wiki puts Wheelie Pete's in Rumble Ridge and puts the Rough Rider Raceway to the south ("South is Sacred Grove, near the Rough Rider Raceway"). The Gateway is probably the wiki's Wheelie Pete's and Rumble Ridge area, but no source says so. The Wilds page's guess that Wade and Fry share the Sunstone inn is supported: the buzzards are at "Wheelie Pete's diner" (2780349277, 3544712199, 3979273499).
7. **Dorn's rank.** Wiki: "co-leader of the Freewheelers" with Gus. Client: "the new leader of the Freewheelers, now that his brother Gus is gone" (1734286197). Client wording used; the wiki quote also shows Gus as the earlier leader.
8. **Where the valley lies.** Wiki infobox: "NE of Shrouded Glade, N of Briarwood", yet its own page also says the south edge is Sacred Grove near the Rough Rider Raceway and that the Atlas shows a "northern extension". The client has the Freewheelers build a bridge "back to Sunstone Valley" from Shrouded Glade (911841567, 2775835795). Not resolved.
9. **Robot Woes in video.** The video quest header reads "Robot Woo" with the banner "Robot Woo Quest Completed!" (`IFnl12lu5JM/00002`, `/00046`). Client title is "Robot Woes" (1458689420). The video is a truncated read; client title used.
10. **Zandro's place.** Wiki: Skullz Base Camp mesa, south-east corner. Video: a red "Leader of the Skullz" nameplate in 33 frames of a recording that is at the Robot Woes hand-in (`IFnl12lu5JM`). Either he roams the open valley or the reader mislabelled another enemy; the recording has no zone label. Row kept with the wiki place.
11. **Stopping the Spread.** The wiki Pony Vale list gives it to Silverspark, but the only wiki links (Nature's Cure "next") say it follows Nature's Cure; the archive has no wiki page for it. The client text about Yarith's potion (1654129863) points the other way (Yarith as giver). Wiki giver used; the client matches are by wording.
12. **Razzle.** The Pony Vale ice-cream seller (client 385075457 "Razzle the Robgoblin") and the Demolition Derby opponent "Razzle" (2175180409) share a name entry (2605501257). One NPC or two is unresolved. See [[Demolition Derby Driver]].
13. **Hutch Diggerson's dig site.** The client calls it "the archaelogical [sic] dig site" (543105992) in a Gateway quest; the only named dig in the valley is The Dig, where Sarkin stands (wiki: northeast of the Precursor Ruins). Whether they are the same place is not stated.
14. **Free to level 20.** The client blurb (2640708938) says Brawler, Miner and Blacksmith play free to 20 in Sunstone Valley. The wiki marks Blacksmith Members Only. Kept as on the [[Blacksmith]], [[Miner]] and [[Brawler]] pages.
15. **Wiki reward figures.** Coin figures on the Sunstone quest pages (for example 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, 50) come from the quest infoboxes, whose revisions are dated 2013 except Bike Retrieval (2025). The client has no numbers. Treat them as wiki-only.
16. **Spacing.** Wiki "Friend or Foe:The Savage" has no space after the colon; the client title is "Friend or Foe: The Savage" (205250295). Client spelling used.
17. **Harley.** The wiki calls Harley a "boar"; the client text calls her "pet hog" (2539367545). Same animal.
18. **Wiki trivia on the valley's age.** The wiki says Sunstone Valley "was the only major location addition" in the 19 September 2012 update, yet Pony Vale (28 January 2014), Rocklar Village (4 February 2014) and the job merchants Fry and Ishi (6 August 2013) and Kallie (15 October 2013) came later. The merchant rows describe the 2013 and 2014 game.

- **No coordinates.** The sources give directions only. Video distance markers do not give a stable position (Dorn is 35 to 99 m away in different frames).
- **The Gateway has no titles.** Of the 28 Gateway quest rows only three titles are matched, all by wording (Faster Than a Speeding Biker, Finding the Missing Greybeard, Prickly Pen for Precious). Candidate unplaced title strings: "Scrap-happy Scavengers" (1240642016), "Precursor Prospecting" (1927835068), "Beyond Rumbledome" (3302381255), "Rumbledome Run-in" (926587141), "NOT LIVE - A Rumble in the Tummy" (767717581), "Skullz Tag" (145556098), "Skullz: Head-on Collision" (1036482165), "Skullz: Burn Rubber" (2102430315). None can be tied to a giver.
- **Wade Shatterslag's four quests** and the Hutch, Zippy, Griff, Ben, Garrick, Tommy, Mac, Mitch, Finnerty and Will Bartley quests have descriptions but no title.
- **Rewards and XP.** The client text has no reward numbers. Wiki coin figures exist for 12 of the Sunstone quests (the first three dwarf quests, four of Pete's, four of Rizzle's and Ruined Return); Pony Vale and the Gateway have none. Item rewards named by the wiki: the Rise of the Skullz comic, Skullz Pants, Mini-Manguana, Cactoid, "Mystery Reward" for Hog Wild Harley and Robot Woes.
- **Prerequisites** are known only from wiki links. Gateway and Pony Vale prerequisites beyond the wiki's Pony Vale order are not known. The client has no gate for the dwarf strand.
- **Video coverage is thin.** The Sunstone Valley zone label appears in a handful of frames; most NPC evidence is one recording (`fMOGHMuA2Hc`, a Brawler-quest tracker with an Adventurer 7) and one (`IFnl12lu5JM`, Brawler 20). No frame of Whirly Willis, Peace-guana Joe, Wade, Fry, Kallie, Ishi, Scrapsmith Crackle, Mac Gravelfist or any Gateway or Pony Vale NPC. Dorn, Golmie, Wheelie Pete and Merk have no dialog frame.
- **Merchant stock.** Fry, Kallie, Ishi and Broheim Backfire come from wiki pages from 2013 to 2025; Scrap Collector Flashbang's stock and place are unknown. No price is recorded.
- **Weak rows.** Dwarf Rally Officer, the Pony Vale horses (Brighteyes, Chance, Dash, Licorice, Lovely, Rhythm, Serendipity), Corykin, Kelbelle, Brom Graveltoe and Flashbang have a name entry or a wiki list line only, no use string.

- **Enemy types** (Skullz Commander, Hooligan, Thug, Brawler, Roughrider, Rumbledome Gladiator, Sandscale Hunter, Magus, Scavenger, Water-seeker, Palescale types, Feathered Drake types, Robgoblin types, cactoids, Precursor spiders) are not given rows; they belong on the dungeon pages.

Tangletrack Speedway is a Briarwood place and is covered on [[NPCs - Briarwood]]; no source puts it in Sunstone Valley.

The wiki says only part of the line was added on 4 February 2014; whether Palescale By Night and Flowers for Xeydia went live is unknown.
