# T421: Job ability bars fit the client's four ability buttons

## What changed

- `src/opening_abilities.rs`:
  - `JOB_ROWS = 3`: a job row must be 1..=3 (`JobAbility::valid`).
  - The loader drops the `slot == index + 1` and `len <= 7` rules. It refuses a second ability
    with the same (row, min_level) instead. Ids stay unique, a basic id still cannot be reused,
    and a missing min_level still fails.
  - `job_bar` places, on each row, the highest-`min_level` ability that is unlocked
    (`min_level <= level`) and not `unavailable`. Rows 4-7 are always `None`, so 36/5 keeps its
    shape (basic plus 7 entries) with kind 0 in rows 4-7.
  - `unlocks_between` ignores unavailable rows. `job_slot`, `weapon_matches` and `activation`
    are unchanged and follow `job_bar`.
- `data/content/job_abilities.json` (CRLF kept): only the `slot` integers changed, plus the
  `source.slot` provenance on moved rows. Every min_level, id, effect, energy, cooldown and
  weapon class is unchanged. The layout is the brief's:

  | Job | Row 1 | Row 2 | Row 3 |

  | Medic 11 | Triage 1 -> Shock Paddles 15 | First Aid 1 | Target Vitals 5 -> Vitamins 10 |
  | Warrior 32 | Kick 1 | Spinning Blade 5 -> Commanding Shout 15 | Quake 10 |
  | Wizard 12 | Zap 1 | Fireball 5 | Ice Nova 10 -> Chaos 15 |

  | Archer 35 | Multi-Arrow 1 | Explosive Arrow 5 -> Sniper Shot 15 | Stunning Shot 10 -> Piercing Strikes 20 |

  Shadow Minion stays in the file on row 3 at 10 with `unavailable: true`, so it is never shown.
  Ninja row 3 is empty at levels 10-14, which still leaves no blank button before a skill.
- Hub files are untouched. `power_ups::SLOT = 2`, the 36/10 route, the level formula and the
  job-switch clear are unchanged.
- Tests:
