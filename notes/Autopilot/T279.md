## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

Attack surface: any client can send 26/8 (arbitrary u64 target), 26/10 (arbitrary
i32 choice), 26/6 (arbitrary row), 49/2 (quest id + accept flag), 49/14 (quest id),
and a shared-field attack target. Refusals:

- 26/8 resolves only a spawned content actor through `NpcVisibility::content_key`;
  an unknown GUID has no menu, an out-of-range/zone actor is refused before any
  state change (`npc.rs` router; test 4).
- 26/10 requires the choice to be an option of the currently open interaction for
  that exact NPC and re-checks range/zone; a forged or stale choice changes
  nothing.
- 26/6 only continues the server-built dialogue; a closed dialogue gets 26/4.
- 49/2 accepts only an actually offered quest (server giver/eligibility); a forged
  accept is denied (test 4).
- 49/14 requires the server-opened `pending_turnin`; no end screen, wrong quest and
  a replay after completion pay nothing (tests 4 and 6). `finish_turnin` applies
  the reward through `RewardState` on live inventory/profiles; no client-reported
  result or quantity is trusted.
- Over-cap coins/XP refuse the whole reward pair for that player (`checked_add`);
  test 7 sets A at `i32::MAX` coins and `u32::MAX` Brawler XP and proves A's kill
  is accepted, A's reward is refused without a panic, and B's same-field kill is
  credited and paid. No `?` escapes a shared-field tick for one player's reward.
- The kills are server-simulated (`handle_shared_field`); the client attack request
  is validated for target/range/cooldown/weapon, and the quest credit comes from
  the engine's `defeated` event, never from a client claim.
- The five coins and 25 XP are fixed content values; no item grant, no free grant,
  no client id. The NPC name uses the recovered `Global.Text` id.
- No new parser, persisted field, role, auth or session change; no new dependency.
