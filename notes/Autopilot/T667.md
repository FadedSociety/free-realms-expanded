## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client could forge a 49/2 accept for a quest never offered or offered to another job, a 49/14
turn-in before the goals, twice, or for a quest never accepted, a level claim, or a Talk to Natali out of
order. The rank is read only from the server's profile state (never a client field), the objectives are
ordered by the engine, and the payout is a fixed data value paid once by the server-judged 49/14. The tests
assert a byte-equal save for the forged accept (wrong job), the early and duplicate 49/14, the replayed 49/2
and Natali's Talk without the quest. Free grants: 15 coins once, no item.
