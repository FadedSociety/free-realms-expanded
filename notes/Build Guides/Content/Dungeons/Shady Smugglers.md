# Shady Smugglers

Part of [[Content]] › [[Dungeons]]. Tags: **[client]** = game locale text (`loc <id>`), **[video]** = player recordings (frame key), **[wiki]** = fan wiki. Precedence client > video > wiki.

> Shady Smugglers is a **node battle**, not an Atlas dungeon. The wiki files it under *Battle* and *Combat Mini-game*, difficulty 4, "started by clicking on a Smuggler in Nettleseed, Briarwood". [wiki: Shady Smugglers!] Not to be confused with [[Croaking Vale]], which also has smugglers and a "Smuggler Boss".

## Conflicts

1. **Name.** The wiki writes "Shady Smugglers!" in places, and the Mushroom Mania! wiki infobox carries the title "Shady Smugglers!" by mistake. The client has no "!" form (`loc 3618485654`).
2. **Panel text punctuation.** Wiki "...defeat them!"; client "...defeat them." (`loc 4090735730`). Client used.
3. **Enemy name.** Wiki "Smugglers"; client enemy "Shady Smuggler" (`loc 3256084479`).
4. **Smuggler Boss.** The client name is shared with the Croaking Vale bosses, so the wiki boss entry may be the generic name.
5. No wiki item name lacked a client match, so no item corrections apply.
