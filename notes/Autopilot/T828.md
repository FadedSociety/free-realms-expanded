# T828 - Pet feeding says when it ate; every pet's treasure find is visible (native 2026-10-27)

Second attempt on the newest `autopilot` tip. The patch `T828-a2.patch` was applied first as the
brief says; two hunks were rejected and both were ported by hand and then superseded (below). Skills
applied: ponytail (full, including its review pass on this diff), secure-code-guardian (threat note
below), and the principles model-the-domain, subtract-before-you-add, minimize-reader-load,

## What the a2 patch got wrong on this tip

The a2 patch was cut before T797 (real pet care items, commit `6c17957`, then its review fix
`2aee5787`) landed. T797 owns the eat animation: at the apply step (900 ms into the feed) the server
sends one 35/8 `care.feed_animation` (5507) to the owner and the viewers, pinned by
`tests/pet_kit_items.rs` `a_real_bowl_and_real_food_fit_feed_and_play_the_pet_feed_animation`.

So on this tip the a2 additions would have (a) sent a **second** eat animation at the 53/20 begin
and (b) gated the animation on a new `models[].feeds` ADR flag. I checked the flag before keeping
it: the importer reports **71** models with the slot, and **all 65** non-vanity models with kit item
classes (the models the fit path accepts a bowl for) declare it, so the gate is a no-op on every
legitimate feed. The `feeds` flag, the importer scan and the 71 content flags were cut (ponytail: no
second flag that must stay in sync). T797's apply-time animation stays; T828's feeding half is the
missing **completion notice**.

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

What a hostile client can send: 53/19 feed requests for its own summoned pet, 53/16/28/7/18/22
care/trick requests, its movement, and 26/8 clicks on its own live chest.
- The animation ids, the completion text and the chest effect ids come from `pets.json` and the
  model rows, never from request bytes. A client cannot choose an animation, an effect or a chest's
  contents.
- A refused 53/19 (`feedable`: no bowl, no food, water, busy, mid-feed, vanity) returns kind 6
  alone: no animation, no shared packet, no notice, no roster or inventory change. Tests snapshot
  the roster and the bag and re-assert them, and the new tests assert the notice is absent. The only
  new output is `PetWire::notice`, a `&'static str` and never client text.
- The find's animation and sparkle are server-side consequences of a server-rolled find; a hostile
  client cannot fabricate one. Opening the chest is unchanged (T533: owner, range, lifetime, one
  grant).
- New content fields are validated at load (`effect_id` 1..=100000; `feed_animation` already bounded
  by T797), so a hostile content edit fails startup instead of sending a wild id.
- The completion line and the chest stay owner-only; viewers get only the pet animation.
