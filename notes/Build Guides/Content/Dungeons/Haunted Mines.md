## Conflicts

1. **Wiki spellings.** The wiki bonus list writes "Enery Transfer" and "Bare Knuckle". The client has "Energy Transfer" and "Bare-Knuckle" (with a hyphen). Client spellings are used. [[Briarheart Caverns]] writes "Bare Knuckle" without the hyphen.
2. **Entrance object.** The wiki says "barrier"; the video frames describe a gate (boarded or vine-covered) under the marker. They may be the same object. Not resolved.
3. **Enemy list.** The wiki lists Black Widows, Ghost Dwarves, Necrowart Zombies and Hulking Shamblers. The client names Ghost Dwarf and Hulking Shambler directly and adds Shambler Miner, Ghost Miner and cave stalker. "Necrowart Zombie" exists in the client (`loc 103459866`, "Ancient Necrowart Zombie") but the strings tied to this mine use "shambler". The wiki itself says the dungeon calls them Shamblers.
4. **Members coin figure.** The wiki's 270 has no client "Bonus: 270 Coins!" string. The client does hold "Bonus: 77 Coins!", which equals the wheel coin slot, but it is generic.
5. **Campaign meaning.** [[Briarheart Caverns]] reads "Campaign 2: Haunted Mines" as a dungeon campaign. The video evidence above points to the Treasure War card campaign. Both pages should be reconciled by the lead.
