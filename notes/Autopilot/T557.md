  row's `cooldown_ms`; load fails if the cake row is missing). Same gate as a boombox (`Player.can_place_box`:

- `consumables.json`: the nine birthday cakes 7966-7974 as `placed_cake` rows (103 rows). The file is still

  "empty result" guard so a silent click is not traced as an unknown message).

## Deviations from the brief (with reasons)
- `Action::Place` carries only `row`, not `position`/`heading`: the host reads the placer's pose from the player
  registry (as the boombox does), so a packet or a stale gameplay copy can never place a cake elsewhere.
- A world-full refusal cannot be known inside the item bar (the registry is the host's), so the press stamps the
  recast like any use and the host calls `Gameplay::refuse_cake_place` on a refusal: it removes the stamp (the press
  had passed the recast check, so any older stamp had expired), and answers with the bar's 36/1 and the bar. The
  player-visible result is the brief's: refusal, no cooldown. Tested at both levels.
- The click is resolved after the commit, like the boombox (the registry is shared world state, not the staged
  gameplay's). The clicker's gameplay is changed only after the registry accepted, and the effect check cannot
  fail (every cake outcome passed `consumable_effects::admits` at load).

## (R) choices and recorded behaviour
- Players who come into range after the spawn do not get the cake (60 s life). A session that was shown the cake
  and left range (more than the interest radius) before the despawn keeps the actor until its next scene load: the
  despawn and the one-shot animations go to the sessions near the cake at that time. Tracking viewers per cake is
  the fix if it matters.
- A refused click (not a cake, out of range, window closed, other instance, busy, expired) is silent.
- A cake transformation replaces a live transform (the effect stack replaces by kind, as OSFR).
- The nearby set for a spawn is taken at the placer's position, not the cake's 1.5 m ahead.
- Cakes can only be placed in the open world (zone 0), the boombox rule.

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)
Hostile input: 26/8 with any guid, any rate; 36/10 and 42/15 presses at any rate; presses while mounted, busy or
outside the open world. A click names only a guid: the clicker's instance and position are the registry's, the
cake must be live and in range, and each cake answers at most once per 5 s whoever asks, so a click flood costs a
hash lookup and changes nothing. A press needs an owned instance of a cake row, a ready recast (60 s per item),
the open-world and not-busy gate; the world holds at most 64 cakes (`max_live`). Open gap: that cap is world-wide
and a player holding all nine cakes can place nine a minute, so a few players can fill it for a minute at a time
(a per-player cap is not in the brief). Per-session errors are logged and skip only that session.

## Checklist
1. Refusal changes nothing: item bar checks first and stamps only on success; `refuse_cake_place` restores the
   pre-press bar (asserted equal to the snapshot); registry refusals are clone-and-compare tested (T556 and the
   gateway tests: window, range, unknown, full). Canary on the refund.
2. Rollback covers all state: the registry is shared world state changed after the commit; the stamp lives in the
   staged item bar. No `Rc`/`RefCell`/`Arc` side table; `Arc<Row>` is read-only content.
3. Deltas, not snapshots: the click applies a spec to the live effect stack; nothing restores a snapshot.
4. Busy rules: the press uses the boombox gate (`blocks(start)`, mounted); the click is refused while busy (test in
   `item_use.rs`). No new activity.
5. Every entry path gated: the press has two entries (36/10 and 42/16+42/15), both through `consume` and `usable`;
   the click has one (26/8). Tested through `handle_in_world`.
6. Reachable in production, tested through it: `Session::dispatch_message` for the press and the click, the shared
   tick for the despawn, with the real loaders for `consumables.json` and `placed_cakes.json`.
7. Reconnect: cakes and effects are session-only by design (T556, T474); the item count is untouched; a placer who
   logs out leaves the cake to expire (tested).
8. Exit paths: nothing is held open; a refused place leaves the player free with the bar redrawn.
9. Economy: nothing is granted or spent; the click only grants the cake row's own timed effect.
10. Wire format: no new packet; every writer is T473/T556's, with their reader citations. The gateway tests check

12. Others see changes: spawn and despawn go to every session near; the clicker's change goes through

13. Content end to end: all nine cakes are bar-usable rows, each with a table row (`Effects::load` fails

14. Values in data: recast, lifetime, window, range, cap and outcomes are JSON; the loader holds sanity bounds.
15. Canaries break production code (listed above), each reverted.
16. Session plumbing: untouched (`commit_outcome` unchanged; the hook sits after the commit beside the boombox).
17. One player's error never stops the server: `cake_routing` returns early or logs per session; the registry uses
