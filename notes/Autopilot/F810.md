# F810: review blockers in T543 (commit a6037bc)

Review: the lead's review of a6037bc (findings 1 to 6). Every BLOCKER is fixed and
the cheap MINOR items are done; no finding was wrong.

## What changed

- **Finding 2 (row layout).** `npc_interaction::dialogue_choice` is now the public
  `dialogue_choice_row(response_id, label)`; `quest_dialogue::DialogueLine::response_rows`
  builds every conversation row through it and the local `ROW_SECOND`/`ROW_FIFTH`
  constants are gone, so the five-dword row (reader `0x00a9b480`) has one writer.
- **Finding 3 (end screen).** A finished `complete_after_dialogue` conversation that
  readies the quest now patches its 49/13 with the runtime actor the client clicked
  (`open.actor_id`, the same patch the held Talk path applies when 26/6 releases it) and
  sends no 26/4 before it (the held path also opens the end screen over the open
  dialog). Client: 49/13 reader `0x00c7b990` reads the actor at `6..14`;
  `ShowQuestEndScreen` `0x00a95420` resolves it through `0x00984960`. Without a
  ready quest the 26/4 still closes first.
- **Finding 4 (range).** `NpcInteraction::in_reach_of_active(npc_id, zone, position)`
  repeats the start gate's `check_range` plus "still the active NPC". Each 26/6 answer
  to an open conversation checks it before reading the answer; out of reach drops the
  cursor, sends only 26/4 and changes nothing (`outcome: out_of_reach` telemetry).
  `npc_dialogue_26_6`/`npc_conversation_answer` now take the character. The cursor is
  cleared by the first away answer rather than on every move or zone change; a player
  who comes back in reach before answering simply continues the same NPC's quiz.
- **Finding 1 (tests).** `tests/quest_dialogue_chains.rs` no longer declares `at`,
  `player`, `accept_quest`, `turn_in_quest` or `tour_to_quiz`. It uses `ZoneQuests`
  (`at`, `accept`, `turn_in`, plus the new `talk` and `answer`), `zone_player` and
  seeds the quest at its conversation step with the new shared `seed_quest`; the full
  Seaside offer/visit/turn-in chain stays in `zone_quests_seaside.rs`.
- **Finding 5 (tests).** New: out-of-reach answers (walked and zoned), the final
  conversation's 49/13 at an extra spawn (Nomi's Little Brother cut to Hasti's step in the
  fixture copy, then paid through 49/14), a refused and a real 26/23, the 3000360
  Tazinna/Emuzz lines and answers, and a `Gateway::handle_at` test for start -> 26/4 -> 26/6,
  start -> 26/8 at Cole -> 26/6 and start -> 26/23 -> 26/6 (with a live-cursor control).
- **Finding 6 (notes).** `notes/Autopilot/T543.md` threat note and open gap corrected.
- Shared support extended (not copied): `Fixture::patch_quest`, `Fixture::session`
  (a logged-in `Gateway`), `gateway_send`, `gateway_talk`, `dialogue_line`,
  `dialogue_closed`, `seed_quest`, `ZoneQuests::talk`/`answer`; `dialogue_body` now calls
  `dialogue_line`. `npc_quest_chain.rs` includes `tests/common/gateway_packets.rs`
  for the login and framing helpers.

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

- A client that starts a quiz in reach and then walks, teleports or changes zone, then
  sends valid 26/6 rows: refused. Each answer needs the same active NPC and the 12 m
  reach (`INTERACTION_RADIUS`), same zone rule, as the 26/10 start; the cursor drops and
  the save is byte-identical (`answers_from_out_of_reach_end_the_quiz`).
- A 26/6 after a 26/4, a second NPC's 26/8 or a real 26/23 finds no cursor (gateway
  test), so a closed or replaced dialog cannot be resumed.
- No economy, item or reward surface is added: the 49/13 now names the clicked actor
  and still pays only through the existing 49/14 path.
