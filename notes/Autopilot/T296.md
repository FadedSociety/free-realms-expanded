## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

Attack surface: none of the new values are client-supplied. The rows and the band
are trusted startup/reload content; the kill payout comes only from the server's
alive-to-dead HP transition on a server-owned field actor and a loader-validated
table. A hostile client cannot create an actor, choose a spawn centre, set a
level, choose a band, award coins/XP, or move another player's field state through
this slice. The field actor's level is content, not a client field, so a client
cannot scale an enemy up for a larger drop or point it at another band.

Refused/ignored by existing production code or this content's loader:

- duplicate actor GUIDs, duplicate area ids, unknown/duplicate group members,
  `centre != position`, radius > leash, level outside 1..=20, a band that excludes
  the actor's level, respawn outside 1..=3600, aggro outside (0, 40], leash
  outside (0, 1000], empty/control-character ids, and a group without a
  reconstructed `(R)` source (`src/encounter/field.rs::load_field_enemies`).
- a band with min < 1, max > 20, min > max, coins or job XP > 1000, an empty or
  malformed source, overlapping bands, or duplicate overrides
  (`src/encounter/field_rewards.rs::parse`).
- a second claim of a consumed credit, an expired credit, a bystander with no
  damage credit, a replay on a corpse, and a payout the live wallet or active job
  cannot represent (existing T240 paths).
- The reward table is local content read from a fixed filename; no client path
  reaches it. A payout that is refused logs and returns `Ok`; nothing panics out
  of the shared tick. The new suite's malformed-band test fails at load, and the
  uncovered-level test kills the actor with `Ok` and no payout.
