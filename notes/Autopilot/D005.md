## Security note

A hostile client can send a truncated Close, spoof an unknown NPC GUID, try a
job/equipment/interaction request during a movie, address a terminal movie event
from outside the station, or finish after changing ingredients. The tests require
the exact refusal and an unchanged full gameplay snapshot. Rewards are accepted
only from the server-owned movie transaction after job, station and ingredient
checks. No client-reported result creates a reward.

One targeted unit characterization calls `apply_smelting` directly with two
21785 grant rows because the shipped fixture has a single product row; the
packet path cannot distinguish summing rows from taking the first row. Every
other completion case uses the real `handle_in_world` packet sequence.
