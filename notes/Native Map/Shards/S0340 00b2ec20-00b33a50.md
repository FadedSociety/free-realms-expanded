## Manager and store flow

- `00b300c0` configures the `InGamePurchase` service with `ServiceId=UP0017-NPUA30048_00` and logs initialization. `00b303e0` sets support-data-ready state and calls `MarketplaceCallbackHandler.OnSystemSupportDataInitialized` on success; `00b304b0` reports progress. `00b335f0` handles deferred marketplace callbacks and `OnInitializeSearchEngine`.
- `00b30200` validates marketplace enablement and product bounds before a Steam wallet-funding path. `00b33760` validates a credit-card payment-source ID, product range, and pending-request state before a funding helper. `00b33900` and `00b33a50` prepare subscription previews for credit card and Station Cash; the latter checks billing state and ZIP requirements. These functions show client-side checks and calls, not authoritative server transaction outcomes.
- `00b30ad0` and `00b30b40` construct and assign `Crypto::StringSecure<24>` and `<8>` values used by the credit-card route. Container/tracker helpers manage bundle IDs, group IDs, integer keys, and purchase-order records. `00b31940` requests node storage, constructs an entry through `00b25bc0`, and passes it to `00b2d890`; the latter appends it to a doubly linked list, links it into one of six key-selected buckets, and increments the count.
