## Promotional data and redemption

`ClientPromotionalManager` is anchored by its vtable and constructor (`00a07b80`). It owns a 64-bucket bundle map at `+0x1fc` (`00a06f60`, insert `00a07700`, remove `00a06b50`, clear `00a06450`) and a 256-bucket datasource map at `+0x308` (`00a07660`, lookup `00a069d0`, unregister `00a06a50`). Datasource registration (`00a07ff0`) replaces a same-name entry, hashes its name, constructs a `PromotionalBundleDatasource`, inserts it, and registers it in the client object table. The neighboring network-dispatch cases `009471a0` identify member-trial datasource names, including `BaseClient.MemberUpgrade.PetTrial` and `...MountTrial`.

The promotional packet family constructor `00a057e0` stores base opcode `0x72`; the network dispatcher `009471a0` routes case `0x72` to `00a08e60`. That function reads subtype 1 or 2 and routes to `00a08d70` (key-code redemption notification) or `00a08b10` (promotional bundle data). Constructors `00a05910` and `00a05a50` independently store those subtype values. The community packet catalogue has no promotional class, and the payload handlers do not contain their subtype constants. Accordingly, only `00a08e60` carries wire entries for its literal subtype constants; payload helpers and the two handlers retain explicit open questions instead of copied or invented opcode fields. The client path supports a receive-flow inference, not any claim about server-side redemption rules or rewards.

`00a08520` converts a redemption result into the `OnCodeRedemption` script event; `00a088c0` logs selection failures and forwards the result. `00a08d70` reads the bounded payload despite its error string saying “Failed to serialize,” a wording/data-flow mismatch. The shared readers (`00a072c0`, `00a07370`, `00a07850`, `00a079e0`, `00a07eb0`, `00a07f40`, `00a082e0`, `00a088f0`, `00a08940`, `00a089d0`, `00a08aa0`) perform bounded reads, update stream error state, and populate fixed-size arrays and keyed lists.
