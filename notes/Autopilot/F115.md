# F115 T020 review repairs

## What changed

- `prototype/osfr-tools/src/social_lists.rs`: an accepted invitation now checks
  `member_group` before consuming the pending invite or mutating either roster.
  A character that joined another party after receiving an invite receives the
  existing 40/5 result 1 and remains in the party it already joined. The invite
  remains pending until acceptance becomes possible, expiry, or teardown. A
  decline still consumes the invite and notifies the original party.
- `prototype/osfr-tools/tests/social_lists.rs`: added the A-invites-B,
  B-invites-C, C-accepts-B, B-accepts-A interleaving. It checks both rosters,
  the one-party mapping, pending-invite preservation, and the unrelated-party
  survivor after B's party dissolves.
- `prototype/osfr-tools/src/bin/osfr-gateway.rs`: added the same interleaving
  through the router with chat-recipient checks, reset the kicked character's
  spam gate before the revocation assertion, and asserted that its chat scope
  is cleared.
- `prototype/osfr-tools/src/bin/osfr-gateway.rs`: added `Server::end_session`
  tests for an owning session and a stale non-owning duplicate. The owner test
  checks group/chat teardown and the survivor refresh; the stale test checks
  that the live owner's active mapping, roster, membership map, chat scope, and
  chat recipients are unchanged.

## Reconstructed policy

- (R) A character in a party cannot accept an older invitation from another
  party. Joining the current party does not disconnect or overwrite the
  membership map. The older invitation stays pending, and its pre-existing TTL
  still bounds how long it can reserve capacity.
- (R) The client exposes no recovered 40/5 result meaning "already grouped".
  F115 reuses result 1, the existing no-group refusal, rather than inventing a
  wire value. The client-side meaning of this refusal remains unverified.
