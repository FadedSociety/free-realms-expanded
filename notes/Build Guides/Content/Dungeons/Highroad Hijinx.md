## Conflicts

1. **Leader name in a quest.** The radio-prank quest (`loc 925272355`) says the hooligan camp's leader is "Cal", while every goal string and the dungeon description name Mac. Cal is the leader of [[Tavern Cellar]]. The goal text wins.
2. **Collin.** The wiki treats Collin as an enemy. The client has a "NewbieZone Collin" flag grant (`loc 279606014`, `2030778605`) that points to a tutorial character. These may be two different Collins.
3. **Enemy list.** The wiki lists Hooligans and Hooligan-At-Arms. The client also names Hooligan Archers for this dungeon.
