## Threat note (rewards touch coins and XP)

A hostile client can forge a 49/2 accept, a 49/14 hand-in or a level claim. Each is refused by the
existing engine: the accept needs the offer, the job gate and the prerequisite
(`the_wrong_job_and_a_missing_prerequisite_change_nothing`); the hand-in needs the held end screen after
the last Talk (`an early 49/14` changes nothing); the rank is the server's own curve, never a client
claim; a replayed 49/14 or 49/2 pays and adds nothing. Rewards are coins and Chef/Warrior XP the job gate
already unlocks; no item is granted, so nothing sellable is created.
