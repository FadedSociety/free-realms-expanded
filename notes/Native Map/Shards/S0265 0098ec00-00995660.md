## Tracked-pointer registry and cleanup

`0081ce10` inserts object-key entries into a global hash table and temporarily increments the object's count at `+4`; the assignment wrappers immediately decrement that count and call vtable `+4` only if it reaches zero. Thus the wrappers at `0098ec00`, `0098ecb0`, `0098ed20`, `0098ed80`, `0098edf0`, `0098ee60`, `0098eec0`, `0098ef20`, `0098ef90`, `0098f000`, `0098f060`, `0098f170`, `0098f1e0`, `0098f5f0`, `00992710`, `00992780`, and `00992960` replace a tracked pointer without leaving a net count increment. `0081ad80` removes a key from the table and decrements the associated object's `+4` count, calling its vtable `+4` at zero.

The short slot-cleanup routines at `0098fc90` through `00990070`, plus `00990290`, `009902c0`, `00992ee0`, `00992f30`, and `00992f80`, are called from MSVC unwind funclets. Their `+0x28` virtual call obtains a key using argument `0`; `0081ad80` then removes that key from the global registry, after which the wrapper clears the slot. `009903c0` and `00990430` are exception-framed variants called by scalar deleting wrappers.
