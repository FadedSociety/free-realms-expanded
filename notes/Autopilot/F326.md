## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

Attack surface: the final diff is test code and notes only. The tests drive the
T240 production seams: `handle_shared_field` kills a server-owned actor,
`reward_for` reads server content, and the payout is checked-added to the live
wallet/active job; `shared_npc_view`/`interest_candidates` are the calls
`Server::sync_field_views` and `tick_shared_instance` make. A hostile client
cannot create an actor, choose a level or band, report a kill, set a payout or
reach the fixture. The canaries temporarily edited `src/world.rs` and the two
content files; all were restored byte-identically and `git status` shows no
`src/` or content change.

Refusals exercised and unchanged: overlapping/overflowing bands refuse at load;
an uncovered level kills with `Ok` and pays nothing; a replay, corpse hit or
second claim pays nothing; a malformed camp reload leaves the live field and
behaviour unchanged; an unrepresentable payout refuses one player without
stopping the field.
