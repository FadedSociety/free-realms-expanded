# Werewolf Dance Practice

Part of [[Content]] › [[Dungeons]]. Tags: **[client]** = game locale text (`loc <id>`), **[video]** = player recordings (frame key), **[wiki]** = fan wiki. Precedence client > video > wiki.

> Werewolf Dance Practice is a **seasonal special-spawn battle**, not an Atlas dungeon. The wiki files it under *Battle*, *Special Spawn*, *Super Spooktacular*, *Seasonal* and *Blackspore*. It is the vampire team's half of the Halloween story; its twin is [[Vampire Dance Practice]]. [wiki]

## Conflicts

1. **Item names.** The wiki writes "Spider Web Sticker", "Jack-O-Lantern Sticker" and "Ghost Sticker". The client has no item with these names. It has "Spider Web Stand-Up" (`loc 1889624620`), "Jack-O-Lantern Stand-Up" (`loc 1551788738`) and "Ghost Stand-Up" (`loc 2079854231`). The wiki's own Vampire page uses "Stand-Up". Not silently substituted: the table keeps the wiki name and the likely client name.
2. **Enemy name.** The wiki writes "Werewolf Break Dancers". The client has "Werewolf Slam Dancer" and "Werewolf Dancer", no "Break Dancer". It is unproven which is spawned.
3. **Quest linkage.** The wiki puts Pre-Emptive Bite with Drake, and the video shows its second objective naming the werewolves' practice. The wiki's final quest title for the vampire series is Another One Bites the Muck. Which title actually triggers the node is unproven.
