## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client could forge a 49/2 accept for a quest it was never offered (job, prerequisite or giver wrong), a 49/14
turn-in before the goals are done, a Talk to a recipient out of order, a position report for the Howling Hills it never
reached, or replay a 49/14. The production paths refuse each (`QuestEngine::can_offer`, `begin_turnin`, ordered goals, the
server-side radius test in `on_event`, the pending-turn-in state); the new tests assert that every such refusal leaves the
serialized save byte-equal (`assert_packet_changes_nothing`, `assert_no_offer`, an unchanged `saved` around a refused position).
Levels come from the server's own `Profiles::rank`, never from a client claim (`job_level`). Rewards are fixed values in the
record (at most 15 coins and 25 XP): no item, no overflow path, no client quantity.
