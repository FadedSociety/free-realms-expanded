## Overview

Seaside is a resort town on the western coast of Sacred Grove, south-west of Wugachug and west of Merry Vale, with a tropical climate and the only ocean view in Sacred Grove. [wiki Seaside] The client greets visitors with "Welcome to Seaside where vacation is an adventure!" (129921736). [client] The warpstone is the arrival point (client 2978274801; video `GCXrT74iaOU/00000`). The **Information Center** (Cole, Brenna) is "down the path to the north past the warpstone" (2194642825); the **boardwalk** runs along the beach with the smoothie stand, coconut vendor and music stand; the **Ocean Treasures Cafe** (Diggy) has the Pet Adoption Center to its north-east (wiki); the **Seaspray Inn** is "the first building ever built in Seaside" (3042670347) and is reached "down the road to the North" (638365284); the **Luxurious Oceanfront Homes** are to the south and east (3939893660 east side, 3524590790 "south of here, the first luxurious home"); the **Fisherman's Shanties** or beach huts are on the north side (4042850801); the **Tide Pools** are at the north end of the beach (439260072, wiki Snatching Snappers); the **Cray Caves** are "along the beach to the north" (1050326289) and the **Flying Dragon Dojo**, also called the **Abandoned Dojo**, is on the south shore with Kai's hut beside it (686974377). **Sandstorm Speedway** is a sub-area reached by "following the beach North" (2670444010). [client] [wiki]

The late Heroes Journal chapter "The Seaside Gloam Invasion" runs through most of the story NPCs below: Captain Sunspark sends the player to Mayor Gilly, then the quests lead through Seaspray, Crabcake, the Great Koodini, Kai and Orchid Eventide to the Nekark kraken fight. [wiki Heroes Journal: The Seaside Gloam Invasion] [client]

## Notes on important NPCs

**Heroes Journal: The Seaside Gloam Invasion.** Order from the wiki: Troubled Waters, Tides of Change, Know Your Enemy, Meet Crabcake, Cleansing Pearls, Stemming the Tide, History of the Dojo, Ghosts of the Past, Not the Ghosts You're Looking For, Unwanted Guests, Defeat the Kraken, Seaside Report. All twelve have an exact title in the client except "Cleansing Pearls", where the client reads "Cleansing the Pearls". Reading the wiki story with the client strings: Mayor Gilly sends the player to the locals about the cray (250427778), Seaspray asks for combat with the cray (3294890624), Diggy makes the gift for the shy cray Crabcake, who has the player cleanse the black Cray Pearls (95626197, 98608559, 1935635432), defeat the corrupted King of Low Tide (3399516797) and visit the Great Koodini (2952273857). The Koodini quest is an instance with Jintaka, Kai and Ty (video `1ps4QFcmi0Q`), after which Kai sends the player against ghost ninjas (2152078178, 686974377), the Koodini stone reveals the Gloam (1448076284), Orchid Eventide is fought in the cave below the dojo (38538413), and Nekark is fought in the water (2444632992). Kai then sends the player back to Captain Sunspark (181704207; "Seaside Report"). Reward: "an ancient pair of pants" (350292118). Quest-by-quest objectives and rewards are not in the sources. [client] [video] [wiki]

**Cole and Spritzy: the tour.** The player is told to see the view from the gazebo, talk to Cole at the Information Center, tour the listed places, visit the Seaspray Inn and finish with Brenna's test. [client] [wiki]

**Diggy and the Ocean Treasures Cafe.** Diggy is the only cafe NPC with a long quest list (nine titles plus the clam errand). The cafe also holds Jammie Swiftsong and Grekel at the card table, and the wiki lists Sockalug, Aziwug and Medina nearby. [client] [wiki]

**Doctor Callahan.** The last four Medic quests and the diploma are his, and he is the only Medic NPC in Seaside. See [[Medic]] for objectives. [client] [wiki]
