## Security threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client can send arbitrary `26/8`, `26/10`, `26/6`, `49/2`, `49/13`, `49/14` and
`35/x`-shaped bytes, replay a completed turn-in, name another quest or NPC, or disconnect and
reconnect at any point. The fix trusts none of it:

- The replay only reads server-saved `pending_turnin` / `pending_turnin_actor` plus the
  content definition; no client packet selects the quest, the actor or the timing. A stale or
  broken definition refuses that player's replay with `pending_end_screen_refused` and sends
  nothing.
- The actor written into 49/13 is the runtime GUID of a server-emitted 35/2 in the same
  batch, resolved from the saved content key; a client cannot inject an actor.
- The replay carries no reward; 49/14 settlement still requires the exact saved pending quest
  and uses the existing idempotent `quest:<character>:<quest>` grant key, so a replay cannot
  pay twice.
- Packets stay per-viewer (interest-filtered shared view); no other player sees or can
  trigger another player's screen.
- The guarded test skip is test-only; it changes no production validation.
