## What changed

- **Opcode 100 encoder now matches the client reader.** `encode_membership_activation` lost the
  extra i16 `kind` it wrote between the opcode and the member i32: it is now
  `encode_membership_activation(is_member: bool)` and the inner payload is exactly
  `[100, 0, is_member as u32 LE]` (6 bytes). `tunnel` and its length framing are untouched.
- **Opcode 189 was already fixed at the tip** by L048 (commit `f06eef0`, carried by L059): the old
  `encode_membership_service_flags(kind, flag_a, flag_b)` is now
  `encode_membership_subscription_info(lifetime_member, referee)` and already writes exactly
  `[189, 0, l, r]` (4-byte inner). The brief's Build section is stale on this point; its own byte
  gate (`[189,0,1,0]`) already passes through `membership_subscription_info_matches_the_native_reader`.
  No rename back to `encode_membership_service_flags`: the tip name describes what the client reads,
  and the lead accepted it in L059. Nothing calls either encoder (graph and `rg`, below).
- **Test updated to the reader layout.** `membership_wire_messages_match_native_reader_layouts` now
  pins the full 13-byte packet for member and nonmember: `[5,0,1,6,0,0,0,100,0,1,0,0,0]` and
  `[5,0,1,6,0,0,0,100,0,0,0,0,0]`. Exact equality also pins the inner length (6), which is what the
  strict reader enforces.
- No caller added (the brief says so); nothing sends 100 or 189 today.

already read without advancing. The `(R)` flag names (`lifetime_member`, `referee`) stay as the lead
accepted them in L059 / L048; the offsets and widths above are certain from the client.

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

- Trust boundary: none new, and none reachable. These are server-to-client encoders with zero callers;
  no client packet is parsed into them, so a hostile client cannot influence their bytes or trigger
  them.
- The fix removes a client parser desync: with the extra i16, the strict wrapper would reject the
  whole packet as trailing bytes. The encoder now always emits a fixed 6-byte (or 4-byte) body from a
  bool, so there is no length or count a client could grow.
- When the lead's moderator brief wires 189 (and if 100 is wired), the flag value must come only from
  server membership state, never from a client report. This diff adds no caller and no saved state.
- No logging, no file, no identity: nothing to leak.
