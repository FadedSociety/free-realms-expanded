   review's no-sign-off option (refuse + report) instead of writing one.

     `RecipeCatalog::load_runtime` records' `minigame_id` (46, 77, 134-137,
     139 on the shipped rows). The fixture recipe row carries a synthetic
     `minigame_id`, so the craft producer is exercised through the production
     loader in the canary.
