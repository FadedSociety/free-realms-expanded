## Overview

Sanctuary is the pixie capital and home of the World Tree and the Royal Family. [wiki Sanctuary] The client calls the inner town the **Inner Circle** (684736461, 980464000, 3313035787) and the south shopping street **Pixiewood** (1543230873, 2844638237); the wiki names it **Pixiewood Hills** and adds that it is the east-west street of shops at the southern entrance. [client] [wiki Pixiewood Hills] **Queensfields** is the farm and bixie area just south of Sanctuary (client 2688336640 via [[Medic]]; Honey 841702301). [client] The wiki groups Queensfields under Sanctuary and says the Bixie Hive and the Harvesting minigame are there. [wiki Queensfields] The **Memorial Caverns** lie north of the World Tree and hold the Forgotten Caves and Darvon's Descent entrances. [wiki Memorial Caverns] [client]

Places named by the client or wiki and used below: Royal Palace (Ayani Palace in one line, 111966493), Town Hall (405061045), Ayani's Pride Hospital (4022146842), the barracks (4170940757), the Royal Smithy (1320704948), Jacque's Cafe (343512814), the Pet Adoption Center and pet park (2741473540, 1958220662), Post Office (403291592), Job Center (3118256436), the cave under the Sanctuary bridge (1778243198), Bixie Defense tables (wiki Wake), the tournament board (679446827) and the Sanctuary warpstone. The Royal Vault is retired. [wiki Royal Vault]

## Notes on quest givers and trainers

**Queen Valerian and Geoffrey (Royal Palace).** The Adventurer story funnels into the palace from four entry quests (Local Hero, Sal's Suggestion, Farnum's Free Advice, Sharing Your Talents) and from the Cobblestone Village arc (Off to the Queen). The palace is gated by "Take a Number" (Pux Frillwillow) and a badge from Quills. Geoffrey gives most of Chapter 1 (22 quests) and is the final boss; see [[Adventurer]] for the full list and the Palace side quests (How to Clean, Belated Bonny, Lofty Letter). [client] [wiki]

**Captain Sunspark.** A Royal Guard captain who also appears in Shrouded Glade. He closes "Visiting the Job Center" and issues the Heroes Journal "report to Captain Sunspark" chains for the Seaside, Shrouded Glade, Sunstone Valley and Wilds chapters. Client line 3440602433 tells new players to visit the Job Center trainers and then speak to him. [client] [wiki] The "report to Captain Sunspark by the warpstone" lines (1920312839, 4240645652) are Shrouded Glade arc speeches, not Sanctuary placements (Conflicts 15).

**Job trainers living in Sanctuary.**
- Medic: Nurse Naia unlocks the job; Doctor Gordon blocks at level 5; Clover blocks at level 10; Beth Callahan gives the repeatable contracts; Shephard sells the Medic set. See [[Medic]].
- Warrior: General Glowlight, Doctor Gordon and Sir Whittington (blocks at level 15). The Warrior line starts in Snowhill with Drill Sergeant Dewey; the old Sanctuary barracks version is client-only. See [[Warrior]].
- Wizard: Osgood (blocks at levels 10 and 15), with Sandbur and Karin Spellweaver. See [[Wizard]].
- Chef: Bordon Cloo (blocks at level 5; recipes). Honey runs the Queensfields harvesting hand-off. See [[Chef]].
- Pet Trainer: Roscoe (levels 1 to 10) and the Master Trainer Kirill Moonrunner. See [[Pet Trainer]].
- Card Duelist: Sam Potts and Poe Tatters. See [[Card Duelist]].
- Postman: Felipe unlocks the job and Flitter runs the Post Office. The Postman line has no content page yet.
- Blacksmith: Steelbloom and his apprentice Nirvi. See [[Blacksmith]].

**Memorial Caverns.** Tori runs the volunteer tour guide quest (Looking for Volunteers), which uses Arnie at the Grand Exhibit Hall, Fhred at the Souvenir Stand and eight murals; Grayfoot narrates the murals. Lorna, the voice in Old Gregory's well, also lives there (see her row); her chain belongs to [[NPCs - Durango Zoo]]. The Forgotten Caves and Darvon's Descent entrances sit in the same area. [client] [wiki Memorial Caverns]

**Queensfields.** Honey (farm teacher), Sunflower (crop foreman), Beth Callahan (Medic contracts), Sergeant Larkspur (Southern Wilds bixie quests), Greyli Dustywings (Medic patient) and Bruce (merchant) stand in or near the bixie fields, with the Bixie Hive door beside them. [client] [wiki]

## Conflicts

1. **Tour guide name.** Wiki names Grayfoot as the resident tour guide of the Memorial Caverns; the client has both Grayfoot (901196118) and a tour guide called Tori (747530949, 1342827743). Both are listed.
2. **Looking for Volunteers giver.** Video shows a Preston Daly marker on the quest frame (`Qn2qi_xWpWA/00000`), but the frame note says the giver is only inferred. Client text puts the offer with Tori (747530949, 76219793). Tori used.
3. **Royal Honey giver.** Client Royal Honey text names Yuri Petaldancer (1199202751, 3639832850) and the video return target is Yuri (`G_ae2dL1V2c/00108`), but two client strings name Sunflower for a honey quest (1809597463, 2233070703). Yuri used for the titled quest; Sunflower's text may be a separate or earlier version.
4. **Sam Potts location.** Client 679446827 (tournament board) against 1155984690 (Pixiewood). Sanctuary used.
5. **Sir Whittington and Nomi.** Client: beside the tower-defense table (1966120509). Wiki Central Storyline: Whittington and Nomi stand in the Lake of Tears around the palace. Client used for Whittington.
6. **Sampson Idlefist location.** Client: Sanctuary (656570057) or south of Snowhill (3842204484). Wiki: south east of Snowhill. Both shown.
7. **Morninglory.** Warrior page: Snowhill. Client 2084804061 and 938380956 place a Morninglory delivery in Sanctuary. Not placed in this zone's main tables.
8. **Gulsh / Glush.** Wiki spells the Royal Vault troll Glush; the client text and name entry spell it Gulsh (1827985187). Client spelling used.
9. **Archmage Petulia / Petunia.** Name entry and most text say Petulia; one text (3256130939) says Petunia. Petulia used.
10. **Cursed Graveyard.** The wiki Sanctuary page lists Cursed Graveyard among local dungeons, but [[Cursed Graveyard]] places it at Stillwater Crossing. Not included here.
11. **Dewey.** The Warrior trainer Drill Sergeant Dewey stands in Snowhill; older client lines put him at the Sanctuary barracks (1388742978). See [[Warrior]].
12. **Sunflower page age.** The wiki page for Sunflower is a 2016 revision; client lines say "he" and "Sunflower in Sanctuary" with no mention of Queensfields.
13. **Medic quest title.** Wiki lists "Medic: A Strong Supplement" and Captain Sunspark's "Troubling Waters"; neither title was found in the client locale.
14. **Wiki NPC titles.** The Sanctuary page lists some shop names (for example "Archer Clothing & Weapons (lvl 19 weapons, lvl 20 clothes)") that no client store name matches exactly.
15. **Captain Sunspark "by the warpstone".** An earlier version of this row cited 1920312839 as a Sanctuary placement. That string is the closing speech of the Shrouded Glade druid arc ("We've regained our strength, but we can't sit around and let Arch Druid Cameilla hold the Athenaeum by herself. Report to Captain Sunspark by the warpstone"). 4240645652 is a matching line, probably from the dwarf arc ("We're almost ready to move out ... Report to Captain Sunspark, by the warpstone"; the arc is by wording only), and 582855445 sends the player to Sunspark "in Shrouded Glade" (3148258200 is another druid-arc "Report to Captain Sunspark" line with no place). The wiki places the finale giver at the Shrouded Glade warpstone. So the warpstone is Shrouded Glade's. What still ties him to Sanctuary is the wiki category and the Job Center line (3440602433: "Visit the different Job Trainers in the Job Center here in Sanctuary ... speak to Captain Sunspark"), neither of which gives a spot. Kept as a Sanctuary row with no stand. See also [[NPCs - Shrouded Glade]] Conflicts 8. [client] [wiki Captain Sunspark]
