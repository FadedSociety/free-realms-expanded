# S0343 findings

## Range and ownership

This shard moves from SoeUtil tree and span-pool primitives into the in-game purchase subsystem. The first range, 00b3f370–00b3f790, removes red-black-tree nodes and allocates/frees 0x58-byte pool blocks; 00b3f780/790 are this-adjusting thunks for the session-statistics tracker list. A second 0x68-byte pool and its tree helpers occupy 00b42210–00b42b80. Both allocator families use free lists, tracked spans, and tree removal at full-span release.

The 00b3f940–00b3fec0 and 00b415c0–00b43ad0 helpers read/write bounded scalar fields and linked collections. Several parsed record formats remain unnamed. In particular, 00b41620, 00b416c0, and 00b41760 consume a leading signed-16 pair, then a status and length-prefixed string before distinct status-1 follow-ups (00b3d8a0, 00b3f940, and 00b3d930 respectively); they are not counted dword sequences. Their callers place them in subscription-SKU, currency-code, and country-code response parsing, but the local fields remain mechanically described. 00b42210/50 are used as node-returning successor/predecessor routines by callers even though Ghidra inferred `void`; the records note this signature mismatch. The observed serialized fields do not establish the domain schema.

## In-game purchase classes

00b3ff00 handles accepted credit-card types in `ClientInGamePurchaseManager`; 00b40050 updates the cached store-bundle table; 00b40370/40930 construct and destroy `InGamePurchaseSessionStats<ClientInGamePurchaseOrder>`. Its dump method at 00b40630 records elapsed shopping time, order/card/wallet transaction lists, and item/group/category selection counters. The manager constructor/destructor at 00b41ac0/41da0 initialize and release substantial marketplace state. Response methods at 00b43de0, 00b44370, 00b44af0, and 00b44ce0 log wallet funding, membership preview/purchase, and third-party order-finalization results and request wallet refreshes. The membership preview/purchase handlers marshal response values into local typed callback arrays and emit events; these bodies do not establish persistent preview or tracked-transaction writes. These are client-side behaviors; they do not establish the missing SOE server's validation or payment rules.
