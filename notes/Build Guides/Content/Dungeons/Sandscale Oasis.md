## Conflicts

1. **Bonus goal in the client.** The client has no "Sandscale Oasis Bonus Objective" string (the index lists it among the dungeons with none), but video shows a bonus goal with a counter. The video wins.
2. **Bonus count.** The wiki gives no bonus count; video reads 10.
3. **Boss name.** One video frame reads "Cactus John". The client and the other frames read "Cactus Jake".
4. **Wiki stub.** The wiki page gives "35 Coins" and "210 Stars" and nothing else. Every other difficulty 2 page has a wheel, so the page is incomplete rather than evidence that none exists.
