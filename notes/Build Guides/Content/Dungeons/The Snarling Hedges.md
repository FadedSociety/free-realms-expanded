## Conflicts

1. **Shard spelling.** Wiki "Wizard's Power shard of Regeneration III" (lower-case s). Client "Shard" (`loc 1168914962`). Client used.
2. **Enemy names.** The wiki lists "Vine Bears". The client says "cursed bears" and "vine golem", and has no "Vine Bear".
3. **Article in titles.** The name string is "The Snarling Hedges"; the Bonus Objective group is "Snarling Hedges Bonus Objective" and the dev strings use "Snarling Hedges". The wiki and the Atlas use "The Snarling Hedges".
4. **Achievement text.** The client writes "the The" (a double article). Kept as it is.
