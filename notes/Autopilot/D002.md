## (R) rules

- Combat: 10 tokens/s, burst 20, spend 1 per shared-combat action, at most
  `MAX_SESSIONS` (16) keys. The `(R)` comment now sits next to those literals in
  `Security::default`.
- Chat: 5/s, burst 10, 1024 retained accounts, 1 action per budgeted
  family/sub.
- Handshake: `HANDSHAKES_PER_IP` = 5/s burst 10, 1024 keys.

## Security note (secure-code-guardian)

What a hostile client controls: source addresses, account churn, repeated
combat/chat requests, and reconnect timing.

- The key set stays bounded. At capacity only buckets that could already pay a
  whole burst are evicted; a busy bucket is never dropped, so reconnect or key
  churn grants no extra allowance. A new key is refused when nothing is idle.
- Keys are server-owned identities (peer address, authenticated account id),
  never wire ids. `Security` lives outside the staged gameplay clone, so a
  rolled-back request can neither duplicate nor refund a token.
- A refusal returns `false` and spends nothing; the caller drops the action and
  the peer stays connected.
