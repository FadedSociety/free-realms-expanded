## Deviation from the brief: learning does not activate the job

The brief's test was `learning_card_duelist_unlocks_and_activates_profile_120_once`. Production
`gameplay/jobs.rs::learn_job` deliberately does not switch jobs ("The job is not made active; the player picks it in
the job list (R)"), and every trainer test pins that (`warrior_trainer`, `postman_trainer`, `job_trainers`). The brief
also says no production Rust. So the test is `learning_card_duelist_unlocks_profile_120_once` and asserts the
active job is unchanged.

Consequence for playtest night: profile 120 has no starter outfit anywhere (Sanctuary `DefaultItems: []`; no
`starter_items["120"]` in `opening-anvil-placement-v1`, `opening-journal-text-v2` or the round70 play package), so
the learn says "Its starter outfit is unavailable, so it cannot be selected yet", and a 26/13 switch to Card Duelist
is refused by the Chest/Legs gate of `ProcessProfileSwap` `0x00bfb620` (`job_trainers::an_ordinary_player_learns_each_
trainers_job_once_and_only_switches_to_a_complete_outfit` runs this row and asserts that refusal changes nothing).
The job is learnable, not playable as the active job. Making it selectable needs either recovered Card Duelist
starter pieces or a lead decision on the Chest/Legs gate for non-combat jobs; both are outside this slice.
