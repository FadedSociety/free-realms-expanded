## Conflicts

1. **Item spelling.** Wiki "Energy Transfer Medic Helment" against client "Energy Transfer Medic Helmet" (`loc 373024539`). Wiki "Action Power Warrior Helmet" against client "Action Power Warrior Helm" (`loc 7513974`). Client used.
2. **Set name.** Wiki bonus set "Enery Transfer" against client "Energy Transfer" (`loc 176942616`). Client used.
3. **Stars.** The wiki gives "62 Stars", a fixed figure, where every other dungeon says "N Stars or less". It may be an editing slip. Unconfirmed by video.
4. **Goal list.** The nine goals are in the wiki's order. All nine have an exact client string; the order is wiki only.
5. **Two Tanglewood Forts.** The Atlas combat dungeon and the "Campaign 6" minigame share a name and a boss (see above).
