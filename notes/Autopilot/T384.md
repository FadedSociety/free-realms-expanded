# T384: every enabled quest is offered by its own giver through the real NPC menu

A test-only acceptance gate. For each enabled quest in the shipped split
catalogue it stands a character on the giver's content row, sends the ordinary
26/8 interact, picks Talk (26/10), picks the 26/3 quest row (26/6) and decodes
the 49/1 with the production reader. No production code changed. The gate found
no defect: all 50 enabled quests at tip `33200d2` are offered once each, by
their own giver, with their own title.

## How the gate works

- **Oracles come from production.** The enabled set, the giver union
  (`quest_ids_for_giver`) and `is_enabled` come from
  `QuestEngine::from_split_content`. The giver's stand point comes from
  `NpcInteraction::location`, the same row the 26/8 range check reads. The
  clicked runtime actor comes from `NpcVisibility::content_key`, the 35/2 draw
  set of every zone. The raw records add only the fields the engine keeps private:
  prerequisites, `job`, `min_level` and the authored `title_string_id`. The
  title id is the independent check on the 26/3 row and on the 49/1 card.
- **Making a quest offerable the way a player would.** Its whole prerequisite
  chain is completed, its job is active and the rank is its `min_level` on the
  local 100-XP curve (`Profiles::rank`, (R) per `character_eligibility`). The
  quest limit is the permissive fallback of 100 (`quest_limits` unset), so no
  offer hides another. Quests share one save per (job, chain size): a quest
  inside another's chain has a strictly smaller chain, so no shared save
  completes a quest it has to offer. At tip `33200d2` that gives 32 saves for
  50 quests and 48 giver walks. The quest content and `jobs.json` are
  unchanged since `8ee1c8f`.
- **A walk** Talks to the giver until it offers nothing more. It accepts each
  offer (49/2) and finishes any turn-in a Talk opens (49/14). Every offer must
  be an enabled quest of that giver, with its authored title on both the 26/3
  row and the 49/1 card. Each target must appear exactly once.
- **Historical prerequisites.** `3020014` (chain `3020012`, `3020013`) and
  `3100020` (`3100019`) need disabled predecessors. `repair_counters` keeps a
  completed disabled entry with empty counters, so the save still loads. These
  are the two quests T314 already reports, and
  `HISTORICAL_PREREQUISITE` lists them by name. Any other quest that needs a
  disabled prerequisite fails the gate.
- **Disabled sample.** 605 of the 655 records are disabled. 586 of them have
  no objectives, so `offer_denial` refuses them on the same line as the
  disabled flag. The test samples the 19 that have objectives, where the disabled flag
  alone stands between the record and an offer. Each gets a save with its chain
  complete, and its own giver is walked. 9 of the 19 givers are placed and
  drawable. The other 10 are `poi_anchor`/`unanchored` rows, which no client
  can click. A scan of the shipped NPC rows found 370 stale listings of a
  disabled quest. Every one of them sits on that quest's own giver, so no
  other menu could leak one (`offer_denial` also refuses `WrongGiver`).

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

This change is test-only and adds no parser, wire or save code. The gate sends
what a hostile client could send to a quest giver: a click from out of range,
a click from the wrong zone, a Talk at the wrong giver, a truncated 26/8, a
26/8 with flag byte 2, a 49/2 for another character's pending offer, and
Goodbye on the quest dialogue. Each is refused with no 49/1, and the whole
`GameplayState` plus its serialized bytes stay equal. The production refusals
are the existing `NpcInteraction` range/zone checks, the 26/8 decoder,
`offer_denial`/`accept` and the 26/6 row match.

## (R) choices and open gaps

- (R) The rank set for `min_level` uses `Profiles::rank` (1 + XP/100), the
  existing reconstructed curve. No new value.
- **`HISTORICAL_PREREQUISITE`** (`3020014`, `3100020`) is a named allowlist of
  the two T314 content gaps. The gate prints them and fails on any other one.
  Authoring `3020012`/`3020013`/`3100019`, or re-pointing those
  prerequisites, is a content task.
- **Members-only quests.** No enabled quest is `members_only` today. A future
  one would fail test 1 ("menu stopped offering"). Seed membership in
  `eligible` then.
- **10 disabled-with-objectives records** have no clickable giver and cannot be
