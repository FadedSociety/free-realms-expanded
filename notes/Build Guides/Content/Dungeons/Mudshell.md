# Mudshell

Part of [[Content]] › [[Dungeons]]. Tags: **[client]** = game locale text (`loc <id>`), **[video]** = player recordings (frame key), **[wiki]** = fan wiki. Precedence client > video > wiki.

> Mudshell is a **wandering boss battle**, not an Atlas dungeon. The wiki files it under *Battle*, *Special Spawn* and *Blackspore*. The player clicks Mudshell; after a battle is accepted "he disappears for an unknown duration of time". [wiki]

## Conflicts

1. **Item spellings.** The wiki writes "Bare-Knuckle Brawler Shoulder Pads". The client item is "Bare-Knuckle Brawler Shoulderpads" (`loc 500631404`). The wiki writes "Brawer's Atlas Hammer of Rage"; the client item is "Brawler's Atlas Hammer of Rage" (`loc 841605518`). Client spellings are used above.
2. **Archer wheel.** The wiki Archer list has no Battle Item Mystery Pack while all others do. It may be an omission on the wiki; unconfirmed.
3. **Stars.** "14 Stars" is far below other difficulty-5 battles' dungeon figures (for example 311 at [[Briarheart Caverns]]), as with the other special-spawn bosses. Unconfirmed.
