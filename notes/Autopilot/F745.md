## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

A hostile client can send: a 49/2 accept with no offer (refused by `QuestEngine::accept`'s
`pending_offer` check; new test), a 49/2 as a non-Kart-Driver (job refusal; test), a 49/14 before
the Talk (no pending turn-in; test), a replayed 49/14 or 49/2 (no state change; test), a 26/10
selection of the new `TurnInQuest` option before the objective completes (refused by
`begin_ready_turnin`'s `is_complete`/turn-in-NPC check; new test, menu preserved), a forged 26/10
option id or a stale menu (the service validates the option against the open menu and unknown ids
are refused; existing `npc_interaction` tests), and an unknown runtime GUID (refused). The client
cannot choose the 26/3 body, the objective text, the reward, the turn-in NPC or the quest state:
those are server content, and the reward is the record's server-computed grant paid once per key.
No client input reaches speech or payout. Refusals never consume the open menu because
`npc_select_26_10` stages the cloned interaction and only assigns it on `Ok`.
