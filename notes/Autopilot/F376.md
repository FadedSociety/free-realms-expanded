## Root cause found while fixing finding 2

The review's 165/4 layout was right: client writer `0x00c75ed0` (called by `BuyItem 0x00c77230`)
writes header, merchant u64, record id, definition, tint, quantity — quantity at offset 24.
`CoinShop::handle_coin_store` was reading quantity from offset 20 (the tint slot) and ignoring the
trailing word. That was a pre-existing wire mismatch. F376 reads quantity at offset 24 and updated
every 165/4 builder in the tree (`tests/coin_shop.rs`, `tests/vendor_sell.rs`,
`tests/coin_store_siblings.rs`, `tests/app_fuzz.rs`, `src/gameplay/w95_vendor_coin_store_regressions.rs`,
`tests/npc_interaction.rs`). `tests/app_fuzz.rs` caught the change in the first gate run; its valid
follow-up buy now sends quantity 1 at offset 24 and its `u32::MAX` quantity corruption targets
24..28.

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

The change touches client input (165/4) and the NPC selection path, so the trust boundaries were
re-checked. A hostile client can send:

- **165/4 with a forged merchant/record id/definition/tint/quantity.** The merchant must match the
  open session's NPC, the item must be in that vendor's `vendors.json` stock, the quantity must be
  positive and fit the price arithmetic, and the price/definition are server-side. The old parser
  read the quantity from the tint slot; a client could therefore put a small count in the tint and
  zero in the real quantity (or a huge tint to probe overflow). The parser now reads the client's
  actual quantity dword and ignores the tint, so that confusion is closed. Refusals return `Err`
  with byte-equal state and no grant.
- **26/10 for a menu it never opened or an option never offered.** `NpcInteraction::select` still
  requires the session's open menu, the exact NPC, range and the offered option id; the clone is
  committed only on `Ok`, so an error cannot consume or mutate the menu.
- **45/7, 49/15, 49/2, 49/14, 26/23 for quests it does not own or ids outside the crosswalk.** The
  engine still decides; the tests snapshot state and assert the refusals change nothing.
- **Malformed/oversized bodies.** Each family keeps its length/shape decoder and returns `Err`;
  no new parser and no `unwrap` on client data was added.

No new persistent field, secret, log of client text, or session-plumbing change. No client-reported
price or result is trusted.
