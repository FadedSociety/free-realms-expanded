  still holds `PLAYER_MAX` = 1000. Index 33 uses `startup_messages::build_hit_points` with
  `Some`, and the captured pair otherwise. Index 34 (mana) is unchanged. The flag is not
  cleared on `reset_session`: the encounter keeps its synced max across a relog, so a relog's
  Ready also reports the live health.
- **Thanks!.** `build_unidentified_193_2` is now `build_announcement_data`. It builds the row
  with piece_096's `AnnouncementData`/`AnnouncementRow`/`InitMessage::encode`, included the

  like piece_077). Only `action` changes, from 'Exit' to 'closeWindow'. The body grows from 62
  to 69 bytes. The typed encode with 'Exit' reproduces the old 62-byte capture byte-for-byte
  (pinned in `tests/world_reload_startup.rs`, and by piece_096's private oracle
  `captured_templates_re_encode_byte_identically`).
- **Private oracles updated** (both `#[ignore]`d, need `OSFR_GATEWAY_REFERENCE`):
  - `tests/gateway.rs` `built_startup_sequence_matches_the_captured_character_byte_for_byte`
  - `tests/startup_catalogues.rs` `mapped_catalogue_and_status_messages_reproduce_the_private_capture`
