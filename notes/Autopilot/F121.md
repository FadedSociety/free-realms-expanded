  grants its owned title in the same staged character transaction. The regression
  crosses level 20 with the real `realm level 20` gameplay request, then selects
  the awarded title through `152/4`.
- The same achievement scan runs on the non-opening login path, so reconnecting
  into an ordinary saved character does not wait for a later application.
- In-world sync is event-driven: it compares authoritative job/quest counters
  before and after the request and observes only changed counters. Existing
  progress therefore does not emit background `150/7` packets or perturb exact
  quest reply contracts.
- Achievement item rewards use a real gameplay caller:
  `sync_achievement_progress` queues their `RewardGrant` in the claim inbox. The
  shipped achievement recovery has no confirmed `item_ids`, so no historical
  item reward is invented. A temporary-content regression adds one recovered
  item binding and proves level 20 queues it and `108/3` pays it.
- Claim rows are no longer opaque caller bytes. Native reader `00a040e0` was
  recovered as: counted `u64` claim IDs; two dwords at `+0x144/+0x148`; an icon
  pair through `008caaa0`; a counted item-entry list through `00a03880`, where
  each entry is two dwords followed by the dword read through `00a033e0`; then
  the final pair at `+0x150/+0x154`. Entry `+8` is the item-definition id used by
  predicate `00a012a0`. `ClaimItem` now has a validated encoder and bounded
  decoder, and the complete response is checked by an independent test decoder.
- Pre-claim `108/5` definitions are authorized against the union of server-owned
  pending claim contents. The server echoes the client tracker, returns a
  complete definitions blob, and consumes no claim. Both native pre-claim entry
  paths represented by `00a04ea0` and `00a04f40` are covered by two distinct
  trackers. Post-claim tracker definitions still work. A failed definition
  lookup leaves no tracker or requested-id set behind.
- Pending claim IDs, grants and structured client rows now persist with titles,
  achievement state and claimed history. Saved rows are revalidated against the
  grant on load and against all bounds. A save/reconnect/claim test proves one
  payout after reconnect and no second payout on replay. Another regression
  changes live coins after queueing and proves the payout adds its item without
  restoring the old wallet snapshot.
- The account action budget is charged before `Gateway::handle_at` clones or
  stages gameplay. Every application child in a bundle is charged independently;
  one refusal drops the whole bundle. `108/1`, `108/3`, `108/5`, `152/4`, and
  `152/5` now share the account bucket. The regression exhausts one token and
  submits a two-child bundle: gameplay state, saved bytes, and replies remain
  unchanged. A separate regression proves a two-child combat bundle consumes
  two tokens, not four.

## Native evidence

- IDA `00a040e0` reads a counted `u64` claim-id list, the two metadata dwords,
  the two icon dwords, a counted list of 24-byte entries, and the trailing
  two-dword pair. `00a03880` owns the entry-list count/stride and
  `00a033e0` reads each entry's third dword. `00a012a0` compares entry `+8`.
- IDA `00a049b0` is the `108/4` reader (tracker, success, counted rows);
  `00a04950` is the `108/2` reader; `00a02ac0` is the `108/6` reader
  (tracker, success, length-prefixed blob). The test decoder consumes the
  complete response rather than an eight-byte fragment.
- IDA `00a04ea0` and `00a04f40` are the two pre-claim definition paths. Both
  create a tracker and send `108/5` before the claim request; the server now
  authorizes the pending row's definition ids, echoes the tracker, and does not
  consume the reward.

## Threat note (secure-code-guardian)

- A hostile client can forge claim ids, tracker ids, item-definition ids, title
  ids, and bundled children. The server accepts only ids present in its own
  pending claim/title state; rewards and title fields never come from the wire.
- Every claim/definition route stages a clone. A refusal returns no payout and
  leaves saved bytes, pending rows, claimed history, trackers and replies
  unchanged; the account budget is the only intended refusal cost.
- The account budget is charged before gameplay cloning and before the recursive
  combat dispatch. Every application child is charged once; an over-budget
  bundle is dropped silently and recorded, and cannot refund itself through a
  staged rollback.
