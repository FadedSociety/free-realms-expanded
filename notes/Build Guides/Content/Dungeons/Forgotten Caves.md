## Conflicts

1. **Archmage name.** The panel text, goal text and ability names say "Petulia". Quest line `loc 3256130939` says "Petunia". The client also has an unrelated "Petunia" dog NPC (`loc 3646026156`). "Petulia" wins. The Petunia spelling looks like a typo.
2. **Cave name.** The client has "Forgotten Caves" for the entrance, panel and quests, but "Forgotten Caverns" in one Ninja quest line and in two developer strings. Text uses "Forgotten Caves".
3. **Goal punctuation.** Wiki writes "Defeat Amoranth!" and "Bonus: Escort Archmage Petulia."; the client strings are "Defeat Amoranth" or "Defeat Amoranth." and "Escort Archmage Petulia." with the "Bonus: " prefix as a separate string. The wiki's combined bonus line is not confirmed in the client.
4. **Enemy name.** The wiki lists "Changeling Arcarus"; the client name is "Changeling Arcanus". The wiki's Darvon's Descent page spells it correctly. Client spelling is used.
5. **Enemy list.** The wiki lists Changelings, Berserkers, Bats and Arcanus. The client text adds Marauders and changeling ninjas for these caves. Marauder and ninja membership in the actual wave table is unconfirmed.
6. **Members coin figure.** The wiki's 151 has no client "Bonus: 151 Coins!" string. The client does hold "Bonus: 80 Coins!", which equals the wheel coin slot, but it is generic.
