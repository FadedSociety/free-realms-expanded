## Conflicts

1. **Stars.** The wiki says "98 Stars or less". The video shows 103 stars. Video outranks the wiki, so the cap reading is wrong or varies with score.
2. **Coins.** The wiki lists only the 49-coin wheel slot. The video shows a separate "Coins x90" payout on the results screen. The wheel coin is probably an extra prize slot, not the base coin reward.
3. **Entrance direction.** The wiki says northeast Wilds. The client text says the trove is near the camp "to the northwest". Both agree it is at the Robgoblin Camp in the northern Wilds.
4. **Bonus item.** Video shows the Medic "Antibiotic Medic Backpack" under the objective "Return to Terrence" in the same frame as the bonus tick, which could suggest a quest reward. It is also in the wiki's Medic bonus set, so it is treated as bonus clothing.
