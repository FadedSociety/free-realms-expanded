# T812 - Pet grooming and needs feedback (Sonnet builder, 2026-10-27)

Native 2026-10-27: a groom ended after about 3 s with only the timer stopping, and nobody could tell the bars fall.

## What changed
- `src/pets/grooming.rs`: a groom that ends because the bar is full now carries `PetWire.notice` ("Your pet is squeaky clean!"). `Gameplay::pet_outcome` turns it into a system chat line ahead of the unchanged 53/3, dry-off, 53/20 kind 4 packets. A timeout end sends no notice (the client timer already ends it). No client string exists for a groom finishing (IDA string search for clean/spotless/groom finds only `StartGroom`, `GroomNeed` and the packet class; the locale has only quest-specific lines), so the line is a chat notice (R).
- `src/pets.rs` + `data/content/pets.json` `need_policy`: new field `initial` (R, 60). `insert_pet` starts food, groom, play and mood there instead of 100, so a first groom is real scrubbing (50 points at the 3-per-second cap is at least 16 s) and feeding has room. Summoned decay raised 0.02 to 0.04 per second (R): 2.4 points a minute, so three minutes of play drops every bar by about 7 and a live 53/3 goes out about every 12 s (`status_epsilon` 0.5). Idle decay stays 0.01.
- Content bounds in `validate`: `initial` in 1..=maximum, summoned decay at most 1.0 per second. Decay stays clamped at 0 (`decayed`) and raises at the maximum (`add_need`), unchanged.
- Tests: `pet_care.rs` (start level and bounds; bars fall visibly in 3 minutes through `care_tick`, then 0 not negative after 2 h), `pet_grooming.rs` (notice on full bar only, a groom lasts at least 10 s, refusal fixture sets the groom bar to the maximum by reload), `pet_wired.rs` (the chat line reaches the owner through `Gateway::handle_at`). Fixture times in pet_grooming, pet_tricks and pet_idle moved from 6,000 s to 1,000 s so the pets keep the same 50 mid-range needs; pet_treasure's mood-threshold test derives its thresholds from the shipped start level; `pets.rs` idle decay is a range. Old pins on 100 and 0.02 were the only breakage.
- Canaries (reverted): without the notice, pet_grooming and pet_wired fail; with the old start level and rate, both new pet_care tests fail.
