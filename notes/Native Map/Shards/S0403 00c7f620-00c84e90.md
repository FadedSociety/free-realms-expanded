# S0403 findings: friend packets and rating protocol

## Friend and ignore family

RTTI identifies `FriendAddPacket` at 00c7f8e0 and `FriendMessagePacket` at 00c7f960; their scalar deleting destructors are 00c7f940 and 00c7f9f0. `FriendUpdatePositionsPacket` (00c80d40), `IgnoreListPacket` (00c80df0), and `FriendListPacket` (00c81040) embed typed SoeUtil arrays/lists. The BasePacket destructors restore base vtables after cleaning those members (00c807c0, 00c80e60, 00c810b0). Generic stream routines read bounded values and update cursor/error state (00c7f620, 00c7fd50, 00c80460, 00c80500); list helpers add and remove doubly linked entries (00c805b0, 00c80660).

`BasePacket::DispatchIgnoreFamily` at 00c81250 parses subcommands 1, 2, and 3. Branch 1 constructs and parses `IgnoreListPacket`; the other branches instantiate separate packet variants. The catalogue associates IgnoreList with family 112, subcommand 1. This shows client parsing capability only. The original server's list construction and policy remain hypotheses. Friends research at `Research/Pieces/087 Friends and presence.md` cites 00c7f960 but does not establish all packet payload semantics.

## Rating packet family

`BaseRatingPacket` stores family value 0xae (174) (00c81880). RTTI identifies `RatingPacketVoteReply` subcommand 0x12 (00c81e30), `RatingPacketSendFeatured` subcommand 0x16 (00c82ad0), and `RatingPacketVersion` subcommand 0x17 (00c82ca0). The incoming handler 00c869e0 explicitly dispatches those subcommands and constructs the corresponding objects. It also contains client-facing strings `Rating Server Version: %s`, `Housing.VoteResponse`, and `Set featured for %s to %s (owner %s, name %s)`. Thus 0xae/0x12, 0xae/0x16, and 0xae/0x17 are observed client protocol branches. The packet catalogue has no rating packet classes, so records retain opcode links without inventing catalogue names.

The range includes 0x48-byte and 0x58-byte variable arrays, including 36-inline `HouseRatingClientInfo` and four-inline `FeaturedInfo` storage (00c82090, 00c821e0, 00c84bc0, 00c84c20). Their entries are deep-copied and parsed by size-specific helpers (00c838a0, 00c83980, 00c83a50, 00c83d90, 00c841f0, 00c842d0). Six-IString records are initialized, copied, and released in 00c828b0, 00c82970, 00c83570, and 00c83650. Meaning of individual entry fields remains unknown.
