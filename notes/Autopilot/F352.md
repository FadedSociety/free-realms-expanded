# F352: Fix review blockers in T331 (commit 38a0d97)

The T331 review (`lab/autopilot/reviews/38a0d97-T331.md`) found one BLOCKER (copies of the
5 m/25-degree terrain sampler and of the profile-seeding/read helpers in the tests) and four
small follow-ups. This diff is **tests and provenance text only**: no production Rust, no hub
line, no new selector, no wire field, no persisted field, no content row added or removed.

The previous F352 patch (`F352-a1.patch`) did not apply on this tip; 10 hunks across
`quest_job_xp.rs`, `sheep_watch_dungeon.rs`, `thugamug_battle.rs` and
`support/npc_quest_chain.rs` were rejected. They were ported by hand. The tip had also moved
on in two ways that changed the shape of the fix:

- The sheep/thugamug sampler copies the review named had already been consolidated into
  `tests/common/field_battle_fixture.rs` by a later task, while `tests/brawler_job_chain.rs`
  kept its own copy. Applying the old patch verbatim would have added a **fourth** copy
  (`tests/support/terrain.rs`) and left the fixture's copy in place.
- `quest_job_xp`'s task table had grown from 17 to 18 rows (T334 added `miner.json`
  3_100_003), so the ported table is 21 rows, not 20.

The fix therefore moves the one remaining fixture copy into `tests/support/terrain.rs` and
imports that module from every placement suite, so there is exactly one owner.
