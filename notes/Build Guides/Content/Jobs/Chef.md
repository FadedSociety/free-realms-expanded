## How to read this page

- Tags: **[client]** is the game's locale text (`en_us_data.dat`), **[video]** is player recordings (frame key `video/frame`), **[wiki]** is the fan wiki, **[analysis]** is our own project notes about the original cooking movie. Precedence is client, then video, then wiki. Numbers in parentheses, for example `(2866001755)`, are client locale ids. Each title has a `ucdt` entry and, usually, a `ugdt` twin with the same text.
- **Matching caveat.** The locale file stores quest titles, descriptions, objectives and dialog as separate entries with no link between them. Every objective below was matched to a quest by wording: dish names, NPC names, places. A dagger (†) means "fits this quest, not proven". No dagger means the string names the quest's own dish or NPC and place. The client has no quest prerequisite or reward table that we could read, so order comes from the wiki list and from the text.
- Rewards (coins, XP, items) for the "Chef:" quests are in no source. Every reward cell reads "unknown" unless a source names one. The only quest or minigame rewards on screen in video are listed under Crafting minigame and Related quests.
- Wiki quest names differ from the client in places. The client spelling is used and the wiki spelling is noted.

## Overview

- Chef is a **crafting, members-only** job. [wiki Chef] Experience comes from Chef quests and from playing the harvesting and cooking minigames. [wiki Chef] The client tip says the same: "play the cooking minigame to level up your chef job." (4024725827) [client] [video: the same tip on a level-up panel, `-itStdznbPo/00064`, `4kAFL0X0KkA/00140`]
- **Members only, with a sample.** The wiki says that as of 5 December 2009 the job is free to sample through level 4: a non-member can change into it but cannot use it until they buy membership. [wiki Chef] The video cooking result screen for a non-member shows "Members Only Bonus! If you were a member, you'd receive this special bonus!" (`3caQP-As968/00754`). [video] The client lists the job name as "Chef" with the achievement ladder 4, 8, 12, 16, 20. [client]
- **Max level 20.** The client has "Reach Level 20 as a Chef." (3190927827) and the main series ends its gates at 20. [client] The video job bar shows Chef 1 and Chef 8 (`3caQP-As968/00644`, `0IaICGnTWWQ/00014`). [video]
- **How to unlock.** The client says "Learn how to cook by finding either Simone in Cobblestone Village, Auguste Frydaze at Highroad Junction, or Helena at Stillwater Crossing." (128204835) and, to a player without the job, "Sorry, you don't have the Chef job! Visit Simone in Cobblestone Village to obtain the Chef job." (304132489). [client] The trainer text offers the job: "Are you here for the Assistant Chef job? Great! I'll teach you how to cook Spiralmint Steak. I can even give you the chef job! Interact with the cooking table to get started." (331589818 and five near-identical variants). [client] The wiki lists a fourth unlocker, Bordon Cloo in Sanctuary, and two retired tutorial routes (see Conflicts). [wiki]
- **Cooking tables.** "There are cooking tables all over the world. Visit any one of them to cook!" (18340082) and "Cooking tables will allow you to cook as a Chef. Visit one to get started." (601631715). [client] Video shows a purple "Cooking Table" marker on the world in many zones (for example `ii3sbIqPM_U/00000` at the Wugachug warpstone, `Oa-JwM60kJQ/00014`). [video]
- **Quest line shape.** Three starting-town series (Cobblestone Village / Simone, Highroad Junction / Auguste Frydaze, Stillwater Crossing / Helena) each send the player to Queensfields or Perry's Pastures, then one main series runs Sanctuary, Merry Vale, Wugachug, Snowhill, the Robgoblin Camp and back to Sanctuary. The wiki says only one trainer series is needed before Sanctuary, "however completing all three will result in extra experience." [wiki] The main series has hard gates at levels 5, 10, 15 and 20 (see Trainers). Completing the questline earns the achievement "The Iron Chef" and a title (see Abilities and traits by level). [wiki Achievements] [client]
- **Counts.** The client has 33 distinct quest titles starting "Chef:" plus 4 "Chef: Reach Level N" gate quests and 9 "Chef: Level N" achievement entries (83 title entries in all). The wiki lists 27 distinct non-tutorial Chef quests plus 3 retired tutorial quests. 23 of the 33 client titles are on the wiki list under the same or a near name. The other 10 are client-only (see Client-only titles).

## Recipes and ingredients

### How recipes work

- Every crafted food needs a recipe. "All items made through Mini Games require a recipe. To view available recipes, interact with a cooking table. You can see a complete listing of the recipes you have collected." (3917233954) [client]. "These are recipes. Each recipe requires ingredients and creates an item you can use. Click on a recipe to see what ingredients it needs, or to select that recipe for the minigame." (3020645530) [client]. The recipe panel in video shows the dish name, an effect line and four rows of ingredients with "have/need" counts, for example Strong Arm Stew shows Bat Meat, Bundle of Gummyfruit, Bundle of Rainleaf and Spice, each 4/4 (`3caQP-As968/00654`). [video]
- Recipes come from quests ("Most recipes are acquired by doing Chef quests, but some are purchased from vendors" [wiki Chef]), from vendors (price 540 coins in video), and from the Coin Shop (client).
- A recipe item reads "This recipe teaches you how to create X." A few add "Requires chef level N." The levels in client text are 5, 10, 15 and 20 (table below). [client]
- Foods give temporary effects. Client food lines include: "increases the health and size of the player." (4207046225), "temporarily increases your attack." (2094613744), "energy" (1249629322), "health" (1336352398), and the Maker's lines: "maker's reach", "maker's sense", "maker's speed" and pair combinations (375258141, 707880, 3034278045, 4022527168, 3551215914, 3918485472). Those client lines are not linked to specific dishes. The effect column below is the wiki's.
- Food is also tradeable between players: "Food can be given or sold to other players to provide bonuses and helpful effects." [wiki Chef quote]

## Crafting minigame

All sources agree there is one cooking minigame, entered from any cooking table ("Cooking Table" interaction), named "Cooking: <dish>". [client] [video]

**Flow.**
1. Interact with a cooking table. A recipe picker opens: "Click Here to Select a Recipe." / "Select a Recipe below to begin crafting". [client] [video `3caQP-As968/00654`]
2. A short "Add these ingredients" panel lists the ingredient order with "Add!" and a "Show again?" option, for example Cooking: Honey Roasted Moonbean (`fjQ7luFN0Kk/00024`) and Cooking: Mystery Pot Roast (`w-9vtebjou4/00058`). Steps 2 and 3 show as "???" until the panel reveals them. Text: "Add ingredients to the recipe in their correct order. Watch out for the fake ingredients!" (1897282178). Controller text: "Use the Left Stick to move the tongs and hold the X Button to drag the ingredients. Watch out for the fake ingredients!" (3525393633). [client] [video]
3. The minigame runs in rounds ("Round Completed! Round Score: ..., Time Elapsed: ..., Perfect Hits: ..., Good Hits: ..., Weak Hits: ...", `IT3hnZ3ZtOE/00022`), with a pot or cauldron counter (24, 32, 12, 20, 25, 31 seen in different dishes) and a running score at top right. [video]
4. A win panel ("YOU WIN!") shows score info, goals, rewards and, for non-members, a "Members Only Bonus!" block. [video]
